Description

Axle stands take over the load once the trolley jack has raised the vehicle, because nobody may work under a vehicle held only hydraulically.

More

Professional and discerning buyers in Germany expect a steel frame with clean welds, a ratchet-and-rack mechanism with secure locking, a wide, stable base and a clearly legible load rating on each stand. The chosen version covers a working height of 275–415 mm, is rated 3 t per stand according to the listing and is supplied as a pair; CE and GS appear in the listing but are self-declarations and must be proven by the test report of an accredited institute. For EU online sales, GPSR labelling with an EU responsible person, a German manual noting paired use on level, load-bearing ground and packaging registration are required.

Return risk is low, liability risk high (safety product): a test report and batch control are mandatory. No sales figures found; the signal comes from range breadth and guides.

Request from the supplier

  • Test report from an accredited institute (e.g. TÜV, DEKRA) for load rating, static overload and endurance test of the ratchet, ideally as a GS certificate in the manufacturer's name; CE and GS in the listing are self-declarations only
  • EU declaration of conformity stating manufacturer, model 38120301 and batch/serial numbering; 3 t per stand permanently marked on the product
  • Clarify the unit in writing: price per pair or per piece (listing says pieces but describes a pair)
  • Material and weld proof, rust protection (paint/powder) with salt-spray test, burr-free feet and locking parts
  • German manual noting paired use, level ground and securing after lifting; EU responsible person and batch number under GPSR on product or packaging
  • Pre-production sample with own load test and 100 % final inspection before shipment (pre-shipment inspection)
