Description

The 6 t class crawler excavator is the workhorse for mid-size demolition, earthworks and pipeline construction and carries demolition hammer, sorting grab and pulveriser.

More

A premium unit offers a torsion-resistant tracked undercarriage, a cab with ROPS/FOPS protection and hydraulic pilot control, an EU Stage V diesel engine and a hydraulic quick coupler. Professional users in Germany expect CE with a declaration of conformity, hose-rupture valves on boom and stick, a German-language manual, clear service intervals and prompt spare-parts supply. At AE Global Traders it complements the mini excavator on larger demolition projects when reach and hydraulic power are needed.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity and CE marking for the complete machine (type, serial number, manufacturer – not only the trader)
  • Stage V type-approval of the delivered Kubota engine under (EU) 2016/1628; EPA/Euro 5 statements in the listing are not sufficient
  • Noise test report for the sound power level under 2000/14/EC
  • ROPS/FOPS test certificates of the cab (ISO 12117 or ISO 12117-2 depending on operating weight, ISO 10262)
  • Hose-rupture protection on boom and stick (ISO 8643) with test evidence
  • German-language manual, spare-parts catalogue, written warranty and a service partner in Germany or the EU
