Description

The brick trowel picks up mortar, applies it and smooths it; in demolition and renovation it is used for closing breakthroughs, repairing masonry and pointing.

More

The blade of hardened carbon spring steel is connected to the lacquered wooden handle by an offset S-neck and withstands bending and abrasion in the mortar bed. Professional users in Germany expect a forged or firmly reinforced blade with neck, a firmly riveted handle, a uniformly hardened and corrosion-protected blade and burr-free edges. Imported goods need GPSR-compliant labelling and a REACH-compliant coating.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EN 10204 type 3.1 material certificate for the blade material (e.g. 65Mn) and hardness record (HRC) of the blade
  • Statement whether the blade is stamped or forged, with blade thickness and neck reinforcement; bending test of the neck
  • Strength test of the handle fixing (riveting) and salt spray test report (EN ISO 9227)
  • REACH/PAH test report for lacquer and handle; for a wooden handle, check timber origin (FSC/PEFC) and the EU Deforestation Regulation
  • GPSR details: EU responsible person with address, batch number, German instructions
  • Approval sample from series production in the German blade shape (brick trowel 180 mm, S-neck) before ordering
