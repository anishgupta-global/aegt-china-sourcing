Description

The nitrile palm-coated assembly glove gives a secure grip on site during assembly, formwork, drywall and material handling, even with oil and dirt film.

More

A seamless knitted nylon liner with sandy-textured nitrile coating is abrasion-resistant, breathable and allows fine tactile feel. Professional users in Germany expect PPE Category II with EU type-examination, marking to EN 388:2016+A1:2018 (e.g. 4121X), skin-compatibility proof to EN ISO 21420 and sizes 7 to 11 with a German-language user manual. As a wear item the gloves are bought by the dozen or carton and replaced pair by pair.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU type-examination certificate (Module B) from a notified body for Cat II incl. body number – cross-check in the NANDO database.
  • Test report to EN 388:2016+A1:2018 for exactly this model; the code (e.g. 4121X) must match the print on the glove.
  • Evidence to EN ISO 21420: pH value, chromium(VI) content, size and marking information.
  • EU declaration of conformity with model designation, German user manual, marking (CE, size, pictogram, code).
  • Samples before series order: coating adhesion, abrasion, fit; incoming inspection per batch.
