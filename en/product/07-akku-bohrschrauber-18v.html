Description

The cordless drill driver is the most-used power tool in fit-out, installation and drywall work and runs in multi-shift operation on German sites.

More

Professional users expect a brushless motor, a robust two-speed metal gearbox, finely adjustable torque, a keyless chuck and batteries with cell monitoring against overload, deep discharge and overheating. For AE Global Traders a uniform 18 V battery platform is decisive so that batteries and chargers stay interchangeable across all cordless tools of the group. From China, CE conformity under machinery, EMC and RoHS law, a tested charger and proof of battery safety (UN 38.3, Battery Regulation (EU) 2023/1542) are mandatory.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 62841-1/-2-1 from an accredited body (TÜV, Intertek, SGS) and EU declaration of conformity (2006/42/EC, 2014/30/EU, 2011/65/EU)
  • Battery evidence: UN 38.3 report, cell test to IEC 62133-2, safety data sheet (MSDS) and conformity with Battery Regulation (EU) 2023/1542
  • Charger test report to EN 60335-2-29, version with EU plug (CEE 7/16)
  • Measurement record for torque, no-load speed and vibration plus endurance test of the gearbox
  • Commitment to supply batteries, chargers and spare parts for at least 5 years (uniform battery platform)
  • For brand imports: WEEE registration (ElektroG) and battery registration (BattDG) of the party placing the product on the market
