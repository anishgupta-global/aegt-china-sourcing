Description

The SANY SYG5260THB 390C-10 carries a placing boom with 38.1 m vertical and 33.4 m horizontal reach on a truck chassis and pumps up to 160 m³/h of fresh concrete.

More

Unfold the boom, set the outriggers, pump: it replaces crane-and-skip placing and saves set-up time on site. Professional buyers expect an EU-registrable chassis (Euro VI), CE conformity of the pump superstructure, a German operating manual and secured spare-parts and service support for boom, delivery cylinders and wear parts.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Not a basket item but a quote-based business: new truck-mounted pumps in this class are priced only on request in Germany by Liebherr, Putzmeister or Schwing, there are no public list prices.

More

The opportunity is the price gap for concrete contractors and rental firms; risks are Euro VI conversion, individual approval and a missing service network. Sell only against a deposit and after registrability has been clarified.

Request from the supplier

  • Chassis: request Euro VI proof and EU type/individual approval (CoC) – the listing states Euro V, which is not sufficient for a new EU registration
  • CE declaration of conformity for the pump superstructure (EN 12001) and German operating manual; have the CE/TUV/ISO claims from the SANY profile backed by certificates
  • Get spare-parts and service coverage in Germany/EU confirmed in writing (delivery cylinders, wear parts, boom joints)
  • Check transport dimensions, axle loads and permissible gross weight against German road rules (StVZO); fix outrigger span and boom length in the quote
  • Plan an acceptance inspection by a competent person under BetrSichV before first use
