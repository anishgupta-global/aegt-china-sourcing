Description

The large H-class construction dust extractor is the central machine for hazardous-material remediators and floor grinders: three motors reach up to 600 m³/h and 240 mbar …

More

a two-stage filter system of conical main filter and three HEPA H13 filters retains asbestos fibres and fine quartz dust. Jet-pulse cleaning de-dusts the filter without opening the machine, and the continuous folding bag allows dust-free sealing and disposal. For asbestos work the proof of dust class H per EN 60335-2-69 Annex AA is decisive; established units such as Dustcontrol are additionally certified by the German IFA, which an SGS certificate does not automatically replace. The machine has a compact footprint of 630 x 840 mm and can be lowered to about 1.10 m height for transport.

Customers are remediators, floor installers and rental companies that re-buy filters, folding bags and hoses (cross-sell). Main risk is the missing German certification (IFA/DGUV) with direct import; clarify proof and warranty handling before launch.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the original dust-class H certificate (SGS number, test report per EN 60335-2-69 Annex AA) and verify it with the issuer
  • Clarify whether an IFA/DGUV certification for the German asbestos market exists or will be obtained; without it, selling to remediators is risky
  • Request HEPA H13 test reports per filter (EN 1822-1) and a filter parts list with spare-part numbers
  • Fix voltage and plug for Germany (230 V, Schuko/CEE); CE declaration of conformity, operating manual in German
  • Test sample: air flow, vacuum, tightness of the folding-bag system, noise level
  • Agree warranty, spare-part and filter supply in Europe and lead time in writing
