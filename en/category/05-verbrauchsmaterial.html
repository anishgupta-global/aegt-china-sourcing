05 · Construction supplies

Consumables

Consumables are used up daily on every demolition and refurbishment site, and volume purchasing offers the biggest saving against German retail prices.

More

Chinese factories ship very cheaply by the container load, but cutting discs (EN 12413/oSa), anchors and screws (ETA/CE) and spray paints (CLP, ADR) need reliable test evidence before the goods reach a German site.

Abrasives cluster around Zibo (Shandong), adhesive tapes around Shanghai and Guangdong, screws in Zhejiang (Jiaxing, Yuyao/Ningbo). The Zhejiang cluster now also has Hangzhou (hand tools/utility knives) and Ningbo/Cixi (plastic anchors) as neighbours; no marking-spray maker with a verified location was found.
