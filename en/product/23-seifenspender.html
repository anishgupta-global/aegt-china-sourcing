Description

The wall-mounted soap dispenser holds 1,000 ml and covers hand hygiene in staff and sanitary rooms on construction sites, as ASR A4.1 requires for washing facilities.

More

A UV-stable ABS housing with key lock, inspection window for level checks and a dosing pump protects against theft and vandalism. A professional user in Germany expects a chemical-resistant pump, fixing with screws and plugs and, for sensor models, CE conformity with EMC and RoHS. Material declarations and pump endurance tests must be evidenced by the supplier before import.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • REACH/material declaration for ABS and pump parts (no SVHC above the limit)
  • Leak and endurance test of the pump (e.g. at least 50,000 strokes) with test report
  • Resistance evidence against soap and alcohol-based disinfectants (stress cracking of ABS)
  • Sensor models: CE declaration of conformity with EMC and RoHS test reports and battery safety
  • Sample with lock and key, mounting kit, dimension sheet and breakage/packaging check
  • German instructions and labelling, spare pumps and warranty
