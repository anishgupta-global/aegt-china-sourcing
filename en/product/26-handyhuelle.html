Description

Two-part smartphone case made of a soft TPU frame and a hard PC back that absorbs impacts and protects the back from scratches.

More

The supplier Prosub offers it as a sublimation blank with options: glossy or matte finish, open or closed frame, large camera cut-out and optionally a magnetic ring for wireless charging. Logo or motif is applied by sublimation printing, and special models and own moulds are possible according to the supplier. Discerning buyers check exact fit including button and camera cut-outs, print colour fastness and yellowing, hazardous-substance limits (REACH) and trademark rights, since Apple, Samsung and other trademarks may only be named to state compatibility.

(iPhone 16 Pro) EUR 10.99, CADORABO EUR 13.99. The offer is highly fragmented by device model, and new iPhone and Galaxy models force short product cycles (inventory risk). The added value lies in printing and customisation (company logo, motif, small runs from 1 piece) and in B2B sets; price pressure at retail is high. Return risk is high (wrong model, fit). Seasonal evidence was not checked.

Request from the supplier

  • Define the exact device models and quantities per model and approve fit samples (buttons, camera, charging port, magnetic ring) per model.
  • Check print process and colour fastness: test sublimation colours for yellowing, abrasion and scratch resistance; agree the artwork with bleed and colour profile.
  • Request a REACH declaration of conformity and test report (phthalates, PAHs, SVHC) and, with a magnetic ring, nickel release per EN 1811.
  • Clarify trademark rights: print no third-party brand or licensed motifs; use model names only to state compatibility; MagSafe is an Apple trademark, write 'magnetic' in the shop.
  • Define packaging (OPP bag, header card, EAN) with manufacturer/EU responsible person details per GPSR and VerpackG registration.
  • Quality agreement with AQL sampling, sample quantity per model and third-party inspection for series orders; Prosub states ISO 9001:2015 and an SGS audit.
