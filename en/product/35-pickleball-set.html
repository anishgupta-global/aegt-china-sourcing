Description

The set contains two pickleball paddles with a carbon face (Toray T700 or fibreglass per listing, 3K/12K/18K) and a polymer honeycomb core (PP/MPP/EPP) plus four balls.

More

The paddle weighs 225 g +/- 5 per listing; surface matte, glossy or sanded, core thicknesses of 11, 13 and 16 mm are stated. In the beginner and leisure segment, a weight around 220 g, a comfortable grip, clean edge finishing and balls for indoor and outdoor play count; association approvals (USA Pickleball) only if documented. As sports equipment the set is outside the Toy Safety Directive as long as it is not advertised for children under 14; GPSR and REACH apply.

According to dadslife.de (as of 30 Oct 2026) pickleball is growing strongly worldwide.

More

On Amazon.de the page lists sets at EUR 47.59 (Bagail set) and EUR 59.45 (Joola Blue Lightning, 2 paddles, 4 balls, bag, RRP EUR 79.95) and single paddles up to EUR 149.95. The China list price of USD 20.70-22.00 per set leaves room for brand, bag and bundle. Competition: brands such as Joola and many Amazon private labels. Return risk low to moderate (ball quality, workmanship); state the set contents clearly because offers contain 2 to 4 paddles.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the set contents in writing: paddle material (carbon vs. fibreglass), ball specification (indoor/outdoor, hole count), bag yes/no.
  • Prove weight (225 g +/- 5) and core-thickness tolerance per batch; have the 11/13/16 mm statement in the listing confirmed.
  • Have the USA Pickleball approval documented only if it is used in advertising.
  • PAH/REACH test report for grip tape, edge guard and plastic parts.
  • One sample (possible per listing) with hitting test, edge check and ball bounce test before release.
  • Define packaging and EU marking under GPSR/VerpackG.
