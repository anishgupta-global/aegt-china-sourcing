Description

The folding rule (colloquially “Zollstock”) is the classic hand measure for carpenters, drywallers and painters and is mostly sold in Germany in EC class III.

More

What matters are play-free joints that allow precise setting up, an abrasion-resistant scale readable on both sides and segments that do not warp after frequent folding. The ABS version is light, insensitive to moisture and low-cost; wood and fibreglass versions remain the premium alternative.
