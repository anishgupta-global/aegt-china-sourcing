Description

The gas heating cannon uses propane and a fan to produce large amounts of heat quickly for halls, shells, tents and winter construction sites; it needs mains power only for the fan (230 V).

More

Because it is direct fired, exhaust gases (CO, CO2, water vapour) enter the room: it may only be used in well-ventilated areas with sufficient supply and exhaust air, not in closed or poorly ventilated rooms. Users in Germany expect CE under the Gas Appliances Regulation (EU) 2016/426 with a certificate from a notified body, flame supervision, overheat protection, a pressure regulator and a gas hose to EN 16436-1, a German operating manual and compliance with the workplace limit values under TRGS 900. For AE Global Traders the unit complements the indirect-fired diesel construction heater as an economical solution for open areas and halls.

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under Regulation (EU) 2016/426 with an EU type-examination certificate from a notified body (not a self-declaration only).
  • Test report to the applicable product standard (e.g. EN 1596) with CO emission values and appliance category for propane (EN 437).
  • Pressure regulator and gas hose (EN 16436-1) proven in the scope of delivery or as accessories; nozzles designed for propane.
  • Functional test of flame supervision and overheat protection per lot; photo of the rating plate showing kW, gas type, pressure, CE and manufacturer.
  • German operating manual with warnings (CO, ventilation, minimum distances, no use in closed rooms), at least 12 months warranty and available spare parts (burner, ignition, fan).
