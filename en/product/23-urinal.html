Description

The wall-hung urinal in white sanitary porcelain is the space-saving, low-maintenance standard solution for staff toilets, sanitary containers and heavily used refurbishment projects.

More

The dense, glazed ceramic with a dirt-repellent surface is quick to clean, and the rear inlet with flush valve or flush pipe keeps water use and installation effort low. A professional user in Germany expects testing to EN 13407, dimensionally accurate fixing and connection points and a complete mounting set with a German manual. Before import, a test report, REACH compliance of the glaze and, if a flushing unit is supplied, its drinking-water suitability must be proven.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 13407 (function, flushing performance, water absorption, glaze quality) from an accredited laboratory
  • Dimension sheet with fixing, inlet and outlet dimensions; sample before series production for a fit check
  • If a flushing unit is supplied: proof to EN 12541 incl. drinking-water suitability and backflow protection (EN 1717)
  • REACH material declaration for the glaze (lead, cadmium) and ISO 9001 certificate of the factory
  • Commitment on packaging and breakage rate (corner protection, palletising, spare pieces), Incoterm and transport insurance
