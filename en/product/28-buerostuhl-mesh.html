Description

The mesh swivel chair is designed for long days in site management, dispatch and administration: a high-back backrest with lumbar support, an adjustable headrest and a tilt mechanism keep posture dynamic.

More

Quality hinges on a certified gas lift, a sturdy base and castors suited to the floor covering. Professional buyers in Germany expect a test report to EN 1335, the GS mark where required, plus documented fire behaviour and substance compliance of the covers. Spare parts for gas lift, castors and armrests should stay available for years.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 1335-1/-2/-3 from an accredited lab for exactly this model (check the model number in the report)
  • Gas-lift certificate (class 3 or 4, TÜV/SGS) with batch number, plus a test report for the castors to EN 12529 (type W for hard floors)
  • GS certificate, if advertised (verify testing body and certificate number)
  • Fire behaviour of cover and padding (EN 1021-1/-2) and a REACH declaration (azo dyes, SVHC)
  • Load rating of at least 110 kg and a written warranty of at least 2 years; spare parts (gas lift, castors, armrests) available
  • German adjustment and care instructions, plus a pre-shipment sample to check mesh tension, armrests and mechanism
