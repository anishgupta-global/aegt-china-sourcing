Description

The heated vest warms stomach, back and neck through carbon-fibre heating elements and can be regulated in three levels; it suits long working days outdoors, security duty or cold workplaces.

More

Professional buyers expect wash- and cold-resistant workmanship, reliable temperature limiting, a safely installed battery with protection circuit and a clear USB-C connection concept. As an electrical device with a lithium battery the product brings clearly more compliance effort for the importer than textiles (EMC, RoHS, Batteries Regulation, ElektroG registration, UN 38.3); the test reports must be on file before ordering. As a trend product of the winter season the vest complements winter jacket and softshell and can be combined with a spare power bank.

Request from the supplier

  • Battery test reports: UN 38.3, IEC 62133-2, cell manufacturer, voltage, Wh rating (observe the 100 Wh air-freight limit)
  • Electrical safety: report to EN 60335-2-17 incl. overtemperature protection; confirm operating voltage in writing (up to 75 V DC outside the Low Voltage Directive, otherwise CE under 2014/35/EU)
  • EMC test report (e.g. EN IEC 55014-1/-2) and RoHS declaration with test report; check the UL, FCC, CE, RoHS certificates named in the profile in the original with numbers
  • Durability: function test after 10-20 washes, flex and pull test on heating wire and cable, check of the connector
  • Clarify registration and marking: ElektroG (stiftung ear), battery registration, LUCID, crossed-out wheelie bin on vest and battery
  • German instructions and safety information, manufacturer details and EU responsible person; agree dangerous-goods shipping (UN 3481) with the forwarder
