More

According to the listing the water column is 1,000–1,500 mm for the fly and over 3,000 mm for the floor; model RZ-8-001 weighs 3.5 kg. Pop-up tents are the classic solution for festivals, the beach and short trips when speed and low weight matter more than weather resistance in continuous rain. Discerning buyers look for welded seams, a documented water column, PFAS-free impregnation, sturdy sheathed fibreglass poles, and pegs, guy lines and a bag in the scope of delivery.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the target size for 2–3 persons (e.g. a larger floor area than 210 x 120 cm); only advertise the person count with a plausible sleeping area per person.
  • Prove the water column with a test report (EN 20811 / ISO 811) for fly and floor; seams welded or taped, seam tightness test on sample and series.
  • Confirm PFAS-free impregnation; REACH/azo report; clarify the flame-retardancy requirement of the target market.
  • Fibreglass poles: bend test, protective sheath, spare poles; pegs, guy and storm lines and bag in the scope of delivery.
  • Folding instructions (folding is the most common return reason) with picture/QR video, bag pack size and German manual; EU responsible person (GPSR), LUCID.
  • Supplier listed on Made-in-China for about 1 year: review the CTI audit report (listing ID QIP-ASI2541851), agree a factory visit or video audit.
