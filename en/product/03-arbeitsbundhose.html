Description

Work trousers are the most-worn garment on demolition, strip-out and renovation sites and must withstand kneeling, crawling and sharp edges.

More

Quality models use dense cotton canvas with triple stitching, Cordura reinforcement at the knees and pockets, and knee-pad pockets for knee protection. A professional user in Germany expects an anatomical fit, a size chart from 44 to 64, a durable zip and freedom from harmful substances under REACH. Normatively the trousers are workwear under EN ISO 13688; with inserted knee protection EN 14404 also applies.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report on tensile strength, seam strength, dimensional change after washing and colour fastness of the fabric
  • REACH declaration of conformity or test report on azo dyes, formaldehyde and heavy metals
  • For knee-pad pockets: declaration of conformity of the knee protection to EN 14404 and Regulation (EU) 2016/425
  • Size chart in EU measurements with fit samples in at least three sizes before production
  • Proof of the Cordura reinforcement (material certificate or supplier invoice) and origin of the zip and trims
  • Production sample with wash test (5 washes at 60 °C) for dimensional change and seam stability
