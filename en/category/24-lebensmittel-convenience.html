Food & Convenience

Food and convenience is a fringe range for a construction group: tea, instant noodles, snacks, drinks, rice and instant coffee serve site catering or a private label.

More

Unlike machines and tools, food law applies instead of CE marking: the importer is liable as the food business operator for labelling, traceability and hygiene.

Instant-food and beverage makers are concentrated in the Yangtze Delta (Shanghai, Hangzhou/Zhejiang) and in Tianjin; the tea suppliers in the listings are in Zhejiang, Hunan and Anhui. Newly added: Uni-President China (Shanghai, neighbour of Want Want) and Hangzhou Wahaha Group (Hangzhou, neighbour of Nongfu Spring), plus Zhejiang Chunli Tea as the tea price source; no further maker besides Tingyi was evidenced in Tianjin. No rice producer in these clusters has been evidenced. Instant coffee comes from Hainan (Haikou, Xinglong beans) and Yunnan (Dehong, Mangshi); jasmine tea brands are based in Fujian (Zhangzhou), among others.
