Description

The lean-to ladder is the light single-section ladder leaned against a wall, roof edge or pole, covering painting, plastering, roofing and maintenance work.

More

Buyers expect extruded aluminium stiles, non-slip rungs at 28 cm spacing, non-slip feet, a wall-friendly ladder top and an EN 131 test report stating the 150 kg maximum load. The maker supplies the AS0106A to AS0113A series with 6 to 13 rungs.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Hornbach lists aluminium lean-to ladders with 12 rungs (WERNER, 3.8 m working height) for EUR 89, 9 rungs (CLASSIK) for EUR 74 and 14 rungs (WERNER) for EUR 99.

More

Bulky goods of about 3.4 m length: shipping and transport damage drive the margin. A ladder fall is a liability risk: an EN 131 test report, labelling and product liability insurance are mandatory.

Request from the supplier

  • Request an EN 131-1/-2 test report (accredited lab, optionally GS mark) for the 12-rung model AS0112A; the listing only says "EN131"
  • Check stile profile and wall thickness, rung joints (pressing/riveting), feet and ladder top on a sample
  • Have load and torsion tests run on a sample (150 kg), marking and pictograms per EN 131-3
  • Request a binding price for 12 rungs (listing: USD 14-17 band for the series, MOQ 100 pcs)
  • Clarify German user instructions, GPSR labelling (manufacturer, EU responsible person) and product liability insurance
