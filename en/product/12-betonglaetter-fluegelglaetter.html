Description

The power trowel smooths and closes the surface of freshly placed concrete floors and industrial screeds with rotating blades and is indispensable for floor slabs, hall floors and yard areas.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a four-stroke petrol engine from a branded manufacturer (Honda GX class) with EU engine approval, a sturdy gearbox head with blade pitch adjustment, a folding guide handle with dead-man switch, a circumferential guard ring and spare blades and finishing discs as wear parts. The machine must be delivered with CE marking, a declaration of conformity under the Machinery Directive and the Outdoor Noise Directive, EU engine approval (Stage V) and a German operating manual. As the importer, AE Global Traders legally becomes the manufacturer.
