Request from the supplier

  • Have a pull and cycle test of the reel documented: pull force, clean retraction, service life (e.g. 10,000 cycles), re-measure cord length (listing 75 cm).
  • Material and pollutants: ABS or PP instead of PVC, REACH/SVHC declaration, cadmium and lead test for plastic and metal parts.
  • Clarify attachment: the listing names a reel with cord and no carabiner; define carabiner or belt clip, have nickel release tested to EN 1811 (alternative Lianxin names belt clip and carabiner in its title).
  • Printing: process (screen, pad, UV, heat transfer), Pantone matching, abrasion test, minimum quantity and surcharge, approve a pre-production sample.
  • Fit test with ID-1 cards (PVC 0.76 mm, thicker RFID cards) and thumb cut-out; edges deburred, no sharp moulding flash.
  • GPSR: manufacturer and EU responsible person on the packaging, note not for children (strangulation risk from the cord), batch traceability.
