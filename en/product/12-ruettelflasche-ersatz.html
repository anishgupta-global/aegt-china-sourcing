Description

The vibrator flask is the wear part of the internal vibrator: it is immersed in fresh concrete, de-aerates and compacts it, and wears on casing, bearing and shaft.

More

Professional users expect a flask of hardened steel with sealed bearing, a flexible shaft in an abrasion-resistant, oil- and acid-resistant protective hose and a drive connection that fits the motor unit exactly (diameter, coupling profile, direction of rotation). Replacement flasks are sold in diameters of 35 to 45 mm and hose lengths of 1.5 to 6 m; references are the STAHLWERK flasks at Hornbach (dia. 35 mm, 2 m and 3 m) and the EBERTH replacement flask at ManoMano. A German manual and an unambiguous statement of the drive connection belong to the delivery.

Request from the supplier

  • Record the drive connection: flask diameter, coupling profile (hex, square or thread), direction of rotation and speed of the motor; fit test on the catalogue internal vibrator
  • Material evidence: flask steel (hardness), shaft construction, protective hose (oil- and acid-resistant); REACH declaration
  • Bearing tightness against cement slurry and endurance-run record (water bath or fresh concrete), including shaft temperature rise
  • Conformity: test report to EN 12649 and EN 60745-2-12 for the system of motor and flask; German manual with dimensions
  • Unit prices per diameter and length (2/3/4/6 m), mixed pack and MOQ; warranty of at least 12 months
