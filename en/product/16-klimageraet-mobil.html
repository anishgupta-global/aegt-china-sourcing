Description

The mobile air conditioner cools site offices, containers, technical rooms and temporary workplaces without fixed installation; with an exhaust hose at the window it is operational in minutes.

More

A sturdy housing with castors, remote control, timer and condensate evaporation are standard for professional use. Users in Germany expect CE, conformity with EN 60335-2-40, a refrigerant compliant with the F-gas regulation (R290 only, GWP 3; R134a, R410A and R32 are prohibited in movable monoblock units because their GWP is 150 or more), the data required by the Ecodesign and Energy Label regulations and a German manual. The manufacturer must prove cooling capacity and sound level per EN 14511 and EN 12102-1.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity with test reports (EN 60335-2-40, EMC) from an accredited body.
  • Test report for cooling capacity and power consumption per EN 14511 and sound power per EN 12102-1.
  • Ecodesign (EU) 206/2012 and Energy Label (EU) 626/2011 data as product data sheet and label.
  • Refrigerant: only R290 (GWP 3) is permitted; R134a, R410A and R32 are prohibited in movable monoblock air conditioners (GWP >= 150) under F-gas Regulation (EU) 2024/573 (formerly 517/2014). Provide charge, F-gas labelling and safety documents for flammable refrigerants.
  • German manual, window kit included in delivery and spare-parts list.
  • At least 12 months warranty and take-back / WEEE proof (registration in Germany, stiftung ear).
