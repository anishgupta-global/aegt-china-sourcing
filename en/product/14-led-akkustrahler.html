Description

The cordless LED work light brightens work areas without a mains connection, for example in basements, shafts, attics and during demolition before the site distribution board is connected …

More

and replaces cable runs that people trip over on site. Professional users expect an impact- and splash-proof housing (at least IP54), a Li-ion battery with charge indicator and deep-discharge protection, flicker-free light without glare and a stable stand, hook or magnet for use in awkward positions. CE conformity (LVD, EMC, RoHS), cell testing to EN 62133-2 and UN 38.3 and ElektroG and battery registration are mandatory; brands such as Brennenstuhl, Makita or Bosch deliver reliable light output and runtime. Imported units from China are only marketable with independent test reports and demonstrably measured light output.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test reports to EN 60598-1/-2-5 and EN 62471 from an accredited lab (e.g. TÜV, VDE, SGS)
  • Battery: test report EN 62133-2 and UN 38.3 summary (transport), charge protection and deep-discharge protection
  • Measured luminous flux, colour temperature and flicker-free operation (integrating-sphere report) against datasheet values
  • CE declaration of conformity (2014/35/EU, 2014/30/EU, RoHS) plus ElektroG and battery registration by the importer
  • Sample test: runtime at full power, IP54 protection (EN 60529), drop test and charge cycles
