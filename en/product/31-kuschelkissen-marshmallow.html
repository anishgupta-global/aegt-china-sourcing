Description

The round marshmallow-style cuddle cushion is the private-label answer to the squishy plush wave: a compact, extra-soft cushion with an embroidered face in the colour and shape of your choice.

More

The supplier's base (model XY-250) is a round cushion of 35 x 40 cm made of 100% polyester with cotton/PP fibre filling, embroidered and customisable in size and colour via OEM/ODM. Face and character must be an own design; copies of known brands such as Squishmallows are ruled out for trademark reasons. Quality markers are firmly embroidered eyes and nose without hard parts, double seams, even soft-and-springy filling, low-odour fabric and needle/metal detection. If the cushion is marketed as a toy, the Toy Safety Directive and EN 71 apply; the supplier's listing does not show this testing.

Request from the supplier

  • Request EN 71-1/-2/-3 test reports from an accredited lab for exactly this design and material; the XY-250 listing names no EN 71 testing (only company certificates ISO 9001/CE, BSCI per profile).
  • Define the age group (under or from 3 years, stricter requirements under 3) and make the face embroidered only, without loose parts; have seams, embroidery threads and filling tested for small parts and tensile strength.
  • Fix fabric quality (pile, weight g/m2), fill weight and recovery behaviour on the sample and weigh it; the price tiers US$ 2.02-2.20 apply to the base model, ask separately for face embroidery and character design.
  • Protect the own character design (e.g. Community design), check against Squishmallows/Jellycat; agree an NDA and an ownership clause for patterns and embroidery files.
  • Define labelling under GPSR and toy law (manufacturer, EU responsible person, type/batch number, German warnings) and care instructions; anticipate the new EU Toy Safety Regulation (digital product passport, PFAS/bisphenol bans) in the supplier agreement.
  • Factory visit in Dongyang: check factory size (per profile about 6,000 sqm, about 200 staff), BSCI report and SGS audit report (ID QIP-ASI253726 per profile); business type is manufacturer and trading.
