Description

The steel shear is the standard tool for dismantling steel structures, beams, pipes and scrap with the excavator, used in demolition, scrap recycling and industrial dismantling.

More

The researched model WB04-08 (Hydraulic Steel Shear from Yantai Wenbo) is designed for excavators of 18 to 27 t, weighs 1,900–2,100 kg, opens 515 mm and, per data sheet, reaches 70 to 236 t cutting force depending on the measuring point at 28–30 MPa working pressure; the round-steel rating is 30 mm. Branded references are the NPK K-7J (excavators 6–9 t, 132 t force) and the Genesis GXT 335R (3.2 t, 584 mm opening, for 18–25 t boom-mounted or 26–37 t stick-mounted), both from Schlueter Baumaschinen on request only. Professionals look for blade material and geometry with reversible blades, a durable slewing unit, speed valve, case-drain connection and a declaration of conformity under the Machinery Directive.

Request from the supplier

  • Request CE marking, EC declaration of conformity and German operating instructions before ordering (Machinery Directive, Machinery Regulation from 20 Jan 2027)
  • Fix the model in writing: the listing carries the code WB03 and a series price, the table names WB04-08 for 18–27 t; have weight, opening, pressure and oil flow confirmed in the quotation
  • Have the fit to the excavator confirmed: carrier weight and tipping stability with 1,900–2,100 kg attachment weight, oil flow and pressure of the auxiliary hydraulics, case-drain line, mounting (pin pattern or quick coupler)
  • Demand EN 10204 3.1 material certificates for blades (NM500) and housing (Q355) plus a hardness report; order reversible spare blades with the unit
  • Ask for make of rotator, hydraulic motor and cylinder (seals) and request a test-run video with round-steel and section cuts before shipment
  • Contractually fix pre-shipment inspection, warranty (12 months per listing) and EU spare-parts supply
