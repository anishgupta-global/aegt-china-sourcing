Description

The A2-P3 combination filter is the standard filter for painters, decorators and cleaning companies that work with solvent vapours and spray mist at the same time: the …

More

activated carbon layer binds organic gases and vapours with a boiling point above 65 °C (gas filter class 2), and the upstream P3 particle filter retains at least 99.95 % of particles. High-quality filters are supplied in pairs in sealed foil with batch number and expiry date and carry the CE marking with the notified body number. Professional users in Germany expect testing to EN 14387 with class A2P3 R D, a bayonet that matches the mask make and German instructions stating the limits of use (not below 17 vol.-% oxygen, no protection against carbon monoxide). As category III PPE the filter needs an EU type-examination certificate with ongoing production control; filter and mask may only be used in the approved combination.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EU type-examination certificate to EN 14387 (A2 P3 R D) with NB number and cross-check it in NANDO; the listing names neither standard nor certificate.
  • Have the connection system confirmed in writing (bayonet, fit to your own mask, e.g. Safetree PFFM750P) and test samples for fit and tightness.
  • Ask for a batch test report: P3 penetration at most 0.05 %, gas service life with cyclohexane (class 2) and breathing resistance to EN 14387.
  • Sealed individual pack for the pair, with colour coding brown/white (A/P), batch number, expiry date and German instructions for use.
  • Have the plant, production control (module C2 or D) and ISO 9001 evidenced; clearly distinguish trading company and production site.
  • Have samples tested by an independent test lab before the series order; request price, Incoterm and port bindingly (listing: single price tier only).
