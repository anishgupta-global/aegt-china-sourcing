Description

The tempered glass protects the smartphone display against scratches, keys and impact and is supplied as a model-specific cut pane of tempered glass with a 2.5D rounded edge.

More

Quality markers that demanding buyers in Germany expect are a thickness of about 0.33 mm, an oleophobic anti-fingerprint layer, bubble-free adhesion, exact cut-outs for camera, speaker and sensors, and an installation kit of frame, dust remover and cleaning cloth, as brand suppliers include it. For trade, fit per device model is what counts: cut files for new devices must be available quickly, and brands such as Apple or Samsung may only be named descriptively for compatibility on carton and title. As a consumer product the item needs GPSR marking with an EU responsible person as well as REACH-compliant adhesives and inks.

More

(VENTARENT 2 x 9H EUR 9.99, MOBSTORE 2 pcs EUR 11.95); brand goods such as Hama are EUR 17.99-27.99 per piece (Clear, Extreme, Reflect Protect). The retail price anchor is therefore the 2-pack under EUR 12; a single piece needs brand, installation aid or a special benefit (privacy, matte) as justification. Sales peaks after device launches are an assumption, not evidenced. Return risk from wrong model and camera cut-outs is high: state compatibility unambiguously in the title.

Request from the supplier

  • Release cut files per device model and test fit on three original devices (camera, speaker and sensor cut-outs, edge clearance for cases)
  • Check hardness and impact resistance: pencil hardness 9H (ISO 15184), ball drop test, thickness measurement, edge grinding without chips; request a lab test report
  • Have glass type and adhesive type named in writing; REACH/SVHC evidence for adhesive, ink and packaging
  • Adhesion and optics: bubble-free application, fingerprint coating (water contact angle), dust-free; agree AQL 1.0/2.5
  • Packaging without third-party brands, neutral compatibility list; clarify GPSR details (EU responsible person, batch) and LUCID registration
  • Tier prices in writing: the listing shows only the range US$0.15-0.40 at MOQ 500; have tiers and sample price confirmed
