Description

The AI construction-site camera monitors demolition and refurbishment areas around the clock, reports missing helmets or high-visibility vests as well as entry into restricted zones …

More

and documents construction progress over 4G without a fixed-line connection. For outdoor use the professional buyer expects a rugged housing (IP66/IP67, IK10), infrared night vision and flexible power via 12 V, PoE or solar. Because the camera processes employee data, GDPR, works-council involvement, signage and local edge analytics without a cloud in China must be ensured. CE, RED and cybersecurity proofs as well as firmware updates over the lifecycle are mandatory.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity (RED, EMC, low voltage) and test report under EN 62368-1.
  • Proof of IP66/IP67 and IK10 ratings from an independent test laboratory.
  • Demonstration of the PPE detection (helmet, high-visibility vest per EN ISO 20471) with accuracy and false-alarm rate on our own test images.
  • Local edge analytics without mandatory cloud, data-flow description and deletion concept for GDPR.
  • Cybersecurity: firmware update commitment for at least 5 years, no default passwords, 4G bands cleared for Germany.
  • German-language manual, warranty and spare-parts supply in the EU.
