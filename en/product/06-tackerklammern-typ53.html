Description

The type 53 staple is the common standard for hand tackers: fine wire with an 11.4 mm crown and a wire gauge of about 0.7 to 0.75 mm …

More

galvanised and available in lengths from 6 to 14 mm. It fixes foils, cardboard, fabric, upholstery and thin insulation to wood and board materials. Quality markers are an even, red-rust-free zinc coating, a dimensionally accurate crown for jam-free feeding in the magazine, clean points and a firm strip bond without breakouts. Imported goods need GPSR-compliant labelling with an EU responsible person and a clear type and length statement on the sales packaging.

More

Evidence: Hornbach lists JC Schwarz 530/xx staples (type 53 per the Hornbach tacker page) at EUR 3.55–7.95 per 1,000–5,000 pieces (Hornbach search, 31 Oct 2026); Amazon.de and Kaufland.de offer packs of 10,000 for about EUR 6–10 per the scan-stage search results (Amazon page B07D19NNB2 confirms the title '10000 staples type 53, 14 mm', price not retrievable); Rapid type 53 is listed at tooled-up (UK) from GBP 6.49. Marketplace price pressure is high, the margin lies in the bundle with the hand tacker (Hornbach names type 53 right on the tacker page). Light and shipping-friendly; wrong purchases through type confusion (53 vs. 140/G) are an assessed risk, so state type, length and compatible tackers on the product page and pack.

Request from the supplier

  • Dimensional check to type 53 (crown 11.4 mm, wire about 0.7–0.75 mm) with measurement record and fit test in a JC Schwarz 530-M or Rapid 53 tacker
  • Penetration and jam test in softwood and plywood (misfires per 1,000 staples), point grinding and strip bonding
  • Zinc coating thickness (µm) and salt spray test EN ISO 9227 with an agreed number of hours without red rust; Cr(VI)-free passivation (REACH declaration)
  • Packaging per length (1,000/5,000/10,000 pcs) with type, length, quantity, batch, EU responsible person (GPSR) and German instructions; LUCID registration
  • Factory proof instead of a trading company: business licence, wire drawing/zinc bath on site, audit or video inspection
  • Samples for each length 6/8/10/12/14 mm before series approval and a batch-consistency commitment
