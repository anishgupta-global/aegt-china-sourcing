Description

The device removes air from embossed bags or rolls via an external suction channel and then seals them; dry and moist programmes are standard in this class.

More

Buyers and retailers look at suction power, seal integrity, continuous operation without overheating, noise level and the availability of bags and rolls as repeat-purchase items. For the EU market, CE (LVD/EMC), RoHS, ElektroG registration and food-safe seals and bags per LFGB must be evidenced. The added value lies in recurring consumables (rolls, bags) and cross-selling with glass vacuum containers.

Request from the supplier

  • Order the 220-240 V variant with EU plug; request CE, LVD/EMC reports and GS/CB certificates for exactly VS2201.
  • Measure vacuum performance (kPa, l/min), continuous operation (cycles without rest) and seal quality in own tests with dry and moist modes.
  • Co-develop bags and rolls as accessories (embossed film, 20/28 cm, LFGB/food contact report).
  • Request the ISO 9001, ISO 14001, BSCI certificates claimed by the supplier as reports with validity dates.
  • Clarify ElektroG registration, LUCID, EU responsible person (GPSR) and German manual before sales start.
  • Negotiate tier prices (listing: US$19.40 from 3,000 pcs, US$25.00 for 1-60 pcs).
