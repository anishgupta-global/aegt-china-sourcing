Description

The ZR160L rotary drilling rig bores piles up to 1,500 mm diameter and 56 m depth for building, bridge and specialist foundation works.

More

At about 50 t operating weight the 154 kW diesel engine delivers 160 kN·m of torque; the main winch pulls 160 kN. For EU use, a Stage V engine, CE conformity under EN 16228 and compatibility of drilling tools (kelly bar, augers, buckets) are decisive.

E-commerce potential

B2B enquiry product for specialist foundation contractors; buyers compare torque, depth, tool compatibility and above all service/spare parts.

More

Returns unrealistic; buy only after inspection/test. New prices of established manufacturers are not public (not evidenced here); demand data were not checked.

Request from the supplier

  • Request an EU Stage V engine (Reg. (EU) 2016/1628) with engine type approval; the listing names only generic certificates ('EPA, CS, CE, RoHS, ISO') and no EU emission stage.
  • CE declaration of conformity, EN 16228 evidence (mast inclination, overload/emergency stop), German operating manual.
  • Get spare and wear parts (drilling tools, kelly bar, cutting teeth) plus service and training in DE/EU confirmed in writing.
  • Match the tool interface (kelly profile, augers, buckets up to 1,500 mm diameter) with the planned drilling methods.
  • Clarify transport (approx. 50 t, mast removed/low-loader), dimensions, Incoterm and inspection before shipment.
