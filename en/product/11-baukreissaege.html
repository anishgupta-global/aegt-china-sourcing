Description

The construction-site table saw is the standard machine for cutting timber on site and delivers clean rip and mitre cuts in square timber, planks and panel materials in drywall …

More

roof-truss and interior fit-out work. Its 315 mm carbide-tipped blade cuts about 80 mm deep at 90°, while a rip fence and side table extensions guide even long battens safely. German professionals expect a rigid table, a riving knife, a blade guard with an extraction port, a no-volt release switch and a motor that withstands hard continuous use on site.
