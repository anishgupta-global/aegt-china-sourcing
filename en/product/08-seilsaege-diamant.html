Description

The wire saw cuts massive reinforced concrete walls, slabs, foundations and machine blocks into transportable pieces where wall saws and chisel hammers reach their limits.

More

A 380 V drive with water cooling, guide pulleys and a wire guard allows cut sizes limited only by the length of the wire. Professional users in Germany expect a machine with emergency stop, safe controls, a wire guard and durable wear parts, as well as trained operators following the requirements of the employers' liability insurance association. Compliance means CE under the Machinery Directive with a risk assessment, EN 60204-1, EMC proof and a German operating manual.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC with a documented risk assessment to EN ISO 12100
  • Wiring diagram and test report on the electrical equipment to EN 60204-1 as well as the emergency stop function (EN ISO 13849-1)
  • Check the wire guard, guide pulleys and wire-break protection on the sample; measure the wire speed in operation
  • Define the scope of diamond wires and wear parts (beads, connectors, pulleys) in quantity and quality in writing
  • German operating manual, spare parts list, training material and at least 12–24 months warranty
