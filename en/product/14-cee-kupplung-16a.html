Description

The 16 A CEE coupling (3P+N+E, 400 V, 6 h) is the movable counterpart to the CEE plug and the wear part of every self-assembled three-phase extension lead with H07RN-F 5G1.5 or 5G2.5.

More

Expected are an impact- and ageing-resistant PA housing, IP44 when mated, solid brass contacts, a firm strain relief with a suitable clamping range and unambiguous 6 h keying to EN IEC 60309 so that the coupling engages securely with plugs from Mennekes, PCE or ABL. On site DIN VDE 0100-704 applies, and regular testing under DGUV Regulation 3 is part of it. Import goods can only be used with a test report to EN IEC 60309-1/-2, a CE declaration and a GPSR responsible person.

Request from the supplier

  • Clarify the voltage in writing: the listing title says 220 V, the datasheet 380-415 V 3P+N+E; quotation and test report must state 5-pole, 400 V and 6 h
  • Test report to EN IEC 60309-1/-2 (temperature rise, degree of protection when mated, retention force) from an accredited lab for model RS-215K1; cross-check the CB and TUEV certificates named in the listing via their certificate numbers
  • CE declaration of conformity (2014/35/EU, 2011/65/EU) referencing the model, with a German version; RoHS/REACH reports
  • Connection data: clamping range 1.5 to 2.5 mm2, strain relief for H07RN-F 5G1.5 and 5G2.5, screw torque
  • Sample mating test against Mennekes, PCE and ABL plugs (6 h keying, insertion and retention force), drop test for site use
  • Marking (type, ratings, manufacturer, EU responsible person), packing unit and third-party inspection before shipment
