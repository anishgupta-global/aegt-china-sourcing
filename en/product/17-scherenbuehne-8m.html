Description

The compact scissor lift with 7.8 m working height and 230 kg platform load covers the classic 8 m class for ceiling, installation and demolition work in stairwells …

More

corridors and halls and replaces mobile scaffolds and ladders there. With a machine width of only 0.79 m according to the manufacturer list, it suits narrow access on strip-out and refurbishment projects. German users expect CE conformity to EN 280, emergency lowering, overload protection, tilt alarm, a German logbook for the recurring inspection under the Industrial Safety Ordinance and instruction under DGUV Principle 308-008. For Chinese-origin machines the EU anti-dumping and countervailing duties apply from 6 m working height; LGMG is not named in the GTAI list, so the rate must be clarified in TARIC.

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity naming EN 280 and the Machinery Directive, plus CE marking on the rating plate

  • German operating manual, logbook and spare parts list before ordering
  • TARIC query: current AD/countervailing duty rate for LGMG and the classification (CN 8427 / 8428)
  • Service partner and spare parts supply in Germany or the EU (LGMG Europe B.V., Dordrecht), warranty of at least 12 months
