Description

The double hammock made of breathable, quick-drying parachute nylon (e.g. 210T) carries 150 to 200 kg depending on the version and packs to the size of a fist.

More

Tree straps and carabiners allow setup between two trees without knots; a mosquito net, tarp or sleeping mat can be added. Quality markers are double-stitched, load-tested seams, a generous lying size (reference: 285 × 190 cm) and a clearly stated load rating of straps and carabiners. For retail, exact weight and size data, REACH-compliant dyes and a clear hanging guide matter.

Request from the supplier

  • Have the load rating evidenced by a test report (static test with safety factor) plus the breaking load of straps and carabiners (kN).
  • Fabric: 210T specification, g/m², double-stitched seams, colour fastness; request REACH / azo evidence.
  • Carabiners: material, stamped breaking load; without a climbing standard mark them 'not for climbing'; have strap length, width and loops stated.
  • Fix the scope of delivery per variant (straps, carabiners, stuff sack, net) with size (L × W) and weight.
  • German labelling: load rating, hanging angle and height, warnings; GPSR label.
