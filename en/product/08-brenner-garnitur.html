Description

The oxy-fuel cutting outfit is the classic tool for severing steel beams, plates and reinforcement on site in strip-out when shears or cut-off grinders cannot reach.

More

The outfit consists of a cutting torch, pressure regulators for oxygen and fuel gas, two hoses and a transport box, and is connected to gas cylinders. Professional users in Germany expect flashback arresters and non-return valves, standard-compliant regulators and hoses, matching cylinder connections and a risk assessment for hot work. Compliance means regulators with CE under the Pressure Equipment Directive, a torch to EN ISO 5172, safety devices to EN ISO 5175-1 and a German operating manual.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Declaration of conformity of the regulator under 2014/68/EU with a test report to EN ISO 2503 (accredited laboratory); torch type-tested to EN ISO 5172
  • Flashback arresters and non-return valves to EN ISO 5175-1 (test proof) for the oxygen and fuel-gas side – usually to be added with Chinese outfits
  • Hoses to EN ISO 3821 with marking (pressure rating, colour per gas type) and connection fittings
  • Confirm cylinder connections for German cylinder valves (EN ISO 5145) or adapters in writing
  • German operating manual with hot-work instructions; nozzle set with cutting chart (nozzle size, oxygen/acetylene pressure)
  • Sampling with a test cut in structural steel, warranty of at least 24 months, Incoterm and HS code in the quotation (negotiate the MOQ of 50)
