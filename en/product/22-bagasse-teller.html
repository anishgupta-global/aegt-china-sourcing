Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Valid EN 13432 certificate from a test body (e.g. DIN CERTCO, TUV Austria) for exactly this product and this manufacturer; verify the certificate number in the body's database.
  • PFAS test report (targeted PFAS analysis and total fluorine) as evidence for the PPWR limits for food packaging.
  • Declaration of conformity under Regulation (EC) 1935/2004 with migration and heavy-metal test report from an accredited lab.
  • Declaration that no plastic coating (PE, PLA) is applied; otherwise clarify single-use plastics obligations.
  • Load, grease and hot-water test on the sample (hot, greasy dish, 30 minutes) with photos.
  • Manufacturer status (factory or trader), GMP and hygiene certificate (BRCGS Packaging or ISO 22000) and German labelling on the pack.
