Description

The single-disc floor grinder is the compact complement to the planetary grinder and works screed, concrete and natural stone in small rooms …

More

edge zones and stair landings where a three-head machine cannot reach. The 250 mm grinding disc is driven by a speed-controlled motor and can be fitted with diamond segments for concrete, screed and coatings. German professionals expect a sturdy frame, a dust skirt with a connection to a class M or H vacuum, speed control for a controlled grinding pattern and operation from a 230 V site socket. Required are CE marking under the Machinery Directive with EN ISO 12100 and EN 60204-1, a German manual, spare parts for motor, inverter and wheels and, where asbestos is suspected, a procedure under TRGS 519.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC, 2014/30/EU and 2011/65/EU with CE marking and a test report (the listing names CE and ISO 9001/14001/45001; the TÜV Rheinland audit covers the management system, not the product)
  • Confirmation of the 230 V / 50 Hz single-phase version with EU plug and protection class and of the motor power (HTG-250D 2.2 kW versus HTG-250VS 3 kW); check current draw for a 16 A fuse
  • Dust skirt and extraction port with a dust-capture measurement on a class M or H vacuum (EN 60335-2-69 Annex AA); clarify handling where asbestos is suspected under TRGS 519
  • Confirm the tool mount and compatible diamond tools; spare-parts list for motor, frequency inverter, belts and wheels, plus delivery times
  • German manual with vibration and noise values; warranty of at least 24 months (the listing names only 1 year); sample delivery with a test run before a series order
