Description

Taba and mochi squishies are soft, stretchy squeeze figures in sugar, fruit and cake shapes, bought mainly for their ASMR touch.

More

Two lines shape the market: soft silicone tabas for adults and the office, and firmer mochi and cake squishies in TPR or PU foam for children and collectors. Discerning buyers look for platinum-cured silicone without plasticisers, low inherent odour, clean mould seams and a material that does not stick or tear. EN 71 reports (mechanical, heavy metals, nitrosamines), phthalate and PAH evidence and, for silicone, proof of odour and siloxane levels are expected; because of the sweets-like look, clear toy labelling and age grading are needed.

E-commerce potential

The ASMR squishy wave has two sub-currents: soft silicone tabas/mochis (adults, office) and firmer cake and fruit shapes.

More

The Star (9 Sep 2026): squishy cakes in strawberry, cherry, croissant and mushroom shapes at about CNY 25 (roughly US$ 3.7) fill pop-up stores in Shanghai; one model from the brand Molynk (CNY 299 per the article) passed 180 million TikTok views since July and sold out at 5,000 units. Amazon.de (3 Nov 2026, per the research brief): 237 results for 'taba squishy' (listings with 500+ purchases per month), 341 for 'mochi squishy' (600+); sugar squishies such as 'sugar axolotl' reach 1,000+ purchases per month. Mueller (search on 3 Nov 2026): Nebulous Stars Squishy Mochi 2.99 EUR, Tataba Series 2 Blind Boxes 9.99 EUR, Mushmeez Sweet Friends 4.99 EUR (sold out). Instagram: no reliable single source found. Risks: sweets-like look (swallowing), odour with silicone and PU, character or brand copies, fast trend cycles.

Request from the supplier

  • EN 71-1/-3/-12 and EN 71-2 (for PU foam) from an accredited lab on the finished product; for silicone also proof of platinum cure, no plasticisers, with odour and siloxane tests.
  • Set the age group: children's collectible under the Toy Safety Directive or 14+ under the GPSR; the safety requirements (small parts, tearing) remain in both cases.
  • Sweets-like look: clear toy labelling, a warning against swallowing and eating, no scent or food-style presentation that invites eating.
  • Phthalate and PAH report, odour and VOC test (formamide too for PU foam); approve samples before series production.
  • Own design without copying protected characters or brands; mould and tooling rights with the buyer.
  • Verify the finished-goods source for silicone taba (Guangdong) separately; align the contract with the new EU Toy Safety Regulation (digital product passport, PFAS and bisphenol restrictions).
