Description

More

and area and volume functions simplify quantity take-off for demolition and strip-out. A professional device offers ±1.5 mm accuracy, a tilt sensor, Bluetooth export to an app or measuring software, protection rating IP54 or better and drop resistance from at least 1.5 m. Laser class 2 to EN 60825-1, CE marking including radio conformity for Bluetooth and a German manual are the minimum standard.
