Description

Snap-off blades are the wear part with the fastest turnover on site: every snapped-off segment gives a fresh edge for sheeting, insulation, floor covering and plasterboard.

More

Dimensional accuracy in width and thickness and clean break lines decide whether the blade sits securely in an auto-lock knife and breaks in a controlled way instead of splintering. German professionals expect uniform hardness, a protective box with safe dispensing and traceable batches. Without a hardness test and dimensional check no blade is released into AE Global Traders stock.
