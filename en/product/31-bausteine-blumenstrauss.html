Description

The flower bouquet and bonsai set is a botanical ABS injection-moulded brick kit: blossoms, leaves and stems are clicked together piece by piece into a durable decorative object for desk, shelf or windowsill.

More

Typical sets contain 130 to 600 pieces and come in a coloured gift box with building instructions. What discerning buyers look for: clean clutch power, colour-fast parts, a reliable piece count with a spare-part service and a legally sound age grading. Positioned as a toy (9+, EN 71) or as a decor item for adults (GPSR), the set can be sold in toy retail as well as in gift and home-accessory retail.

Request from the supplier

  • Request EN 71-1/-2/-3 test reports from an accredited lab (ISO 17025) for this exact model; compare test date and sample with the series.
  • Fix the age group (9+ or 14+/adults) and align the small-parts test and warnings with it.
  • Agree the piece count per set, missing-part rate and free replacement service in writing; check clutch power and colour fastness on the sample.
  • Request chemical tests (EN 71-3, REACH phthalates/SVHC) for ABS and colourants; check odour and discolouration.
  • Have the own design confirmed: no copy of known brand sets (e.g. LEGO Botanicals), no licensed motifs; clarify rights to design and instructions.
  • GPSR documents: EU responsible person, batch marking, German instructions and warnings, packaging registration (LUCID).
