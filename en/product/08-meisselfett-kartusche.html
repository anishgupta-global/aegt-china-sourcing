Description

Chisel grease protects the tool shank and guide bush of chiselling, demolition and breaker hammers against wear and seizing.

More

The lithium-complex base withstands high surface pressure and temperatures; solid lubricants such as MoS2 or copper, as usual in chisel pastes, additionally form a dry lubricating film. The 400 g cartridge fits standard grease guns and can be dosed cleanly on site. Professional buyers demand a safety data sheet and CLP classification, dropping-point and consistency data (NLGI) and correct packaging registration under VerpackG.

Request from the supplier

  • Formulation and data sheet: base oil, thickener (lithium complex), solid lubricants (MoS2, copper), NLGI grade, dropping point, worked penetration and temperature range.
  • Safety data sheet per REACH Annex II in German, CLP classification and labelling of the cartridge (pictograms, UFI code if classification applies).
  • Have the transport classification confirmed (probably not dangerous goods, proof via section 14 of the safety data sheet).
  • Cartridge format 400 g: dimensions, head thread and tightness for common grease guns, carton dimensions and weight per carton.
  • Private-label filling: batch traceability, shelf life and laboratory sample test (dropping point, oil separation) before the series order.
  • Clarify VerpackG/LUCID registration and, for consumer sales, the EU responsible person under GPSR.
