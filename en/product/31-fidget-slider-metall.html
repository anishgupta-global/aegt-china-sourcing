Description

The fidget slider consists of CNC-machined metal parts (aluminium or zinc alloy) that glide against each other on a guide; enclosed magnets create the typical snap and click.

More

The card-format push card (approx. 45 x 33 mm) fits any pocket and is sold as an anti-stress gadget and desk accessory. Quality markers are tight manufacturing tolerances, a clean surface finish (anodised, sandblasted, polished or PVD), firmly enclosed magnets and deburred edges. Positioned for 14+/adults the GPSR applies; as a toy for younger children EN 71-1 with its magnet requirements and EN 71-3 would apply.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

After the squishy wave, haptic fidgeting for adults is the second trend wave: ASI Research measures +26% searches for 'fidget' in Q3 2026 year on year.

More

Per the scan, Amazon.de shows 224 results for 'fidget slider' (700+ purchases/month); the search page 'magnetic slider fidget' lists 247 results. Price anchor: a marketplace push-card slider (TASSFROO) costs EUR 8.16 gross at 3.6 of 5 stars. Social evidence: the TikTok hashtag #sliderfidget and TikTok Shop listings for EDC sliders (US) can be found; reach not verified (TikTok Creative Center not accessible), Instagram not checked. The EDC community calls the category matured in 2026 (edc4me.com). Competition: many no-name imports, premium brands far more expensive. Risks: magnet and age statements (one marketplace listing gives an age recommendation of 18 months to 3 years for the metal magnet object - implausible), surface wear, sticking slider.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix positioning and age grading (14+/18+) and align magnet warnings (swallowing, pacemakers) with the supplier.
  • Magnets: have type, flux index and secure encapsulation proven (EN 71-1/ISO 8124-1); no loose magnet parts in the set.
  • Metal: test report on nickel release (EN 1811), lead and cadmium (REACH entries 63/23); durability of anodising/PVD, deburred edges.
  • Check the sample (3-7 days): click feel, play, wear; request a 20-piece sample check and CMM inspection records for the tolerance.
  • If positioned as a toy, an EN 71 test report from an accredited lab; otherwise GPSR documentation with an EU responsible person.
  • Audit: the showroom mostly lists unrelated products (tin boxes, matcha-sifter accessories, titanium EDC parts) - confirm in-house production and capacity for the sliders during the Dongguan visit (optional Tue 8 Dec) or by video audit.
