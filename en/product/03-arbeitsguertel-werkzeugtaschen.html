Description

The tool belt with pouch set moves the weight of hammer, pliers, drill bits and nails from the trousers to the hips and keeps standard tools within reach.

More

Commercial users expect an adjustable, padded belt band with a sturdy buckle, bar-tacked seams at load points and pouches made of robust polyester (600D to 1680D) or leather with hammer loop and tape holder. Because the belt is not protective equipment, CE marking under the PPE Regulation does not apply as long as it is not advertised as a protective product; GPSR and REACH for dyes and metal parts do apply. Single pouches can be repurchased, turning the belt into a modular system similar to the e.s.tool concept of engelbert strauss.

Request from the supplier

  • Waist band: confirm adjustment range in cm (e.g. 80-130 cm), width and padding and measure on the sample
  • Load test (pull on buckle, seams, hammer loop) with test report; have the 3-10 kg capacity claim evidenced
  • Material: fabric (600D/1680D), coating, tear strength; azo, PAH and nickel tests (REACH) for fabric and metal parts
  • Fix pouch scope in the set (number and type: hammer, nails, tape) and price per set in writing
  • GPSR: EU responsible person, manufacturer and batch details on the product or packaging
  • Clarify OEM details: logo (embroidery, patch, print), colour, packaging and sample cost before ordering
