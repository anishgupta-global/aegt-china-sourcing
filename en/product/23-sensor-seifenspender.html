Description

The sensor soap dispenser delivers the dose without contact and is therefore the hygienic upgrade of the manual 1,000 ml dispenser in office, practice, catering or hotel washrooms.

More

The ABS housing is wall-mounted and key-locked; depending on the pump type it dispenses liquid soap, foam or spray, and the dose size is selectable. Commercial buyers expect a tight refill system without dripping, reliable sensing under changing light, a battery life in the tens of thousands of cycles and a clean housing without sharp edges. For the EU, EMC, RoHS, electrical and battery obligations and GPSR labelling apply.

In B2B the Tork sensor dispenser 561608 costs EUR 95.90 net at Jungheinrich PROFISHOP; according to the scan, Kaufland lists 1,000 ml foam sensor dispensers at about EUR 93. The ex-works price of USD 15.50 to 18.50 leaves a wide margin; demand is driven by touchless hygiene in offices, catering and practices. Risks: battery life and sensor faults (returns), the pump type must suit the soap (foam needs diluted soap), and WEEE/battery obligations make the market launch more demanding than for manual dispensers.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the CE declaration of conformity with EMC test report and RoHS proof (the listing mentions CE and FCC; only certificate and report count).
  • Match pump type and soap viscosity: test liquid, foam and spray pumps separately, check drip stop and tightness of the refill.
  • Battery compartment: replaceable batteries (Batteries Regulation), EU-suitable battery labelling; for the adapter version EU plug and LVD certificate of the adapter.
  • Sensor endurance test: detection distance, false triggering in light, battery life (listing states up to 60,000 cycles) measured on the sample.
  • Factory proof: Smarlean states its own moulding shop (16 injection moulds, 6 assembly lines, 900 sqm) and a BV audit; request the audit report and a factory visit.
  • Clarify WEEE, battery and packaging registration (stiftung ear, LUCID) before launch and define key, mounting kit and German instructions.
