Description

The laser viewing glasses raise the contrast of green laser lines from cross-line and rotary lasers in bright light and at longer range, without a receiver and without power.

More

The green-tinted lens makes the line stand out more clearly on most surfaces. For responsible sales the distinction is decisive: the glasses are a visibility aid and not laser protection under EN 207 or EN 208, and this belongs on the product page, the packaging and in the manual. Quality markers are a clean, scratch-resistant lens, a light frame with adjustable temples and an extra such as a pouch or cloth; GPSR documentation and REACH for lens coating and frame must be clarified.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

The Laserliner LaserSight Green costs EUR 18.95 at Hornbach (item no. 10698116) and was the only hit for ‘Lasersichtbrille’ at Hornbach on 31 Oct 2026, so supply there is thin.

More

Impulse-purchase accessory for green cross-line and rotary lasers, small and light to ship. The main risk is confusion with laser protection eyewear: Hornbach’s own image alt text calls the product ‘Laserschutzbrille’ while the description says viewing glasses; product page and packaging must state clearly that there is no protective effect. Bestseller ranks were not checked; among the Chinese listings only EN 207 laser safety goggles turned up, no viewing glasses.

Request from the supplier

  • Written confirmation from the supplier that the product is delivered as a visibility aid without protective effect; no print such as ‘Laser Protection’, ‘OD’ or ‘CE EN 207’
  • Transmission curve or data sheet for 520 to 532 nm and a field test of the sample with a real green cross-line laser in daylight
  • Have the material of lens and frame and the coating named; REACH/SVHC declaration
  • GPSR documents: type designation, manufacturer address, EU responsible person, German warning ‘Nicht als Laserschutzbrille geeignet’
  • Packaging with EAN, pouch or cloth and VerpackG registration; clarify sample and MOQ before ordering
