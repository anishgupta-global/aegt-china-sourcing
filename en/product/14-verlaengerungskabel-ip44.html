Description

The site extension cable consists of 10 m of H07RN-F 3G1.5 mm² rubber-sheathed cable with a moulded-on Schuko plug and a Schuko coupling with flap lid (IP44).

More

Professionals expect a neoprene-sheathed, cold-, oil- and abrasion-resistant stranded pure-copper conductor, moulded connectors with brass contacts and strain relief, plus VDE/GS marks and CE marking. On German sites DIN VDE 0100-704 calls for rubber-sheathed cables of at least type H07RN-F for portable leads; PVC cables such as H05VV-F are not intended for this. From China the item can only be placed on the market with a test report from an accredited lab, a German-language declaration of conformity and an EU responsible person under GPSR.

Request from the supplier

  • Request VDE/GS marks and test reports for the cable (H07RN-F marking) and the connectors; cross-check certificate numbers with the test body.
  • Pure-copper, finely stranded conductor, 3 x 1.5 mm2: weigh a cable sample and have the conductor resistance measured (no CCA, no undersized cross-section); check the metre print H07RN-F 3G1.5.
  • IP44 test report to EN 60529 for the coupling with flap lid; moulded plugs with brass contacts and strain relief; have 100 % high-voltage testing confirmed.
  • Obtain a written quote for exactly 10 m H07RN-F with price tiers (the Zhonghui listing gives neither length nor price per length) and fix the length tolerance without minus deviation.
  • Clarify the declaration of conformity (LVD/RoHS), German-language labelling with EU responsible person (GPSR) and packaging registration (LUCID) before the first container.
