Description

The 4.5 t variant is designed for the heavier vehicles in the yard, such as vans with flatbed or box body, large SUVs and light commercial vehicles, and allows servicing and repair without an external workshop.

More

Professional users in Germany expect reinforced, corrosion-protected columns, a reliable locking latch on both sides, overload protection in the hydraulic circuit and a 400 V drive unit. Required are the CE declaration of conformity under the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC, a test report to EN 1493 and exact foundation data (concrete grade and thickness), because the higher load stresses the floor more. The lift may only be operated after inspection before first use and with recurring inspection by competent persons (BetrSichV, DGUV).

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EC declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC stating manufacturer, model and serial number, plus technical documentation with risk assessment
  • Test report to EN 1493 with the notified body number for exactly the 4.5 t capacity (CE claims on marketplaces are seller statements only)
  • Written confirmation of the 400 V/50 Hz version and of the overload protection in the hydraulic circuit
  • Foundation drawing with concrete grade, minimum thickness and anchor data, plus German operating manual and inspection logbook
  • Spare parts list with delivery time, written warranty of at least 24 months and factory acceptance before loading
