Description

The set combines a club hammer of about 1,000 g with flat and pointed chisels of 250 mm length and a hand guard for chiselling, plaster and masonry work.

More

The hammer head is drop-forged from 45# carbon steel and hardened on the striking faces; the chisels are made of forged, hardened steel (requirement: chrome-vanadium). Professional users expect a secure head fit, burr-free and crack-free striking faces, deburred chisel heads without mushrooming and a hand guard that sits firmly on the shank. Imported goods need GPSR-compliant labelling with an EU responsible person, German safety instructions (safety goggles, remove mushrooming) and REACH-compliant handles and coatings.

A complete set replaces several single purchases (club hammer, two chisels, hand guard) and complements the crowbar and brick trowel; the low-price segment is a price war, and the return risk is medium because of steel quality (mushrooming of the chisel head, head fit) (assessment).

Request from the supplier

  • Hammer: test report on head fit (pull-off and impact test) and HRC record of the striking face (listing states HRC 47–53); shape and weight to DIN 6475 (1,000 g, tolerance ± 3%)
  • Chisels: EN 10204 type 3.1 material certificate (Cr-V or 45#), hardness zones of edge and head, deburred chisel heads against mushrooming, length 250 mm
  • Hand guard: material (rubber/plastic), REACH/PAH report, firm fit on the shank
  • Handle: wood species (ash/hickory), residual moisture, wedging and timber origin (FSC/PEFC) incl. EU Deforestation Regulation
  • Set assembly: Pangu lists only the hammer (H2701), chisels must be sourced separately or requested as OEM; obtain a complete quote with set MOQ
  • GPSR details (EU responsible person, batch) and German safety instructions (safety goggles, remove mushrooming); sales packaging with LUCID registration
