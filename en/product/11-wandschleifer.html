Description

The wall and ceiling sander (giraffe) sands filler, plasterboard joints and plaster on walls and ceilings without scaffolding or a ladder and is the standard machine for …

More

flat surfaces in interior fit-out and renovation. The gimbal-mounted 225 mm pad sits on a light telescopic arm that reaches room heights of over 3 m. Professional users expect adjustable speed, effective extraction via a ring or brush skirt connected to a class M or H vacuum, a weight below 4 kg and spare parts for pads and carbon brushes. Required are CE marking to EN 62841, a GS mark for professional use, a 230 V / 50 Hz design with EU plug and a German manual; where asbestos is suspected no sanding is allowed, only low-emission procedures under TRGS 519 by specialist firms.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC, 2014/30/EU and 2011/65/EU with CE marking (listing claims CE and ISO; have these proven by certificate)
  • Test report from an accredited laboratory to EN 62841-1 and EN 62841-2-3; ask about the optional GS mark
  • Confirmation of the 230 V / 50 Hz version with EU plug and protection class II (the listing states 110-220 V / 60 Hz)
  • Suitable suction-hose connections for common industrial vacuums and a dust-capture measurement with a class M/H vacuum
  • German manual with vibration and noise values; spare parts (sanding pad, hook-and-loop, carbon brushes, gimbal joint)
  • At least 24 months warranty; MOQ 50 pieces: check a sample first and clarify packaging rules (ElektroG, Packaging Act)
