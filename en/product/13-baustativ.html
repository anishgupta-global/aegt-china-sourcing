Description

The building tripod gives a level, rotary laser or line laser a rigid stand on uneven ground.

More

The aluminium build with a flat head (160 mm diameter on the RTA20) carries instruments with a 5/8-inch thread; smaller lasers with a 1/4-inch thread need an adapter. For professionals, torsional rigidity, play-free leg locks and a head that does not distort the instrument vial are decisive; buyers criticise exactly this wobble on bundled set tripods. The benchmark is the Bosch BT 160 with 0.97 to 1.60 m working height at 4.1 kg; the listed Mount Laser model RTA20 weighs 4.3 kg net. For EU sales, GPSR details, REACH for plastic parts and transport-safe packaging are required.

Request from the supplier

  • Check the 5/8″-11 instrument thread on the head with a sample and thread gauge; 1/4″ adapter in the delivery; trial mounting with a rotary laser and a level
  • Record working height min/max, load rating and rigidity in writing and check with a wobble and torsion test at full height
  • Assess the leg lock (twist lock, dual lock or quick clamp) in an endurance test; ask about spikes and rubber feet
  • Confirm weight (4.3 kg net) and carton (110 × 21 × 21 cm); carrying strap or bag are not stated in the listing
  • Order a sample, have the lead time of up to 15 working days confirmed; GPSR details, German manual and no copy of Bosch or Stabila design
