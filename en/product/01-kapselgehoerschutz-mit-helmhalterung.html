Request from the supplier

  • List of helmets the cups were tested on (EN 352-3 names the helmet types) and fit test on the target helmet.

  • Sample: clamping force, cushion quality, fold-away and working positions, arm detents.
  • German instructions for use with attenuation values, care and cushion replacement.
