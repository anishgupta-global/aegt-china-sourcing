E-commerce potential

Price anchor: uvex lens cleaning wipes (100 individually packed, manufacturer no. 9963100) cost EUR 11.14 net at Hoffmann Group (about EUR 0.11 per wipe).

More

A repeat-purchase consumable and an ideal add-on to every pair of safety glasses; the high minimum of 20,000 bags suits a private-label programme better than an assortment test.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the exact wipe count per bag, packaging type (individually wrapped or flow pack) and price per bag in writing in the quotation – the listing is inconsistent here.
  • Request the safety data sheet or formulation declaration (CLP, detergents regulation if applicable) for the impregnating liquid.
  • Test compatibility with polycarbonate lenses, anti-fog coatings and safety-glasses markings on a sample.
  • Check shelf life (expiry date) and drying out in storage; open a sample after 6 months.
  • Request copies of the certificates (ISO 9001, ISO 13485, GMP per the website) before they are advertised.
