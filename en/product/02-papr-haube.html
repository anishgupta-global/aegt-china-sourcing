Description

Powered respirators with hood or helmet (EN 12941, category TH) deliver filtered air under slight positive pressure over head and face; the loose-fitting headtop needs no skin seal …

More

suits beard wearers and noticeably reduces strain on long shifts in asbestos remediation, mould remediation, strip-out and dust work. High-quality systems offer a constant flow of at least 120 l/min, alarms for clogged filters and low battery, a washable or decontaminable hood or a safety helmet with visor and a battery lasting at least one shift. Professional users in Germany expect EU type-examination to EN 12941 with class TH2 or TH3 for the complete system including the supplied headtop, available replacement filters, batteries and hoods, and German-language service. NIOSH approvals and GB standards do not apply in the EU; for asbestos work with high exposure TRGS 519 requires TH3P or TM3P devices.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU type-examination certificate to EN 12941 (TH2/TH3) with NB number for the complete system including the supplied hood or helmet – the listing names no EN 12941 certification, clarify in writing; NIOSH, ANSI and GB do not apply.
  • Test report on air flow (≥ 120 l/min, also with a nearly empty battery and loaded filter), alarm function and total leakage with the supplied headtop.
  • Have battery runtime at rated flow proven (listing: > 4 h, i.e. shorter than a shift) and plan a second battery; UN 38.3, IEC 62133 and a charger with CE/EMC conformity.
  • Hood or helmet: material, decontaminability, spark and heat resistance (for welding hoods) and availability of replacement hoods and visors.
  • German-language manual, replacement filters, batteries and hoses for at least 5 years, EU service partner and warranty; accept ATEX/Ex claims only with category and testing body.
