Description

True wireless Bluetooth 5.3 earbuds with a pocket-size charging case, ENC microphones for clearer calls and IPX4 splash protection.

More

According to the supplier, model LDNIO-T01 delivers 4 hours of music playback and 3.5 hours of talk time per charge; the 350 mAh case recharges the earbuds several times. Logo printing and custom packaging are offered as an OEM service, so the item works as a promotional product, staff gift or entry-level trade good. Discerning buyers look for measured rather than stated battery life, a stable connection without dropouts, a functional final inspection and full RED conformity with test reports; a USB-C charging port has been mandatory in the EU since 28 Dec 2024 and should be checked on the sample.

E-commerce potential

MediaMarkt shows 431 headphone hits for the German-language search for TWS earphones, of which 338 with Bluetooth (2 Nov 2026).

More

Prices range from the own brand ISY at EUR 8.99 and no-name marketplace goods from EUR 9.99 (CORN TECHNOLOGY TWS-BT-V9i) via EUR 21.99 to 22.46 (PREISKU ENC, Baseus Bowie E18) up to branded devices at EUR 77 (JBL Live Pro 2) and EUR 189 (Sony WF-1000XM5). Worldwide, true wireless earbuds account for about 68% of 2025 headphone revenue according to a market study (Mordor Intelligence, only the summary in the search hit was read). Competition is dense and price-driven; the opportunity lies in printed promotional/B2B business (logo, gift box, minimum quantity) rather than a price fight with brands at retail. Return risk is high (connection problems, battery, hygiene); as a radio device the item needs RED conformity and a GPSR responsible person.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the CE/RED declaration of conformity and test reports (EN 300 328, EN 301 489-17, EN 62368-1, EN 62479) for exactly model LDNIO-T01; the listing names CE, FCC, RoHS, UL, BIS and SASO without reports.
  • Test a sample: measure battery life per charge and case recharge cycles, connection and latency test, call with ENC; require UN 38.3 and IEC 62133-2 reports for the earbud and case cells.
  • Define the logo print (process, position, abrasion resistance) and approve colour samples; packaging with German mandatory marks (WEEE symbol, battery marking, CE, name and address of the EU responsible person).
  • Clarify EU duties: GPSR responsible person, registration with stiftung ear (ElektroG), BattG, VerpackG/LUCID; check USB-C charging port and sound pressure limit (EN 50332) on the sample.
  • Quality agreement with AQL sampling at final inspection, warranty on failure rate and third-party inspection before shipment (SGS, TUV or BV).
  • Logistics: Li-ion cells in equipment are dangerous goods (UN 3481, class 9); agree shipping mode and documents with the forwarder in advance.
