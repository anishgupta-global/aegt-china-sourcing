Energy

Energy equipment for sites and buildings: standby generators, battery storage and PV modules through to heat pumps and inverters.

More

Chinese makers offer large price advantages here, but Stage V, CE and the VDE grid-connection rules decide whether the goods can be approved and insured in Germany.

Standby generators cluster in Fujian (Xiamen, Fuan, Fuzhou) and Shandong (Weifang, Jinan), battery storage in Guangdong (Shenzhen, Foshan) and Jiangxi, PV modules in Jiangxi and Zhejiang. New in this version: heat pumps in Guangdong (Foshan) and Shandong (Qingdao), hybrid inverters in Zhejiang (Ningbo).
