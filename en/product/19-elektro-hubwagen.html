Description

The electric pallet truck moves pallets of insulation, plasterboard, tiles or tools of up to 1.5 t across the construction site and materials store without physical effort.

More

A professional unit has a robust steel frame, low-maintenance drive motors, polyurethane wheels for screed and industrial floors and a safety-reverse belly button against crushing. Users in Germany expect an EU declaration of conformity under the Machinery Directive, a German manual, secured spare-parts supply and the annual inspection under DGUV Regulation 68 and the BetrSichV. Lithium batteries must come with a UN 38.3 test report and the charger with a CE mark.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC (or Regulation (EU) 2023/1230) referencing model and serial number, plus CE marking on the unit
  • Test report from an accredited lab (TÜV, SGS, Intertek) covering EN ISO 3691-1, EN 1175 and EN 12895
  • UN 38.3 test report and datasheet of the Li-ion battery (EN IEC 62619) as well as dangerous-goods packaging for sea freight
  • German operating manual and rating plate with rated load as the basis for the annual inspection under DGUV Regulation 68
  • Spare-parts list with wear parts (wheels, motor, charger), written supply commitment for at least 5 years and a warranty of at least 12 months
  • Verify the individual listing: have battery type (Li-ion), capacity and fork dimensions confirmed in writing and test a sample before the series order
