Request from the supplier

  • Request EN 71-1/-2/-3 test reports for model H525-T128AB-100 in the 100 cm size; reports for smaller sizes do not automatically apply.
  • Fix plush fibre, pile, fill weight and total weight in writing and weigh the sample; the price range (US$5.00-17.60 at 100 cm) is usually explained by material and fill amount.
  • Have eye and nose attachment (pull test), seam strength, needle/metal detection and odour checked on the sample.
  • Define labelling under GPSR and toy law (manufacturer, EU responsible person, type/batch number, warnings) plus care instructions.
  • Plan packaging (compression bag, carton, VerpackG/LUCID) and commission a pre-shipment inspection per AQL with colour and size matching.
