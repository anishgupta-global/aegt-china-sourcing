Description

Suan La Fen (酸辣粉) is the Sichuan speciality of chewy, translucent starch noodles in a broth of brewed vinegar, chili and Sichuan pepper.

More

The bowl (approx. 105 g) contains the noodle block and several seasoning sachets for vinegar sauce, chili oil and powder, which are only combined when hot water is added. Quality markers are springy noodles that do not stick together in the bowl, a balanced interplay of sourness and “mala” heat, and oil-tight sealed sachets. For the German market, complete FIC (LMIV) labelling including allergens (wheat from the vinegar seasoning, sesame), permitted additives and bowl material that complies for food contact all count. Without meat and dairy components the import hurdle for animal products does not apply; an artificial “beef flavour” is not meat, but should not be advertised as vegan without confirmation from the manufacturer.

Request from the supplier

  • Check the factory's certificates (HACCP, ISO 22000, BRCGS or IFS) for a scope covering instant/starch noodles: certification body, validity, factory address.
  • Request the full recipe per sachet: exclude meat extracts and animal fats; have the origin of the flavouring and E635 confirmed in writing before claiming “vegan” or “vegetarian”.
  • Lab report per batch: pesticides in chili and spices (Reg. (EC) 396/2005), aflatoxins and 3-MCPD/glycidyl esters in palm and chili oil (Reg. (EU) 2023/915), microbiology; check the ingredients against the current Reg. (EU) 2019/1793.
  • Declaration of compliance for bowl, lid and sachets (Reg. (EC) 1935/2004); drop, vibration and oil-sachet leak test in the shipping carton.
  • Check the German label under the FIC Regulation: ingredient list, allergens (wheat, sesame, possibly soy/peanut), additives with class name, nutrition values, importer; register under VerpackG (LUCID).
  • Clarify samples (taste, noodle bite, sachets) and OEM terms: MOQ, recipe adaptation, private label and remaining shelf life on arrival (listing: 10 months shelf life).
