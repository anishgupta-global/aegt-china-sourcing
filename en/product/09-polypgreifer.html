Description

The orange-peel grapple is the classic handling tool for scrap, wood, stone, slag and loose demolition material: several shells closing against each other enclose irregular loads so that nothing falls through.

More

The researched model HM08A5 in the rotating version is designed for 20 to 24 t excavators, holds 0.5 m³ and, according to the manufacturer, rotates endlessly through 360° via a slewing bearing with an Eaton hydraulic motor. Q355Mn body, shells with NM400 wear plates and heat-treated pins are the quality markers professional users ask for. They also expect a CE-compliant attachment with a declaration of conformity, a mounting that matches the excavator (quick coupler or pin pattern), cleanly routed hydraulic lines and EN 10204 3.1 material certificates. Flow (60–80 l/min) and pressure (180–220 bar) must be matched to the data of the carrier excavator.

Request from the supplier

  • Request CE marking, EC declaration of conformity and German operating manual before ordering (Machinery Directive, Machinery Regulation from 20 Jan 2027)
  • Have fit to the excavator confirmed in writing: carrier weight, oil flow, pressure, mounting (quick coupler or pin pattern), hydraulic ports
  • Demand EN 10204 3.1 material certificates for Q355Mn and NM400 and a hardness report for the wear plates
  • Check the rotator: maker and type of the Eaton motor, slewing bearing, speed, test-run video before shipping
  • Fix warranty (12 or 18 months per listing) and EU spare-parts supply contractually
  • Agree a pre-shipment inspection (SGS, TÜV or Bureau Veritas) and plywood-case sea packing (gross weight over 2 t)
