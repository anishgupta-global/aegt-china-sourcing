Description

The folding gazebo offers 9 m² of shade and rain protection for garden, market, camping and parties.

More

The scissor frame of powder-coated steel (alternatively aluminium) is erected without tools, the water-repellent polyester roof protects with UV factor 50+ against sun and light rain; four side walls enclose the space. Quality markers are tube cross-section and wall thickness, taped seams, effective wind securing (guy lines, pegs, sandbags) and a sturdy carry bag. For retail, documented water and UV tests matter, as does a clear note that a folding gazebo is not a permanent roof for storms or snow.

Request from the supplier

  • Frame: tube cross-section, wall thickness (mm), powder coat or galvanising, salt-spray report, weight; request the aluminium variant separately.
  • Roof fabric: request denier, coating, water column (mm) and a UPF 50+ test report to EN 13758-1.
  • Wind securing: guy lines, pegs, sandbag set and stated wind rating; warning 'not for storms or snow' in the instructions.
  • Side walls: included in the price or extra, windows / door, quantity; have prices with and without walls stated separately.
  • German labelling and instructions, GPSR label; for event use ask for the fabric's fire behaviour (e.g. DIN 4102-B1 / EN 13501-1).
