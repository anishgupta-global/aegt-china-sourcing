The silicone mask body with double head harness, the scratch-resistant polycarbonate lens and a speech diaphragm provide a secure seal, clear vision and understandable communication in the team. German professionals expect EN 136 with a stated class (2 or 3), a standardised filter thread, a manual in German and spare visors, valves and straps. As category III PPE an EU type-examination certificate with NB number is mandatory; suitability for TRGS 519 work follows from the risk assessment.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU type-examination certificate for EN 136 (class 2 or 3) with NB number – cross-check in NANDO.
  • Test report on total leakage/seal and visor (mechanical strength, field of vision) to EN 136.
  • Thread/connection test to EN 148-1 or proof of compatibility with the supplied P3 filters (request the filters' EN 143 certificate separately).
  • Written commitment to German-language instructions, spare parts (visor, valves, straps) and warranty.
  • Demand the plant name and ISO 9001 proof; have samples tested independently (e.g. fit test) before a series order.
