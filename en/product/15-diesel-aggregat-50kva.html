Description

The silent 50 kVA diesel set supplies 400/230 V three-phase power to strip-out, demolition and hazardous-material remediation sites when there is no grid connection or the grid fails.

More

A professional user in Germany expects a robust steel frame with a sound canopy, a large tank for at least one working day of autonomy, an EU Stage V engine and a clear controller with emergency stop. The unit can only be approved with CE conformity under the Machinery Directive, the Outdoor Noise Directive 2000/14/EC with LwA marking and a German operating manual. When buying, distinguish standby from continuous power (PRP), because 50 kVA standby equals only about 42 kVA continuous.
