The German trade supplies factory-made boards with CE marking, a declaration of performance under EN 13164, thermal conductivity class WLS 034, stepped-lap edges and clear marking of compressive stress. Professional users also expect fire class information, an HBCD-free product and suitability under DIN 4108-10. Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Declaration of performance (DoP) under Reg. (EU) 305/2011 and CE marking with the number of the test laboratory or notified body involved
  • Test reports from an accredited laboratory for thermal conductivity, compressive stress and reaction to fire for exactly the 100 mm thickness
  • Declaration on blowing agent and HBCD freedom (POP Regulation (EU) 2019/1021)
  • Sampling of board size, rebate and surface before series production plus incoming inspection of every delivery
  • Moisture- and UV-protected packaging, palletising and German labelling on every pack
