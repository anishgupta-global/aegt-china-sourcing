Description

The device wraps around neck and shoulders and kneads in shiatsu style; an infrared heat function loosens the muscles further.

More

According to the maker, the battery gives 1.5 to 2.5 hours per charge, cord-free in the office, car or on the road. Discerning buyers look for quiet running, soft covers, adjustable intensity, a tested surface temperature, overheat protection and auto-off. Positioning as a wellness product is equally important: therapeutic claims would pull the device into medical-device territory.

Request from the supplier

  • Request test reports EN 60335-1/-2-32, EMC and surface temperature (heat function) for ADS-002; do not accept listing certificates unverified.
  • Battery: have mAh, cell type, UN 38.3 report, MSDS, charging circuit/overcharge protection and charging time documented.
  • Advertising copy without therapeutic or disease claims (competitor listings partly cite 'cervical spondylosis'): otherwise MDR risk.
  • Check samples: kneading pressure, noise, cover material for harmful substances (REACH, PAH); negotiate MOQ 1,000 pcs, tiers up to 3,000+.
  • OEM: logo, colour, packaging, German manual, GPSR details; importer's ElektroG/battery/LUCID registrations.
  • Clarify production site (office in Xiamen, planned new plant near Ningde); request the BV audit report.
