Description

The USB webcam delivers Full HD video at up to 30 frames per second and works as a UVC device without driver installation on Windows, macOS, Linux and ChromeOS.

More

Demanding buyers in Germany expect a sufficiently wide angle for group calls, a microphone with noise reduction, reliable auto exposure and above all a mechanical privacy cover, because data protection is a purchase argument. The model selected here, C1920MF, combines a 100-degree wide angle, dual stereo microphone and H.264 output in a housing with desktop base. For sale in the EU, CE conformity (EMC), RoHS, ElektroG registration and GPSR marking are required; because of the firmware, duties under the Cyber Resilience Act will also apply in future.

The privacy cover is an evidenced selling feature (Hama C-400 lists 'SPY - Protection', the Spy Protect line carries it in its name). Return risk: weak image quality in low light, focus and microphone level; reviews decide. Demand from home office is an assumption, not evidenced.

Request from the supplier

  • Have focus type (fixed or autofocus), sensor and chipset type confirmed in writing; test low-light image quality on five samples
  • EU declaration of conformity and EMC test report (EN 55032/EN 55035) from an accredited lab for exactly model C1920MF; the listing names 'CE & CCC', request the reports
  • Firmware and data protection: no radio or cloud function, UVC without extra software; ask for the manufacturer's update and security process (Cyber Resilience Act)
  • Endurance-test privacy cover and base (switching cycles, stability on monitor and desk)
  • RoHS and REACH report for plastics and cable (phthalates); clarify ElektroG registration, WEEE symbol and EU responsible person on packaging
  • OEM release: packaging and German manual, measure microphone level, agree AQL 1.5/4.0 and warranty terms
