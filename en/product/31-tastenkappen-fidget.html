Description

The keycap fidget keychain combines the satisfying click of a mechanical switch with small-format collectible looks and can be carried as a key or bag charm.

More

The keycap-shaped body of about 2 cm is made of plastic, with customisable colours, print and logo; an LED version with a button cell is possible. For the European market, cleanly finished edges, firmly seated keys, tested metal rings (nickel release) and a clear positioning as a children's toy (EN 71) or an adult gadget (GPSR) matter. Own designs instead of copying known collectible series keep the product legally safe.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the positioning before testing: children's toy 3+ (EN 71-1/-3, warning) or gadget 14+ (GPSR only, not presented child-appealing).
  • Accredited lab test reports: small parts/pull-off of keys, EN 71-3, nickel release of the ring (EN 1811), lead, cadmium.
  • LED variant: screw-secured button-cell compartment, EN 62115/EN 71-1, EMC, RoHS; clarify battery registration under BattG.
  • Switch durability (click cycles), click sound and consistency across the lot as a test report or sample test.
  • Own designs: design rights and mould ownership in writing; no copies of Clickeez or Sanrio designs; samples before series approval.
  • Packaging with German warnings and GPSR information (EU responsible person, batch); set blind-bag distribution key.
