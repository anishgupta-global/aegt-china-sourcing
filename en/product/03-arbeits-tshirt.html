Description

The work T-shirt is the base layer in summer and under a hi-vis vest or pullover and is consumed in large quantities.

More

Quality shirts use dense single jersey of ring-spun cotton with double-stitched hems, a shape-retaining round neck with an elastane rib and low shrinkage. A professional user in Germany expects correct fit in EU sizes, colour-fast goods that survive industrial laundering and harmful-substance testing to OEKO-TEX or REACH. The shirt is not PPE; a hi-vis shirt additionally needs EN ISO 20471.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Fibre composition test report (100% cotton) and fabric weight in g/m² per colour
  • Test report on dimensional change after washing and colour fastness (ISO 105-C06) for all ordered colours
  • OEKO-TEX certificate or REACH test report from an accredited lab on azo dyes and formaldehyde
  • Size chart in EU measurements with a fit sample before production
  • Label with fibre, size and care marking in German
  • For logo print or embroidery: durability sample after 10 washes and sample approval
