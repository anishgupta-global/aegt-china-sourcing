Checks & customs

Six checks before every first order, and the customs traps of this list.

01

Business licence

Check the 18-digit code in the gsxt.gov.cn register: active, matching business scope.

02

Maker or trader

Verify the factory address; for Tier B order an audit by SGS, TÜV or Bureau Veritas.

03

Certificates

CE only with a notified body — check its number in NANDO; confirm ISO with the certifier.

04

Payment

No full prepayment: 30 % deposit, 70 % against bill of lading or letter of credit.

05

Importer duties

The importer is liable for CE; Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 applies from 20 Jan 2027; duties in TARIC.

06

Support

AHK Greater China, GTAI and IHK Berlin for partner checks and credit reports.

Anti-dumping

Mobile access platforms

Self-propelled platforms ≥ 6 m from China: anti-dumping 20.6–54.9 % plus countervailing 7.3–14.2 %, up to 66.7 % combined (Reg. (EU) 2025/45, 2025/796).

Steel screws and bolts

Anti-dumping up to 86.5 % (Reg. (EU) 2022/191), under expiry review in 2026: check TARIC before every order.

Excavators

No EU anti-dumping duty on Chinese excavators found; the UK has levied anti-dumping duties since May 2025.

Machinery Regulation

From 20 Jan 2027 the Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 applies; the importer is liable for CE conformity and a German manual.
