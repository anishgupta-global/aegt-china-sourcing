Description

The tracker locates dogs via GPS/BeiDou and adds AGPS, Wi-Fi and cell-tower positioning (LBS), so an approximate position remains available indoors and at the edge of woods.

More

Data travels through a 4G cellular module to a platform with app and PC access, including track history. The 4,000 mAh polymer battery targets long standby for free-roaming and hunting dogs, and the housing is designed to be waterproof. For EU sales, what matters is a clean RED proof, suitable EU cellular bands, a GDPR-compliant backend and a white-label subscription model; according to the maker, software and branding can be customised via OEM/ODM.

Demand is evidenced: Tractive sells dog trackers from EUR 69 (homepage: 69/79/99) and advertises over 50,000 Trustpilot reviews (4.7); Hornbach lists Weenect dog trackers from EUR 44.99 …

More

Risks: the device price is only part of the business (connectivity, app, subscription, support), returns due to coverage and battery, and radio compliance. Without its own platform the product is hardly competitive; the subscription carries the economics.

Request from the supplier

  • Check EU cellular bands: the listing names LTE-FDD B1/B3/B5/B8 and China TDD bands but no B20 (800 MHz, common in Germany); require an EU variant and a network test in Germany.
  • Prove RED compliance: test reports (radio, EMC, safety, cybersecurity) and EU declaration of conformity; CE/FCC/RoHS in the listing are self-declarations.
  • Battery: UN 38.3 report, cell manufacturer, protection circuit, transport classification and the requirements of Regulation (EU) 2023/1542.
  • Platform: white-label app for iOS and Android (listing mentions only Android/PC), EU hosting, GDPR contract, data deletion, subscription billing/API.
  • Ingress protection (IP code) by test report; CA25 is a hunting-dog model, so request a cat and small-dog variant with weight and dimensions separately.
  • Test samples with an EU SIM/eSIM (accuracy, real battery life, OTA updates) and fix the warranty/RMA process in the contract.
