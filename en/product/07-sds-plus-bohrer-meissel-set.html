Description

The 12-piece SDS-plus set combines nine hammer bits from 5 to 20 mm diameter and three chisels (point, flat and groove chisel) in a plastic case.

More

All tools have the SDS-plus shank and fit standard rotary and combination hammers of this class. According to the manufacturer the bits carry a straight carbide tip (YG8C) on a 40Cr steel body, the chisels are 40Cr steel; all parts are through-hardened and sandblasted. For trade businesses and ambitious DIYers the set is the complete starter kit for anchor holes, slots and light demolition work in concrete, natural stone and masonry. What matters on the EU market: legible size marking, clean shank run-out checks and a REACH-compliant surface.

More

bundles with rotary hammers at Contorion/Toolbrothers (per scan research). Typical add-on to a rotary hammer, light and shipping-friendly, repeat purchase as bits wear. Competition: brands (Einhell, Bosch, Makita), DIY-store own brands (Pattfield 3-pc chisel set EUR 8.95 at Hornbach) and discounter sets from EUR 3.99 (Lidl/Parkside, per scan). Low return risk; quality risk with single-cutter bits (less removal than 4-cutter) - state cutter shape and carbide grade openly.

Request from the supplier

  • Have the carbide grade (YG8C), brazed joint and cutter geometry documented; run test drillings in C20/25 concrete (hole count, drilling time) against a branded set.
  • Gauge SDS-plus shank dimensions and run-out on samples; test fit in at least three hammer makes.
  • Request a test report (SGS or similar) on 40Cr material and REACH/SVHC for steel, coating and plastic case; have the 'CE, SGS, ISO9001' claim in the listing substantiated.
  • Case: check latches, hinges, size print per slot and drop test; ask for the aluminium box as a variant.
  • Clarify marking: brand/unbranded, manufacturer or importer details, EU responsible person under GPSR, batch number.
  • Approve a sample before series production and agree a pre-shipment inspection (AQL).
