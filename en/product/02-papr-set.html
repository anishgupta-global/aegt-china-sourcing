Description

A powered air-purifying respirator (PAPR) delivers filtered air under positive pressure to a mask …

More

hood or helmet and noticeably reduces breathing effort: it is the tool of choice for long shifts in asbestos remediation, strip-out and mould remediation where TRGS 519 demands high protection levels. High-quality units offer breath-controlled or constant flow with alarms for clogged filters and low battery, a robust, easily decontaminated blower housing and a battery lasting at least one shift. Professional users in Germany expect approval to EN 12941 (TH) or EN 12942 (TM) with class marking, German-language service, available replacement filters, batteries and hoses, and compliant battery transport approval. As category III PPE the EU type-examination by a notified body is mandatory.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU type-examination certificate for EN 12941 (TH3) or EN 12942 (TM3) with NB number – the supplier partly cites both standards inconsistently, clarify the assignment in writing.
  • Test report on flow rate, alarm function (filter/battery) and total leakage of the complete system including the supplied facepiece.
  • Battery proofs UN 38.3 and IEC 62133 as well as a charger with CE/EMC conformity.
  • Accept an ATEX/Ex certificate only if category and testing body are stated (do not blindly adopt suppliers' Ex claims).
  • German-language manual, availability of replacement filters, batteries and hoses for at least 5 years, plus EU service partner and warranty.
