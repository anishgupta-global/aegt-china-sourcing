Construction machinery

Chinese construction machinery – from mini excavators through skid-steer and wheel loaders to demolition breakers – covers the fleet for demolition, strip-out and remediation.

More

For the EU market, the CE declaration of conformity, an EU Stage V engine and a secured spare-parts and service chain matter more than the list price.

Construction machinery clusters in Changsha (Hunan: SANY, Zoomlion, Sunward), Xuzhou (Jiangsu: XCMG), Liuzhou (Guangxi: LiuGong), Shandong (Jining: Shantui; Qingdao: Fullwin; Linyi, Tai'an: mini and compact machines) and Shanghai (crushers).

Duty: EU third-country duty rates (TARIC) are not verified and therefore left empty; HS 8429 52 applies to excavators with a 360-degree superstructure. CN codes were chosen from the description text (zolltarifnummern.de, 2024): skid-steer 8429 51 99 (front-end loader, self-propelled; tracked version would be 8429 51 91), electric tracked dumper 8704 10 90 (dumper for off-highway use, engine other than piston combustion engine; EU Regulation 1114/2012 classifies a tracked mini transport vehicle with tipping body as a dumper under 8704 10). None of the classifications is confirmed by a binding tariff information; clarify before ordering. Approval: demand the CE declaration of conformity, EU Stage V engine and noise compliance; EPA statements in Chinese listings do not apply in the EU; battery dumpers need a UN 38.3 transport report for lithium. The dealer listing for the Shantui SE18SR contradictorily names SE17SR and Stage V or Euro 5. Images of the three existing products come from dealer or manufacturer listings; clarify usage rights. The Zenith website was derived from the e-mail domain only.
