Description

The softshell work jacket protects against wind and light rain during demolition, strip-out and outdoor work without restricting movement.

More

Quality models combine a stretch polyester outer fabric laminated to a breathable TPU membrane and a fleece backing, with YKK zips, adjustable cuffs and a detachable hood. A professional user in Germany expects clean workmanship, well-planned pockets for phone and folding rule, reflective details and a PFC-free finish. If the jacket is sold as rain protection (EN 343) or hi-vis (EN ISO 20471), it is category II PPE with an EU type-examination.

Request from the supplier

  • Test report on water column (EN 20811) and water vapour resistance (EN 31092), new and after washing
  • If claimed as PPE: EU type-examination certificate from a notified body and EU declaration of conformity under Regulation (EU) 2016/425
  • Proof of a PFC-free finish and REACH conformity (test report from an accredited lab)
  • Instructions and care labelling in German, size chart in EU measurements
  • Approval sample from the series in three sizes with zip and seam pull test before shipment
  • Warranty and claims terms (zip, membrane delamination) set out in writing in the supply contract
