The advantage over branded goods is the price; with ready-built units (pre-installed, tested, EU warranty) there is additional time saving for B2B customers. Risks: RED/cybersecurity evidence for the Wi-Fi module, Windows licence issues, returns due to drivers or display outputs.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the configuration in writing (barebone or populated, e.g. 16 GB DDR4 + 512 GB NVMe + Windows 11 Pro OEM) and request separate prices for RAM, SSD and licence; the listing shows no configuration.
  • Test the display outputs on a sample: the data sheet quotes HDMI only at 4096 x 2160 at 30 Hz and VGA at 1080p; for office monitors with QHD/4K at 60 Hz the suitability (no DisplayPort/USB-C in the data sheet) has to be checked.
  • Have the radio module (Wi-Fi/Bluetooth) evidenced with module maker, RED test reports (EN 300 328, EN 301 893, EN 301 489-17) and cybersecurity evidence EN 18031-1/-2; otherwise sell only without radio module.
  • Request a power supply with EU plug, test report (EN 62368-1) and ecodesign efficiency to (EU) 2019/1782; declaration of conformity with EU responsible person.
  • Check BIOS/firmware source, TPM 2.0 and secure boot, no pre-installed third-party software; Windows only with a genuine OEM/COA licence, driver pack and update commitment.
  • Run a burn-in and temperature test on a sample (fan noise in dB(A), throttling under load); fix the 3-year warranty quoted in the listing together with an EU return process in the contract.
