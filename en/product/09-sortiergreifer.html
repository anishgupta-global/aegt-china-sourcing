Description

The demolition and sorting grapple separates demolition material by type, such as wood, metal, masonry and concrete, and loads it straight into containers or onto trucks.

More

On German sites it is indispensable for separating waste streams under the Commercial Waste Ordinance and the Circular Economy Act. A professional user expects a wear-resistant steel frame with reinforced tines, two hydraulic motors for continuous 360-degree rotation and cylinders protected against hose rupture. It must be delivered with CE conformity under the Machinery Directive and a proper fit to the carrier.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • CE declaration of conformity with serial number and technical file under 2006/42/EC.
  • Written confirmation that it is a demolition/sorting grapple with rotation (marketplace titles often name a grapple only generally), with a data sheet on weight, opening width and rotation motors.
  • Fit to the carrier: mounting, pin size, oil flow and pressure confirmed in writing.
  • Material certificate (EN 10204, type 3.1) for frame and tines; hardness data.
  • German operating manual, spare parts (seal kits, tines, hydraulic motors) and at least 12 months of warranty.
  • Test-run video showing 360-degree rotation and gripping movement before delivery.
