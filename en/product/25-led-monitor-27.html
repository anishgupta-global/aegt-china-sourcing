Description

The 27-inch monitor with IPS panel resolves 2560 x 1440 pixels (QHD) and delivers stable colours with 178/178 degree viewing angles and 99% sRGB for office work, spreadsheets, CAD views and image editing.

More

Professional buyers expect a flicker-free backlight with low-blue-light mode, a height-adjustable stand or VESA 100 mount and digital inputs (HDMI, DisplayPort) instead of VGA. Pixel-fault class to ISO 9241-307, reliable test reports for safety and EMC and the ecodesign data (EPREL entry, energy efficiency class) are prerequisites for sale in the EU. Without USB-C docking the unit is positioned as a price-aggressive standard solution next to branded monitors; warranty handling and an exchange service from an EU warehouse decide customer satisfaction.

Selling points against no-name goods are CE/EPREL documents, an EU warranty process and a bundle with a monitor arm or USB-C dock (assessment). Risks: pixel faults and backlight bleeding, warranty cases and returns of bulky goods; an RGB/gaming look slows office sales.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the variant: confirm model HP-XL2701 (27 inch, 2560 x 1440) and refresh rate in writing; the listing also offers a 23.8-inch variant (HP-XL2301) and options up to 240 Hz.
  • Contractually fix panel origin (maker, type), pixel-fault class to ISO 9241-307, warranty period (min. 24 months) and a free spare-unit ratio shipped with the order.
  • Request test reports: EN 62368-1, EN 55032/EN 55035, RoHS test (EN IEC 63000) and the energy-label data for EPREL; declaration of conformity with EU responsible person under GPSR.
  • Clarify the office version: neutral housing without RGB lighting, own-brand/OEM print, German-language manual and packaging.
  • Check a sample (listing quotes USD 83): brightness in cd/m2, colour gamut with a colorimeter, flicker, backlight bleeding, stand stability, EU power supply/cable.
  • Agree transport packaging with a drop test and carton/pallet sizes; register packaging under LUCID/VerpackG.
