Description

The trailer-mounted work platform is towed to the site by a suitable towing vehicle and suits facade, roof and installation work at changing job sites without a special transporter.

More

Typical features are hydraulic outriggers, an articulating or telescopic boom and an approved trailer design; the manufacturer must prove stability to EN 280 and road approval as a trailer. German users expect CE conformity, a logbook, instruction under DGUV Principle 308-008 and the operating permit or type approval of the trailer. No trailer model was found on the manufacturer pages checked (LGMG, Sinoboom), so manufacturer, model and price must be determined via RFQ.

Request from the supplier

  • Naming of a specific model with data sheet (working height, outreach, platform load, total weight, axle load)

  • EN 280 test report from an accredited body including outrigger operation and stability
  • EU declaration of conformity (Machinery Directive) and CE marking on the rating plate
  • German operating manual, logbook, spare parts list and service partner in Germany or the EU
  • TARIC query: classification (work platform or vehicle) and AD/countervailing duty rate of the chosen manufacturer
