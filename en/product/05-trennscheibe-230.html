Description

The 230 mm disc is the standard choice for steel girders, pipes, reinforcement and sections on strip-out and shell-construction sites because 3 mm thickness combines long life and deep cuts.

More

It runs on the large angle grinder at high peripheral speed, so reinforcement, bore tolerance and a legible maximum-speed marking are safety-critical. German professionals expect EN 12413, the oSa mark, a manufacturing date on the disc and operation only with the guard fitted. Every batch needs a test report including an over-speed test before it goes onto site.

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 12413 including over-speed test per batch
  • Verify the manufacturer's oSa licence number; request the CE, MPA and EN 12413 certificates (stated by Tianqi) in original
  • Dimension and bore check (outer diameter, thickness, 22.2 mm bore)
  • Prove reinforcement (number of glass-fibre layers) with a cut-through sample
  • Label with maximum speed, dimensions and manufacturing date
  • Trial run on our own 230 mm angle grinder before series release
