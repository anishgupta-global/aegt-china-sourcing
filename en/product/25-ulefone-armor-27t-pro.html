Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under RED 2014/53/EU for exactly this model variant, with test reports from an accredited lab (radio, EMC, safety, RF exposure)
  • Evidence of the cybersecurity requirements under Art. 3(3) d/e/f RED (e.g. assessment to EN 18031-1/-2/-3) for the firmware actually shipped
  • Technical data sheet of the thermal camera (resolution, measuring range, accuracy, emissivity setting); explicitly indicative measurement only, request a calibration certificate if needed
  • Independent test report for IP68/IP69K (EN 60529/ISO 20653) and the drop test instead of a pure marketing claim
  • Battery: test report to EN IEC 62133-2 and UN 38.3 test summary; labelling under the EU Battery Regulation (EU) 2023/1542
  • EU frequency bands (LTE B1/B3/B7/B8/B20/B28), USB-C charging port, ecodesign data sheet/energy label (EPREL) and update commitments under (EU) 2023/1670
