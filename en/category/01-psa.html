Personal protective equipment (PPE)

PPE is the largest consumable line in demolition, strip-out and asbestos remediation and also the most heavily regulated: coveralls, gloves and hearing protection fall under Category III …

More

helmets, goggles and safety shoes under Category II of the PPE Regulation (EU) 2016/425. Chinese manufacturers supply it very cheaply, but without a valid EU type-examination certificate and declaration of conformity AE Global Traders may neither import nor use it in Germany.

PPE clusters in China by sub-product: safety shoes in Gaomi/Weifang and Linyi (Shandong), disposable nitrile gloves in Zibo (Shandong), nonwoven protective coveralls in Xiantao (Hubei), safety helmets in the Yangtze Delta (Nanjing/Jiangsu, Tianchang/Anhui). New: safety goggles and hearing protection come from the Changzhou/Suzhou area (Jiangsu), Nanjing's neighbours in the Yangtze Delta; in Xiantao (Hubei) Rhycom sits alongside Fushi and Zhongtai.
