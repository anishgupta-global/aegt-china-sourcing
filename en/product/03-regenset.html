Description

The rain set reliably protects against continuous rain during scaffold, roof, demolition and outdoor work and can be worn over normal workwear.

More

Quality sets use PU-coated polyester with welded or taped seams, an adjustable hood, hook-and-loop cuffs and reflective stripes for visibility, with a jacket and elastic-waist trousers. A professional user in Germany expects tested waterproofness to EN 343 (typically class 3/1), robust workmanship and, for use near roads, additionally EN ISO 20471. As category II PPE, CE marking with an EU type-examination is mandatory.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU type-examination certificate (Module B) from a notified body for EN 343, with the body's number and a current date
  • EU declaration of conformity under Regulation (EU) 2016/425 stating model and class (e.g. 3/1)
  • Test report on water penetration (EN 20811) and water vapour resistance (EN 31092), new and after washing or abrasion
  • Proof of seam taping (seam tape) and a leak test of the finished garment
  • Marking (pictogram, EN 343 class, size, care) and instructions in German
  • Approval sample from the series in two sizes with a rain test before shipment
