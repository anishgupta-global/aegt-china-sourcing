The sourceready market report (third party) names PFAS-free coatings and induction as trends. Risk: ceramic layers often lose their non-stick effect after months, causing returns and poor reviews; the selling point is a verifiable lab report.

Request from the supplier

  • Clarify the coating in writing: ceramic (sol-gel) or Whitford/PTFE; for an own brand claiming PFAS-free, request lab analysis (total fluorine).
  • Request LFGB and Regulation 1935/2004 test reports for the specific coating and model (migration, heavy metals) from an accredited lab.
  • Measure 28 cm size, wall thickness, weight and base flatness on the sample; check the induction plate for adhesion and warping.
  • Agree a durability test of the non-stick coating (abrasion and dishwasher test) beforehand and test samples yourself.
  • Clarify manufacturer versus trader (profile: Factory & Trading), request audit/BSCI certificate.
  • Prepare GPSR data, EU responsible person and LUCID packaging registration.
