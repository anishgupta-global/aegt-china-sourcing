Description

The trimmer combines beard grooming and body-hair trimming in one fully waterproof device (IPX7 per the maker) that can be cleaned under running water.

More

A rotating 360-degree stainless-steel blade at 6,300 rpm works with five guide combs (0, 1, 2, 3, 5 mm); a cleaning brush, oil and a Type-C charging cable are included. According to the maker, the 600 mAh Li-ion battery gives 90 minutes of run time with a 1.5-hour charge. Discerning buyers expect skin-friendly, low-nickel blades, quiet running, a secure comb fit and verifiable tightness.

Request from the supplier

  • Request test reports EN 60335-1/-2-8, EMC and an IPX7 report (EN 60529) for model S11; do not accept listing claims unverified.
  • Skin contact: have nickel release of the stainless-steel blade (EN 1811) and REACH compliance of the soft-rubber material (PAH, plasticisers) tested.
  • 600 mAh battery: UN 38.3 report, MSDS, charge protection; transport as Li-ion contained in equipment.
  • Clarify intended use: two listings exist for S11 (beard/body groomer and 'Intimate Personal Body Groomer'); define the area of use and advertising claims unambiguously.
  • Samples (MOQ 1,000 per tier; sample about US$10): shaving result, noise, comb fit, immersion and cleaning test.
  • OEM: brand, packaging, German manual, GPSR details; ElektroG/battery/LUCID registrations; ask for the BSCI and TUV Rheinland audit reports.
