Description

Buckets and quick couplers turn the excavator into a multi-purpose machine: rock, thumb, grab and screening buckets can be swapped within minutes.

More

A premium attachment is made of high-strength structural steel with wear-resistant cutting edges, is matched exactly to the tonnage and coupling of the carrier machine and has a mechanical secondary lock on the quick coupler against unintended release. Professional users in Germany expect a declaration of conformity for the attachment, material certificates to EN 10204 3.1, a rating plate with working load and own weight and instruction on safe handling. At AE Global Traders they raise the utilisation of the excavators in civil engineering and demolition and save machine changes.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Declaration of conformity for buckets and quick couplers; for the quick coupler, proof of the secondary lock under ISO 13031
  • Material certificate 3.1 under EN 10204 for base material and wear parts (state grade and hardness)
  • Agreed production drawing with make, type and tonnage of the excavator, pin and coupling dimensions; approval before series production
  • Rating plate with working load, own weight and manufacturer; bucket capacity under ISO 7451
  • Welding records (welder and procedure qualification) and a sample or first-article inspection before the series order
  • German-language manual with notes on safe changing and at least 12 months of warranty
