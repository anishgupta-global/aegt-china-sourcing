Description

Still bottled drinking water in PET bottles to supply site crews in hot weather and dusty work.

More

Clear, odour- and taste-neutral goods in tightly closed bottles with tethered caps and unambiguous labelling as table water are expected, unless an official mineral water recognition exists. In Germany single-use plastic bottles carry a mandatory deposit and must be registered in the LUCID packaging register. Because of weight and freight, importing water from China makes economic sense only in exceptional cases.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Valid HACCP / ISO 22000 / FSSC 22000 certificate of the bottling plant.
  • Water analysis per batch (microbiology, chemistry) against drinking-water limits, issued by an accredited lab.
  • Clear classification: table water under the MinTafWV or official recognition as natural mineral water in the EU.
  • Declaration of compliance for PET bottle and closure under Reg. (EU) 10/2011 and proof of tethered caps (Directive (EU) 2019/904).
  • Clarify deposit eligibility (DPG mark) and system participation under the VerpackG before import.
  • Cost calculation including freight and deposit versus delivery from a German supplier.
