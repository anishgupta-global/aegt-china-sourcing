Description

The hose kit connects the carrier to the breaker or another hydraulic attachment via two assembled DN25 hydraulic hoses, the flat-face couplers retain the oil when disconnecting.

More

The kit is designed as a target specification for wire-reinforced pressure hose (class 4SP/4SH per EN 856) and up to 350 bar; the working pressure of the reference breaker is 160-180 bar. Professional users expect crimped fittings with marking on each hose, a nominal size matched to the oil flow of 150-190 l/min, protective caps on the couplers and a test report with pressure test. Connection threads and hose length must fit the carrier and the attachment.

Request from the supplier

  • Hose type and standard (EN 856 4SP/4SH or EN 853) with burst-pressure and impulse test certificate from the hose manufacturer.
  • Fittings and crimping: ferrule type, crimp dimension report, marking on each hose (manufacturer, type, pressure, date of manufacture).
  • Flat-face couplers: standard (ISO 16028), thread (BSP/NPT), pressure drop at 190 l/min, seal material, dust caps.
  • Check the design with the breaker manufacturer: calculated approx. 4.9-6.3 m/s in DN25 at 150-190 l/min, if necessary a larger nominal size for the return line.
  • Length, bend radius, hose protection (spiral or sleeve), temperature range and oil compatibility in writing.
  • Sample with pressure test report before series order, 12 months warranty, product liability evidence.
