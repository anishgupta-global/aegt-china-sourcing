Request from the supplier

  • Battery evidence: EN 62133-2 test report for cell and battery pack, UN 38.3 summary, safety data sheet; verify the capacity claim (3,000 mAh) by a discharge test on a sample
  • Have luminous flux and run time per mode measured in a lab (the listing gives no lumens); reconcile with the later product claim
  • CE conformity: EMC (2014/30/EU) and RoHS (2011/65/EU) referencing model HL70664; the Low Voltage Directive does not apply at 3.7 V, product safety runs via GPSR and EN 60598-2-8; clarify the applicability of Ecodesign (EU) 2019/2020 case by case
  • Registrations before first sale: battery (BattG/stiftung ear), electrical equipment (ElektroG), packaging (LUCID); clarify with the supplier the replaceability of the battery under Art. 11 of Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 from 18 Feb 2027
  • Sample tests: holding force of the magnet, hinge and hook life (fold cycles), IP54, 1 m drop, charging behaviour and temperature
  • German instructions and safety notes (lithium battery), marking with the EU responsible person, third-party inspection before shipment, shipping mode for lithium batteries (UN 3481, packed with equipment)
