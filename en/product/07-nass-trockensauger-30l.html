Description

The construction vacuum keeps workplaces clean during strip-out, renovation and deconstruction and picks up water, slurry and dry debris.

More

Professional users in Germany expect an impact- and corrosion-resistant housing, a removable stainless tank, a power socket for tool-triggered switching, a robust hose and easily accessible filters. What matters most for AE Global Traders is the dust class under EN 60335-2-69: for mineral dust (TRGS 559) at least class M, for asbestos and other hazardous dusts (TRGS 519) class H with HEPA filter and dust-free disposal system is mandatory. A unit without EN 60335-2-69 testing can only be used as a coarse/workshop vacuum.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 60335-2-69 stating dust class M (separation efficiency at least 99.9 %) or H (at least 99.995 %) from an accredited body
  • HEPA H13/H14 filter certificate to EN 1822 and filter spare part numbers
  • For class H: proof of a dust-free disposal system (disposal bags/Longopac) and safety filter monitoring
  • EU declaration of conformity (incl. 2014/35/EU, 2014/30/EU, 2011/65/EU) with EU authorised representative, 230 V version with Schuko plug (CEE 7/7)
  • German operating manual, filter and hose spare parts available in the EU, at least 24 months warranty
