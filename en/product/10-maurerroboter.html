Description

The bricklaying robot places masonry units in bond, applies mortar or thin-bed adhesive and takes over the repeatable laying work on large wall areas in building construction.

More

On the Chinese market a manufacturer-confirmed, series-ready wall-building robot is currently hard to evidence: Bright Dream's current catalogue lists only a block-handling and a mortar-spraying robot, and a broker portal quotes 200–350 units/h at ±1 mm (not manufacturer-confirmed). Professional users in Germany expect masonry to DIN EN 1996 (Eurocode 6) and DIN 18330 with units to DIN EN 771 and mortar to DIN EN 998-2, plus CE conformity, safeguarding of the working area to EN ISO 13849-1 and a German manual. A live demonstration on your own unit format must be demanded before any order.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Written confirmation, with model designation, that the machine LAYS units in bond (not merely carries them or sprays mortar)
  • Signed EU declaration of conformity with technical file and risk assessment to EN ISO 12100 (2006/42/EC, from 20 Jan 2027 (EU) 2023/1230)
  • Live or video proof of wall laying with your unit format and mortar, including laying rate and dimensional tolerance
  • Safety concept for the working area (laser scanner/fencing, emergency stop) to EN ISO 13849-1
  • List of approved unit formats and mortar types with testing to DIN EN 771 / DIN EN 998-2
  • German or English manual, spare-parts list, warranty and remote-service terms with an EU service partner
