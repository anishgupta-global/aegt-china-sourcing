At HORNBACH (02.11.2026) marketplace 4-packs cost EUR 23.99 (Globus ball lights) and EUR 24.99 (Palma, stainless-steel look, bollard shape), the ENOVALITE 4-pack (3,000 K, IP54, 4,000 mAh) EUR 34.95 and the Lindby 4-pack Sirita EUR 41.70; single lights of the HORNBACH own brand are EUR 2.95-3.95 with only 1 lm, simple 4-pack ground lights EUR 6.95-8.95. This shows the quality gap: whoever documents measured lumen, IP65 and battery data can position at about EUR 20-30. Price pressure high, returns rare, but complaints about early battery failure are typical.

Request from the supplier

  • Have luminous flux measured: measured lumen at 3,000 K per light (target clearly above 10 lm; many market models are around 1 lm), beam pattern, run time after full charge and after several overcast days.
  • Fix solar panel (Wp, mono- or polycrystalline) and battery (NiMH or Li-ion, mAh) in writing; the listing names neither. For lithium: UN 38.3 and IEC 62133-2; battery documentation under (EU) 2023/1542 and BattG.
  • Fix the IP65 version and prove it by test report (EN 60529); check tightness after water jet and rain cycle and the seal on the battery compartment.
  • Material and ageing: UV-stabilised ABS (yellowing, brittleness after a 500 h UV test), ground-spike material and stability, temperature resistance of the battery (the listing states -60 to +60 °C, which is not plausible for batteries).
  • Define the set: 4 lights with 4 ground spikes in the carton, colour box, German manual, GPSR details (EU responsible person), price per set; the listing applies per single piece at MOQ 1,000 pcs.
  • Request test reports (EMC EN IEC 55015/EN 61547, RoHS, REACH) as well as registration with stiftung ear and LUCID; have company certificates (ISO 9001, CE) proven with validity and test lab.
