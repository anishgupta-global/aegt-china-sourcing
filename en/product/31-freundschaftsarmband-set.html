Description

The friendship bracelet kit combines plastic and polymer clay beads, letter beads and elastic cord in a resealable box.

More

Children and teenagers design their own bracelets and name bands and give them away, a summer-camp classic with a high gift value. Buyers look for colour-matched bead palettes, complete alphabet sets, a durable cord and clean, sorted packaging. As a toy or craft kit, every batch must be placed on the market with EN 71 test reports, age grading, a small-parts warning and German labelling under the GPSR.

Request from the supplier

  • Request EN 71-1/-2/-3 test reports from an accredited lab (ISO/IEC 17025) for the fully assembled kit incl. beads, letters, cord and box
  • Fix age grading and the small-parts warning (not for children under 36 months) in the test report; do not adopt the listing's 3 to 7 years unchecked
  • Chemistry: nickel release (EN 1811) for metal parts and clasps, lead/cadmium in beads and coatings, phthalates and PAH in elastic cord and plastics
  • Have cord length, tensile strength and strangulation/swallowing risk tested per EN 71-1
  • Define completeness per box (pieces per colour, full alphabet) and colour consistency contractually; pre-shipment inspection with AQL
  • GPSR: agree manufacturer and EU responsible person details, batch number and German instructions/warnings on the packaging
