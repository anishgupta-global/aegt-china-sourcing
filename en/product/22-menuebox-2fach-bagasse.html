Description

The menu box separates main dish and side in two compartments and is thus the upgrade of the simple hinged box for menu sales, canteens and delivery services.

More

Moulded fibre from sugarcane bagasse is grease- and water-repellent, stackable and suitable for fridge and microwave; the integrally moulded hinged lid closes without extra parts. The German specialist trade expects a PFAS-free barrier, an EN 13432 certificate for the specific model and a clear allocation to packaging registration. As food packaging the box is subject to Reg. (EC) 1935/2004, the German Packaging Act and PPWR (EU) 2025/40.

Request from the supplier

  • Request an EN 13432 certificate (OK Compost, BPI) for model B002 and exactly this manufacturer; verify the number in the certifier's database.
  • PFAS test report (targeted PFAS analysis and total fluorine) for the exact article number: the manufacturer itself writes that the PFAS status must be confirmed per SKU.
  • Declaration of conformity under Reg. (EC) 1935/2004 with migration and heavy-metal test report from an accredited laboratory.
  • Leak, grease and hot-fill test on the sample with closed lid, stacking and drop test with photos; compare size with the German reference (24.5 x 16 x 6.2 cm).
  • Confirmation that no plastic coating (PE, PLA) is applied; otherwise clarify single-use plastic duties (marking, fund levy).
  • Price: Bioleader publishes no prices; obtain a quote with tiers (from 50,000 pieces, per container) and compare it with the Huainan Wenyou listing (USD 0.07 to 0.092) and ECOLIFE (USD 0.07/0.06/0.05 per tier).
