Description

The Ulefone Armor 26 Ultra is a 5G outdoor smartphone with a 6.78-inch display (120 Hz), 12 GB RAM and 512 GB storage for users who must stay reachable in dust, water and shocks.

More

The Armor series is typically built for protection ratings such as IP68/IP69K and military drop tests; the exact values of the 26 Ultra must be confirmed in writing before ordering. The satellite messaging function (via Skylo/Bullitt on the predecessor 23 Ultra) requires a paid subscription, has no global coverage and does not replace a satellite phone. A professional user in Germany expects CE/RED conformity with cybersecurity proof, ecodesign-compliant spare parts and updates, and a German manual.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under RED 2014/53/EU with test reports (radio, EMC, safety) and cybersecurity proof under EN 18031
  • SAR test report to EN 50566 / EN 62209-1 (head and body use) from an accredited lab for the delivered model
  • Written confirmation that the satellite function is included in the delivered variant, which network it uses, whether Europe is covered and what the subscription costs
  • Frequency band list for Germany (e.g. LTE B3/B7/B20, 5G n78) and a sample test with a German SIM
  • Lab test reports for IP68/IP69K and MIL-STD-810H
  • Ecodesign proofs: energy label, repairability information, spare parts (battery, display) and software updates for at least 5 years
  • German manual, warranty of at least 24 months, service point in the EU and WEEE/battery registration of the importer
