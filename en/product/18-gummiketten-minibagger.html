Description

Endless rubber track for mini excavators in the roughly 1.5 to 1.8 t class, in the widespread size 230 × 96 × 33 (width × pitch × number of links).

More

It replaces the worn original track; two tracks are needed per machine, ideally as a pair from the same batch. What professional buyers look for: a continuous steel-cord reinforcement without a splice, a cut- and abrasion-resistant rubber compound, and accurate pitch and link count so that sprocket and guide engage cleanly. The size must be checked on the machine before ordering, because the same width and pitch also exists with 31, 32 or 35 links; the tread pattern can also vary between batches and should be fixed contractually.

E-commerce potential

Wear part with recurring demand.

More

On the German market, eBay dealers sell VMT tracks 230×48×70 from EUR 285 (up to EUR 545 depending on selection), a Kleinanzeigen dealer sells 230×96×30 new for EUR 299, and the specialist shop baumaschinenverschleissteile.com (Trusted Shops 4.85/5) sells the 230/33x96 for EUR 262.99 (EUR 248.71 from 2 pcs, gross, checked 2 Nov 2026). DIY chains such as Hornbach and toom sell the machines but no replacement tracks on the pages checked: a gap for an online shop. Competition comes from specialist dealers with machine fitment lists. The main risk is returns due to a wrong size (width × pitch × links) and bulky freight (listing carton 110 × 90 × 60 cm, 130 kg gross, pieces per carton not stated). Calculate the margin after freight, duty and import VAT before entering; note that one machine needs two tracks, so pair offers raise the basket.

Request from the supplier

  • Confirm the size bindingly: width × pitch × links (230 × 96 × 33), drive-lug and guide profile and machine list; compare a sample or drawing with the original.
  • Ask for the construction: endless steel-cord reinforcement without a splice, inserts, rubber compound; request test reports for tensile strength (ISO 37) and abrasion (ISO 4649).
  • Fix profile and batch: approve a tread drawing or sample so that repeat orders deliver the same profile (German dealers warn about profile changes between batches).
  • Deliver in pairs from one batch with batch number and production date on the track; test one sample set (1 pair) before ordering the 10-piece tier (US$ 140).
  • Agree the warranty (listing: 12 months) and the claims process in writing (photos, replacement instead of credit note); fix packaging, Incoterm and lead time (profile: 15 working days off season, one month peak season).
  • Check the factory: review the audit report (BV, ID MIC-ASR254112 on Made-in-China), verify the ISO 9001 certificate; do not allow OEM logos (Kubota, Bobcat) on the track.
