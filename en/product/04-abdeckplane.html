Description

The PE cover tarpaulin protects floors, machines, furniture and stored goods from dust, moisture and soiling during strip-out and remediation and serves as a base for material storage.

More

Quality product is made of tightly woven, double-side coated PE fabric with a reinforced hem, eyelets at about 50 cm spacing and UV stabilisation. Professional users in Germany expect an honestly weighed area weight, a tested tear strength and, for indoor covering, a flame-retardant material. For sealing off the black area, PE films (typically >= 200 µm) are used in addition; the tarpaulin does not replace them.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Data sheet with weighed area weight (g/m²) and dimensional tolerance; weighing report on the sample
  • Tear strength to DIN EN ISO 1421 and seam strength by an independent body
  • Fire-behaviour evidence (DIN 4102-1 B1 / EN 13501-1) if indoor covering is required
  • REACH declaration of conformity (PAH, phthalates, heavy metals) and UV stabilisation
  • Eyelets and hem reinforcement: spacing, pull-out test, material proof
  • Packaging/pallet size and binding delivery time
