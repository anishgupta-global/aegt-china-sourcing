Description

A soluble 3-in-1 coffee mix of coffee, dairy ingredients and sugar for break rooms, site tea kitchens and canteens, supplied as single-serve sticks or in bags.

More

A uniform, lump-free dissolving behaviour, a clean roast flavour without off-odours and tight, aroma-protecting stick packs that survive transport and storage on site are expected. A professional buyer in Germany demands German labelling under the FIC Regulation (LMIV) including the milk allergen declaration, traceability under Regulation (EC) 178/2002 and proof on acrylamide, ochratoxin A and pesticide residues. As a food product it carries no CE mark; the importer is liable as the food business operator, and milk-containing mixes from China must be cleared in advance with customs or the veterinary office.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Valid HACCP / ISO 22000 / BRCGS / IFS certificate of the production site.
  • Lab report per batch on pesticide, ochratoxin A and acrylamide levels.
  • Complete recipe (coffee content, milk source, sugar) with allergen matrix.
  • Written clarification with customs or the veterinary office whether the milk-containing mix may be imported.
  • Declaration of conformity for stick film and carton under Reg. (EC) 1935/2004.
  • German label draft under the FIC Regulation (ingredients, milk allergen, nutrition, importer, best-before date) and a tasting sample before series release.
