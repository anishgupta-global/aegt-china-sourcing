Description

The kit bundles the parts that are replaced regularly on a hydraulic breaker according to operating hours: seal kit, upper and lower guide bushing, retaining pins and chisel paste.

More

Seal kits and bushings are model-specific; professional users therefore expect a parts list with item numbers, defined seal materials and ordering only against model and serial number. The chisel paste must come with a REACH safety data sheet and CLP-compliant labelling. Timely replacement of bushings and seals prevents consequential damage to the chisel, cylinder and hydraulics and avoids carrier downtime.

Request from the supplier

  • Exploded drawing and parts list of the SB81 with item numbers and dimensions (seal kit, bushings, pins); kit only against model and serial number of the supplied breaker.
  • Confirm seal materials, temperature range and oil compatibility (HLP/HVLP) in writing.
  • Bushings: material, hardness, inner and outer diameter and wear limit (clearance chisel/bushing) in the manual.
  • Chisel paste: safety data sheet (REACH/CLP), German labelling, cartridge type suitable for the grease gun.
  • Unit prices per item for resale, batch marking of the parts and 12 months warranty (listing).
  • Accept a total quote for the kit only if the item list and quantities are included.
