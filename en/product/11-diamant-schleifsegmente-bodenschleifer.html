Description

Metal-bond diamond grinding shoes are the consumable of every floor grinder: they remove concrete, screed, terrazzo and natural stone …

More

smooth power-troweled floors and remove adhesives and thin coatings, dry or with water. Grit decides the pass, from coarse (16/20 to 30/40) for removal through medium (60/80) to fine (100/120) as preparation for polishing. Professionals expect a bond matched to the floor hardness (hard bond for soft, soft bond for hard substrates), even diamond distribution and an exact fit to the machine's mounting system. Required are segments to EN 13236, a REACH-compliant metal bond, GPSR-compliant labelling with an EU responsible person and, when grinding quartz-bearing substrates, dust protection under TRGS 559.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Pure wear part: every floor grinder needs several grits and regular replacement, hence repeat purchase for every machine.

More

Evidence from the scan (pages not reopened, partly HTTP 403): dealers list grinding plates and segments separately from the machine (Baustoffshop: Nozar Butterfly, Global Diamond; eBay shop American Diamond Tools: 3 segments 30/40 med bond for EDCO, STOW and Husqvarna EUR 164.80; Eisen-Wolff: cup wheel 125 mm EUR 34.50 net, a different product); Laegler fits its stainless plate with six segments, PCD rings are the premium tier. Competition: specialist dealers with machine-specific systems (HTC, Husqvarna/Redi-Lock, Lavina). Return risk is high if the mounting system does not fit the machine. The China purchase price (Sunny, HTC arrow segments: USD 6–12 per piece per the Made-in-China listing) is far below the German reference; Ashine lists no prices.

Request from the supplier

  • Match mounting system and hole pattern against the Xingyi floor grinders in the shop (11-bodenschleifer, 11-bodenschleifer-klein) and test a sample on the machine; Ashine states no machine compatibility on the page.
  • Have segment size, diamond concentration and bond grades (XS to XH) confirmed in writing per grit; the page gives no segment dimensions.
  • Request the product certificate (linked as PDF on the Ashine page) and the EN 13236/MPA proof in the original; the website names ISO 9001, ISO 14001, MPA and SMETA.
  • Compare grinding performance and tool life per grit and floor hardness (test protocol, m² to wear-out) against a branded reference segment.
  • Request a REACH declaration of conformity and material declaration for the metal bond (cobalt, nickel).
  • Ask for price and MOQ (Europe contact per website: ashine-eu@ashinetools.com); fix pack unit (set) and private-label label with German GPSR marking.
