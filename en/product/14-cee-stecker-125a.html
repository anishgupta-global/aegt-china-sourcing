Description

The 125 A CEE plug (3P+N+E, 400 V, IP67) is the heavy-duty connection for main boards, mobile generators, site cranes, large fans and the supply of site distribution boards on large construction sites.

More

At these currents, low contact resistance, solid contact pieces, a strong strain relief for cross-sections up to 70 mm² and an impact-resistant IP67 housing to EN 60529 that withstands site traffic are expected. The product must be CE-compliant and meet DIN VDE 0100-704 and the inspection duties under DGUV Regulation 3; brands such as Mennekes, ABL or PCE set the benchmark for contact safety and temperature rise. Imported goods are only marketable with a test report to EN 60309-1/-2 including a temperature-rise test at 125 A and a German declaration of conformity.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 60309-1/-2 from an accredited lab (e.g. TÜV, VDE, SGS), including temperature rise at rated current
  • CE declaration of conformity (2014/35/EU, 2014/30/EU, RoHS) referencing the exact model and manufacturer
  • Proof of IP67 protection to EN 60529 on a test sample (mated and unmated)
  • Material evidence: contact material (copper alloy, tin- or silver-plated) and UV- and flame-resistant housing
  • Sample test of mating compatibility and 6 h coding against Mennekes or ABL counterparts
  • Routine testing and traceability (batch number, production date) plus a take-back clause for serial defects
