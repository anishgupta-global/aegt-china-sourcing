Description

The material airlock separates the black area from the rest of the building and lets packed waste, tools and equipment be cleaned and passed out under control without releasing fibres into the surroundings.

More

Quality versions consist of a foldable aluminium or steel frame with a washable, flame-retardant cover, doors that open alternately and a connection for the negative-pressure unit. German specialist contractors expect smooth, washable surfaces and a design that reliably separates the black area in line with TRGS 519 and DGUV Regel 101-004. Verifiable fire-protection and electrical documentation, spare parts and assembly instructions in German are part of the minimum standard.
