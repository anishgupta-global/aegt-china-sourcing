Description

The passive shoulder exoskeleton carries part of the arm weight when drilling, chiselling, mounting and grouting overhead and thereby lowers shoulder load during long shifts.

More

Spring mechanism, light metal structure and washable pads work without battery or power supply, even on dusty strip-out sites. Professional users in Germany expect adjustable support force, suitable sizes, skin-friendly materials and a documented benefit within the risk assessment. Compliance means a clean classification of the product, a CE declaration, material tests and a German manual with user instruction.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Written product classification (machine, PPE, medical device or consumer product) and an EU declaration of conformity based on it, with a risk assessment to EN ISO 12100
  • Test report on skin compatibility (ISO 10993-1) and REACH compliance of pads, straps and coatings
  • Endurance test report of the spring mechanism (number of cycles) and proof of the support forces per setting level
  • Size chart for European body measurements and a fitting test with at least three test persons on the sample
  • German manual with user instruction material, spare parts (springs, pads) and a warranty of at least 24 months
