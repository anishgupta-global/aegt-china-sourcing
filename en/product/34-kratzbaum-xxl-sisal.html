Competition is large and price-driven; return risk from bulky goods, transport damage and assembly problems. Entry should compete on quality features (stability, sisal thickness, anti-tip fixing) rather than price.

Request from the supplier

  • Request test report for formaldehyde emissions of the wood-based panels (E1 / current REACH limit) and a REACH declaration for plush and sisal
  • Stability and tip test (load sample with a 6–8 kg cat), check screwing and wall-fixing kit
  • Confirm carton size, weight and pieces per 40' HC in writing (set bulky-goods freight against retail price)
  • Sisal: test yarn thickness, bonding and fibre shedding on the sample; check plush for pilling and colour fastness
  • Agree German assembly instructions, GPSR labelling (EU responsible person) and batch number on the carton
  • Define sample dispatch with factory audit (ISO/BSCI) and pre-shipment inspection before the first series
