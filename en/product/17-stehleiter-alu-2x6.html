Description

The aluminium double ladder is the standard tool for work at working heights up to about 3 m, such as installation, painting or removal of ceiling panels.

More

The aluminium rails with slip-resistant steps and spreader restraint give a safe stand and low weight when carried between floors. Professional users in Germany expect an EN 131 Professional ladder with at least 150 kg rated load, legible German warnings and inspectability per DGUV Information 208-016. A test mark (e.g. GS) and an EN 131 test report from an accredited body belong to the purchase.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EN 131 test report (Professional type, 150 kg) from an accredited body, not just a manufacturer statement
  • Valid GS certificate or commitment to have the sample tested
  • Material certificate for the aluminium alloy and wall thickness of the rails
  • German warning and use instructions on the ladder and user information per EN 131-3
  • Sample before series order and incoming inspection per piece (step joints, spreader restraint)
  • Warranty and complaint handling for cracks or deformation
