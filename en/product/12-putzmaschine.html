Description

The plastering machine mixes dry mortar with water and conveys it through a hose to the spray gun …

More

on large refurbishment and finishing areas it replaces manual application and saves considerable time with gypsum, lime and lime-cement plaster. Professional users in Germany expect a low-wear rotor-stator feed screw with replaceable wear parts, stepless water dosing, emergency stop, remote control, good cleanability and a dealer and service network. The machine needs CE marking under the Machinery Directive, electrical equipment to EN 60204-1, a German operating manual and an inspection under DGUV Vorschrift 3. Only plaster mortars marked for machine application may be processed.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity and CE marking under 2006/42/EC and 2014/30/EU with model and serial number
  • Risk assessment and test report under EN 12001 and on electrical equipment (EN 60204-1), including emergency stop and pressure monitoring
  • German operating manual, wiring diagram and wear parts list (rotor, stator, hoses) with prices and lead time
  • Trial run with German machine plaster (gypsum or lime-cement) with a record of delivery rate and pressure
  • Matching mains connection (CEE 16/32 A for 400 V), written warranty (at least 12 months) and a spare parts stock in the EU
