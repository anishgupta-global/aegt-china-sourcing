Description

More

strip-out sites: every outlet is protected by an RCD (30 mA) and circuit breaker. Professional users in Germany expect an impact-resistant, wall-mountable or portable enclosure, sockets to EN 60309-2 and a type-tested build to DIN EN 61439-4 (site distribution boards) with wiring diagram and German manual. Makers such as Brennenstuhl, Mennekes, As-Schwabe or Doktorvolt set the benchmark with type and routine testing; boards without RCD/MCB or without type testing are not acceptable on site. The test under DGUV Regulation 3 must be documented before commissioning.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Type-test report to EN 61439-1/-4 (temperature rise, short-circuit strength, protective measures) from an accredited lab with type and serial number.
  • Brand and approval mark of the protective devices (RCD type A 30 mA, circuit breakers), e.g. ENEC/VDE; exclude no-name components.
  • Routine-test record per unit (insulation resistance, protective-conductor continuity, RCD trip test) with serial number.
  • EU declaration of conformity (LVD, EMC), wiring diagram, German manual and rating plate with manufacturer and address.
  • IP44/IK proof, sockets to EN 60309-2, and samples inspected by a German qualified electrician (DGUV V3) before the series order.
