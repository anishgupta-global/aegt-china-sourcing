Description

The spirit level aligns door frames, drywall profiles, tiles and installations during fit-out and checks the flatness of screed and walls.

More

The extruded, anodised aluminium box profile is torsion-resistant, the measuring faces are milled, the vials sit shock-proof in the profile and are protected against drops by rubber end caps. Professional users in Germany expect an accuracy of ±0.5 mm/m in normal position, vials that can be read without parallax, a calibration certificate and reproducible adjustment. Imported goods must be labelled in line with GPSR and REACH; a test report is to be demanded for accuracy and long-term stability.
