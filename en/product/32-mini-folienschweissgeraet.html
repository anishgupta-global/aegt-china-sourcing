Request from the supplier

  • Fix the configuration in writing: USB-C battery (mAh), cutting blade, heat levels, magnet - listing TX-007 confirms magnet, digital display, integrated cutting blade and 95 mm sealing width, but not the USB-C battery capacity.
  • UN 38.3 report and safety data sheet for the cell, battery marking under (EU) 2023/1542; clarify shipping class (air/sea).
  • Request CE reports (LVD/EMC) and RoHS report for exactly this model; check the company's electronics competence (main products brushes/massage).
  • Seal test with PE, PP, OPP and laminated films, pull test of the seal, check overheating and burn protection.
  • Clarify ElektroG/BattG registration, LUCID, EU responsible person (GPSR) and German manual before sales start.
  • Confirm production by audit or video tour (supplier is Manufacturer/Factory & Trading).
