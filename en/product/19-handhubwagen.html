Description

The hand pallet truck is basic equipment on every construction site and in every materials store: it moves Euro pallets carrying building materials …

More

tool boxes or waste containers without power and without a driving licence. Professional units have a welded steel-profile frame, a sealed hydraulic pump with overload valve, polyurethane wheels and fork-tip rollers for entering pallets. Users in Germany expect CE marking under the Machinery Directive, a GS mark as proof of quality, a tested rated load and spare parts such as seal kits and wheels. A regular inspection by a competent person under the BetrSichV is recommended.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC referencing the model, and CE marking on the rating plate
  • Test report from an accredited lab (TÜV, SGS) on rated load and fatigue strength to the EN 1757 series; if a GS mark is advertised, request the certificate with test body and number
  • Hydraulics documentation: pump type, seal quality, overload valve, hydraulic oil
  • Material certificate for fork profile and frame with steel grade, wall thicknesses and weld inspection committed in writing
  • German manual, rating plate with rated load and spare-parts list (wheels, seal kit, pump) with supply commitment
  • Factory audit (ISO 9001) or sample test before the series order, warranty of 12 to 24 months
