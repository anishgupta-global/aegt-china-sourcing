Description

The GNSS RTK receiver delivers centimetre-level coordinates on site for setting-out, terrain models, utility records and quantity take-off without setting up a station point.

More

An integrated tilt sensor (IMU) allows measuring with a tilted pole, for example at building corners or manhole covers, and the IP67 housing weighing about 800 g withstands dust, rain and a full survey day. Professional users in Germany expect NTRIP access to SAPOS or comparable correction services, a field test report to ISO 17123-8 and CE conformity under the Radio Equipment Directive, because readings are only reliable for acceptance and as-built records with these proofs. The importer remains responsible for product safety, radio compliance, battery transport and the German manual.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • CE declaration of conformity under RED 2014/53/EU with type designation and test reports for radio, EMC and safety
  • Field test report to ISO 17123-8 with the serial number of the delivered unit; sample for counter-check by a DAkkS-accredited body or a surveying engineer in Germany before the series order
  • Datasheet with RTK accuracy (mm + ppm), IMU calibration record and accuracy with a tilted pole, guaranteed in writing
  • UN 38.3 test report for the lithium battery and transport documents; clarify tariff code 9015 80 40 and any dual-use notes beforehand by a binding tariff information
  • Proof of NTRIP/RTCM 3 compatibility with SAPOS or the correction service in use, German manual and field software in German
  • Warranty of at least 24 months, firmware updates and a named service partner in the EU
