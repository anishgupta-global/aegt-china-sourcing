Description

The 20,000 mAh power bank with 65 W USB-C Power Delivery supplies not only smartphones and radios but also rugged tablets and laptops in the field …

More

at a calculated 74 Wh it stays below the 100 Wh limit for hand luggage in air travel. Professional buyers expect real rather than printed capacity, a 20 V output profile for notebooks, protection against short circuit, overvoltage and over-temperature, a display for the remaining charge and a robust housing. Legally the power bank counts as a lithium battery: it needs CE (low voltage, EMC, RoHS), the UN 38.3 transport test, labelling under the EU Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 and registration of the importer for waste electronics and batteries. Cell quality and rated capacity must be proven by a test report, not by what is printed on the case.

E-commerce potential

Upgrade to the 10,000 mAh power bank (25-powerbank-10000) and charging source for rugged tablet and laptop.

More

For the search 'powerbank 20000 65w' Alternate.de lists 24 hits: 20,000 mAh models range from 14.29 EUR (MediaRange, without laptop output) to 68.90 EUR (UGREEN 165 W); laptop-capable models are at 41.59 EUR (UGREEN Nexode 67 W) to 49.99 EUR (goobay laptop power bank), the UGREEN Nexode 100 W at 42.99 EUR (previously 59.99 EUR). Brand competition is tough; differentiate via bundles with rugged tablet and laptop, company logo (OEM) and a verified capacity statement. Return risk: actual capacity below the printed figure and missing laptop charging power on the 20 V profile; organise shipping and storage under lithium rules (UN 38.3, ADR/IATA).

Request from the supplier

  • Test reports EN IEC 62133-2 (cell and finished HY-66) from an accredited lab plus UN 38.3 report with test summary; listing and description name CE, RoHS, MSDS and UN 38.3, the originals must be requested
  • Discharge test on a sample batch: usable capacity in Wh at 5 V/9 V and on the 20 V profile plus continuous 65 W load on a laptop (run time, housing temperature) compared with the 20,000 mAh statement
  • Have cell origin (manufacturer and cell model), cycle life and protection functions (short circuit, overvoltage, over-temperature) confirmed in writing
  • CE declaration of conformity (EMC, RoHS) with exact model designation HY-66, German user manual with safety instructions, GPSR information (EU responsible person) and labelling under (EU) 2023/1542
  • Importer duties: ElektroG registration (stiftung ear), battery take-back scheme and LUCID for the packaging; negotiate at least 24 months warranty instead of 1 year in the listing
  • Shipping: clarify lithium transport rules (UN 38.3, ADR/IATA) with the forwarder; the calculated 74 Wh is below the 100 Wh limit for hand luggage, the actual Wh figure must be printed on the device
