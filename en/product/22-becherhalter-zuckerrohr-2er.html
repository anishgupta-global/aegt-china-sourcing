Description

The moulded-fibre cup holder takes two cups and makes multi-drink orders carriable with one hand.

More

High-quality holders stay dimensionally stable even with two hot 12 oz cups, have clean cut-outs for 8 and 12 oz cups, nest for stacking and need no plastic coating. Sugarcane bagasse and recycled-paper pulp are the established plastic-free materials in German specialist trade; buyers expect a PFAS-free test report and, where advertised as compostable, an EN 13432 certificate. Whether the holder counts as packaging under the German Packaging Act and PPWR must be clarified with the supplier and a dual-system operator.

The holder is bought per order rather than per cup, promotes multi-drink orders and fits double-wall and 8 oz cups. Risks: bulky packing volume, exact cut-out per cup diameter, softening from condensation.

Request from the supplier

  • Match the cut-out diameter to the range's cups (8 oz and 12 oz double-wall) and test with samples.
  • Carrying test with two filled hot 12 oz cups (at least 85 °C) and with condensation: no sagging, no softening.
  • PFAS test report (total fluorine) and declaration of conformity under Reg. (EC) 1935/2004; if compostability is advertised, an EN 13432 certificate for exactly this model.
  • Clarify factory status: the profile shows partner production capacity and an incubator office address; request the plant name, an audit (inspect SGS report QIP-ASI269348) or a factory visit.
  • Define carton size, packing density in nested stacks and pieces per 20 ft / 40 ft container (volume freight).
  • Obtain a binding quote with price tiers from 50,000 pieces, colour (whiteness), odour test and embossing request; the listing only shows a price range.
