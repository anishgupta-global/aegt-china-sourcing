Description

The mini condensate pump collects condensate in a tank of about one litre and automatically pushes it through a thin hose to the drain once the fill level is reached – also over several metres of lift.

More

It closes the gap wherever there is no fall for the condensate drain, for example with wall units, floor and cassette units or construction dryers in continuous operation. Professional buyers expect thermal protection, an integrated check valve, quiet running and traceable CE documentation (Low Voltage and EMC Directives). As a small, easily shipped accessory it complements air conditioners, dehumidifiers and installation kits.

Risks: electrical product (LVD/EMC/ElektroG duties), water-damage liability, noise-sensitive buyers and compatibility with the air conditioner – state noise level and connection sizes clearly.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the CE declaration of conformity and test reports (LVD to EN 60335-1/-2-41, EMC) from an accredited lab; Mepcato states CE for all products and ISO 9001:2015 – have the documents shown.
  • Fix the variant (MEC-20 1 l tank pump or in-line mini pump) and get the flow at 1 m and 5 m head guaranteed in writing; the listing gives only a maximum value.
  • Ask for sound level in dB(A) at 1 m, continuous-duty rating and any alarm/safety contact; confirm mains cable with EU plug and 230 V.
  • Break the USD 10-50 per piece listing range down to a price for the chosen variant and quantity; negotiate sample price and tiers.
  • Run the sample through an endurance test (leak-tightness, check valve, dry running); agree OEM label, German manual and EU responsible person.
  • Secure the ElektroG/WEEE registration number and LUCID packaging registration before selling in Germany.
