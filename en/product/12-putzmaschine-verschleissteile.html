Description

Rotor and stator form the screw pump of the plastering machine and wear on the abrasive mortar; timely replacement keeps delivery rate and pressure constant and prevents downtime on site.

More

Commercial users expect a precisely machined, hardened or hard-chrome-plated rotor, a dimensionally accurate elastomer stator with even wall thickness and a reinforced mortar hose NW25 with matching couplings. The set is expressly offered as 'fitting' pumps of size D6-3; sizes D6-3 and D7-2.5 are not interchangeable, so the assignment to the machine belongs in every order. Scope of delivery, installation notes and spare-part numbers must be documented in German.

Request from the supplier

  • Match pump size and dimension sheet (rotor diameter and pitch, stator length, connections) with the catalogue plastering machine; do not mix D6-3, D7-2.5 and D8-2
  • Samples of rotor and stator with a fit test on the machine and endurance run (delivery rate and pressure before and after e.g. 8 operating hours)
  • Material evidence: rotor steel (grade, hardness, chrome layer), stator elastomer (Shore hardness); REACH declaration
  • Mortar hose NW25: test certificate for working and burst pressure, coupling type and length
  • Neutral packaging without third-party brand names (PFT, Putzmeister) and batch marking
  • Price tiers from MOQ 99 pieces (rotor/stator) or 10 sets (wear-part set), warranty of at least 12 months
