Description

The sanding robot works puttied wall and ceiling surfaces automatically up to 3.3 to 6 m working height with integrated dust extraction …

More

it delivers uniform surfaces for the following coating and reduces painters' dust exposure. Chinese references are the combined spraying / putty / sanding robots from Fangshi (JPD433D, DPD445E, DPD460E) and the sanding models in Bright Dream's catalogue. In Germany, class M dust extraction, compliance with TRGS 519 and TRGS 559 and an asbestos check before sanding older putty and plaster layers are prerequisites. CE conformity, EMC and battery testing (UN 38.3) are mandatory.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity and technical file under Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC or (EU) 2023/1230 incl. risk assessment to EN ISO 12100
  • Proof of class M dust extraction (EN 60335-2-69) with filter class and dust measurement to TRGS 559
  • Factory acceptance test (FAT) with a sanding sample on a puttied plasterboard wall: Q3 / Q4 result, area output, dust-free finish
  • Notice in the manual and operating release: no sanding of suspect old putty containing asbestos without investigation under TRGS 519
  • Battery test reports (UN 38.3, IEC 62619), German manual, spare parts (sanding discs, filters) and 24-month warranty
