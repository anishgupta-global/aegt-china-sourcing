Description

For longer jobs such as ceiling installation or removal of installations, the mobile platform ladder offers an ergonomic standing surface with a guardrail instead of a ladder rung.

More

Spring-loaded castors allow the ladder to be moved unloaded; under load they compress and the ladder rests firmly on its feet. Professional users in Germany expect the type to DIN EN 131-7 with at least 150 kg rated load, a GS mark and a platform guardrail. The German construction insurer BG BAU subsidises platform ladders to DIN EN 131-7 that can be climbed from one or both sides under its occupational-safety premium scheme.
