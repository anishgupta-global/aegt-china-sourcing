Description

At the press of a button the bubble gun produces swarms of bubbles that stand out at dusk and at parties thanks to the LED light effect.

More

The bazooka housing is made of robust ABS with child-appropriate rounded edges; the built-in lithium battery (1,200 mAh per listing) makes disposable batteries unnecessary. Good devices are recognised by a leak-proof solution container, a screw-secured battery compartment, cleanly finished nozzles and complete electric-toy testing. The set should include a refill bottle and USB charging cable; German instructions and warnings are mandatory.

Request from the supplier

  • Request EN 71-1/-2/-3 and EN 62115 test reports from an accredited lab for exactly this model; the listing only says 'EN71, ASTM, CE'.
  • Li-ion battery: IEC 62133-2 and UN 38.3 reports, clarify shippability (UN 3481) with the forwarder; charger/USB cable with CE and testing.
  • Bubble solution: allergens/preservatives per Toy Safety Directive Annex II, leak protection, warning (do not drink, avoid eye contact); clarify refill bottle in the set.
  • Battery compartment screw-secured, LED light effect without glare; fix age grade and German instructions.
  • Check the factory in Yiwu: storefront lists 'Trading Company'; have ownership of the Yiwu factory and the BSCI audit report confirmed on site.
  • Registrations before sale: ElektroG (stiftung ear), battery (BattG), LUCID packaging and GPSR EU responsible person.
