Description

High-endurance cards are built for constant overwriting in dash cams, surveillance cameras and body cams, where standard cards show write errors after a short time.

More

Professional buyers expect the true rated capacity, class U3/V30 for 4K recording, a defined endurance rating (write cycles or recording hours), temperature resistance in the vehicle and a complete chain of origin for flash and controller. The reference model Lexar High-Endurance comes from Longsys in Shenzhen, a listed memory manufacturer that runs firmware, memory design and testing in-house and also owns the industrial brand FORESEE. For trade, counterfeit protection is what counts most: buy only directly or via authorised distributors, test batches before resale, clear warranty and RMA rules.

E-commerce potential

Dash cams and surveillance cameras overwrite constantly, so the card is a repeat-purchase item and often not included.

More

MediaMarkt.de lists SanDisk High Endurance 128 GB at 35.99 EUR (RRP 39.99 EUR), Alternate.de at 41.59 EUR; Kingston High Endurance 128 GB is at 84.90 EUR at Alternate, ADATA High Endurance 128 GB at 87.34 EUR at MediaMarkt. The Lexar card was not found with a reliable price at German retailers; EU retailers quoted roughly 40 EUR to 80 EUR (partly not available), and prices fluctuate strongly. Counterfeit and complaint risk is high, so carry only goods with traceable origin and test batches. Offer as a cross-sell with the dash cam (25-dashcam-4k) and the site camera (25-baustellenkamera-4g-solar).

Request from the supplier

  • Proof of origin: buy only directly from Longsys/Lexar or via authorised distributors; compare the manufacturer part number LMSHGED128G-BCNNG and the batch code on the packaging with the manufacturer
  • Test true capacity and write speed per batch (capacity test such as f3/h2testw, continuous write test); claim deviations before resale
  • Fix endurance in writing: write cycles or recording hours for 128 and 256 GB, temperature range in the vehicle and whether continuous operation is covered by the warranty
  • Clarify the speed class of the delivered variant (U1/V10 versus U3/V30); a 4K dash cam requires V30
  • Request terms: price on request, minimum quantity, lead time, possible private-label production and direct purchase versus EU distributor
  • RoHS/REACH declarations, GPSR information (EU responsible person), LUCID registration and a clear RMA process; at least 24 months warranty
