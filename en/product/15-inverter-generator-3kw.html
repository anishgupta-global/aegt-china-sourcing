Description

The silent 3 kW petrol inverter generator supplies small sites, container offices and hand tools with clean 230 V sine-wave power where no site distribution board is available.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a compact, robust housing, low noise, low-oil shutdown, Schuko sockets with RCD protection and a tested voltage quality for sensitive equipment. CE under the Machinery Directive, the Outdoor Noise Directive 2000/14/EC with LwA marking, EMC and an engine to EU Stage V are mandatory; the GS mark is a strong quality argument in commercial use. For the price listing, it must be clarified which model the 3 kW price applies to.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC, 2000/14/EC (LwA value, notified body) and 2014/30/EU for exactly this model
  • Stage V evidence for the engine with approval number
  • GS certificate from a recognised test body (TÜV Rheinland, TÜV Süd or similar) if the GS mark is advertised
  • Written clarification of which model the listing price applies to (rated and peak power, engine displacement)
  • Test report on voltage quality (THD, frequency) under load, plus German operating manual
  • Spare parts supply (carburettor, spark plugs, inverter module) and warranty of at least 12 months
