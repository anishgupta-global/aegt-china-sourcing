Description

The adjustable dumbbell replaces 15 pairs of dumbbells: a dial selects steps from 2.5 to 24 kg, the chosen steel plates lock into the handle and the remaining ones stay in the storage tray.

More

The listed model BF010P consists of steel plates, an ABS cover and an ABS tray; one dumbbell with tray measures 44.5 x 21 x 22.5 cm. Buyers look at locking safety, play in the handle, rust protection, tray stability and PAH-free grips. The listing price applies per dumbbell, one pair consists of two pieces.

Request from the supplier

  • Load test report to EN ISO 20957-1 (by analogy) and a failure test of the locking mechanism (dial, plates, handle) from a test lab.
  • Clarify factory status: Made-in-China lists Benjin as a trading company; confirm factory audit and plant address before ordering.
  • PAH test for grip, tray and plastic parts; material specification of the tray (ABS, wall thickness).
  • Corrosion protection (salt-spray test of the steel plates), weight accuracy per step (tolerance in %) and play in the handle.
  • Transport packaging with drop test (carton 57 x 36 x 31.5 cm per dumbbell, heavy parcel), calculate shipping costs per pair.
  • Clarify product liability insurance and GPSR details (EU responsible person, lot number) as well as weight marking in kg.
