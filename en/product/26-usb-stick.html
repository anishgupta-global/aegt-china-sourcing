Description

The USB flash drive carries plans, expert reports and measurement data between site, office and client without depending on cloud access.

More

Professional users in Germany expect the capacity actually printed on the product, stable write speeds and a housing that survives a key ring and a tool bag. Data security also matters for AE Global Traders: drives holding client data should be encrypted, and counterfeit memory chips that report more capacity than they have are a known risk. As electrical equipment, the drive is subject to CE, RoHS, REACH and the importer's ElektroG obligations.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Capacity test (e.g. H2testw or F3) on at least 5 samples from the series to detect counterfeit chips
  • Have the controller and NAND chip type named in writing; exclude relabelled goods
  • EU declaration of conformity and EMC test report (EN 55032/EN 55035) from an accredited lab
  • RoHS and REACH test report for housing, paint and engraving
  • Approval sample with logo: check durability of engraving and print, cap and ring connection and labelling before series start
  • Clarify ElektroG registration and put the WEEE symbol and importer address on the packaging
