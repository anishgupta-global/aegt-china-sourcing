Description

The trowel set bundles the four tools needed one after another when bricklaying, plastering and pointing: brick trowel with S-neck (180 mm), Berne plastering trowel (140 mm) …

More

Hamburg pointing trowel (10 mm) and finishing trowel with cranked handle (280 mm). The blades are made of hardened steel and sized for the task – from 0.7 mm for the finishing trowel to 1.9 mm for the pointing trowel. Professional users expect evenly hardened, corrosion-protected blades, firmly riveted handles and burr-free edges. Imported goods need GPSR-compliant labelling with an EU responsible person, REACH-compliant handles and coatings and registered sales packaging.

E-commerce potential

A set instead of a single trowel raises the basket, and set suppliers are rare.

More

Evidence: Strauss lists the e.s. trowel set 4 pcs (item 5001360, made in Germany) at EUR 21.30 incl. VAT (EUR 20.11 from 5 sets, EUR 18.92 from 10). Hornbach sells only single trowels (EUR 4.25–15.75, e.g. Maurerlob S-neck 18 cm EUR 7.95, Berne plastering trowel 16 cm EUR 9.95) and, as its only set, a Lugato notched trowel set 7 pcs for EUR 54.95 (Hornbach searches, 31 Oct 2026). Per the scan stage, Otto lists a 3-piece TrendLine set and Bauhaus the Wisent pointing trowel as a single item. Competition in 4-piece sets is therefore low; the 'made in Germany' premium reference does set a high quality expectation (blade shape, hardness, handle). Return risk low to medium (assessment), shipping small and light (about 0.9 kg per the single weights of the reference).

Request from the supplier

  • Approval samples in the German pattern: brick trowel S-neck 180 mm, Berne plastering trowel 140 mm, Hamburg pointing trowel 10 mm, finishing trowel 280 mm with cranked handle, with drawing and blade thicknesses (1.2/1.0/1.9/0.7 mm)
  • EN 10204 type 3.1 material certificate and HRC hardness record; statement whether the blade is stamped or forged, and bending test of the neck
  • Handle fixing (riveting) with pull-out test, wood species and lacquer; check timber origin (FSC/PEFC) and the EU Deforestation Regulation
  • Salt spray test report EN ISO 9227 and rust protection for sea freight (oiled paper/VCI)
  • GPSR details (EU responsible person with address, batch, German instructions), REACH/PAH report for lacquer and handle, set packaging with LUCID registration
  • Factory proof and set assembly: MOQ per set and per single trowel, lead time, audit
