Description

The negative air machine draws the room air of the work area through a multi-stage HEPA filter cascade and keeps the area under negative pressure during remediation so that no fibres reach the surroundings.

More

Quality units have a robust housing, an H13/H14 main filter to DIN EN 1822, filter monitoring and an hour meter, and a low-contamination filter change. Professional users in Germany expect 230 V/50 Hz, CE conformity, a tested air flow of at least 1,000 m³/h and a traceable individual test of the main filter. Only then can the unit be used in the work planning under TRGS 519 and towards the authority.

Request from the supplier

  • Test report of the H13/H14 main filter to DIN EN 1822 with serial number (individual test, not only type test)
  • EU declaration of conformity (CE) with technical documentation for Machinery, Low Voltage and EMC Directives
  • 230 V / 50 Hz version with EU plug and electrical test report
  • Measurement report on air flow (m³/h with loaded filter) and noise level
  • German manual, spare filters (pre, fine, main filter) and spare-part supply for at least 5 years
  • Warranty of at least 12 months; low-contamination filter change, differential pressure and hour-meter display
