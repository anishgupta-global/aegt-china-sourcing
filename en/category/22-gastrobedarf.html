Catering Supplies

For the canteen, site catering, topping-out events and promotional items, AE Global Traders buys napkins, cups, take-away packaging and disposable tableware that are made in China mainly in Fujian …

More

Shandong, Guangdong and Hubei. What matters is not a cent of difference in unit price but food-contact compliance, the EU single-use plastics rules and the obligations under the German Packaging Act.

According to directory hits, napkins and paper cups cluster in Fujian (Quanzhou, Jinjiang), Guangdong (Jiangmen) and Shandong (Qingdao, Weifang); cups additionally in Hubei, Anhui and Zhejiang. For bagasse tableware, take-away boxes and wooden cutlery there are no documented cluster hits in this version.

China prices: napkins at 0.01 USD FOB (MOQ 20,000) come from a directly fetched PaperIndex page (Qingdao Robana); the upper bound of 0.03 USD and the cup price (0.02 to 0.04 USD, Global Sources tier) come only from search-result summaries and are not confirmed on the original page; no prices directly from Alibaba, Made-in-China or 1688. Mind the volume tiers: 0.02 USD per cup applies only from 1 million pieces. All prices are FOB list prices excluding printing plates, packaging, freight and duty. No EU duty rate documented (check TARIC), so duty_rate_pct is empty; image URLs only as a symbolic photo for napkins (Wikimedia Commons). Phone, email, contact person and EU office do not come from any company website and stay empty. The B-tier suppliers come from directory snippets (made-in-china, PaperIndex, Global Sources); manufacturer or trader status, website and certificates (FSC, BRC) are unchecked; for Hengan a napkin range is not documented. The websites of Qingdao Robana Paper, Quanzhou Yaosheng Paper and Wuhan Measy were not found in two search rounds each (directory profiles only), so website stays empty. The German cup price (0.063 EUR) is an unprinted marketplace price (other sellers range roughly 15 to 45 EUR per 1,000 pieces according to search hits); the German napkin price (0.029 EUR each) is only an unprinted white reference price (Papstar pack of 250, 7.29 EUR gross plus 6.99 EUR shipping, per billiger.de; other sellers up to about 0.046 EUR each). No German price for logo-printed napkins was found (print shops quote on request), so the comparison with the Chinese print price is not like-for-like and a print quote must be requested. Check before import: FSC licence in the FSC database, food-contact test reports under Regulation (EC) 1935/2004 incl. printing inks, the Single-Use Plastics Directive (EU) 2019/904 and German obligations (marking of PE-coated cups, VerpackG/LUCID, single-use plastics fund). Volume relevance for a construction group is low; mainly canteen and promotional items. NEW in v2: the four new products (double-wall coffee cup 12 oz, bagasse plate, bagasse take-away box, wooden cutlery) are set up only with description, specification, standards and checklist. China price, German price, price sources and top makers were NOT researched because this session's web-search quota was exhausted; the fields are deliberately left empty. No new manufacturers were added because without search no verifiable companies from the same clusters could be found (repeat the web search in a follow-up session). The 8-digit CN codes for cups (4823 69 90, wording Cups and the like), moulded articles of paper pulp (4823 70 90, bagasse plates and boxes) and wooden cutlery (4419 90 00) were seen on tariffnumber.com (CN 2025); 2026 may differ. 4818 30 00 for napkins comes from standard knowledge and was not fetched. The plastic lids of the coffee cups have their own tariff line (plastic articles, not checked). For wooden cutlery check FLEGT/EUDR evidence per TARIC. As far as known, the PPWR (EU) 2025/40 applies from 12 Aug 2026 and contains PFAS limits for food packaging: check the wording yourself before ordering. The round 23 cm bagasse plate is not in the brochure's bagasse table; the square 9-inch plate MVP-022 is evidenced. The German placemat price is an unprinted eBay reference; no German price was found for paper carrier bags.
