Description

On site the circular saw cuts construction timber, floorboards, OSB and chipboard as well as facade and drywall boards quickly and straight.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a sturdy aluminium or magnesium base plate, a free-moving retractable blade guard, a riving knife, continuously adjustable depth and bevel setting up to at least 45° and a dust extraction port. The machine must be CE-compliant and tested to EN 62841-2-5; saw blades must meet EN 847-1, and extraction is intended when sawing wood under TRGS 553. German operating manual, GS mark and available spare parts and blades are mandatory for professional use.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 62841-1/-2-5 from an accredited body with model and serial number; proof of retractable guard and riving knife
  • EU declaration of conformity (2006/42/EC, 2014/30/EU, 2011/65/EU) with EU authorised representative; GS certificate or written commitment (listing states only FCC, CE, RoHS)
  • Saw blades to EN 847-1 with test evidence; fix blade diameter 185 or 190 mm and bore 20/30 mm in the order
  • Extraction port compatible with common site vacuums; check bevel and depth adjustment on a sample
  • German operating manual with vibration/noise values, spare-parts list (carbon brushes, guard, switch) and at least 24 months warranty
