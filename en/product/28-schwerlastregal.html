Description

The heavy-duty boltless shelving organises material, spare parts and archive boxes in storage rooms, workshops and site cabins and can be assembled and reconfigured without tools.

More

What matters is a load-bearing steel frame with galvanising or powder coating, strong shelves and a clearly stated shelf load. In Germany, load marking, tip-over protection and regular rack inspection by a competent person under DGUV Information 208-016 are part of operation. Shelves of thin MDF and posts that are too weak are typical weak points of imported goods and should be specified explicitly before ordering.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Load test report from an independent lab: shelf load evenly distributed (kg) and total capacity per bay
  • Load plate on the rack and German assembly instructions with notes on tip-over protection and wall fixing (EN 15635, DGUV Information 208-016)
  • Material certificate for the steel (wall thickness of posts and beams) and proof of galvanising or powder coating; salt-spray test for damp site cabins
  • Shelf board material: for MDF or chipboard, prove formaldehyde class E1 (EN 717-1)
  • Inspection record template for the annual rack inspection by a competent person
  • Warranty and spare parts (shelves, posts, connectors) and a pre-shipment sample of the plug connection (play, fit)
