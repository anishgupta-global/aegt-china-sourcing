Description

The lockable four-drawer steel filing cabinet organises site files, plans and contract documents in offices and site cabins and protects them from unauthorised access.

More

It calls for a robust steel construction of adequate sheet thickness, a powder-coated surface, smooth drawer runners and an anti-tilt device that releases only one drawer at a time. Professional users in Germany expect documented testing to EN 16121/16122, a high-quality cylinder lock with key numbers and clear marking of the load per drawer. The current offer with only 0.5 mm sheet should be raised contractually to a greater material thickness.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 16121 / EN 16122 (stability, drawer loading, drawer stops) for the model actually supplied
  • Material certificate for the steel sheet: fix a thickness of at least 0.7 mm in the contract (the offer states 0.5 mm)
  • Coating adhesion (EN ISO 2409) and salt-spray test record for the powder coating
  • Lock concept: cylinder lock with central locking, at least 2 keys per cabinet, spare-key service; anti-tilt device (only one drawer open at a time)
  • German assembly instructions, load per drawer marked on the cabinet; GS mark optional
  • Packaging with edge and corner protection, pallet dimensions and weight (bulky freight) as well as warranty and spare parts (locks, drawer runners)
