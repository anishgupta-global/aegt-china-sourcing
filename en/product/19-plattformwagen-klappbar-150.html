Description

The folding platform trolley moves cartons, tool cases, Euro containers and event equipment of up to 150 kg across level hall and warehouse floors.

More

The steel platform with anti-slip texture measures about 72 x 47 cm, two fixed and two swivel wheels keep the trolley manoeuvrable in narrow aisles, and the padded handle folds down flat so that the trolley needs only about 11 cm of pack height. A professional unit has a load rating marked on the trolley, a securely latching folding handle without pinch points and floor-friendly wheels. Commercial buyers in Germany expect test proof (EN 1757 or GS), REACH-compliant wheels and grips and GPSR labelling with an EU responsible person.

Risks: overload above 150 kg, complaints about the folding hinge and handle latch, abrasion of hard wheels; a test proof is the selling advantage over no-name marketplace goods.

Request from the supplier

  • Load test report for 150 kg (static and dynamic) including folding hinge and handle latch; clarify the conflicting load statements on the listing page (150, 200 and 300 kg) in writing and have the rated load marked on the trolley.
  • Wheels: 4-in PVC is hard and can mark floors and be noisy; request a sample with rubber or TPR tread and demand a REACH test report (incl. phthalates) for the wheel material.
  • Platform and coating: powder coating, salt-spray test to ISO 9227, burr-free edges, anti-slip texture.
  • Handle and folding mechanism: latch without pinch points, endurance test of folding cycles on the sample.
  • Packaging: flat single carton (740 x 490 x 110 mm), assembly for 2-packs/3-packs, GPSR labelling with EU responsible person, LUCID registration.
  • Production proof: approve a production sample and view the BV audit report (MIC-ASR2651136); factory audit or inspection before shipment.
