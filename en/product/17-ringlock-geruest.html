Description

Ringlock scaffolding consists of standards with rosettes at 0.5 m spacing to which ledgers and diagonals are connected with wedge connectors.

More

It suits facade scaffolds, working platforms in halls and support scaffolds in refurbishment and demolition, because scaffold geometries can be built flexibly with few component types. Professional users in Germany expect hot-dip galvanised steel components, test reports to EN 12810 and EN 12811, a general building-authority approval (abZ) for the system and assembly and use instructions in German. Without this evidence a Chinese system can hardly be used as a standard design under German law, so approval and compatibility must be clarified before purchase.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • General building-authority approval (abZ) for exactly this Ringlock system or written confirmation of which standard design is permitted
  • Test reports to EN 12810 and EN 12811 from an accredited body, including tensile tests on the rosette connection
  • Mill certificates to EN 10204 type 3.1 for tubes and rosettes (steel grade S355 or Q345)
  • Galvanising evidence to EN ISO 1461 (coating thickness) and third-party surveillance of production
  • German assembly and use instructions and compatibility of the components with existing scaffold material
  • Sample container and incoming inspection (dimensional accuracy, welds, galvanising) before series delivery
