Description

The set contains 100 coloured magnetic tiles for free 2D and 3D building of towers, houses and vehicles.

More

The magnets are sealed in the plastic housing; because loose magnets are a swallowing hazard, professional buyers demand an EN 71-1 test report including magnetic flux index, welded housing seams and German warnings. 100 pieces are the retail entry size for larger structures at a gift price point. Quality markers are clear, scratch-resistant faces, even holding force, clean edges and a sturdy colour box with a shape overview.

Request from the supplier

  • Request EN 71-1/-2/-3 test reports from an accredited lab for exactly model KSL779206, including magnetic flux index and loose-magnet test.
  • Have magnet type, housing seam (ultrasonic welding) and drop/torque tests documented; check samples yourself for magnet release after a drop test.
  • Specify German warnings, CE marking, type/batch number plus name and address of manufacturer and EU responsible person (GPSR) on product and carton.
  • Plan packaging under VerpackG/LUCID (registration, material data), agree EAN/GTIN and storage box.
  • Commission a pre-shipment inspection per AQL with a holding-force sample and tile colour matching; clarify business type and factory site by audit.
