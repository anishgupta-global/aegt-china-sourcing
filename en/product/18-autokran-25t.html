Description

The STC250E5 truck crane combines a three-axle 6x4 chassis (80 km/h) with a five-section U-profile telescopic boom and 25 t maximum capacity.

More

With 213 kW engine power and about 31.9 t gross weight it is designed for erection, building and plant construction. For use in Germany, road registration, emission stage, EN 13000 conformity and the ongoing EU anti-dumping case on new mobile cranes from China must be clarified before ordering.

More

Main risks: the ongoing EU anti-dumping case AD749 (registration of Chinese new mobile cranes until 27 Nov 2026) and German road registration. New prices of German manufacturers are not public (not evidenced here); demand data were not checked.

Request from the supplier

  • Check trade defence first: AD749 / Implementing Regulation (EU) 2026/441; secure possible retroactive anti-dumping duties by contract (price adjustment/withdrawal clause).
  • Road registration: chassis with Euro VI and EU type approval or individual approval (Sec. 21 StVZO); get axle loads, dimensions and emission stage confirmed in writing.
  • CE declaration of conformity, EN 13000 evidence, load chart/load moment limiter, German operating manual.
  • Initial inspection by a competent person before use (DGUV Regulation 52 / BetrSichV); clarify spare-parts and service network in Germany.
  • Transport (3 axles, approx. 31.9 t): RoRo/break-bulk, dimensions, Incoterm; inspect before shipment.
