Description

The builder's bucket with 12 litres capacity is used on site to carry and mix mortar, plaster, adhesive, water and bulk material.

More

It is made of impact-resistant, UV-stabilised polyethylene with a litre scale and has a galvanised steel handle with plastic grip that carries safely even when full. Professional users in Germany expect a handle that does not pop out of its seat, a stable base, cold and impact resistance and low-pollutant material, partly with recycled content and GS/TÜV testing. Imported goods must comply with the EU General Product Safety Regulation 2023/988 and REACH (in particular cadmium and lead in colourants).
