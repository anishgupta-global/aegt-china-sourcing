Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Quote-based business: in Germany truck mixers are priced on request only by manufacturers and dealers (e.g. Liebherr HTM, Schwing Stetter).

More

Demand comes from ready-mix plants and contractors; hurdles are Euro VI, left-hand drive, individual approval and service coverage. Sell only against a deposit and with registration clarified.

Request from the supplier

  • Euro VI proof and EU type/individual approval (CoC) for the chassis; confirm the left-hand-drive version in writing
  • CE declaration of conformity for the mixer body and German operating manual
  • Get spare parts and service in Germany/EU confirmed (drum drive, hydraulic pump, blades, Hino engine)
  • Check dimensions, axle loads and permissible gross weight against StVZO; clarify RoRo transport or driving on its own wheels
