Description

On site, the Matrice 4E is used for fast aerial surveys of demolition and refurbishment areas, for documenting roof and facade conditions and for estimating stockpile volumes.

More

As a compact enterprise platform with RTK positioning it delivers georeferenced orthophotos and point clouds without anyone having to enter scaffolding or roofs, a real safety gain where asbestos is suspected and there is a fall risk. Professional users in Germany expect the EU variant with class marking, full CE/RED conformity, German-language documentation and spare batteries with UN 38.3 proof. Operation requires operator registration with the Federal Aviation Office (LBA), a remote pilot certificate and liability insurance.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity (CE, RED 2014/53/EU) and class marking under Reg. (EU) 2019/945 on the device and packaging.
  • Proof of genuine EU-market goods (serial number, authorised enterprise dealer) instead of grey-market imports.
  • UN 38.3 test report for the batteries and dangerous-goods transport documents.
  • German-language manual and a description of the data flow (local data mode without mandatory cloud) for GDPR documentation.
  • Warranty and a service point in the EU, availability of spare parts (propellers, batteries, gimbal).
  • Current check of the sanctions and subsidy situation (US lists) before ordering.
