Description

The HZS60 with 60 m³/h theoretical output suits small to medium ready-mix and precast plants.

More

According to the listing it is available with cylinder or electric push-rod discharge and can be equipped with a hot-water or steam boiler for cold weather. Commercial buyers expect a CE-compliant control system, a weighing system with provable batching accuracy, German-language plant documentation and a clarified permit under emission-control law.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Quote-based business for concrete plants and contractors: Liebherr (Mobilmix, Betomix) and Schwing Stetter offer batching plants in Germany on request only.

More

The Chinese reseller offer shows a large price gap; risks are scope of supply (silos, conveyors, installation), permitting and service. No direct sale without an OEM quote and a clear scope of supply.

Request from the supplier

  • Obtain an OEM quote from Zoomlion and have the authenticity of the reseller offer (Evangel Industrial, Shanghai) confirmed
  • Define the scope of supply in writing: mixer, scales, silos, conveyors, controls, installation and commissioning
  • CE declaration of conformity, electrical documentation (EN 60204-1) and German manual; German-language control interface
  • Clarify the BImSchG permit as well as noise and dust control at the site before ordering
  • Get spare parts (mixer arms, wear liners, load cells) and service technicians in DE/EU confirmed
