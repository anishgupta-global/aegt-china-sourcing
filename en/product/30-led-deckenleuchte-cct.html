Description

Plain round surface-mounted luminaire (ABS housing, only about 22 mm high according to the manufacturer) for living rooms, bedrooms, children's rooms and hallways.

More

Brightness and colour temperature are controlled with a 2.4 GHz radio remote; the manufacturer states three colour temperatures within 3000–6500 K and a colour rendering index above 90. Quality markers are a low-flicker driver (limits under (EU) 2019/2020: Pst LM ≤ 1.0, SVM ≤ 0.4), an even light distribution without visible LED dots and a clean, play-free mounting. Selling in the EU requires CE (LVD, EMC and, because of the radio remote, RED), RoHS, an EU responsible person under GPSR and the EPREL data of the integrated light source.

More

remote control is very common there and several models offer 3000–6500 K (e.g. Nettlife, Hanseatic); a flat round model (JDONG) has 114 reviews. At Hornbach (checked 2 Nov 2026) CCT ceiling lights with remote cost EUR 29.95 (18 W, Ø 29.3 cm), EUR 39.95 (22 W, Ø 42 cm) and EUR 69.95 (60 W, Ø 76 cm). A steady all-year seller with an autumn peak; competition is very high (DIY-store own brands, Amazon white label); return risk comes from wrong size/wattage, remote pairing and flicker complaints.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the exact variant (watts, diameter, lumens, 3000/4000/6000 K or 2700–6500 K): the list price does not state the wattage step.
  • EPREL/ecodesign data of the integrated light source (lumens, efficacy, CRI, flicker values Pst LM and SVM) and whether driver and LED can be replaced.
  • LVD (EN 60598), EMC (EN 55015/61547) and RED (EN 300 328) test reports for the luminaire incl. remote from an accredited lab; have the UL/ETL/SAA claims proven.
  • Remote control: pairing with several luminaires, battery type and BattG marking, memory of the last setting.
  • Check samples: light distribution, flicker (slow-motion video test), mounting kit, German manual; sample lead time per maker 3–5 working days.
  • Packaging and registration: EU responsible person on the pack (GPSR), VerpackG/LUCID, ElektroG/stiftung ear, drop test of the packaging.
