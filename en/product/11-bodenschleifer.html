Description

The planetary floor grinder prepares screed, concrete and old floor coverings for recoating, levelling or flooring installation and removes adhesive, filler and paint residues in a single pass.

More

Three planetary heads on a 550 mm working width give an even grinding pattern and high area output, and a frequency inverter allows speed adjustment to tooling and substrate. Professional users in Germany expect a torsion-resistant frame, permanently lubricated bearings, a tight dust port for class H industrial vacuums and spare parts available for years. Compliance means CE marking under the Machinery Directive, a 400 V three-phase design and a German manual; on contaminated coverings (asbestos, TRGS 519) it may only be used as a tested complete system with extraction.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC and 2014/30/EU with manufacturer address and serial number, and CE marking on the rating plate
  • Risk assessment to EN ISO 12100 and test report on the electrics to EN 60204-1, designed for 3~ 400 V / 50 Hz (not only 380 V / 60 Hz)
  • German manual with safety, maintenance and dust-protection instructions and a spare-parts list with exploded views
  • Proof that the dust port fits class H industrial vacuums; suitability for asbestos environments (TRGS 519) only as a tested complete system
  • Wear-parts package (heads, belts, bearings), at least 24 months warranty and a service partner in the EU
  • Factory acceptance with a test run (video, serial number) and a sample machine before the series order
