Description

The multi-processor demolition shear combines several specialist tools in one attachment: instead of keeping separate shears, concrete and crushing tools …

More

the jaws are swapped and the excavator stays on the job. The researched model Ligong L08 Multi-Kit Processor is offered by the manufacturer for excavators of 18 to 30 t, with six interchangeable jaws, hydraulic jaw change, 360° rotation and replaceable blades of heat-treated alloy steel. Branded references on the market are the NPK M-28K (excavators 28–40 t, 3,130 kg, 638 mm opening, force up to 459 t) and the Genesis GVP 15 (excavators of about 9–12 t, 1,450 kg, 406 mm opening), both available from Schlueter Baumaschinen on request only. Professional users look for wear-resistant jaw material, a sturdy slewing unit, an oil flow matching the excavator including a case-drain line, connection via quick coupler, and a CE declaration of conformity with EU spare-parts supply.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Professional B2B niche, not a mass market: Schlueter Baumaschinen lists NPK M-20K, M-28K and M-38K and Genesis GVP 07/15 as multi-demolition shears without prices (on request).

More

Profi-Bau-Shop carries the Darda Multi Cutter MC200 B and MC300 B; the MC300 B (carrier 2.5–9 t, 310 kg, 60 t cutting force) costs EUR 24,779 gross (EUR 20,822.69 net). For the 18–40 t class the research found no public EU price, so a like-for-like comparison is not possible. Buyers are demolition and recycling firms, rental companies and dealers with excavator fleets; cross-sell via jaw sets and replacement blades (09-zangen-backen-kit), hose kit and quick coupler. Return risk is high with wrong oil flow or mounting; a unit weight of 2–4 t requires freight forwarding. No bestseller rank data found.

Request from the supplier

  • Request CE marking, EC declaration of conformity and German operating instructions before ordering (Machinery Directive, Machinery Regulation from 20 Jan 2027)
  • Demand a data sheet with weight, opening width, cutting and crushing force, oil flow and pressure: the listing gives no performance data, and the code L08 is also used by the same storefront for a double-cylinder shear
  • Fix the scope of the six jaw sets in writing (type, material, hardness, EN 10204 3.1 material certificate) and have the change time proven by a test-run video
  • Have the fit to the excavator confirmed: carrier weight and tipping stability with the attachment weight, oil flow and pressure of the auxiliary hydraulics, case-drain line, mounting (pin pattern or quick coupler)
  • Ask for the make of rotator, hydraulic motor and cylinder seals; order replacement blades and one jaw set as spare parts
  • Contractually fix pre-shipment inspection, 18-month warranty and EU spare-parts supply
