Description

The roll-off container takes rubble, mixed waste, demolition timber and scrap on demolition and refurbishment sites and is picked up, hauled and emptied by a hook-lift truck.

More

A professional container is made of welded steel with a reinforced floor, cross members, hook eye at the standard height, roll-off rollers and a tight wing door or tailgate. Users in Germany expect dimensions to DIN 30722 so that any hook-lift truck can lift the container, documented weld quality, corrosion protection, lashing points for load securing and a version suitable for road travel. The container is generally not a CE-marked product, but waste transport and disposal are subject to the KrWG, NachwV and the disposal contractor's requirements.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Dimension drawing to DIN 30722 (hook height, roller spacing) and a trial lift with a hook-lift truck before series delivery
  • EN 10204 3.1 material certificate for the sheet (wall approx. 3 mm, floor approx. 5 mm)
  • Welding procedure and welder qualification (ISO 3834-2, ISO 9606-1)
  • Measurement record of paint thickness and system to ISO 12944, galvanising proof if required
  • Load and leak test of door or tailgate and the locking (e.g. 1.25 times rated load)
  • Lashing points, weight marking and serial number on the container; packing and loading plan for sea freight
