Description

The 15 kg demolition hammer is the workhorse for concrete breaking, screed and tile removal and wall openings in strip-out work.

More

A sturdy housing, a continuous-duty 1,500 W motor and a hammer mechanism with around 50 J single-blow energy are built for shift operation. Professional users in Germany expect effective vibration damping, a vibration value declared under EN 62841-2-6 and readily available spare parts for carbon brushes, hammer mechanism and handles. Compliance means CE under the Machinery Directive, the sound power level under 2000/14/EC on the rating plate and a German operating manual.

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC with a test report to EN 62841-2-6 from an accredited lab (TÜV, SGS, Intertek)
  • Proof under 2000/14/EC: conformity assessment by a notified body (state its number) and sound power level LWA on the rating plate
  • Vibration value to EN ISO 28927-10 (m/s², uncertainty K) in data sheet and manual
  • German operating manual, safety instructions and spare parts list (carbon brushes, hammer mechanism, handles) before ordering
  • Sampling with an endurance run, written warranty of at least 24 months, Incoterm and HS code in the quotation
