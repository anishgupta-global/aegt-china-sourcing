Description

The double-wall cup carries hot coffee safely from the canteen machine or site cabin to the workplace without the hand feeling the heat and without a cup sleeve.

More

A professional cup has a clean, tight seam, a dimensionally stable rim that holds the sip lid securely, and a print that does not run under heat and moisture. Because cup and lid fall under the single-use plastics rules in Germany, the trade expects marking, registration with the single-use plastics fund and separate declarations of conformity for the lid. Food contact under Regulation (EC) 1935/2004 and a PFAS-free coating are standard.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Separate declarations of conformity and migration test reports for the cup (board and coating) and for the lid (Regulation (EC) 1935/2004, Regulation (EU) No 10/2011).
  • PFAS test report (targeted PFAS analysis and total fluorine) for the coating as evidence for the PPWR limits.
  • Fit and leak-tightness test of cup and lid with 90 °C water, documented with photos or a lab report.
  • Marking per Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/2151 on cup and lid as a sample; plastic content in grams per cup and per lid for registration.
  • Outer surface temperature test (handling with 90 °C filling) and check that the print does not run under heat.
  • Have the FSC licence number, manufacturer status (factory or trader) and hygiene certificate (BRCGS Packaging or ISO 22000) confirmed.
