Description

Steel-frame formwork absorbs the fresh concrete pressure in walls, columns, foundations and extensions and, when used correctly, produces dimensionally accurate, smooth concrete surfaces.

More

Professional users in Germany expect welded, low-distortion steel frames (galvanised or powder-coated) with a plywood or plastic facing, positive frame locks, tie points for formwork ties to DIN 18216 and a documented permissible fresh concrete pressure to DIN 18218. A German assembly and usage manual, a structural check of the permissible fresh concrete load to DIN 18218 (plus EN 12812 for slab props) must also be supplied. Accessories such as locks, ties and alignment props must remain reorderable over the long term.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Material certificate 3.1 (EN 10204) for the frame steel and proof of weld quality
  • Permissible load table with structural check or load test for the fresh concrete pressure to DIN 18218
  • Proof of galvanising or coating (thickness, EN ISO 1461) and corrosion protection of the frame locks
  • German assembly and usage manual, accessory list (locks, ties, alignment props) with spare parts commitment
  • Sample panels with dimensional check (tolerance, squareness) before series production and a sample of the facing
  • Plywood origin and formaldehyde class (E1) as well as timber origin evidence for the facing
