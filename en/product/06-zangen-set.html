Description

The pliers set bundles the three most used pliers on site: the combination pliers for gripping, bending and cutting, the side cutter for wire, cable and nails …

More

and the water-pump pliers with adjustable joint for pipes, nuts and fittings. Professional users expect forged chrome-vanadium or carbon steel, induction-hardened cutting edges, joints that close cleanly without play, two-component handles and a polished or matt chrome finish. Combination pliers are covered by dimensions and test values to DIN ISO 5746; imported goods also need GPSR-compliant labelling with an EU responsible person and REACH-compliant handles and surfaces.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the set composition: combination pliers 180 mm, side cutter 160 mm, water-pump pliers 200–250 mm with samples of all three pieces – Sinotools lists only 5-piece (PE0242) and 6-piece sets (PE0240), so a 3-piece OEM set must be requested
  • Steel and hardness: forged, cutting edges induction-hardened, hardness record per batch (ISO 6508-1) and cutting test on hard wire; testing to ISO 5743/ISO 5744
  • Joint and adjustment: freedom from play, smooth action, adjustment steps and teeth of the water-pump pliers, endurance test of the joints
  • Surface and handles: salt spray test (ISO 9227), REACH report for coating (Ni, Cr(VI), Pb, Cd) and handles (PAHs, phthalates)
  • Marking and packaging: length, brand, origin on every plier; GPSR details (EU responsible person, batch), German notices, double blister, VerpackG/LUCID
  • Factory proof: clarify the manufacturer status of Hebei Sinotools (Shijiazhuang); obtain comparison quotes from Shall (Hangzhou, 4-piece set model 3202108 US$ 3.25, MOQ 100) and GreatStar (WORKPRO 5-PC Pliers Set)
