Description

The vest makes workers visible from a distance on demolition and renovation sites, at container yards and in site traffic.

More

Quality vests use easy-care polyester with a fluorescent base material and sewn-in, wash-resistant reflective stripes that keep the required visible area after many washes. A professional user in Germany expects clean workmanship (bound edges, hook-and-loop or zip closure), clear class and size marking and the choice between class 2 and 3. As category II PPE it needs CE marking under Regulation (EU) 2016/425 with an EU type-examination.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU type-examination certificate (Module B) from a notified body for EN ISO 20471, with the body's number and a current date
  • EU declaration of conformity under Regulation (EU) 2016/425 stating model, class (1/2/3) and applied standard
  • Test report on colour and luminance factor of the base material and the retroreflection of the tapes, also after washing
  • Marking on the product (pictogram, class, size, care label) and instructions in German
  • Approval sample from series production before shipment, plus retained sample and random inspection (AQL)
  • Verify any stated OEKO-TEX or BSCI certificates in the issuers' registers
