Description

Stress squeeze cubes are soft, translucent bodies with a viscous gel or dough fill that slowly return to shape after squeezing, the tactile workhorse of 2026 sensory products.

More

The category original is protected by the NeeDoh (Schylling) trademark, and design rights must be checked; the opportunity lies in own shapes (cube, gumdrop, fuzzball), own colour worlds such as marble or glow variants and an own brand name. Quality markers are a tear-resistant TPR shell, a demonstrably non-toxic, non-swelling and non-leaking fill, low odour and even recovery. Buyers ask for EN 71 reports on the finished product including burst and tear tests, phthalate and PAH evidence, and GPSR labelling. Flour or dough fills must also be checked for germs and mould.

Request from the supplier

  • EN 71-1 (with burst/tear tests, fill leakage, small parts after tearing), EN 71-3 and EN 71-12 from an accredited lab on the finished product, with batch reference.
  • Declare the fill in writing (gel, flour or maltose dough) with an ingredient list; no water beads or swelling gel balls; have flour/maltose fill tested microbiologically.
  • Own shape and own tooling: mould rights with the buyer; supplier confirms in writing not to use the NeeDoh/Schylling brand or its packaging or design.
  • Phthalate and PAH report (REACH Annex XVII entries 51/52/50) and odour test; agree a durability squeeze test (e.g. 5,000 cycles) as quality evidence.
  • Age grading only after passing EN 71-1 (otherwise position as 14+ under the GPSR); pack with EU responsible person, batch and German warnings.
  • Align the contract with the new EU Toy Safety Regulation (digital product passport, PFAS and bisphenol restrictions).
