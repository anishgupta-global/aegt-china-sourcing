Description

Mochi are soft, elastic glutinous rice cakes made of glutinous rice flour, filled with sesame, peanut or sweet red bean paste (anko) and mostly sold as a gift box of individual pieces.

More

Discerning buyers expect a soft, non-hardened shell, a clean filling without milk and egg components, clear allergen labelling (peanut, sesame, soy) and sufficient remaining shelf life on arrival. Because of the import restrictions on products of animal origin from China, only purely plant-based recipes come into question; variants with egg yolk or cream filling are out. The importer is responsible for LMIV labelling, additive declaration and residue and aflatoxin evidence for peanut and sesame.

E-commerce potential

Price points tightly tiered: at Asiafoodland the 210 g boxes by Yuki&Love (peanut, sesame, anko, taro, green tea) all cost 3.99 EUR (19.00 EUR per kg) …

More

the brands are distributed by importers in the Netherlands (Liroy B.V., Heuschen & Schrouff OFT B.V. per product page), and the shop does not state the country of manufacture. Asian Food Lovers lists mochi among its popular categories, Asiafoodland has 19 mochi items according to the scan research. A private label needs differentiation through varieties, packaging and gift occasion (for example with tea). Risks: short shelf life (listing 6-12 months), hardening, choking risk, peanut, sesame and soy allergens and sensitive sea freight. Without a recipe and sample for the plant-based varieties the item is a to-do for checking, not an order recommendation.

Request from the supplier

  • Plant-based recipe in writing: no milk, egg, gelatine or cream components in shell and filling (exclude the supplier’s “cream filling” listings); “vegan” only with proof of all ingredients and processing aids on the label.
  • Request the target varieties sesame, peanut and anko from the supplier as an OEM recipe (the listing has only fruit variants LB1249-1); sample and recipe per variety before any order.
  • Residue and aflatoxin reports for peanut and sesame per batch (Regulation (EU) 2023/915, Regulation (EC) 396/2005; for peanuts from China check the annex of Regulation (EU) 2019/1793), for sesame also ethylene oxide residues.
  • Agree a minimum remaining shelf life on arrival contractually (listing 6-12 months); test individual packaging, seal quality and sea-freight conditions (heat, hardening).
  • Original certificates (ISO, HACCP, BRC, Halal) and factory audit: the supplier is a confectionery maker (jelly, candy), mochi is a side line; check allergen management (peanut, sesame, soy).
  • Approve the German label under LMIV with allergens and a choking-risk note in advance, register packaging with LUCID (VerpackG), secure the customs classification via binding tariff information (vZTA).
