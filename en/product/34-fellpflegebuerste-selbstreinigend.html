Description

The slicker brush removes loose hair, undercoat and light tangles, and its self-cleaning function frees it from fur quickly without pulling hair off by hand.

More

According to the listing, the pins are stainless steel and the body is plastic (PP); it measures 20 × 10.5 cm and weighs 250 g, and is waterproof and also suitable for wet brushing. Professional buyers look for rounded pin tips, a durable retraction mechanism, a non-slip handle and tested plastics. Logo printing is possible.

Request from the supplier

  • Pin tips: rounded (ball tips) or protective pins? Injury test on the sample, skin tolerance, pin height and spacing per size S/M/L.
  • Self-cleaning mechanism: test operation (push button/retraction), life in cycles and spring material on the sample; agree a defect rate.
  • Stainless-steel grade and corrosion resistance (salt-spray/water test), pin-to-plate joint.
  • REACH test report for plastic and TPR (PAH, phthalates) and a 'no dog/cat fur' declaration for customs (Y-922).
  • Weight/size variants, handle comfort and hang tag with German instructions and safety notice.
  • Sample before series order, random inspection (AQL) before loading.
