Description

The hi-vis work trouser combines visibility to EN ISO 20471 class 2 with the freedom of movement of a modern stretch work trouser and completes the hi-vis jacket and …

More

vest for anyone working on roads, rail, yards or sites with vehicle traffic. Quality models use a polyester blend fabric (benchmark engelbert strauss e.s.motion 2020: 85 % polyester, 15 % cotton) with a stretch component, 50 mm retroreflective bands, reinforced knee pockets with top entry, rule and tool pockets and robust contrast panels at knees and pockets. Professional buyers in Germany expect class 2 marking with pictogram, standard number and wash count on the label, an EU type-examination report (PPE category II) per model and, for knee protection, pads tested together with the trouser to EN 14404. The trouser works best as a set with knee pads, tool pouches, a hi-vis jacket and safety shoes.

E-commerce potential

Hi-vis is a buyer world of its own in the German market: OTTO lists hi-vis trousers from Wuerth and Nitras among others (short Nitras model from about EUR 29.90) …

More

Hornbach offers the waterproof Hekla hi-vis trouser (EN ISO 20471 class 1 plus EN 343) as an entry point at EUR 38.75, and Strauss sells the e.s.motion 2020 hi-vis trouser at EUR 89.13 gross (volume prices down to EUR 77.23). Between the discount entry of roughly EUR 30 to 40 and the branded stretch trouser around EUR 90 there is room for a mid-range own brand with class 2, stretch and knee pockets. Typical cross-sells are knee pads, belts and tool pouches and the matching hi-vis jacket or vest. Return risk is medium to high because of fit and size range (waist and length), so a size chart and a complete label are mandatory. Demand runs all year and peaks in autumn and winter with short days (assessment, no sales figures found).

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EU type-examination certificate and EU declaration of conformity (PPE category II) for exactly this article, with the notified body number; Kinglong names CE EN 20471 and EN 13688 only in its company profile, check the certificate itself.
  • Test report to EN ISO 20471 per class and size: minimum areas of fluorescent and retroreflective material, colour values, reflectance after the wash count stated on the label.
  • Retroreflective tape with manufacturer proof (e.g. 3M or equivalent), 50 mm width; check seam placement and wash durability on the sample.
  • Have the knee pockets sized for a pad tested to EN 14404; sell the pad combination only if a test report for trouser plus pad exists.
  • Measure fabric weight (g/m²), stretch share, shrinkage and colour fastness on the sample; approve size chart and label (pictogram, standard number, care symbols, EU contact details).
  • Have the model number and price tiers confirmed in writing (the Kinglong listing shows no price tiers); request comparison samples of Xintex 903647 and Hollydare HD20260294.
