Description

The diamond wet core bit is the wear part of every core-drill rig: Ø 250 mm for pipe penetrations and ventilation and cable openings in reinforced concrete.

More

The 1 1/4 inch-7 UNC connection fits the common drill stands and motors of this machine class; the diamond segments remove the concrete under water cooling. Professionals look at segment count and height (reference Bosch Best for Concrete: 16 segments, 11.5 mm), the bond for reinforced concrete and the dimensional accuracy of the thread. For imported goods, GPSR labelling and conformity details are mandatory.

Request from the supplier

  • Request Ø 250 mm x 450 mm with 1 1/4 inch-7 UNC as a sample: Sunny lists Ø 203 mm (8 inch) and states that diameter, length and segment count are customisable.
  • Fix segment count, dimensions and height in writing (reference: 16 segments, 11.5 mm) as well as the bond for reinforced concrete.
  • Check the 1 1/4 inch-7 UNC thread fit on the drill stand and motor (e.g. Maxxon VKP250 in the catalogue) and clarify any adapter need.
  • Test report on service life (drilled metres in reinforced concrete) and radial and axial run-out; batch number per bit.
  • Record permissible speed and EN 13236 marking on the bit, water slots, carton dimensions and weight per bit.
  • Request a quote FOB Xiamen (Sunny names Xiamen port) with tiers from 5/10/50 pieces: info@sunnydiamondtools.com.
