Description

The electric construction heater warms site cabins, shell rooms and refurbishment areas without exhaust fumes and can therefore be used in closed rooms.

More

A powder-coated sheet-steel housing, continuously adjustable thermostat, overheat protection and splash-proof design are the standard for rough site operation. Users in Germany expect CE, EN 60335-2-30, a robust H07RN-F cable with CEE 16 A plug, a German manual and operation on a site distribution board with RCD protection per DIN VDE 0100-704. The unit must be inspectable at regular intervals under DGUV Regulation 3.

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity with test reports (EN 60335-2-30, EMC) from an accredited body; GS certificate if advertised.
  • Proof of ingress protection (IPX4) and overheat protection (safety thermostat, functional test per lot).
  • Photo of the rating plate showing 400 V, 9 kW, CE and manufacturer; cable cross-section 5G2.5 mm² and CEE plug per DIN EN 60309-2.
  • German manual with notes on minimum distances, RCD protection and fire protection.
  • Spare parts (heating elements, fan, thermostat) available and at least 12 months warranty.
  • Sample for DGUV V3 inspection (insulation and protective-conductor resistance) before series delivery.
