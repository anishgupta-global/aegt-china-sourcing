E-commerce potential

Reference Lao Gan Ma Crispy Chilli in Oil 210 g: 4.89 EUR gross at Asiafoodland (23.29 EUR/kg), alongside Go Tan Crispy Chilli Oil 190 g at 4.49 EUR (23.63 EUR/kg) …

More

per the market scan an eBay seller charges 11.60 EUR incl. shipping, which shows the markup resellers achieve. The trend is evidenced: Lee Kum Kee has been pushing Chiu Chow chili oil at Gen Z since late 2024 and Rossmann stocks the German brand Pretty NamNam. The market is occupied (Lao Gan Ma as price anchor, German D2C brands, marketplace sellers); a private label wins through recipe (crunch share, heat), glass look and bundles with noodles and rice. Return risk: glass breakage and leaks, oil separation, heat complaints; state refrigeration after opening and best-before date clearly.

Request from the supplier

  • Full recipe with allergens (soy, sesame, peanut, wheat, sulphite) and confirmation of no animal flavourings or ingredients; request recipe evidence for any vegan claim.
  • Batch laboratory report: pesticides incl. ethylene oxide and 2-chloroethanol (Reg. 396/2005), aflatoxins and ochratoxin A, heavy metals, Salmonella in the chili fraction, peroxide and acid value of the oil.
  • Glass and closure: declaration of compliance under Reg. (EC) 1935/2004, tight twist-off lid with vacuum button, drop and transport test, palletising.
  • Check valid BRC/IFS certificates with scope and site in the certifier directory; request the audit report (Made-in-China audit: TÜV Rheinland, ID MIC-ASI265196).
  • Samples in the 210 g format with your own recipe (crunch share, heat level) before the series; fix a remaining shelf life of at least 12 months on arrival contractually.
  • Secure the tariff code: obtain a binding tariff information (BTI) for 2103 90 90 versus chapter 20 and for the subheadings carrying an anti-dumping additional code.
