Description

Sichuan pepper (huajiao) consists of the dried fruit husks of Zanthoxylum species and is not a pepper in the botanical sense: it delivers the citrusy, floral seasoning and the tingling …

More

numbing mouthfeel (ma) that defines Sichuan cuisine. For trade, whole red husks with intense colour, few black seeds, stalks and thorns and low residual moisture count; whole goods keep their aroma much longer than ground ones and are classified more favourably for customs (0 % instead of 12.5 % for ground goods). According to the listing, the chosen supplier offers BRC-certified, steam-sterilised whole goods with metal detection and batch traceability; the 100 g bags are filled from the 25 kg sack, either ex works (OEM) or by a filler in the EU. Key points for the EU market are residue analytics (pesticides, ethylene oxide), mycotoxins and clean origin and allergen labelling.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Sichuan pepper is a niche spice with tailwind from chili crisp and mala dishes.

More

Evidence: Asiafoodland lists two items in its pepper sub-category (own brand 'Wild Szechuan pepper' 50 g for 5.79 EUR = 115.80 EUR/kg, the label states origin PR China; Eagleglobe 57 g for 4.99 EUR = 87.54 EUR/kg). The comparison site feinkosten.de quotes 6.77 EUR for 50 g (Gewuerzgarten, via BOS FOOD) and 7.99 EUR for a Szechuan pepper mill (Piccantino) and puts the usual price at 5-10 EUR for 30-80 g. Retail is therefore about 90-135 EUR/kg; the suppliers' listing prices of 1-4 USD/kg (Tai Foong) and 10-16 USD/kg (Sinospices) are far below, but exclude freight, duty, filling and analytics. Competition: spice merchants and Asian shops. Low return risk (dry, long-life goods); the main risks are quality spread (black seeds, stalks) and residue findings. Sales volumes are not evidenced; suitable as a cross-sell to chili crisp and glass noodles.

Request from the supplier

  • Have the botanical species (Zanthoxylum bungeanum or similar), growing region and harvest year confirmed in writing; check samples with the quality grade (share of black seeds, stalks, thorns, colour) before ordering
  • Per batch: analyses for pesticides (Reg. (EC) 396/2005), ethylene oxide/2-chloroethanol, aflatoxins/ochratoxin A, lead/cadmium and Salmonella; check the listing's ochratoxin A <20 ppb against the EU maximum level in Reg. (EU) 2023/915 and tighten the specification accordingly
  • Request BRC certificate with scope, metal detection, batch traceability and steam sterilisation record; have it confirmed in writing that the goods are not irradiated
  • Clarify filling: 100 g bags ex works (OEM, minimum quantity, label, seal seam, batch code) or a filler in the EU; 25 kg sack with inner bag, moisture-proof
  • Do not source ground product (duty 12.5 % instead of 0 %, faster aroma loss); agree whole goods with a low share of dust and breakage
  • Given the price range (1,000-4,000 USD/t) and MOQ 15 t, obtain a binding quote per quality grade; consider a trial order or a smaller quantity via alternatives (Sinospices MOQ 500 kg)
