More

The small pack size (about 63 × 63 × 12 cm) lowers freight and breakage risk; competition and price pressure are high (Black November discounts, as of 3 Nov 2026). For LED, dimmability and colour temperature are purchase arguments.

Request from the supplier

  • Request test reports for exactly this model (EN 60598-1/-2-1, EMC, RoHS) from an accredited lab (e.g. TUeV, VDE, Intertek); do not treat CE/VDE/GS claims in the listing as proof.
  • Order 1 sample: check workmanship, crystal/glass quality, weight, suspension and installation; measure colour temperature and flicker.
  • Agree on breakage-proof packing (carton + foam or wooden frame) with a drop test; fix spare parts (crystals, chains, bulbs) contractually.
  • Nameplate and documents for the EU: manufacturer/importer address, EU responsible person (GPSR), German installation manual, 230 V/50 Hz, protection class.
  • LED driver and optics: have luminous flux (lm), CRI, colour temperature/CCT switching, dimmability and lifetime (L70) confirmed in writing.
