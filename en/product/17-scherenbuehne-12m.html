Description

The electric scissor lift reaches working heights of up to 12 m and replaces scaffolds and ladders on strip-out, refurbishment and asbestos sites for ceiling …

More

installation and demolition work in halls and stairwells. The emission-free battery drive allows use in enclosed spaces without exhaust load, and proportional drive control enables precise positioning. Professional users in Germany expect CE conformity to EN 280, emergency lowering, overload protection, tilt alarm and a German logbook for the recurring inspection under the Industrial Safety Ordinance. For Chinese-origin machines the EU anti-dumping and countervailing duties must be considered.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity naming EN 280 and the Machinery Directive, plus CE marking on the rating plate
  • EN 280 test report (stability, overload) from an accredited test body, e.g. TÜV
  • German operating manual, logbook and spare parts list before ordering
  • TARIC query: current AD/countervailing duty rate for exactly this manufacturer and the classification (CN 8427 / 8428)
  • Spare parts supply and service partner in Germany or the EU, warranty of at least 12 months
  • Battery and charger evidence (CE, EMC) and transport dimensions for the container
