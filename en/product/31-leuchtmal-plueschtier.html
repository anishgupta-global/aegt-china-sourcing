Market readiness depends on three points: glow pigments firmly bound in the fabric and tested to EN 71-3, a glow pen with an LED or UV-A source and a screwed battery compartment to EN 62115 and EN 62471, and a sturdy plush body to EN 71-1/-2. Request from the supplier

  • Clarify in writing before ordering: glow pen in the scope of delivery (LED or UV-A, wavelength, battery type), glow pigment (type, binding in the fabric), glow duration and size; the listing states none of this.
  • Request EN 71-1/-2/-3 test reports from an accredited lab for the plush including glow pigments (migration to EN 71-3); have the age statement 14+ in the listing corrected to children's toy with matching testing.
  • Have the glow pen tested as an electric toy: EN 62115, EN 62471 (no UV-C/laser source), EMC, RoHS; button cells only in a screwed compartment (EN 71-1), Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 and WEEE registration (stiftung ear) for the pen.
  • Clarify the business type: Dongyang Youge Toy Factory is a trading company per its storefront (2 staff, founded 2024-12-17); have the actual factory, address and test reports named, or enquire about production at a verified plush maker in the Dongyang/Yiwu area (e.g. Dongyang Xinyuan).
  • Assess samples before ordering: glow intensity and duration in the dark, drawing contrast, odour, pigment abrasion on the fabric, washability; because of missing demand evidence for Germany, a test buy instead of a bulk order.
  • Define labelling under GPSR and toy law (manufacturer, EU responsible person, type/batch number, German warnings) and anticipate the new EU Toy Safety Regulation (digital product passport, PFAS/bisphenol bans) contractually.
