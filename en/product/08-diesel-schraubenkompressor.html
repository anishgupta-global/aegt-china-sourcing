Description

The towable screw compressor supplies pneumatic hammers, chiselling tools, blasting equipment and pneumatic drills on demolition sites without mains power and can be moved between jobs on its chassis.

More

An oil-flooded screw element, diesel engine and sound-proof canopy are built for continuous outdoor operation. Professional users in Germany expect an engine with EU Stage V type approval, a road-approved chassis, a service point for the engine and reliable free-air-delivery figures per ISO 1217. Compliance means CE under the Machinery Directive, the Pressure Equipment Directive, noise marking under 2000/14/EC and a German operating manual.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Stage V type approval of the engine under Reg. (EU) 2016/1628 with approval number and engine rating plate; engine maker with EU service (e.g. Kubota, Yanmar, Deutz, Cummins)
  • Declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC, 2014/68/EU (pressure vessel, module and notified body) and 2000/14/EC
  • Acceptance test of the delivery per ISO 1217 (FAD in m³/min at 7 bar) with a test record
  • Road approval of the chassis (ABE or individual approval under StVZO) including brake and towing eye
  • German operating manual, spare parts catalogue, maintenance intervals and a named service partner in Germany
  • Warranty of at least 12–24 months and scope of supply (couplings, oil drain, sea-freight packaging) in writing
