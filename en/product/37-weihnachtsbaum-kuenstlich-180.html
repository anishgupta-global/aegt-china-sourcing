Description

The 180 cm tree is made of a PE/PVC material mix that reproduces the growth of a fir with dense, evenly inserted branches.

More

A sturdy metal stand provides safe footing; the tree ships compactly in a single carton (manufacturer data 60 × 40 × 60 cm, 5.0 kg gross) and stores flat after the season. Discerning buyers look for branch density, even colour, a stable stand and verifiably flame-retardant needles. For EU distribution, GPSR labelling with an EU responsible person and REACH and POP compliance of the PVC parts are standard.

Request from the supplier

  • Request a reaction-to-fire test report (e.g. DIN 4102-1 B1 or EN 13501-1) for the needles; 'flame-retardant' is not evidenced in the listing.
  • Require a REACH declaration on phthalates, lead and cadmium in the PVC as well as freedom from short-chain chlorinated paraffins.
  • Specify the stand: metal, stability and tilt test, screw connection, spare parts in the carton.
  • Approve branch density, colour and plug-in system on a sample and compare against a Hornbach reference tree.
  • Test the single carton (60 × 40 × 60 cm, 5.0 kg) in a shipping test; define the GPSR label with EU responsible person and batch number.
  • Verify the factory audit: request BSCI/SGS reports and check TÜV Rheinland audit ID MIC-ASR2531122 on the official site.
