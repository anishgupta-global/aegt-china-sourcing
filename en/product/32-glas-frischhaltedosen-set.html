Description

The containers made of heat-resistant borosilicate glass are neutral in taste and odour, do not absorb colours and, according to the manufacturer, are suitable for microwave, oven, freezer and dishwasher.

More

The silicone ring in the lid provides an airtight, leak-proof closure and can be removed for cleaning. Quality markers are uniform glass wall thickness, a passed thermal shock test, BPA-free lids and complete test reports under LFGB or (EC) 1935/2004. Well suited as an add-on to vacuum sealers and as a meal-prep set.

Request from the supplier

  • Request a test report under (EC) 1935/2004 or LFGB for glass, silicone ring and, if applicable, plastic lid (migration, lead/cadmium release).
  • Have thermal shock testing (e.g. hot water to cold bath) and wall thickness/burst behaviour proven per batch.
  • Test leak-tightness (24 h upside down, leakage) and airtightness of the lid closure.
  • Transport packaging with drop test and dividers; breakage rate in the sample and contractually agreed claims handling.
  • Evidence of the expansion coefficient (borosilicate 3.3, <= 5 x 10^-6 per Kelvin) as a data sheet for customs classification.
  • Confirm factory location (office address Jinan, origin Shandong); request audit report.
