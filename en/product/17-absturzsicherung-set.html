Description

The set combines a full-body harness with dorsal attachment (rear D-ring) and an energy-absorbing lanyard made of 12 mm twisted rope, fitted with one karabiner and two forged scaffold hooks.

More

The manufacturer states testing to EN 361 for the harness TP-SH3201 (buckle strength 18 kN, D-ring 25 kN) and testing to EN 355:2002 for the lanyard TP-SL5216 in lengths of 1.5, 1.8 or 2.0 m. Fall-arrest equipment of this kind is category III PPE: in the EU it may be placed on the market only with an EU type-examination by a notified body and ongoing production control, with German instructions, serial number and date of manufacture. Professional buyers expect a set with a traceable service life and annual inspection by a competent person; the set is assembled here from two separate listings, a joint certificate for both parts was not found.

BG BAU funds fall protection within its occupational-safety premiums and introduces new premiums for temporary fall and fall-through protection from 1 July 2026 (Handwerksblatt); the funding eligibility of specific sets has not been checked. The set complements every scaffold, roof and platform item but is a wear part with limited service life (Kratos states 10 years from first use) and a high liability risk: without a valid EU type-examination the product may not be placed on the market.

Request from the supplier

  • Request a valid EU type-examination certificate (module B) from a notified body for harness and lanyard (PPE category III) and cross-check the notified body number in the EU NANDO directory; certificate holder, model and factory address must match the supplier.
  • Demand proof of production control (module C2 or D) with the notified body number on the product label; do not order without it.
  • Check marking to EN 365 (manufacturer, model, serial number, month and year of manufacture, standard references, CE with notified body number); approve German instructions incl. inspection and discard criteria beforehand.
  • EU declaration of conformity per lot and lot traceability; sample testing (harness fit, absorber, hook closure) by an independent laboratory before series ordering.
  • Define the set composition (harness TP-SH3201 + lanyard TP-SL5216, 2.0 m, scaffold-hook variant), packaging (bag or carton) and price per set (listing: harness MOQ 500, lanyard MOQ 100).
  • Fix service-life and inspection information (annual inspection) as well as the recall and spare-part process with the supplier by contract.
  • Use on mobile elevating work platforms: BGHM requires fall-protection PPE on all boom platforms (whip effect); whether a restraint or a fall-arrest system and which lanyard length is permitted is set by the platform manufacturer's manual, so do not advertise the 2 m set generically as platform accessory.
