More

The supplier cites FDA and LFGB certificates and offers samples with a later test report. As a reusable product the set does not fall under the bans and levies on single-use plastic products (e.g. single-use plates and cutlery, EPS cups, fund levy on single-use cups), provided it is demonstrably designed for repeated use (wall thickness, wash cycles). It suits cross-selling with paper and bagasse tableware, napkins and folding tables for garden parties, clubs and daycare centres.

E-commerce potential

The segment exists: an editorial comparison on kita.de lists five reusable party tableware sets, including MATANA 150 pieces for about €42.99 …

More

(25 place settings) and 96 pieces for about €39.99; PartyDeko.de sells plastic cups as 8-packs for €1.59–2.49 (€0.20–0.31 each) as single-use. The driver is the cost lever against single-use: single-use cups are subject to the single-use plastics fund in Germany, reusables are not. Risks: buyers compare by price per piece, thin walls lead to reviews criticising breakage, returns for discolouration after wash cycles.

Request from the supplier

  • Request migration test reports under (EU) 10/2011 and LFGB for each piece (cutlery too) and a declaration of conformity with traceability
  • Evidence of reusable design: wall thickness, test after 100 dishwasher cycles, dimensional stability and discolouration
  • Fix the bill of materials: number of plates, bowls, cups, cutlery, colours and sizes (the listing only says '24 pieces')
  • Clarify composition: pure PP without melamine or polyamide parts (cutlery, cups), as special rules for China apply to those materials
  • Sample cycle before series: check colour accuracy, flash and odour; have the supplier's trading status evidenced
