Party, seasonal & décor

Seasonal goods are the most plannable growth field in online retail: Christmas décor, Halloween, carnival and party supplies sell on the same calendar every year …

More

at low unit prices and with large baskets through accessories and sets. The range is for AE Global Traders and independent e-commerce sellers who want to source seasonal assortments directly from Chinese manufacturers. The buying cycle is early: for the 2026 season new sea-freight orders are practically too late; Christmas goods for 2027 are ordered from January 2027 (Christmasworld Frankfurt, 29 Jan – 2 Feb 2027).

Yiwu (Zhejiang) is the trading hub for Christmas and party goods; per a figure from The Economist quoted on Wikipedia, over 60% of Christmas trinkets sold worldwide in 2013 came from Yiwu (not verified by us). Lit goods (LED strings, LED candles, light figures): Zhongshan Guzhen (Guangdong), described on Wikipedia as China's lighting capital, plus Shenzhen/Dongguan. Artificial trees: per Wikipedia mostly the Pearl River Delta (Guangdong). Balloons, costumes and toy-adjacent items: Shantou-Chenghai (Guangdong), Yiwu and Guangzhou; paper and packaging items (Advent calendars, gift wrap): coastal provinces such as Zhejiang and Guangdong. The attribution of Chenghai, Guangzhou and the packaging locations is general industry knowledge and not individually sourced. Yiwu is mostly a market/trader structure: verify factory status before ordering.

Lit goods: Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU (light chains EN 60598-2-20), EMC 2014/30/EU, RoHS 2011/65/EU, ElektroG registration (stiftung ear), for battery-powered items the Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 and BattG; Ecodesign/energy label for light sources (EU) 2019/2020 and 2019/2015 to be checked per product. Children's costumes and toy-adjacent items: Toy Safety Directive 2009/48/EC with EN 71-1, EN 71-2 (flammability) and EN 71-3; adult costumes: GPSR plus textile labelling (EU) 1007/2011. Balloons: EN 71-1 with warning text (choking/suffocation, latex balloons), check toy classification; in Germany balloons and beverage cups are subject to the single-use plastics levy (Einwegkunststofffondsgesetz, Annex 1: registration and levy). Single-Use Plastics Directive (EU) 2019/904: bans, among others, EPS cups and containers and oxo-degradable plastics (verified); the directive and the German Einwegkunststoffverbotsverordnung also cover single-use plastic cutlery, plates, straws and balloon sticks (not re-verified, check against the current text before import). Reusable party tableware must be genuinely reusable, otherwise the single-use regime applies. REACH (EC) 1907/2006: phthalates and lead in PVC trees, figures and coatings. VerpackG/LUCID registration. Sourcing: respect the season cycle (sea freight about 5–6 weeks plus production), samples and test reports before series orders, clarify trader vs. factory status in Yiwu.
