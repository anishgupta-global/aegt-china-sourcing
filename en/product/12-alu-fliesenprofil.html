Description

Tile profiles protect external corners and tile edges, form clean terminations at walls, floors and stairs and are part of the standard range in every bathroom or tiling refurbishment.

More

Professional users in Germany expect extruded profiles in EN AW-6060 or 6063 with uniform wall thickness, a cleanly punched anchoring leg, deburred edges and a uniform finish (natural, anodised or powder-coated), supplied in 2.5 m lengths without distortion and without colour deviation between batches. Dimensional accuracy to EN 12020-2, coating evidence (Qualanod or Qualicoat) and a REACH-compliant finish are required. Premium systems such as Schlüter set the benchmark for system accessories such as corners and connectors.

Request from the supplier

  • Material certificate 3.1 (EN 10204) stating alloy and temper (EN AW-6060/6063, T5/T6)
  • Dimensional inspection record per batch with tolerances to EN 12020-2
  • Proof of finish: anodic layer thickness (EN ISO 7599, Qualanod) or powder coating (Qualicoat) including adhesion and salt spray test
  • REACH declaration of conformity (SVHC) for coating and paint
  • Sampling of colour and finish as well as accessories (corners, connectors) before series production
  • Edge-protected packaging (bundle/carton) and a clear price per bar or metre with Incoterm and MOQ
