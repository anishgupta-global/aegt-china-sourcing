Description

More

the combustion gases are routed outdoors through a flue pipe so the room air stays free of exhaust. A steel combustion chamber and heat exchanger, flame monitoring, overheat cut-out and a 230 V blower are mandatory. Professional users in Germany expect CE (EMC, low voltage, machinery), conformity with EN 60335-2-102, a German manual and clear guidance on flue routing, CO monitoring and fire protection. Direct-fired hot-air cannons are not suitable for closed rooms or refurbishment work.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Written confirmation with drawing that the unit is indirect fired (closed heat exchanger, flue pipe connection).
  • EU declaration of conformity with test reports (EN 60335-2-102, EN 267, EMC) from an accredited body, and GS certificate if advertised.
  • Measurement protocol for efficiency and CO and flue-gas values at rated output.
  • Proof of testing for flame monitoring and overheat cut-out (functional test per lot).
  • German manual with installation, flue, CO and fire-protection instructions.
  • Spare parts (nozzle, ignition electrode, pump, blower) available and at least 12 months warranty.
