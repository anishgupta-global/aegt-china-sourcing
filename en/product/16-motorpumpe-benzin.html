Users in Germany expect CE under the Machinery Directive, an engine with EU type approval to Stage V, a German manual and spare-parts supply at the level of branded units such as the Makita EW3050H. Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC with technical documentation and risk assessment (EN ISO 12100).
  • EU type approval of the engine to Stage V (EU) 2016/1628 naming engine maker and type (e.g. Loncin, Zongshen, Honda).
  • Pump curve (Q-H) and suction-head test as a measurement protocol.
  • German manual with notes on exhaust fumes, refuelling and fire protection.
  • Spare-parts list (mechanical seal, impeller, spark plug, carburettor) and at least 12 months warranty.
  • Sample for comparison against a branded reference (e.g. Makita EW3050H) before series delivery.
