Description

The torque wrench tightens wheel nuts and chassis bolts to the torque specified by the vehicle manufacturer and thus prevents over-tightening and loose wheels …

More

guides name re-tightening after about 50 km as standard. Discerning buyers in Germany expect a click-type tool made of chrome-vanadium steel that releases audibly and noticeably, a clearly readable scale with a lock for the set value, a non-slip grip and a test certificate to ISO 6789 with a stated tolerance. The chosen set covers 28–210 Nm and contains a 125 mm extension, an adapter and 17 and 19 mm sockets; a case is not shown in the listing and must be specified if needed. For EU online sales, GPSR labelling with an EU responsible person and a German manual with the instruction to relax the wrench to its lowest setting after use are required.

Request from the supplier

  • Calibration and test certificate to ISO 6789-2 per batch or per piece (serial number) with readings at 20, 60 and 100 % of the range and a stated tolerance (target ±4 %)
  • Range 28–210 Nm with an even scale and lock; if the 40–210 Nm designation usual in German retail is wanted, ask the supplier for a 40–210 Nm variant
  • 17/19 mm sockets (optionally 21 mm) and square drive dimensionally correct to the ISO standard, proof of wall thickness and hardness; specify a case (plastic, blow-moulded) separately, the listing only mentions a box
  • Material proof (Cr-V) and proof of chrome plating without chromium(VI) residues (REACH test report)
  • German manual noting relaxing to the lowest setting, re-tightening after about 50 km and tightening torques only as specified by the vehicle manufacturer; EU responsible person under GPSR
  • Order a sample (listing: sample about 16.41 USD) and test it in your own lab against a calibrated reference meter; fix OEM logo and colour box
