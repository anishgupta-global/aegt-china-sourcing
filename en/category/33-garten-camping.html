Garden, balcony & camping

Garden, balcony and camping is a strongly seasonal but very broad online range of light to mid-weight consumer goods, served densely by DIY chains (OTTO, Hornbach …

toom) and camping specialists (Fritz Berger, Globetrotter, Bergfreunde). The category is aimed at AE Global Traders and independent e-commerce sellers who want to source own-brand or trading goods for spring and summer directly from Chinese manufacturers: irrigation, solar water features, camping furniture, tents and gazebos, pools, raised beds and BBQ accessories. Late October is the sourcing window for the season from March, because samples, test reports and sea freight together take several months.

By industry knowledge, to be verified per factory on Global Sources / Made-in-China: Zhejiang (Ningbo, Yuyao, Taizhou, Yongkang, Yiwu) for plastic and metal outdoor goods, hoses, camping furniture and gazebos; Guangdong (Zhongshan incl. Guzhen, Shenzhen, Dongguan) for solar pumps, lanterns and lighting, Yangjiang and Chaozhou for stainless-steel cutlery and BBQ accessories, Foshan for steel fabrication; Fujian (Xiamen, Quanzhou) for tents and outdoor gear; Hebei, Tianjin and Shandong for galvanised steel products such as raised beds; Jiangsu and Zhejiang for PVC pools and textiles (hammocks). Shantou Chenghai (toys) is only relevant for add-on water toys and is not listed here.
