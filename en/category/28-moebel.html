Furniture

Office and site-cabin furniture is a plannable but freight-heavy purchasing item for AE Global Traders, where China offers clear unit-price advantages on sit-stand desks, office chairs and steel furniture.

More

What matters is documented conformity with standards (EN 527, EN 1335, EN 16121), a clean customs classification and a workable warranty route in Germany.

Office furniture and standing desks cluster in Zhejiang (Hangzhou, Ningbo, Xinchang); chairs, sofas and custom furniture are strong in Guangdong (Foshan, Guangzhou). New: Lumi Legend (Ningbo) and Jiecang (Xinchang) join Loctek and Sunon in Zhejiang, and Zebai (Shunde, Foshan) joins the chair group in Guangdong. Steel filing cabinets, lockers and shelving tend to come from Luoyang (Henan), so GDLT sits outside the existing clusters.
