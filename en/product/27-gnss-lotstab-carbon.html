Description

The two-section carbon pole carries a GNSS antenna or RTK rover at 2.0 m and holds it plumb over the survey point: a circular level (30 minutes) is used for alignment …

More

the sections lock with snap-locks, and the tube is 32 mm in diameter. Carbon keeps the weight low and remains comfortable to grip in the cold, which makes a noticeable difference on long survey days. The 5/8 inch connection is the standard for GNSS receivers and antennas; a controller or smartphone can be added with a clamp bracket on the pole. Professional buyers look for straightness, play-free locking, an adjustable vial and a cleanly cut thread.

Request from the supplier

  • Check the 5/8 inch thread (5/8"-11 UNC) and thread length on a sample with your own receiver; not explicitly stated in the Mount Laser listing.
  • Measure weight and straightness on a sample (reference Nestle 2 × 100 cm: 0.52 kg) and have the 32 mm tube diameter confirmed.
  • Circular level (30 minutes): adjustable? Calibrate against a reference vial and check the spread across several samples.
  • Snap-lock: engagement at the stated heights, no play under load, repeatability; have the 3-year warranty confirmed in writing.
  • Controller/smartphone bracket for 32 mm: clamping range, material, drop protection; request price and MOQ separately (reference Geomaster GPB2-55/100).
  • Company data is contradictory (founded 2021 or 1999, Changzhou or Suzhou): request business licence, the original SGS report QIP-ASR254991 and a factory audit; appoint an EU responsible person under GPSR and clarify packaging for LUCID.
