Description

Tea drink (iced tea) in 500 ml PET bottles for drinks supply on construction sites and in break rooms.

More

A stable, clear taste without sediment, tightly closed bottles with tethered caps and a clean ingredient list are expected. In Germany single-use plastic beverage bottles carry a mandatory deposit and must be registered in the LUCID packaging register and take part in the deposit system (DPG). Additives, sugar declarations and the PET food-contact material must be documented under EU law.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Valid HACCP / ISO 22000 / FSSC 22000 / BRCGS certificate of the filling site.
  • Recipe with sugar content and proof that all additives are authorised under Reg. (EC) 1333/2008.
  • Declaration of compliance for PET bottle and closure under Reg. (EU) 10/2011 and Reg. (EC) 1935/2004.
  • Proof of tethered caps under Directive (EU) 2019/904 and deposit eligibility (DPG mark, deposit system) under the VerpackG.
  • Lab report per batch (microbiology, pesticide residues from the tea) and German label draft under the FIC Regulation.
  • Sample, tasting and storage test before series release.
