Description

The promotional power bank keeps the smartphones of site managers and foremen running when no socket is within reach on site.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a scratch-resistant housing, an abrasion-proof logo print and lithium cells of traceable origin instead of anonymous budget cells. As importer, AE Global Traders is responsible for CE conformity, the battery regulation, ElektroG registration and the UN 38.3 transport proof, so the test reports must be on file before ordering. The entry-level model suits customer and staff gifts, not a professional device under permanent heavy use.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity and test report to EN 62368-1 plus EMC (EN 55032/EN 55035) from an accredited lab (e.g. TÜV, SGS, Intertek)
  • UN 38.3 test report with transport summary and safety data sheet (MSDS) for the installed battery
  • Name of the cell manufacturer and cell type; measure real capacity by a discharge test on samples and compare it with the printed value
  • Approval sample with logo before series start, including an abrasion test of the print
  • Marking on device and packaging: manufacturer and importer address, Wh rating, crossed-out bin and battery symbol, German manual
  • Clarify who handles ElektroG and battery registration for the German market (Stiftung ear)
