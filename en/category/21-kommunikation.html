21 · Construction supplies

Communication

On sites with basements, shafts and patchy mobile coverage, reliable communication decides safety and workflow, from licence-free handheld radios to rugged outdoor smartphones.

More

China supplies radios and outdoor smartphones of good series quality, but Chinese satellite phones (Tiantong) only work in Asia-Pacific and are unsuitable for Germany.

Satellite-capable phones cluster in Shenzhen (Guangdong) around Ulefone, Blackview, ZTE and Tiantong suppliers such as Aoro and Leikan; further suppliers are based in Guangzhou and Wuhan. Radio makers such as Hytera (Shenzhen) and Retevis (Shenzhen according to general knowledge) complement the cluster. Satellite messenger suppliers: Shenzhen Maojiexin (Alibaba, Iridium SBD) and Guangdong Hai Liao (BeiDou).

One-stop supplier for this category
Hytera Communications Corporation Limited ↗TIER AShenzhen, Guangdong
