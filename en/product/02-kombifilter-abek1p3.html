Description

The ABEK1-P3 combination filter protects simultaneously against organic and inorganic gases and vapours, acid gases, ammonia and particles …

More

making it the universal filter for remediation with solvents, adhesive and coating removal, mould and hazardous-material work. It is used in pairs in half or full-face masks with a matching bayonet or Rd40 connection and consists of an activated-carbon gas filter bed with a downstream P3 particle filter in a robust, colour-coded housing. Professional users in Germany expect marking to EN 14387 with class designation, CE with NB number, manufacturing and expiry date, and a company replacement schedule, because service life depends on the contaminant concentration. As category III PPE the EU type-examination certificate is mandatory; type A gas filters do not apply to substances with a boiling point below 65 °C.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU type-examination certificate for EN 14387 with classes A1 B1 E1 K1 P3 and NB number – cross-check in NANDO.
  • Test reports on breakthrough times per test gas (cyclohexane, chlorine, sulphur dioxide, ammonia) and breathing resistance.
  • Prove compatibility with the mask connection (bayonet or Rd40); filters individually sealed with caps/foil.
  • Marking with colour code, manufacturing and expiry date, batch number and German instructions including use limits (gases with boiling point below 65 °C).
  • Have samples tested independently before a series order; request the plant name and quality management (ISO 9001).
