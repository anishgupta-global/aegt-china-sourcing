Description

The set bundles 30 bits with 1/4-inch hex shank (slotted, Phillips, Pozidriv, Torx, hex) and a bit holder with adapter in a plastic case, covering the common screw drives on site and in the workshop.

More

S2 tool steel at 58 to 62 HRC, surface treatment (manganese phosphate, nickel, sandblasting) and a magnetic seat ensure a snug fit, corrosion protection and one-handed work. Torx screws are useless without the right bit, so the set belongs in the basket of every screw and dowel order. Buyers in Germany expect dimensionally true drive profiles to ISO 10664 and ISO 1173, clearly marked sizes and a REACH-compliant coating.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the exact bill of contents per set in writing (sizes TX10 to TX40, PH1 to PH3, PZ1 to PZ3, lengths, holder, adapter, case)
  • S2 material certificate and hardness test (58 to 62 HRC) per batch; dimension and torque test of the profiles to ISO 10664 / ISO 1173
  • Check magnet in the bit holder and coating: salt-spray test, adhesion, nickel and Cr(VI) freedom, REACH declaration
  • Test samples against Bosch 32-pc and FORTIS 31-pc: fit in the screw head, break-out rate with an impact driver, durability
  • Packaging with German labelling, EAN, EU responsible person (GPSR) and LUCID; confirm customs classification 8207 90 30 in advance (listing states 8205 40 00)
