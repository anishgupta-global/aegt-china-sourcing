Description

The 2-chamber lock in rigid module or container construction separates the black and white areas: personnel or material pass through two chambers in sequence so that the …

More

negative pressure in the working area is maintained. Smooth, waterproof, easily decontaminated surfaces, a load-bearing steel frame and thermal insulation make the set-up practical in winter as well. Chamber dimensions and equipment (hinged doors with viewing window, supply-air flaps, shower and water connection) are defined per project; the reference is the Deconta ECO-NEXT material lock with 1,800 x 1,400 x 2,196 mm per chamber. Unlike folding locks, the rigid version stays dimensionally stable over many deployments but is transported as a module on pallet or truck.

E-commerce potential

High-ticket project business rather than a shopping-cart item: locks are sold by makers such as Deconta (ECO-NEXT, SMART-LINE) and by specialist dealers.

More

Evidence (as of 2 Nov 2026): the ASUP online shop publishes net prices: 2-chamber material lock AirWay M2K (aluminium, 400 x 180 x 215 cm) EUR 10,500, enviro 4-chamber personnel lock EUR 9,500, DECONTA 1-chamber personnel lock SMART-LINE EUR 1,539.10; prices for the Deconta ECO-NEXT line are not published. Sells well in a bundle with negative pressure units, supply-air flaps and water management; Deconta also runs a rental fleet that competes with buying. Main risks are transport cost, assembly effort and proof of TRGS 519 conformity, so offer consulting and drawing sign-off before ordering.

Request from the supplier

  • Agree a specification with drawing: number of chambers, dimensions, door sequence black -> white, interlock, supply-air flaps, shower and water connections
  • Inner surfaces smooth, waterproof and with few joints (decontamination); material proof for fire behaviour (EN 13501-1)
  • Have the electrical and water installation proven per DIN VDE 0100-701/-704 incl. RCD protection and IP rating
  • Check structural design, lifting and forklift points and shipping dimensions; for sea freight clarify container format and loading
  • Agree factory acceptance before shipment (photos/video) and seal/spare-part supply
  • Put payment terms, warranty and assembly instructions in German in writing
