Description

The FFP2 mask with activated carbon combines the particle protection of class FFP2 …

More

(at least 94 % filtration) with a carbon fleece layer that binds odours and makes wearing more pleasant in paint, cleaning or welding environments. High-quality masks have a shape-retaining three- to four-ply construction, an adjustable nose clip with nose pad and latex-free head straps; an exhalation valve reduces heat and moisture build-up during longer wear. Professional users expect testing to EN 149:2001+A1:2009 with CE marking and NB number, hygienic packaging in boxes of 10-20 pieces and a clear note that the carbon layer only reduces odour nuisance and gives no protection against gases and vapours. As category III PPE the mask needs an EU type-examination certificate.

E-commerce potential

Classic upsell on the basic disposable mask.

More

Price anchors per NonPaints (retrieved 2026-10-31): COLAD FFP2 without valve EUR 1.63, with valve EUR 3.46 and with activated carbon + valve (item no. 5525) EUR 4.90 per mask gross, i.e. about three times the base version; tiered price down to EUR 3.68 from 36 pieces. Target group: painters, welders and cleaning staff, consumable with repeat purchase. Risks: misleading protection claims about the carbon layer (odour only, no gas and vapour protection), hygiene article without return option, and certification effort; bestseller ranks were not checked.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EU type-examination certificate to EN 149:2001+A1:2009 (FFP2 NR D) with NB number and cross-check it in NANDO; the listing only names the standard EN 149:2001+A1:2009, no certificate.
  • Fix the model: P812VC (carbon, with valve) or P842C-5 (carbon, without valve); each variant needs its own certificate and its own samples.
  • Request a test report: penetration at most 6 % (NaCl and paraffin oil), breathing resistance, total inward leakage and dolomite clogging test.
  • Clarify packaging: boxes of 10 pieces (24 boxes per carton), batch number, expiry date, German instructions for use and a note that the activated carbon only reduces odour.
  • Have the carbon layer (material, basis weight), latex-free straps and skin compatibility documented.
  • Confirm price tiers and MOQ bindingly (listing: USD 0.50 from 10,000 pieces) and clarify the plant status (profile: 5 employees, office address); test at least one box of samples before the series order.
