Description

The 180 cm HDPE folding table is the robust solution for break rooms, training and meetings in site cabins: the blow-moulded top is weatherproof, wipe-clean and insensitive to dirt and moisture.

More

A steel-tube frame with secure locking ensures a firm stand, and the briefcase-style folding mechanism with carrying handle eases transport and storage. Professional buyers in Germany expect testing to EN 15372, for dining areas food-contact compliance of the top and pinch protection on the folding mechanism. Requirements for break rooms on construction sites follow ASR A4.2.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 15372 (strength, durability, stability) including testing of the folding mechanism (pinch protection, locking)
  • Food-contact compliance of the HDPE top: declaration of conformity under Regulation (EC) 1935/2004 and (EU) 10/2011 with migration report; proof of UV stabilisation
  • Load-capacity proof (evenly distributed, in kg) and material certificate for the steel-tube frame (wall thickness, powder coating or galvanising)
  • REACH/SVHC declaration (pigments, heavy metals, additives)
  • Pack size per table and palletising (bulky freight), pre-shipment sample to check locking and carrying handle
  • Warranty of at least 2 years and spare parts (legs, hinges, locking)
