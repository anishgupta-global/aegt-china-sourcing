Toys

Toys are a core China-sourcing field: Shantou alone exported toys worth CNY 8.56 bn in the first three quarters of 2025, with the EU as its largest market (+11.4%).

More

The range bundles ten sub-segments with documented online demand in Germany (from magnetic tiles and plush to water blasters) for AE Global Traders and independent sellers building own-brand or private-label lines. Margin comes less from the purchase price than from test reports, bundles and packaging.

Chenghai (Shantou, Guangdong) is the hub for building blocks, magnetic tiles, RC vehicles, water toys and STEM sets (over 10,000 toy companies, roughly 90% of components available locally). Dongguan (Guangdong) is the OEM hub for brands, including puzzles, plush and electronic toys; Shenzhen supplies electronics and batteries (RC, STEM). Yiwu (Zhejiang) serves small runs and sensory/fidget items with low MOQs. Ningbo and Yunhe (Zhejiang) are centres for wooden toys; plush often comes from Yangzhou (Jiangsu), kids' balance bikes from Pingxiang/Xingtai (Hebei) and Tianjin (the last three clusters are experience-based, verify the supplier per item).

EU toy law: Toy Safety Directive 2009/48/EC with EN 71-1 (mechanical/physical incl. magnets and small parts), EN 71-2 (flammability), EN 71-3 …
More

(migration of certain elements) and EN 62115 for electric toys; CE marking, EU declaration of conformity, technical documentation, safety assessment and German warning/age labelling are mandatory. Radio toys (RC 2.4 GHz) additionally RED 2014/53/EU; batteries: Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 + BattG, ElektroG/stiftung ear, VerpackG/LUCID. Online sellers need an EU-based responsible person and traceability information under GPSR (EU) 2023/988. Outlook: the new EU Toy Safety Regulation (EU) 2025/2509 (published 12 Dec 2025, replaces Directive 2009/48/EC and applies in full from 1 Aug 2030) brings a digital product passport, a PFAS ban and stricter chemical rules (incl. bisphenols, CMR, endocrine disruptors): specify new goods PFAS- and bisphenol-free now. Sourcing: demand test reports (SGS/TUV/Intertek) per model, not per factory; building bricks only with own design, no licensed motifs and no copies of third-party sets (trademark/design/copyright, customs seizure); plan for price pressure from direct platforms such as Temu (15% usage for toys/baby).
