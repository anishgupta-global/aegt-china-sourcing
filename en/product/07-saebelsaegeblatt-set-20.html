Description

The 20-piece reciprocating saw blade set covers the typical jobsite cuts with bi-metal blades: wood with nails, pallets and beams with coarse tooth pitch, pipes, profiles and sheet metal with fine pitch.

More

Bi-metal means a high-speed-steel (M2) tooth edge welded to a flexible steel back (D6A), combining service life and break resistance. According to the manufacturer the universal shank fits reciprocating saws from Bosch, Makita, DeWalt, Metabo and Hitachi/HiKOKI. Sorted in a case or plastic box, the blades are at hand for tradespeople and attractive to retailers as a recurring consumable. Decisive for quality are a clean weld seam, even tooth hardness and clear marking of length and teeth per inch.

Return risk through shank compatibility (U/T shank): show shank type in title and image; label blade lengths and teeth per inch on each blade.

Request from the supplier

  • Have the bi-metal weld checked (bend and fatigue-bend test, set target values for tooth and back hardness), require a test report.
  • Service-life test: cuts in 1.5 mm steel sheet and steel tube as well as in square timber with nails, compared with a Bosch reference blade.
  • Test shank dimensions and fit on at least three reciprocating saws (Bosch, Makita, DeWalt); specify U/T shank unambiguously.
  • Define the set assortment (length, TPI, wood/metal); marking on each blade and print on the box.
  • Plastic box: latch, phthalates/REACH, print; protect blades individually against rust (oil/film).
  • Brands: unbranded goods without third-party marks; compatibility statements only descriptive; agree a sample and a pre-shipment inspection (AQL).
