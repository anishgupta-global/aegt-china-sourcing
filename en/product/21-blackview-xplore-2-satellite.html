Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under RED 2014/53/EU with test reports (radio, EMC, safety) and cybersecurity proof under EN 18031
  • SAR test report to EN 50566 / EN 62209-1 (head and body use) from an accredited lab for the delivered model
  • Written confirmation of the satellite function for the delivered variant: network (Skylo), coverage in Europe, scope of voice/text, subscription costs
  • Frequency band list for Germany and a sample test with a German SIM
  • Lab test reports for IP68/IP69K and MIL-STD-810H
  • Ecodesign proofs: energy label, repairability information, spare parts and software updates for at least 5 years
  • German manual, warranty of at least 24 months, EU service partner (the country shop names no EU office) and WEEE/battery registration of the importer
