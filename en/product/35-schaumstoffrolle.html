Description

The fascia roller made of high-density EPP (expanded polypropylene) keeps its shape even under frequent use and is light enough for home, studio and travel.

More

The honeycomb surface supports self-massage of back, legs and glutes and helps release tension. Quality markers are even density, no deformation under load, a cleanly fused surface and low odour. For EU sales, GPSR labelling, a PAH test report and a German exercise guide are advisable.

Request from the supplier

  • Specify density (kg/m3), hardness, length and diameter in writing and measure on the sample
  • Load/deformation test (sustained load) on the sample; document shape after the test
  • Request a PAH/REACH test report for exactly this EPP material
  • Clarify what a 'set' in the listing contains (1 roller or several parts)
  • Define a German exercise leaflet, packaging with GPSR details and EU responsible person
