Description

The reciprocating saw is the tool of choice in deconstruction and strip-out for cutting timber beams, metal pipes, profiles and embedded nails, even in confined installations.

More

Professional users expect a strong 1,200 W motor with orbital action, tool-free blade change, a sturdy shoe guide and a low-vibration design. The machine must be CE-compliant and tested to EN 62841-2-11; a GS mark from an EU test house and vibration data under the EU vibration directive are standard. Operating manual and declaration of conformity are supplied in German, and matching blades and spare parts must be available in Germany.

Request from the supplier
