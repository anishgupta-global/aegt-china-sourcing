Description

The dust-suppression machine breaks water into fine droplets of 30–150 µm at a nozzle ring and drives the mist 25–30 m into the dust cloud with a powerful fan …

More

the particles become heavier and settle without soaking the surfaces. The unit swivels horizontally by up to 340°, can be controlled locally, by remote control or by PLC and, according to the data sheet, covers around 1,800 m² – enough for building demolition, crushing and screening plants or handling yards. Nozzles and water ring are made of 304 stainless steel, the frame of carbon steel (5 mm plate) with anti-corrosion paint. Professional buyers expect a complete CE conformity package under the Machinery Directive, a water filter upstream of the nozzle ring, a backflow preventer to EN 1717 on the drinking-water connection and a German operating manual with maintenance schedule.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EU declaration of conformity and technical file under Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC (Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 from 20 Jan 2027); check the CE, ISO 9001/14001/45001 certificates named in the listing and the CTI audit report (ID QIP-ASR2691446) in the original.
  • Fix configuration and price in writing: the listing shows USD 500–1,000 per set; clarify whether pump, slewing drive, elevation drive, control cabinet and remote are included, and have 400 V/50 Hz with a CEE plug confirmed.
  • Material proof for nozzles and water ring (304 stainless steel), 5 mm plate thickness and paint build-up; demand a water filter and a nozzle test with hard (limescale) water.
  • Acceptance test by video or factory acceptance: measured throw (25–30 m), water use (15–18 l/min), droplet spectrum and noise (65 dB at 20 m) with a test report; have the protection rating of motor and controller (target IP55) documented.
  • Put the German operating manual, warnings, spare-parts list (nozzles, seals, motor bearings) and warranty terms (listing: 1 year on core components) into the contract.
  • Clarify contradictions in the listing: weight 400 kg versus 340 kg package gross weight, company age (16 versus 20 years); audit report and factory visit before a series order, and confirm ISPM 15 marking of the packaging.
