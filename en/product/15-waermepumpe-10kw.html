Description

The air-to-water heat pump in monobloc design with natural refrigerant R290 supplies space heating and hot water for renovation and new-build projects without refrigerant lines running into the building.

More

Professional users in Germany expect an A+++ rating, low sound emissions (relevant for TA Lärm), flow temperatures of at least 65 °C for existing radiators and a tested controller. CE (low voltage, EMC), ecodesign and energy label data, a Heat Pump Keymark and EN 14511 test reports are mandatory; only listed units are eligible for BEG subsidies. Because of the flammable refrigerant R290, the maker must evidence a safety concept per EN 378 and EN 60335-2-40.
