Description

Masking tape is pure consumable stock in interior fit-out: it holds masking film, sets sharp paint lines and protects windows, frames, skirting boards and floors during painting and filling work.

More

What matters is an even crepe that follows curves and an adhesive that comes off the substrate residue-free after its dwell time without lifting paint or plaster. German professionals expect a stated maximum dwell time, adhesion tested to EN 1939 and a REACH-compliant adhesive. Without a sample test on plaster, paint and glass the goods do not enter standard stock.
