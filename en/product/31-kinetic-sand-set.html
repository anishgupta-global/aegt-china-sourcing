Description

Magic sand is a mouldable play sand made of fine quartz sand with a binder: it can be moulded like wet sand but stays dry, does not stick to the hands and is reusable.

More

The maker's listing states silica sand, non-drying, age 3+, pack sizes from 100 g through 500 g and 1 kg to 25 kg bulk, and Pantone colours of choice. Buyers look for even grain size, low dust and oil film, colour fastness, test reports for heavy metals (EN 71-3) and a documented binder formulation (silicone oil). As a private label the sand is combined with a tray and moulds, without licensed motifs; the Kinetic Sand brand (Spin Master) must neither be named nor imitated.

Request from the supplier

  • Request EN 71-1/-2/-3 test reports from an accredited lab for the exact formulation and batch; the certificate entries in the listing (EN71, ASTM, CE, TRA, USP) are maker statements only.
  • Formulation and safety data sheet: binder (silicone oil), content of siloxanes D4/D5/D6, asbestos/quartz freedom of the raw sand, fine-dust behaviour.
  • Colours and scent: colour fastness, no staining of textiles, allergen labelling if fragrances are included.
  • Age grading 3+ and warnings in German (moulds with small-parts notice); file GPSR data and the EU responsible person.
  • Sample with a 1 kg bag including packaging: check tightness, weight, dust and oil film on the inside of the packaging.
  • Factory visit in Yiwu: the profile gives an office building (Wealth Building) as address; confirm production site and raw-sand origin during the appointment.
  • Brand name: keep 'Kinetic Sand' (Spin Master registered trademark) out of product title, packaging and listing; call it magic sand / play sand neutrally.
