Description

The forestry combination unites safety helmet, mesh visor and ear defenders into a matched set for chainsaw, brush cutter and timber harvesting.

More

The helmet protects against falling branches, the stainless-steel mesh visor keeps chips and splinters away from the face while staying see-through, and the capsules attenuate machine noise. Expected are an adjustable six-point suspension with a wide head-size range (reference: 52–62 cm), ventilation openings and a signal colour such as orange. Because visor and capsules must fit the helmet mechanically, the combination should be tested as a set; helmet and visor are Category II PPE, the hearing protection is Category III, and EU type-examination is mandatory.

Request from the supplier

  • EU type-examination certificates from a notified body for helmet (EN 397), mesh visor (EN 1731) and capsules (EN 352-3), each for the exact model and the chosen combination; verify in NANDO.
  • Evidence that the capsules are tested for exactly this helmet (EN 352-3 names the helmet types) and that the visor uses the helmet mount without play.
  • Hearing protection: SNR/H-M-L values, instructions and production monitoring (Cat. III, Module C2 or D).
  • Test head-size range, weight and centre of gravity on the sample; define chin strap and sweatband in the scope of delivery.
  • Marking on helmet, visor and capsule (CE, standard, date of manufacture) and German instructions with service-life and replacement notice.
