• GPSR (EU) 2023/988: an EU responsible person, name/address on the product or packaging …
More

and German instructions and warnings are mandatory for all consumer products sold online. • Stationary training equipment (treadmill/walking pad, dumbbell systems, pull-up bars): demand written proof of EN ISO 20957 conformity (Part 1 general, Part 6 treadmills; replaces the old EN 957 series) via test report; suppliers sometimes still cite EN 957. Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 applies from 20 Jan 2027, so clarify applicability to motorised fitness equipment before ordering. • Electrical devices (walking pad, massage gun): CE under LVD 2014/35/EU and EMC 2014/30/EU, RoHS 2011/65/EU; with Bluetooth/app also RED 2014/53/EU; ElektroG registration (stiftung ear) and VerpackG/LUCID. • Lithium batteries (massage gun): Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 and BattG, UN 38.3 for air/sea freight. In 2024 Stiftung Warentest found pollutants (including naphthalene in plastic) and breakage in massage guns: require a PAH test report (GS/AfPS level) up front. • Mats, grips, bands, rollers: REACH Annex XVII (including PAHs, phthalates for skin contact), textile labelling (EU) 1007/2011 for fabric bands. • Padel/pickleball: no CE mark for rackets, but GPSR applies; use claims such as FIP-approved or USA Pickleball-approved only with proof. • Sourcing: power figures such as 2.75 HP in listings are usually peak power; get continuous power, load rating (user weight) and noise in writing; plan for a high return rate on electrical devices.
