Request from the supplier

  • Request an EU Stage V engine variant (Reg. (EU) 2016/1628) with engine type approval; the listing states no emission stage.
  • CE declaration of conformity, technical file, load chart/load moment limiter and German operating manual under 2006/42/EC, from 20 Jan 2027 (EU) 2023/1230.
  • Clarify road registration in Germany (EU type approval or individual approval) incl. lighting, top speed and vehicle papers.
  • Get spare parts, service and warranty in DE/EU confirmed in writing (dealer, lead times, parts list).
  • Clarify transport dimensions and weight (approx. 11.8 t), loading method (container/flat rack/RoRo) and Incoterm; inspect before shipment.
