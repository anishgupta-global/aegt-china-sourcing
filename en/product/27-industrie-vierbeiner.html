Description

The industrial quadruped carries out inspection rounds in buildings at risk of collapse, in contaminated areas and in plant rooms where staff cannot work, or only with high protection effort.

More

With LiDAR, depth cameras and optional thermal imaging or gas sensor it documents condition and progress and overcomes stairs and obstacles where wheeled vehicles fail. Professional users in Germany expect IP66/IP67 protection, an emergency stop, a risk assessment under machinery law, swappable batteries and secure radio and cloud connectivity. For use in asbestos areas, decontaminability of the surfaces under TRGS 519 must be proven.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC or, from 20.01.2027, the Machinery Regulation, plus RED and EMC, naming the manufacturer.
  • Risk assessment under EN ISO 12100 and proof of the emergency stop under EN ISO 13850 and of the safety control under EN ISO 13849-1.
  • Independent laboratory test report for IP66/IP67 and a statement on decontaminability of the surfaces under TRGS 519.
  • Description of radio and cloud connections, offline operation and firmware update commitment (cybersecurity, GDPR).
  • Battery test report (UN 38.3 / IEC 62133-2) and proof of compliance with Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542.
  • German-language manual, training, warranty and spare-parts supply (legs, joint motors, batteries) in the EU.
