Description

The 8 oz cup is the standard for espresso, tea and canteen coffee on site and in the office.

More

A professional cup withstands a 90 °C drink without the seam softening, is neutral in odour and taste and is cleanly printed on the outside. As board with a PE inner coating it falls under the single-use plastics rules in Germany: marking under Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/2151 and payments into the single-use plastics fund. Food contact under Regulation (EC) 1935/2004 and, for the PE layer, Regulation (EU) No 10/2011 must be documented.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Declaration of conformity and test reports for board and PE layer (Regulation (EC) 1935/2004, Regulation (EU) No 10/2011, overall and specific migration).
  • Test report on the outer printing inks, including set-off into the drink space.
  • Proof of leak-tightness and hot filling (e.g. water at 90 °C, 30 minutes, seam and base) as a lab report or factory test with photos.
  • Marking per Implementing Regulation (EU) 2020/2151 on the cup as a sample; plastic content in grams per cup for the single-use plastics fund registration.
  • Check the FSC licence number and chain-of-custody certificate.
  • Have the manufacturer status (factory or trader) and hygiene certificate (BRCGS Packaging or ISO 22000) confirmed.
