Description

The lanyard carries an ID, access card or keys visibly on the body and makes employees and subcontractors instantly recognisable on site.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a soft, colour-fast strap, a cleanly hemmed edge and a safety release at the neck that opens if it snags on scaffolding or machinery. Metal parts must be low in nickel and free of lead and cadmium, the textile free of banned azo dyes. As importer, AE Global Traders needs the REACH evidence and the economic operator details required under the EU General Product Safety Regulation.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • REACH/SVHC test report for metal parts (lead, cadmium, nickel release per EN 1811) and textile (azo dyes)
  • Oeko-Tex certificate or an equivalent textile test report from an accredited lab
  • Colour-binding sample (Pantone) with print approval before series start; prove colour fastness to EN ISO 105-X12
  • Function test of the breakaway release (opening, re-closing) on several samples
  • GPSR details on the packaging: manufacturer, EU responsible person or importer, batch code
  • Check that carabiner and clip do not copy protected designs or trademarks of third parties
