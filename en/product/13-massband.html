Good add-on to laser and levelling instruments.

Request from the supplier

  • Have the accuracy class (EC II) evidenced with a test report from an accredited body for exactly this length and width.
  • Check samples against a calibration standard: blade deviation, hook play, recoil and lock after 500 cycles.
  • Confirm legibility of the scale (mm/cm, digit height) and abrasion resistance of the print; German labelling/marking.
  • Fix blade width, case material and single-unit packaging for sale in Germany (GPSR details, VerpackG).
  • Ask for the production site of the tapes (listing: company address Pudong, Shanghai).
