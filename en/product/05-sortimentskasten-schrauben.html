Description

The assortment combines 420 chipboard screws with countersunk head and Torx drive in diameters from 3.0 to 5.0 mm in a plastic storage box with compartments; four matching bits are included.

More

The yellow-zinc finish and Torx drive stand for secure engagement, low cam-out and an even seat in chipboard, MDF and wood-based panels. The box replaces the single pack for fitters and workshops that need several lengths to hand, and can be resold through refill packs of the single sizes. Expected are dimensionally true threads, a hexavalent-chromium-free passivation, a reliable count per compartment and packaging with German labelling.

(330-pc EUR 8.45, 1145-pc EUR 13.95). The box is the classic upsell from the single pack with high margin, and the single sizes can be sold as refill packs. Key sourcing warning: per TARIC, screws from China carry an anti-dumping duty of 86.5% (only listed manufacturers with their own certificate pay 22.1 to 48.8%) on top of 3.7% duty; at a market price of EUR 6.99 for 420 screws plus dowels a direct import without a listed manufacturer does not pay. Check EU purchasing or kitting with non-AD origin in advance.

Request from the supplier

  • Clarify anti-dumping first: name the screw manufacturer and check against the TARIC list (individual rates 22.1 to 48.8%, otherwise 86.5%) or choose a non-AD origin
  • Fix the bill of contents per compartment in writing (sizes 3.0 to 5.0 mm, lengths, pieces per compartment) with count tolerance and counting control
  • Test report on thread, hardness and zinc layer (salt-spray test), proof of Cr(VI)-free yellow passivation and cadmium freedom
  • Check Torx profile and bits (ISO 10664), driving test in chipboard of samples against Parkside and Dresselhaus
  • Box with German labelling, EAN, EU responsible person (GPSR), LUCID registration; closure and drop test (compartments must not open in transit)
