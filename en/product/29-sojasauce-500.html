Description

Light soy sauce (shengchou) is the salty, savoury standard sauce of Chinese cooking for seasoning, marinating and dipping, while dark sauce mainly adds colour.

More

Quality markers are genuine brewing from soybeans (sun fermentation of over 120 days per the manufacturer profile), a clear light-brown colour, balanced saltiness with umami and as few additives as possible. Kwangna in Zhongshan (Guangdong) supplies the Smiki Light Soy Sauce 500 ml (model KNSA1-500ML) in cartons of 12 and 24 with BRC, HACCP, ISO 22000, Kosher, Halal and FDA statements; private label (OEM) and glass bottle are possible. For Germany, allergen labelling (soy, wheat), the 3-MCPD limits and a German label are decisive.

More

Joybuy quotes about 2.59 EUR for 500 ml in the official Lee Kum Kee store (search hit). Steady demand with a price band of 2.59–4.79 EUR for branded goods: private labels need price or added value (organic, gluten-free, glass, origin). Per Lebensmittel Zeitung (14 Feb 2025) Lee Kum Kee wants to win market share from German Asian-sauce brands. Weight (about 0.6 kg per bottle per manufacturer data), glass breakage and leaks drive shipping and return costs.

Request from the supplier

  • Written recipe: wheat/gluten, preservatives (e.g. benzoate), colour (caramel), salt content; brewed product only without flavourings of animal origin; vegan evidence if claimed.
  • 3-MCPD and glycidol laboratory report per batch against Reg. (EU) 2023/915; also heavy metals, mycotoxins and pesticides.
  • Bottle and closure: decide glass or PET, seal, leak test and drop test in the 12-bottle carton; compliance under Reg. (EC) 1935/2004.
  • Verify certificates (BRC, ISO 22000) and the TÜV audit report; require proof of manufacturing (production site, licence), as Kwangna describes itself as a trading company with its own factories.
  • Samples for colour, salt content and aroma; German label with allergens and best-before date; agree a remaining shelf life of at least 18 months on arrival.
  • Confirm price tiers and Incoterm (profile: FOB, CFR, CIF) in writing; clarify costs for the OEM label and label deposit.
