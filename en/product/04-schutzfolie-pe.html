Description

The 0.2 mm protective film lines floors and walls of the work area, seals openings and penetrations and protects surfaces from dust and fibres.

More

Quality product is a tear-resistant PE film of even thickness without holes or gels, folded on the roll so it can be spread quickly to a width of 4 m. Professional users in Germany expect a verified thickness tolerance, clean winding and good bonding with masking tape so the film can be disposed of contamination-free together with the waste during dismantling. REACH conformity and a material data sheet belong to the documentation.
