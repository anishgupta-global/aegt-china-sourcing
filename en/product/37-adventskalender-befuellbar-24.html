Description

The Advent calendar consists of 24 numbered cotton drawstring bags that are filled with sweets, small gifts or vouchers.

More

The bags are reusable and can be hung as wall decoration or on a string; the size can be customised with the manufacturer. Buyers look for clean seams, colour-fast material, clearly legible numbers and a clear accessory package of stickers, clips and string. Compliance covers GPSR labelling, REACH-compliant textile dyes and clarifying whether food may be filled in directly.

Request from the supplier

  • Clarify the unit: 1 piece = 1 set of 24 bags? Have the price per set confirmed in writing in the quote; fix the accessories (number stickers 1-24, clips, string).
  • Define the bag size (DE reference: approx. 9.5 × 13 cm) and check seams, drawstring and lining on a sample.
  • Request colour-fastness and an azo dye/formaldehyde test report (REACH Annex XVII no. 43); label the fibre composition.
  • Regulate food proximity: inner lining or note 'wrap contents individually'; if promoted for food, require an LFGB/1935 declaration.
  • As a children's item: label cord and small parts (clips, stickers), define the age warning.
  • Have the production site named and request audit evidence: the company profile lists only 95.2 m² plant area, and 'Audited Supplier' status is not proof of a factory.
