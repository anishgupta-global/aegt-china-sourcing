Description

A paving stone laying machine places paving and concrete stones layer by layer into the prepared grit bed in one pass and raises area output considerably compared with manual laying …

More

it pays off on large yard, road and square areas such as those created after demolition and new site development. Professional users in Germany expect a torsion-resistant frame, adjustable laying and joint width, reliable controls and complete guarding of crushing and shearing points. Market references for mechanical laying in Germany are vacuum laying attachments, for example from Optimas and Probst, as well as crawler machines such as the Tiger Stone. The machine must be delivered with CE marking, a risk assessment, a declaration of conformity under the Machinery Directive and a German operating manual; as the importer, AE Global Traders legally becomes the manufacturer.
