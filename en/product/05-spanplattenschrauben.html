Description

Chipboard screws join wood-based panels, battens and drywall substructures quickly and without pre-drilling, for example when rebuilding after a strip-out.

More

Good goods have a case-hardened steel core, a cleanly rolled thread, milling ribs under the countersunk head and a Torx recess that does not cam out even at high torque. A professional user in Germany expects dimensional accuracy to DIN 7505, a Cr(VI)-free zinc coating and, for structural use, CE marking with an ETA. Corrosion protection must be proven by a salt-spray test and every batch must be traceable.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Mill certificate EN 10204 3.1 for the raw material incl. surface and core hardness
  • Salt-spray test report to EN ISO 9227 and zinc coating thickness (EN ISO 4042)
  • Proof that the yellow passivation is Cr(VI)-free (REACH)
  • For structural use: ETA with declaration of performance and CE marking, otherwise a written statement for non-structural use only
  • Dimensional check (diameter, length, Torx recess) and torsion/drive-out torque on samples per batch, batch traceability
  • Origin and customs papers: clarify anti-dumping duty for steel screws from China beforehand via a customs tariff ruling
