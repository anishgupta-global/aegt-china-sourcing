Description

The concrete pulverizer breaks reinforced concrete quietly and with little vibration and exposes the rebar so that concrete and steel can be separated by type.

More

This is an advantage in city centres, for noise-sensitive deconstruction and for subsequent recycling. Professional users in Germany expect a high-strength steel frame, replaceable crushing jaws and cutting blades and a hydraulic 360-degree rotation unit. Imported units must be delivered with CE conformity under the Machinery Directive, a proper fit to the carrier and a German operating manual.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • CE declaration of conformity with serial number, risk assessment and technical file under 2006/42/EC.
  • Data sheet with actual own weight, jaw opening, crushing force and oil flow/pressure; confirm the marketplace listing details (weight class) in writing.
  • Fit to the carrier: mounting plate, pin size, hoses and rotary manifold included in the scope of delivery or quoted separately.
  • Material certificate (EN 10204, type 3.1) for frame, jaws and blades; hardness data.
  • German operating manual, spare jaws, spare blades and seal kits; at least 12 months of warranty.
  • Test-run video showing crushing and rotation movement before delivery.
