Description

The extra-thin disc cuts steel sections, pipes, threaded rods and stainless sheet quickly and with little material loss on a strip-out site, even in continuous angle-grinder use.

More

For stainless steel the bond must be low in iron, chlorine and sulphur (INOX) so that no contamination rust forms on the workpiece. A professional user in Germany expects a disc to EN 12413 with the oSa mark, clearly legible maximum-speed marking and glass-fibre reinforcement against fragmentation. Without a test report per batch the goods do not go onto a AE Global Traders site.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 12413 (spin/burst speed test) from an accredited body, for the specific batch and dimension
  • Manufacturer's oSa certificate with registration number and validity date, verify authenticity
  • Fe/Cl/S analysis report (each at most 0.1%) as INOX release for stainless steel
  • Print on every disc: manufacturer, dimension, 12,200 rpm / 80 m/s, production and expiry date, German safety instructions
  • Samples before the series order (at least 1 carton): cutting test, measure thickness tolerance and run-out
