Description

The WC seat is made of duroplast (urea resin), a hard, scratch-resistant surface that is easy to clean and tolerates common household cleaners.

More

The soft-close mechanism lets lid and seat close quietly, and the quick-release hinge removes the seat at the push of a button so that ceramic and hinges can be cleaned thoroughly. Discerning buyers look for an adjustable hole spacing, a stated load rating, clean gap dimensions without voids and dampers that stay gentle after many closing cycles. For EU sales, dimensional data (length, width, hole spacing), GPSR labelling with an EU responsible person and a REACH-compliant, low-formaldehyde resin are mandatory.

Evidence (as of 2 Nov 2026): Hornbach lists soft-close WC seats from EUR 19.95 (form&style Thun) through EUR 24.95 (SAMAR, duroplast, quick release) and EUR 29.95 (Ronde) up to EUR 49.95 …

More

(REIKA MINO); according to the scan stage, no-name seats on eBay sell at EUR 19.99 to 24.90, the branded Keramag Premiano at EUR 89.90, and toom lists a duroplast seat from the Chinese manufacturer Jiangsu Innovate Sanitary Ware. The price point is transparent and highly contested (e.g. Songmics offers on mydealz per the scan). Opportunity: small, light, shipping-friendly cross-sell to the WC (renovation, new build, hotel rooms) with a wide gap between ex-works and shelf price. Risk: fit (O, D, V shape, hole spacing) is the main cause of returns, hence a dimension drawing and shape finder in the shop; freight, duty (6.5%), testing costs and returns are not yet included in that gap.

Request from the supplier

  • Request a dimension sheet with length, width, hole-spacing range and a fit table (O, D, V bowls); as a benchmark toom states 90 to 155 mm hole spacing and 150 kg load rating.
  • Agree a sample with endurance testing: damper cycle count, load test, hinge strength (no harmonised EN standard for WC seats is known, so fix test values contractually).
  • Request a formaldehyde-release test report or REACH declaration for the UF resin and a declaration of conformity; advertise antibacterial treatment only with an approved biocidal active substance.
  • Clarify GPSR data: EU responsible person, type/batch number and warnings on product or packaging; provide packaging data for LUCID.
  • Factory audit and capacity proof: Aobo is a manufacturer and trading company with 25 employees per its profile; verify the stated capacity (600,000 urea seats, period not stated in the listing).
  • Order a first sample batch with own logo and carton and run incoming inspection (gap dimensions, colour, damper) before the series order.
