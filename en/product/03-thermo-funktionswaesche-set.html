Description

Thermal base layers form the bottom layer in the layering principle: they retain body heat and move moisture away from the skin.

More

Professional users in construction, landscaping and logistics expect a skin-friendly material with brushed fleece lining that keeps its shape over many washes and does not pill. For the EU market, correct fibre labelling under (EU) 1007/2011, a REACH-compliant dye proof and ideally an Oeko-Tex Standard 100 certificate are an obligation or a purchase argument. The set of long-sleeve top and long johns raises basket value alongside the winter jacket and is repurchased seasonally from October to February.

Request from the supplier

  • Have fibre composition and fabric weight confirmed with a lab report (listing only states spandex/polyester)
  • Obtain an Oeko-Tex Standard 100 certificate or REACH test report (azo, formaldehyde) with a valid number
  • Confirm set composition (shirt + long johns) and price per set in writing; EU size chart and measurement tolerances
  • Require a pilling test (ISO 12945-2) and dimensional change after washing (ISO 6330/ISO 5077) on the fleece fabric
  • Clarify labels: fibre labelling in German, care symbols, manufacturer details and EU responsible person on the packaging
  • Check pre-production samples in two sizes (seam position, cuffs, stretch) before the first order
