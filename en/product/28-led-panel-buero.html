Description

The backlit LED panel in 595 × 595 mm (usually sold as 62 × 62 cm) replaces fluorescent grid luminaires in grid ceilings.

More

An LED board at the back emits through a light-guide plate and diffuser; the aluminium frame is slim (25–30 mm). For office workplaces EN 12464-1 requires glare limitation of UGR ≤ 19, which backlit panels with a prismatic diffuser are meant to meet; request a test report as proof. Professional buyers look at luminous flux and efficacy, colour rendering (Ra > 80), a flicker-free driver with a mark of conformity, lifetime, and CE, RoHS and ecodesign/energy-label data.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Hornbach (marketplace) lists 62 × 62 cm panels with UGR < 19 between EUR 27.95 (Enovatek, 40 W, 4,400 lm, 4,000 K) and EUR 34.99 (Enovalite, 36 W, 6,000 K); a 4-pack costs EUR 129.99 (about EUR 32.50 each).

More

Buyers are office and commercial operators, electricians and facility managers; deciding factors are UGR < 19, colour temperature, driver quality, energy class and lead time. Competition: many marketplace brands, prices mostly below EUR 35 each; differentiation only through evidenced quality (test reports, known driver maker, warranty). Return risk: dimension deviation to the ceiling grid, colour temperature and brightness, driver failures. Important: electrical conformity (LVD/EMC/RoHS, WEEE registration) lies with the importer.

Request from the supplier

  • Request photometric test reports from an accredited lab: luminous flux, efficacy, UGR, Ra, flicker, lifetime.
  • Specify the driver: maker and type, insulation (SELV/protection class), power factor, harmonics and mark of conformity; exclude 'non-isolated' drivers as on some standard panels.
  • Require conformity documents: LVD/EMC test reports, RoHS report, EU declaration of conformity, data for EPREL and energy efficiency class.
  • Confirm the fit for a 625 mm grid (595 mm); clarify surface frame, suspension and connection cable with plug.
  • Marking: rating plate with manufacturer, type and EU responsible person, WEEE symbol; packaging registration (VerpackG) and WEEE registration by the importer.
  • Pre-production sample, burn-in test and AQL inspection before shipment; fix the warranty (3–5 years) in the contract.
