Description

Loose jasmine green tea for site tea kitchens, canteens and repacking under a private label.

More

A uniform, low-dust leaf with a fresh jasmine aroma and sealed, food-grade packaging is expected. A professional buyer in Germany demands documented residue levels under Regulation (EC) 396/2005, batch traceability and German labelling compliant with the FIC Regulation (LMIV). As a food product it carries no CE mark; the importer bears responsibility as the food business operator.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Valid manufacturer certificate to ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, BRCGS or IFS (check certification body and validity).
  • Independent lab report per batch on pesticide residues under Reg. (EC) 396/2005, heavy metals and pyrrolizidine alkaloids.
  • Batch traceability (lot no., harvest date, source garden) and a specification sheet per delivery.
  • Declaration of compliance of the packaging for food contact under Reg. (EC) 1935/2004.
  • Sample with tasting and a German label draft under the FIC Regulation before series release.
  • Certificate of origin and packing list; importer registered with the food control authority as food business operator.
