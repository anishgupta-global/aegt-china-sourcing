Request from the supplier

  • Battery: request cell maker, protection circuit, UN 38.3 report and EN 62133-2 certificate; re-measure the capacity.
  • Lumen and runtime: require a measurement protocol (integrating sphere) for 250 lm plus runtime at 100% and 50% brightness.
  • Ingress protection: the listing only says 'water-resistant'; request the exact IP class with test report; check the hanging point and drop test on the sample.
  • Low Voltage Directive (LVD) does not apply at 5 V (limit 50 V AC / 75 V DC); have safety evidenced via GPSR, EN 62471 (photobiological safety) and EN 62133-2; clarify whether a USB-C cable is included.
  • Prepare registrations: EU responsible person, ElektroG WEEE number, battery registration, LUCID; check replaceable battery (Art. 11 Regulation 2023/1542, from 18 Feb 2027).
