Description

The kit bundles six licence-free PMR446 handheld radios with IP67 protection (waterproof to 1 m, 30 minutes) with a 6-bay desktop charger into a ready-to-use fleet solution.

More

Each radio offers 16 channels with 121 privacy codes each (1,936 combinations), 0.5 W transmit power and a 1,200 mAh Li-ion battery; the station charges all six radios at once. For construction sites, events, clubs and hotels, the kit replaces single purchases and charging-cable chaos. Professional buyers look for the EU variant (PMR446 instead of FRS), the RED declaration of conformity, documented IP67 tests and a charging dock that fits the radio.

Return risk: confusion of PMR446 vs FRS, exaggerated range claims and submersion expectations. Season (assessment): Q4 (Black Friday, Christmas, ski season) and spring to autumn (construction, camping, festivals).

Request from the supplier

  • Order the EU version: the RT648 page lists PMR446 and FRS as two versions; the single pack has SKU A9155B, the 6-set A9060B-C9059H. Have it confirmed in writing that the 6-set is the PMR446 version (446.0-446.2 MHz, 0.5 W, fixed antenna).
  • Request the RED declaration of conformity and test reports (EN 300 296, EN 301 489, EN 62368-1); check the German manual and GPSR data (EU responsible person).
  • Require the IP67 report (EN 60529) and a sample immersion test (1 m, 30 minutes) including battery compartment and USB/charging contacts.
  • Charging station C9059H/RTC48 and battery: LVD/EMC/ecodesign evidence, UN 38.3 report, marking under the Batteries Regulation.
  • Ask for dealer price and MOQ instead of the web-shop list price (US$253.99) ('Become a Dealer'), including an OEM/private-label option.
  • Budget accessories: headsets (check the socket type on a sample), spare batteries, spare clips; clarify warranty and RMA process for fleets.
