Description

The fairy lights with warm-white LEDs run from a plug-in power supply and offer eight lighting modes from steady light to wave and chasing effects, switched at the press of a button.

More

The base model JMK-STR01 from Shenzhen JMK is a 10 m string with 100 LEDs for 110-220 V, available in IP54 and IP66; the wanted version with 26 m, 360 LEDs, timer and IP44 must be specified as a special version. In the German market a timer with 6 hours on and 18 hours off with memory function is usual. Discerning buyers look for a power supply with EU plug and test report, proof to EN 60598-2-20, sealed connectors, a flexible cold-resistant cable and an even light pattern without failure of single segments.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix and quote the target version: 26 m, 360 LEDs (about 14 LEDs per metre), copper or PVC cable, lead-in cable, timer 6 h on/18 h off with memory, 8 modes, IP44; the listing applies to 10 m with 100 LEDs and contains neither timer nor power-supply details.
  • Name a power supply with EU plug (230 V) and request test reports: LVD (EN 60598-2-20, EN 61558 or EN 62368-1), EMC (EN IEC 55015), Ecodesign (EU) 2019/1782; the named EMC certificate dates from 2015, ask for current reports for the delivered version.
  • Prove IP44 and outdoor use: test report EN 60529 for chain, connectors and controller, cold test at -15 °C, strain relief on the cable, behaviour in permanent outdoor operation.
  • Check series samples: brightness and colour temperature over the full length, failure behaviour (does the chain keep running with one defective LED), solder joints, flicker in the 8 modes, heating of the power supply.
  • Season planning: listing lead time 7-15 days plus freight; order for Q4 2027 in spring/summer 2027 at the latest and take the return rate of the previous season into account.
  • Clarify commercials: MOQ 100 pcs, price for the target version, OEM packaging with German manual and GPSR details, registration with stiftung ear and LUCID, sample cost.
