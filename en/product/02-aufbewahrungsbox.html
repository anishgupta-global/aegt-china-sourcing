Description

The hard-shell EVA case holds a reusable half mask together with its filters and keeps it clean, dust-free and protected from knocks between uses.

More

The heat-pressed EVA shell with water-repellent nylon cover, terry lining and die-cut foam insert protects the sealing lip and valves from deformation and dirt; the nylon zipper and webbing handle make it a robust companion in a tool case or van. Professional buyers expect exact inner dimensions, clean stitching, low-odour materials and discreet logo embossing for private label or company marking. Important for classification: the case is not a respiratory protection product and carries no PPE CE mark; because the zipper does not close airtight, it is no substitute for a sealed box for long-term storage of gas filters.

More

Moldex 7995 is 6.54 EUR net at Strenge; according to search snippets other shops sell the Moldex box at roughly 6-9 EUR and the Sundstrom SR 230 at roughly 22-28 EUR. Hoffmann (HF-3021), the Sundstrom BASIC PACK and the COLAD set already ship with a box, and one shop quotes a 9.95 EUR box surcharge in a set according to a snippet. As a basket add-on next to half mask and filters the case is high-margin and shipping-friendly (assessment: low return risk, no PPE approval effort). The risk lies in fit and expectation: buyers of the Moldex/COLAD boxes expect an airtight hard box, a zipper case will be seen as a different product and should be positioned as a transport and dust-protection case. Request from the supplier

  • Check fit on your own model with a sample: half mask (e.g. Safetree PFFM750P) with filters mounted; fix inner size and die-cut shape in writing. The external size of 24 × 11.5 × 10 cm is small, a full-face mask will not fit (special shape needed).
  • Clarify airtightness: a zipper case is not airtight. If stored gas filters are to be protected as well, request an alternative (PP box with sealing lip, own tooling, higher minimum quantity).
  • Require material evidence per batch: REACH/SVHC declaration and a test report (PAHs, phthalates, azo dyes where relevant) from an accredited lab; assess odour/outgassing of EVA and PU on the sample.
  • GPSR documents: type/batch marking, manufacturer details, name and address of the EU responsible person on product or packaging; polybag with suffocation warning.
  • Private label and terms: logo embossing (vector file), minimum quantity 1,000 pcs, tooling cost for a special shape, lead time (per profile 15 working days to 1 month), payment terms and Incoterm (FOB/CIF/EXW) fixed in the contract.
  • Vet the supplier: request business licence and ISO 9001 certificate, factory visit or third-party audit, sample quality of seams, zipper and foam density before releasing series production.
