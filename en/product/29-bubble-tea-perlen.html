Description

Boba pearls made from tapioca starch (cassava) are coloured and flavoured with brown sugar or caramel and, after short cooking, served in brown-sugar syrup in bubble tea, milk tea or desserts.

More

Buyers expect uniform pearl size, a soft core without a hard spot, no disintegration when cooked, a short cooking time and an honest ingredient list without milk components (vegan). The manufacturer in focus is Guangzhou Haoen Fengshi, per its own website and exhibitor profile one of China's large bubble-tea ingredient makers with its own pearl processing base in Guangzhou and FSSC 22000, ISO 22000, HACCP, FDA and Halal. A per-kilo price for dry black tapioca pearls comes from the trader Guangzhou Zio Chemical.

E-commerce potential

Asiafoodland lists black 5-minute boba pearls (black sugar) 250 g for 6.29 EUR (25.16 EUR/kg) and instant bubble tea pearls 250 g for 6.49 EUR (25.96 EUR/kg); white tapioca pearls 180 g cost 2.89 EUR.

More

Per the market scan Asian Food Lovers has bubble tea as its own category with a party kit and instant boba (brand O's bubble); per Rheinpfalz (paywalled, only teaser seen) the trend returned via TikTok and Instagram. Season mainly spring/summer. Risks: complaints about pearls that are too hard or disintegrate, wrong preparation, choking hazard for small children (print a warning); plastic straws in sets are banned under Directive (EU) 2019/904.

Request from the supplier

  • Recipe in writing: tapioca share, sugar or caramel share, additives (caramel colour, preservatives), no milk components; vegan evidence.
  • Test cooking time (5 minutes), pearl size, cooking stability and disintegration rate on a sample; print German cooking instructions.
  • Approach Haoen directly: model number, 250 g bag, MOQ, FOB Guangzhou price; the Zio listing is only a price reference, Zio and Royal Queen are traders.
  • Batch laboratory report: pesticides, heavy metals, mycotoxins, Salmonella, yeasts and moulds; check FSSC 22000/ISO 22000 certificates with scope.
  • Print a choking-hazard note and age warning; no plastic straws in sets (Directive (EU) 2019/904).
  • Agree a remaining shelf life of at least 18 months on arrival; sea freight dry and cool, bags with seal and batch code.
