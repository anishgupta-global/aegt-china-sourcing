Description

The spade with a flat, rectangular steel blade is the tool for trench excavation, cable and pipe trenches, foundation edges and cutting turf and soil.

More

The hardened steel blade has a sharply ground edge and is joined to the wooden handle through a closed socket; a D-grip gives secure guidance and lets the user press down firmly with the foot. Professional users in Germany expect a torsion-resistant socket with tread edges, a smooth splinter-free ash handle and a coating that withstands soil and moisture. Imported goods must comply with the EU General Product Safety Regulation 2023/988 and REACH, and wooden handles also with the EU Deforestation Regulation.

Request from the supplier

  • Hardness test report of the blade to ISO 6508-1 (agree target hardness) and material declaration (e.g. 50Mn)
  • Pull-out and bending test of socket/handle plus a cross-section through the socket joint on the sample
  • Approve blade size and handle length as an OEM drawing to German specification (D-grip, tread edges)
  • REACH/PAH test report for lacquer and handle; wood origin (FSC/PEFC) and due-diligence statement under the EU Deforestation Regulation
  • GPSR details: EU responsible person with address, batch number, German instructions
  • GS certificate if required, and approval sample before series production
