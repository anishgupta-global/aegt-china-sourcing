Description

The step ladder replaces narrow rungs with wide, slip-resistant treads (target tread depth 20 cm) and thus allows low-fatigue work during installation, painting and assembly indoors.

More

A handrail, spreader lock and slip-resistant feet are the minimum equipment, and the frame must be built without play and with clean joints. German commercial customers expect a test to EN 131, a GS mark, German-language user information and legible marking of the permissible load. The Zhejiang Leader reference listing gives no tread depth, contradictorily names steel and aluminium as material and states EN 131 only as a claim; the supplier must confirm the 200 mm tread depth and the material in writing before ordering.
