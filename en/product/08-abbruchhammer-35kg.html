Description

The 35 kg demolition hammer is the heavy class for foundations, reinforced concrete slabs, industrial floors and machine plinths when 15 kg units no longer deliver enough impact energy.

More

The 2,000 W motor and a powerful hammer mechanism are built for continuous operation in strip-out, and the high own weight lets the tool do the work instead of straining the user. Professional users in Germany expect effective vibration damping, a declared vibration value with the permissible daily exposure under 2002/44/EC, a transport trolley and readily available hex chisels. Compliance means CE under the Machinery Directive, sound power level under 2000/14/EC with a notified body, a test report under EN 62841-2-6 and a German operating manual.

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC with a test report under EN 62841-2-6 from an accredited laboratory (TÜV, SGS, Intertek) for the specific model
  • Sound power level LWA under 2000/14/EC with conformity assessment by a notified body (state the number) and marking on the rating plate
  • Datasheet with actual weight, single impact energy and tool holder (28 or 30 mm hex); vibration value under EN ISO 28927-10 with daily exposure under 2002/44/EC
  • German operating manual and spare parts list (carbon brushes, hammer mechanism, handles, trolley) as well as hex chisels in the scope of supply or as follow-up delivery
  • Sampling with endurance test, written warranty of at least 24 months, Incoterm and HS code in the quotation; confirm the 230 V EU version
