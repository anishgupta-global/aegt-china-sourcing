More

Hornbach lists 140 l mixers from EUR 229 (Pattfield, kit) to EUR 349-399 (MIRAL) and EUR 269 (Altrad Lescha 135 l); spare parts (ring gear, drum, motor) are a buying argument.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the CE declaration with test report (2006/42/EC, EN 12151, LVD, EMC) and an LWA measurement report under 2000/14/EC; clarify the ISO 9001 claim (listing says both 2000 and 2015) by certificate
  • Motor and switch: 230 V/50 Hz, protection class, thermal protection, mains cable with EU plug; guarding of ring gear and belt/gearbox
  • Check drum sheet, welds, ring gear (cast/steel) and mixing blades; test tipping and standing stability on a sample
  • Demand a spare-parts list with prices (ring gear, drum, motor, wheels) for the German trade
  • Bulky-goods packaging (94 x 67 x 94 cm, 80 kg), German assembly manual, GPSR labelling and ElektroG applicability
