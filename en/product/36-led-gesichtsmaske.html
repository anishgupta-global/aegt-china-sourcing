Description

The LED face mask is made of medical-grade liquid silicone and carries, per the listing, 240 LEDs on the face mask plus another 240 on a neck mask.

More

Four wavelengths (460, 580, 630 and 850 nm) cover blue, yellow, red and near-infrared light. The flexible design comes in sizes S, M and L and charges via USB (5 V/1 A). Buyers expect even illumination, a timer, eye protection and reliable safety documentation. Claims stay cosmetic (skin appearance, care); medical efficacy promises should be avoided in the EU because of the MDR boundary.

The EUR 57-349 band leaves room for a mid-range private label. Risks: efficacy claims (MDR borderline), skin tolerance, eye protection, many no-name offers.

Request from the supplier

  • Request photobiological testing per EN 62471 and a safety report (LVD/EMC) for exactly this model; ISO 13485 and FDA statements in the listing do not replace EU conformity.
  • Clarify irradiance (mW/cm2) per wavelength, timer (auto switch-off) and eye protection/goggles.
  • Name the electronics maker: Kingod is a silicone specialist; have PCB/LED supplier and battery cells evidenced with test reports (IEC 62133-2, UN 38.3).
  • Skin tolerance: REACH/SVHC, silicone migration test, allergen test for skin-contact material.
  • Have advertising claims cleared by legal counsel (cosmetic only, no therapeutic promises); German manual incl. contraindications (epilepsy, photosensitivity, pregnancy).
  • Negotiate tiers: the listing shows USD 35-40, MOQ 1 pc; ask for price at 500/1,000 pcs and sample lead time.
