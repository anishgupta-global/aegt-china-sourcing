Description

Logo napkins are AE Global Traders's most visible catering promotional item: in the canteen, at topping-out ceremonies and when hosting clients.

More

A high-quality napkin is soft, wet-strength and does not shed lint; the logo must be saliva-proof and must not rub off. Professional users in Germany expect virgin-fibre pulp from certified forestry (FSC) and printing inks approved for food contact. Regulation (EC) 1935/2004, the GMP Regulation (EC) 2023/2006 and BfR Recommendation XXXVI apply; the declaration of conformity must explicitly cover the printing inks.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Declaration of conformity under Regulation (EC) 1935/2004 and GMP declaration under Regulation (EC) 2023/2006 that explicitly covers the printing inks.
  • Test report from an accredited lab (SGS, TUV, Intertek) per BfR XXXVI or EN 646 for exactly the printed colours of the AE Global Traders logo.
  • Valid FSC chain-of-custody certificate; verify the licence number in the FSC database.
  • Print approval before series production: pre-production sample, Pantone tolerance, cost and ownership of printing plates in writing.
  • Proof of manufacturer hygiene (BRCGS Packaging, ISO 22000 or FSSC 22000) and confirmation whether the company is a factory or a trader.
  • German labelling on the pack and clarification of obligations under the Packaging Act (LUCID, system participation).
