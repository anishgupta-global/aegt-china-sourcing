Description

Knee pad inserts are standard accessories for work trousers with knee pockets: they spread the pressure when kneeling on tiles, screed or pipework and protect kneecap and joint.

More

Commercial buyers expect a resilient, shape-retaining EVA or PE foam that does not harden after washing and a cut that fits common knee pockets. EN 14404 (Type 2, performance level 0 or 1) is only tested together with the matching trousers; a corresponding test report is therefore a prerequisite before a pad is advertised with the standard. Because pads become thin and hard over time, repurchase is built in and the pad is a natural add-on in the trouser basket.

Return risk: fit (pads must match the pocket size); EN 14404 claims only with a test report for the pad-plus-trouser combination.

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 14404 (Type 2, level 0/1) stating the trousers or pocket used for testing (test house, date)
  • EU declaration of conformity under (EU) 2016/425 and marking on the product (standard, type, level); have the manufacturer confirm the category
  • REACH/SVHC report for the EVA foam (PAHs, phthalates, heavy metals) from an accredited laboratory
  • Check samples in 2-3 sizes against the knee pockets of the target trousers; fix thickness and foam hardness (Shore) in writing
  • Clarify the unit: price per pair or per piece (listing says pair; alternatives only state pieces)
  • Wash and ageing test (recovery and hardening after 20-50 washes) and tooling cost for a custom contour
