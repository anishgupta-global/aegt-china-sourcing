Description

The plastic barrier board (2,000 x 1,000 mm) visibly delimits work areas, excavations and walkways and replaces the classic wooden or steel barrier on private sites and at events.

More

Professionals expect an impact-resistant, UV-stabilised plastic (typically HDPE) without sharp edges, adjustable or rotating feet for a stable stand, connectors to line up several barriers and a red/white version with retroreflective film. For barriers on public roads the RSA and the TL for barrier boards with reflective film to DIN 67520 (class RA1/RA2) apply; the Chinese listing shows neither film nor test certificate, so the barrier should initially be positioned only for internal site use and events. From China the item can be placed on the market with GPSR information, an EU responsible person and packaging registration.

Baustellenabsicherung24 offers the Nissen barrier RA2 B (2,000 x 1,050 mm, 16 kg, tested to TL/RSA) from EUR 211.00 net, EUR 183.38 from 10 pcs. Hornbach shows in its search only the Schake Euro-Schrankenzaun (type 1, white-red, L = 1,200 mm, EUR 124.95) and red/white barrier tape 100 m for EUR 7.65 as a cheap substitute. The Chinese price of USD 20 from 100 pcs leaves margin against EUR 100, but the buyer group with TL/RSA requirements (road works, municipalities) demands test evidence that a standard listing does not provide. Bulky 2 m goods: high freight cost, returns uneconomical; seasonal peak with the building season and events (spring to autumn).

Request from the supplier

  • Have the material confirmed in writing (HDPE/PE, UV-stabilised, weight per variant 11/13 kg) and test samples for impact strength at -20 C and wall thickness.
  • Define the reflective film: for public roads RA1/RA2 film with a test certificate to DIN 67520, otherwise mark the product for private sites and events only.
  • Clarify scope of supply: feet, connectors (hooks/eyes), stackability and stability in a wind test; customisation (OEM, e.g. logo) is possible per the listing.
  • Sample colour fastness and the red/white version (two-colour moulding vs. adhesive film) and fix the reject rate in writing.
  • Clarify palletising and container loading (pieces per 20 ft container) as well as GPSR labelling, EU responsible person and LUCID registration before delivery.
