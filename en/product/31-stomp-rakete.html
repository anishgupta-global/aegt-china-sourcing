Description

In the stomp rocket set a firm stamp on the foot pedal creates air pressure that catapults the foam rockets through the tube into the air.

More

The set combines outdoor exercise with a playful physics experience and suits children, siblings and parents together. Quality markers are soft, safe rocket tips, a stable launcher with a tight, pressure-resistant hose, a sturdy pedal and, if present, a securely built-in light function. For the EU market, EN 71 test reports including projectile testing, a clear age grade and German warnings count.

Request from the supplier

  • Request EN 71-1 (projectile toys, projectile energy), -2, -3 and, for the light arrows, EN 62115 as reports from an accredited lab for exactly this model (OC1070372).
  • Test the foam for odour/VOC, phthalates (REACH entries 51/52) and PAHs (entry 50); no hard projectile tips.
  • Hose, coupling and pedal: test tightness, burst pressure and durability (burst hoses are the typical return reason).
  • Light arrows: screw-secured battery compartment (button cells), battery labelling under (EU) 2023/1542; fix age grade and German warnings.
  • Order samples and measure flight height/range; have MOQ tiers (360 and 3,600 pcs) and pieces per carton confirmed in writing.
  • Location: factory in Chenghai/Shantou, no Yiwu office or showroom stated; plan a separate visit or arrange sample shipment to Yiwu.
