Description

Concrete screws replace the plug on site for window and door frames, counter-battens and sections: pre-drill, drive in, load-bearing and removable again.

More

Quality depends on hardened steel and a precisely cut high-low thread, because a screw that is too soft shears off in concrete and one that is too hard snaps. German professionals expect a setting instruction with hole depth and drill diameter, corrosion protection suited to the application and, for structural use, an ETA to EAD 332347-00-0601. Without proven hardness and tensile strength and Torx dimensional accuracy the screw is released for non-structural fixings only.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Hardness test (surface and core hardness) and breaking torque per batch
  • Present the ETA to EAD 332347-00-0601 or mark the screw clearly as 'non-structural'; pull-out tests in C20/25 concrete by an independent laboratory
  • Salt-spray test report (ISO 9227) for the blue zinc finish
  • Check Torx T30 fit and thread geometry on sample
  • Chromium(VI)-free declaration and REACH declaration of the coating
