Description

Printed placemats and tray covers keep tables clean in the canteen, site cabin and at topping-out events, absorb spills and carry the company's logo or notices at the same time.

More

Professional product lies flat, has a non-linting surface and a print that does not bleed when damp; for trays the format must match the tray dimensions exactly. Because food can touch the printed side, German professional buyers expect inks under the German Consumer Goods Ordinance, a declaration of conformity under Regulation (EC) 1935/2004 with good manufacturing practice under Regulation (EC) 2023/2006, and paper from an FSC-certified source. For recycled fibre, evidence on mineral oil residues is added.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Declaration of conformity under Regulation (EC) 1935/2004 and evidence of good manufacturing practice under Regulation (EC) 2023/2006 for paper and printing.
  • Ink specification with conformity to Annex 14 of the German Consumer Goods Ordinance and a colour rub or migration test report (moisture, fat, heat).
  • For recycled fibre: test report on mineral oil residues (MOSH and MOAH) and BfR XXXVI evidence.
  • Pre-production sample with print proof and size check against your tray or table, with the colour tolerance for the logo agreed in writing.
  • FSC licence number with chain-of-custody certificate (verifiable in the FSC database) and origin data for EUDR.
  • Have manufacturer status (factory or trader) evidenced by business licence and factory photos; have the quotation show printing plate and packing costs separately.
