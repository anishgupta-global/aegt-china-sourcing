Description

The connection set of 20 m layflat hose (2 inch, about 50 mm inside diameter) with mounted Storz C couplings (52 mm) connects dirty-water and clear-water pumps quickly and tightly to the outlet.

More

The polyester fabric hose with PVC/TPR lining rolls up flat when empty, saves space in the vehicle and is considerably more resistant to abrasion and UV than simple garden hoses. Professional buyers look at working and burst pressure, properly crimped couplings, the standard stated (EN 14540) and a uniform coupling standard so that several lengths can be joined without adapters. As an add-on to submersible pumps and flood sets it also gets replaced when worn.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix coupling type and material (Storz C 52, aluminium or brass) and clarify whether the couplings are included in the per-metre price – ZYfire lists couplings separately at USD 0.80-8.00 each.
  • Request working and burst pressure and a test certificate to EN 14540; agree pressure testing per batch.
  • Have the method of coupling attachment (binding wire, clamp ring, crimping) and coupling pull-off force checked.
  • Request phthalate/SVHC evidence (REACH) and UV/ageing data for the fabric.
  • Clarify MOQ: the listing requires 1,000 m (= 50 sets of 20 m); order a sample with 2 inch / 50 mm inside diameter.
  • Agree packaging (roll, bag), label, German notes on operating limits and EU responsible person (GPSR).
