Description

The hand stapler (staple gun) with 4-14 mm staple length fastens foils, vapour barriers, insulation sheets, fleece, fabrics and battens and belongs in every site toolbox.

More

The metal body takes fine-wire staples of type 53, and a spring mechanism with adjustable impact drives them into wood and softer substrates. Professional users in Germany expect a strong, reliable drive without staple jams, a stable magazine and low-fatigue handling. Imported goods must comply with GPSR, REACH and the German Packaging Act; the staple format must be matched to common types (e.g. type 53).

Request from the supplier

  • Endurance test (e.g. 5,000 shots) with record of staple jams and penetration in softwood and hardwood
  • Proof of staple compatibility (type 53, 4-14 mm) with samples of the target staples
  • Material/finish declarations (body, spring, coating) and corrosion test
  • REACH/PAH test report for grip and plastic parts; GS certificate if required
  • GPSR details: EU responsible person with address, batch number, German safety and usage instructions
  • VerpackG/LUCID registration of the distributor and approval sample of the packaging
