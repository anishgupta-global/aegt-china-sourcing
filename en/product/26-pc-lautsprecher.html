Description

Active 2.0 speaker system with bookshelf speakers made of high-density MDF for desk, TV and living room.

More

Model TR501 connects via Bluetooth V5.0+ and also plays from USB stick and SD card or receives radio; the stated frequency response is 45 Hz to 20 kHz. Power is supplied by an external 19 V adapter, and logo, colour, size and material can be customised according to the supplier. Discerning buyers check sound and hum-free operation on the sample, measured output power rather than peak figures, cabinet workmanship and CE, EMC and RED conformity including a compliant power supply.

The market is dominated by brands (Edifier, Logitech); a generic 2.0 bookshelf speaker has to win on price, customisation (OEM branding, finish) or bundles (PC workstation set). Return risk is medium: sound/hum complaints, transport damage on heavy speakers (about 3.3 kg per package), power-supply conformity. Seasonal or trend evidence was not checked.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the CE/RED declaration of conformity and test reports for model TR501 including the matching 19 V power supply; the listing names CE, ISO, RoHS and FCC without reports.
  • Listen to and measure a sample: sound, idle hum, Bluetooth range, output power (RMS rather than peak) and volume control; check USB/SD/radio functions.
  • Cabinet: MDF formaldehyde proof (E1), surface and veneer quality, screws and weight; carton drop test for 3.3 kg packages.
  • Have the power supply documented with EU plug, efficiency per (EU) 2019/1782 and safety marks; German user manual.
  • Clarify customisation (logo, front grille, colour, packaging) and minimum quantities for special colours; confirm tier prices at 500 and 1,000 pcs.
  • EU duties: GPSR responsible person, stiftung ear registration, VerpackG/LUCID; final inspection with AQL sampling before shipment.
