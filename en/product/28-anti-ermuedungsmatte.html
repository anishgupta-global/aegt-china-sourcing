Description

Anyone standing longer at a height-adjustable desk relieves feet, knees and lower back with an anti-fatigue mat: the closed-cell foam gives slightly with each step and encourages small compensating movements.

More

The surface is slip-resistant and wipeable with a damp cloth, and the bevelled edge reduces the risk of tripping. The mat is available in thicknesses from 9 to 15 mm and sizes of about 90 x 60 to 150 x 90 cm, ribbed or with diamond, stone or bark embossing. Professional buyers look for recovery, slip resistance (e.g. class R10), low odour and plasticiser conformity under REACH.

Request from the supplier

  • Define the material (PVC foam or PU foam) and check a sample for thickness, density, recovery after sustained pressure and Shore hardness; demand a recovery test after 24 h load.
  • Request slip-resistance results for top and underside per DIN 51130 (target R10) or EN 16165 and a test report from an accredited lab.
  • REACH test report (phthalates, PAHs, SVHC) and odour test; for PU foam a statement on F-gas blowing agents under (EU) 2024/573.
  • Check dimensional tolerance, edge finish (bevelled, no gap), colour fastness and abrasion; agree sizes 90 x 60, 120 x 80, 150 x 90 cm and optionally 100 x 50 cm.
  • Packaging: roll or flat packing without creases, confirm carton of 10 pcs, print with EU responsible person (GPSR) and LUCID number.
  • Secure sample and first order (MOQ 300 pcs at Hongde) with batch inspection and a retained sample.
