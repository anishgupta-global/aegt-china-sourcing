Description

Final building cleaning and glass acceptance call for a set of washer with sleeve and rubber squeegee that pulls window surfaces dry without streaks.

More

Quality shows in a squeegee channel of stainless steel or aluminium instead of plastic, in a rubber blade that does not tear or streak, and in a washable microfibre sleeve. German cleaning professionals expect replaceable rubbers, spare sleeves and, for use on telescopic poles, a secure non-slip fitting. Without a sample, proof of replaceability and details of the rubber blade's composition (REACH, PAH) no set enters AE Global Traders stock.
