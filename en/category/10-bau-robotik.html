10 · Construction supplies

Construction robotics

Construction robotics automates repetitive finishing work such as tile laying, puttying and painting, layout marking, ceiling drilling, sanding and screed finishing …

More

and pays off for AE Global Traders mainly on large areas in refurbishment and fit-out after strip-out. The market is young: only a few Chinese makers deliver series machines, mostly without an EU office and with unproven CE conformity, so certificates, references and service matter more than price.

Construction robotics clusters in the Greater Bay Area (Foshan/Dongguan, Guangdong – Bright Dream, Partner Robotics; neighbours Zanecon in Shenzhen and CSC Robotic Engineering in Hong Kong) and in the Yangtze Delta (Shanghai/Suzhou/Huzhou – Weibuild, Fangshi, neighbour RoboticPlus.AI in Baoshan), with Beijing as a second R&D location.

All CN prices are listing prices without Incoterm. 3) Germany (research 29/30 Oct 2026): no public German purchase price could be found for any product – de_price deliberately left empty. According to press reports (2025), painting robots in Germany are practically only rented (ConBotics Berlin, Paint Buddy); the Hilti Jaibot is offered as a service/rental model without a list price. 4) Certification: mobile construction robots fall under the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC and, from 20 Jan 2027, the Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230, plus EMC, radio and battery rules (UN 38.3); CE is stated only by Fangshi (CERTING Srl, 09/2026) and Weibuild (manufacturer announcement), certificates not seen. Tile robots (640–880 kg) need load-bearing, level sub-floors and large formats. 5) Duty: 8424 89 70 (spraying robot) and 8479 50 00 (industrial robots n.e.s.) at 1.7 % each; the new products are only provisionally classified under 8479 50 00 or 8479 10 00, duty rate not evidenced – a binding tariff information (BTI) is recommended. 6) Not included: Legend Robot (Shenzhen), Hangzhou Guozi Robotics, Nanjing Zhuling, Fulltime Robotics (Hangzhou), Tuochi (Chongqing), Hunan Googol Robot (layout marking robot, no price) – search hits only, not checked; made-in-china.com and 1688.com were not accessible. 7) New products (layout, troweling/screeding, drilling, sanding): no documented manufacturer unit price and no German shop price found in this round (Alibaba showroom pages returned HTTP 410, search hits were catalogue, patent and directory pages only); price fields deliberately left empty, typical specs are market guide values and must be verified with the manufacturer before ordering. 8) New makers RoboticPlus.AI, Zanecon and CSC Robotic Engineering come from directory or exhibition profiles only (BuiltWorlds, CB Insights, CITAC); own websites and contact data were not checked, and CSC is based in Hong Kong. 9) Reference images of German brands (Hilti, Makita, Leica Geosystems, Hitachi etc.) were not collected in this run (schema without image field, tool budget); the earlier image_url entries for the tile and painting robots remain in research/10_bau-robotik.json.
