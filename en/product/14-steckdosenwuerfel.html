Description

The socket cube (distribution cube) splits one supply into several Schuko sockets and serves on site as a mobile distributor for hand tools, lamps and extraction units right at the workplace.

More

Required are an impact-resistant rubber or PVC housing rated IP44 with hinged lids, a carrying handle or hanging eye, a permanently connected H07RN-F supply cable with kink protection and clean contact in every outlet at 16 A total current. The product must be CE-compliant, meet DIN VDE 0620-1 and DIN VDE 0100-704 and be testable under DGUV Regulation 3; GS-tested branded goods from ABL, Brennenstuhl or as-Schwabe set the benchmark. Imported goods from China can only be used with a test report, a temperature-rise proof under full load and a German-language declaration of conformity.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to DIN VDE 0620-1 (EN 60884-1) from an accredited lab, including temperature rise at 16 A total load in all four outlets
  • CE declaration of conformity (2014/35/EU, 2014/30/EU, RoHS) referencing the exact model
  • Proof of IP44 protection to EN 60529 with hinged lids closed and open
  • Cable evidence: HAR/VDE marking H07RN-F, conductor cross-section and copper content
  • Sample test: strain relief, 1 m drop test, impact test at low temperature
  • GS mark or equivalent certification (ProdSG), preferably from an independent test house
