Description

The dirty-water submersible pump drains excavations, cellars, lift shafts and flooded strip-out areas, even with sludge, plaster and mortar residue.

More

A cast-iron or stainless-steel housing, mechanical shaft seal and float switch ensure safe operation in rough site conditions. Professional users in Germany expect a CE-compliant unit with IPX8, an H07RN-F connection cable, a German manual and spare-parts supply that can be inspected under DGUV Regulation 3. The EU machinery, EMC and low-voltage requirements are decisive.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity citing 2006/42/EC, 2014/35/EU and 2014/30/EU plus test reports from an accredited lab (EN 60335-2-41, EN 60529).
  • Test report or photo proof for IPX8 (immersion test) and the mechanical seal, cable type H07RN-F with core cross-section.
  • Pump curve (Q-H) with measurement protocol and stated maximum solids passage.
  • German manual and rating plate with CE mark, manufacturer and importer details.
  • Spare-parts list (impeller, mechanical seal, float switch) and at least 12 months warranty.
  • Factory test per lot (high-voltage and protective-conductor test) and a sample for DGUV V3 inspection before series delivery.
