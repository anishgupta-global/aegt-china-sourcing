Description

Work and scaffold trestles provide stable support for work platforms, scaffold planks and workpieces in plastering, screed, drywall and painting work and are basic equipment for every fit-out crew.

More

Professional users in Germany expect welded steel tube frames with a clear load rating per trestle, a lockable height adjustment or folding lock, anti-slip feet and durable corrosion protection by galvanising or powder coating. When used as a base for work platforms, the requirements of the Industrial Safety Ordinance, TRBS 2121 and the scaffold standards apply; premium suppliers such as Zarges prove load capacity and stability by testing. A load test report, a declaration of conformity under product safety law and German instructions for use must be delivered.
