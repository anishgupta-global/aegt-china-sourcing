Description

The 8-tonne-class backhoe loader combines a front loader (1.0 m³) and a rear backhoe in one road-going machine and replaces wheel loaders and excavators on smaller sites …

More

in municipal operations and in pipe-laying. More

new-machine prices in Germany are mostly not public ('on request'). No bestseller or trend data was collected for this class. Return risk is high (transport, registration, Stage V) – only offer with documents and a service partner clarified in advance.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EU type-approval or Stage V certificate (Reg. (EU) 2016/1628) for the exact engine fitted; the listings state no emission stage; XCMG offers two engines (TD226B-4IG2, Cummins QSB4.5) – fix the variant. A Stage V compliant engine is a prerequisite for placing the machine on the EU market.
  • Request the CE declaration of conformity, technical file and a German operating manual; check the nameplate with serial number and LWA sound power level.
  • Get spare-parts and service coverage in Germany/EU confirmed in writing (dealer, parts lists, response time, warranty terms).
  • Clarify road registration: lighting, mirrors, brakes, 40 km/h approval and individual inspection under StVZO; reconcile the weight (7.8 t vs. 8.1 t).
  • Brand/manufacturer is not unambiguous in the dealer listing: XT870 is the XCMG model designation – have XCMG confirm original manufacture and the serial number in writing.
