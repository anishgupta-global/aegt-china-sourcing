Concrete, flooring & finishing equipment

Compaction, plastering, formwork, insulation and tile profiles are trades where Chinese machines and standard components are often priced well below the German market.

More

The success of an import, however, depends on CE conformity, engine approval and test evidence, so for each product this catalogue gives a price reference, the applicable standards and the proofs AE Global Traders should demand before ordering.

Plate compactors and compaction equipment cluster in Henan (Xuchang, Changge) and Hunan (Changsha), formwork in Beijing/Tianjin, Shandong (Rizhao) and Hebei (Tangshan), XPS around Beijing/Tianjin/Hebei as well as in Zhejiang and Guangdong. According to sourcing-aggregator pages, plastering machines are mainly offered from Henan and Shandong (Yantai), while aluminium tile profiles come from the extrusion cluster in Foshan (Guangdong).

(9) Customs: HS 84306100 comes from the heading description on zolltarifnummern.de; HS 39211100 (XPS) extends the earlier 6-digit classification 392111 to 8 digits; 84672990 (concrete vibrator), 84248900 (plastering machine), 73084000 (formwork) and 76042990 (aluminium profiles) are own classifications without a page source. Duty rates were not verified and stay empty: check TARIC or a binding tariff information. Import VAT of 19 % applies on top. (10) Certification traps (general expertise, not from the sources, please verify): plate compactors with combustion engines need EU engine approval (Regulation (EU) 2016/1628, Stage V), CE under the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC (from 20 Jan 2027 Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230) and sound power marking under the Outdoor Noise Directive 2000/14/EC; the importer legally becomes the manufacturer. CE under the Machinery Directive applies analogously to concrete vibrators and plastering machines (plastering machines additionally EN 12001). XPS needs CE marking in the EU under EN 13164 with a declaration of performance; check fire class and lambda value. Formwork: request EN 12812/EN 12811 evidence and a structural check; no EN certificates and no EU office are evidenced for Zulin. (11) Not covered: wall-mounted masonry crane, formwork props/trestles, other insulation (EPS, mineral wool) and accessories were not researched; no Shandong XPS maker was found. Henan Be Focus, Yantai Chonho and Changtian Aluminum are evidenced only via aggregator/marketplace pages; their manufacturer or trader status is open.
