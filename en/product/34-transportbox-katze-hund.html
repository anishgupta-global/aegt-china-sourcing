More

The listing calls it 'airline approved'; for air travel, however, only compliance with the IATA container requirements counts, which must be proven with documents. Professional buyers look for a securely latching door, burr-free injection-moulded parts, sufficient ventilation and sturdy handles.

Request from the supplier

  • Have the price per size confirmed in writing: the listing tiers apply to the S-XXL range without size assignment.
  • Advertise IATA compliance (Container Requirement 1) only with test documents; 'airline approved' in the listing is unverified.
  • Check plastic type (PP/ABS), wall thickness, door latch and fasteners; measure burr-freeness and ventilation area on the sample.
  • Request a REACH/SVHC test report for the plastic and the metal door (coating, heavy metals).
  • Ask about seat-belt slots/belt guidance and stackability (nesting design), pack size and carton quantity for sea freight.
  • Factory audit (inspect BSCI/ISO certificates) and sample before series order; record tolerances for dimensions and weights.
