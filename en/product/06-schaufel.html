Description

The Frankfurt-pattern shovel is the standard tool for picking up and loading rubble, sand, gravel and mortar during strip-out, demolition and refurbishment.

More

The blade of hardened 50Mn steel is joined to the handle by a closed socket and withstands the shocks of coarse debris; the wooden handle is sanded smooth, splinter-free and lacquered. Professional users in Germany expect the classic Frankfurt or Holstein blade shape, a 130 cm handle, a firm socket joint and a low-pollutant finish. Imported goods must comply with the EU General Product Safety Regulation 2023/988 and REACH, ideally with the GS mark.

Request from the supplier

  • Test report from an accredited body (TÜV, DEKRA, Intertek) with GS certificate or an equivalent test of the handle-socket joint (tensile and bending test)
  • EN 10204 type 3.1 material certificate for the 50Mn blade plus hardness record (HRC)
  • REACH declaration with SVHC evidence and a PAH test report for lacquer and grips
  • Proof of timber origin (FSC/PEFC) and a check against the EU Deforestation Regulation (EU) 2023/1115 for the wooden handle
  • GPSR details: EU responsible person with address, batch/type number, German safety instructions
  • Approval sample in the German Frankfurt pattern with a 1,300 mm handle before series production
