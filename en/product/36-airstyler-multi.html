Description

The multi air styler combines a high-speed base unit (up to 110,000 rpm per manufacturer, ionizer) with four attachments: auto-wrap curler, paddle brush, oval brush and concentrator.

More

Warm air instead of heated plates is gentler on hair and attachments are swapped via a plug-in connection. Power is in the range of 1,001-1,499 W at 220-240 V and the listing quotes a quiet 50-60 dB. Buyers expect clear attachment labelling, a storage case and a German manual; for private label the IP position of the attachment geometry has to be cleared in advance.

Request from the supplier

  • Have the exact model, model number and scope of delivery confirmed in writing; the listing page gives no model number.
  • Request CE/LVD/EMC test reports (EN 60335-2-23, EN 55014-1/-2) for the EU variant (230 V/50 Hz, EU plug).
  • Clear IP: check the auto-wrap/Coanda geometry and housing shape against Dyson and Shark patents/design rights; never adopt brand names from listings (e.g. FlexStyle).
  • Test attachments: latching, temperature limiting of curling attachments, hair snagging; agree storage case and labelling.
  • Negotiate tiers (listing: USD 39.00 from 1,000, USD 35.00 from 3,000, USD 30.00 from 5,000 pcs) and also get a quote from the alternative Bidisco (model F8118, MOQ 500, price on request).
  • Secure German manual, GPSR label and ElektroG registration before market launch.
