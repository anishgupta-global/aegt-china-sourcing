Description

Food squishies are soft figures that slowly return to their original shape, made as dumplings, buns, butter, croissants or cake slices.

More

They sell as stress relievers, collectibles and party favours, singly or as surprise mystery bags. Discerning buyers look for a solid foam or soft-rubber body with no leaking fill, even recovery, low odour and scratch-resistant, solvent-free printing. Test reports on the finished product are expected (EN 71 mechanical, flammability, heavy metals), plus phthalate and PAH evidence and German labelling under the GPSR. Water beads or swelling gel balls in the fill must be excluded for the EU market.

Request from the supplier

  • EN 71-1/-2/-3 test report (plus EN 71-12 for soft rubber) from an accredited lab on the finished product, not just the raw material; check report date and batch reference.
  • Age grading and small parts: tear, tension and bend tests; foam or rubber pieces must not break into swallowable small parts.
  • Written confirmation: no water beads, no swelling gel balls, no release for heating; 'do not heat' warning on the pack.
  • Phthalate, PAH and odour tests (VOC and formamide for PU foam) with a test report; approve samples before series production.
  • Own design without copying protected characters or faces; mould and tooling rights in writing with the buyer.
  • Pack with EU responsible person, batch number and German warnings; supplier agrees to cover the new EU Toy Safety Regulation (digital product passport, PFAS and bisphenol restrictions) in the contract.
