Description

The material cart moves building materials, tool cases, cartons and equipment of up to 500 kg across level hall and site floors without a forklift.

More

With the handle folded down the carton measures only 620 x 130 x 925 mm, so the cart fits flat into a van, a storage rack or a container corner. A professional unit has a sturdy steel platform, four rubber wheels of 125 mm diameter (two swivel, two fixed) and a securely latching folding handle; parking brakes and toe guards add safety on ramps. Commercial buyers in Germany expect a load rating marked on the unit, test proof to EN 1757:2022 or GS, REACH-compliant wheels and grips, and GPSR labelling with an EU responsible person. As work equipment the cart is included in the risk assessment under the BetrSichV and checked regularly for frame, folding mechanism and wheels.

Request from the supplier

  • Load test report for 500 kg (static and dynamic) to EN 1757:2022 with platform size, wheel type, test date and serial number; confirm the model designation in writing (listing shows both LH10-500 and LH11-500)
  • GS certificate or test report from an EU-accredited body; cross-check certificate numbers and validity of ISO 9001, GS, SGS, REACH and RoHS, named EU responsible person under GPSR
  • REACH/PAH test report for rubber wheels, grips and coating (PAHs, lead, cadmium)
  • Endurance test of folding hinge and wheel bearings with test protocol; ask for rubber or PU wheels, swivel and fixed casters, brake as option
  • Salt-spray test to ISO 9227 with agreed hours, weld seam and coating quality; production-standard sample (listing: USD 30 per piece) before ordering
  • Check factory audit or the SGS report QIP-ASR239552; reconcile the company address (Made-in-China: 219 Changzheng Road, own website: 95 Changzheng Road, Xiaogan)
