Description

The H07RN-F rubber-sheathed cable is the standard cable prescribed for mobile power supply on construction sites: it connects distribution boards …

More

cable reels and machines and must withstand tensile forces, abrasion, oil, moisture and cold. A high-quality product has a fine-stranded class 5 copper conductor to EN 60228, EPR insulation and a polychloroprene sheath, clearly marked with manufacturer, type and VDE/HAR mark. German professional buyers expect pure copper without aluminium or CCA content and a conductor resistance within the standard, backed by a recognised approval mark. Cheap cables with undersized cross-section or recycled copper overheat and are a liability risk for AE Global Traders.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • VDE/HAR approval or equivalent certification by a recognised body (not just a printed mark) with certificate number.
  • Conductor resistance and copper purity proven by an independent lab test (EN 60228); exclusion of aluminium/CCA and recycled copper.
  • Test report on insulation thickness, sheath quality (cold, oil and abrasion tests) and a high-voltage test per batch.
  • Check metre marking and print (manufacturer, H07RN-F, 3G2.5, standard); cut lengths on drums with batch number.
  • EU declaration of conformity (LVD, RoHS), REACH SVHC declaration and a sample delivery with acceptance test before the series order.
