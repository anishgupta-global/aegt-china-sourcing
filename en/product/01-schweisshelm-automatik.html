Description

The auto-darkening welding helmet darkens the filter within a fraction of a millisecond when the arc strikes and gives the welder a clear view of seam and workpiece in the light state.

More

A shade range of DIN 9–13 for MMA, MIG/MAG and TIG, solar cells as power source and shade 4 in the light state are typical on the market. Professional buyers expect optical class 1/1/1/1, marking to EN 175 and EN 379 or EN ISO 16321-2, a robust, light helmet body and cover lenses that fit the model and can be swapped quickly. The cover lenses are the recurring consumable; the helmet is Category II PPE with EU type-examination, and because it contains electronics EMC and RoHS also apply.

Request from the supplier

  • EU type-examination certificate (Cat. II) from a notified body for exactly this helmet-filter model, with EN 175 and EN 379 or EN ISO 16321-2 in the test scope; verify in NANDO.
  • Test report on optical class (1/1/1/1), switching time, angle dependence and shade levels in light and dark state, plus behaviour at low currents (TIG from approx. 5 A).
  • Clarify fit and availability of cover lenses (inner/outer, size, price per piece) for the model; set a minimum quantity for spare-lens sets.
  • EMC and RoHS evidence and details of the power supply (solar cell, coin cell: Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542).
  • Marking on helmet and filter (CE, standard, manufacturer, optical class) and German instructions with notes on welding processes, maintenance and lens replacement.
  • Factory audit and sample with a field test by a welder before series order.
