Description

The carbide-tipped circular saw blade Ø 315 × 30 mm with 48 teeth is the wear part of the site table saw and cuts construction timber, solid wood and board materials in rip and cross cuts.

More

Brazed-on carbide teeth sit on the body; they last many times longer than steel blades and can be resharpened several times. Professional users expect a flat-running, tensioned body, clean tooth brazing, expansion slots with noise damping and clearly legible marking of diameter, bore, tooth count and permitted maximum speed. Conformity with EN 847-1, a kerf that suits the machine (mind the riving knife) and GPSR labelling with an EU responsible person are required.

More

Price ladder in the German market: Hornbach lists the 315 × 30 mm Optiline Wood blade with 48 teeth (Bosch line) at EUR 99.00 gross (item no. 4652335, page opened) and the coarser Construct for Wood S with 20 teeth at EUR 33.95 (item no. 10106938, price from the Hornbach search); per the scan Wolfcraft Serie orange 48 teeth EUR 104.95 (Bauhaus) and the original Einhell 48-tooth blade about EUR 39–56 (price comparison), both pages not re-opened. The market is crowded with Bosch, Wolfcraft and Einhell; a private label needs verifiable service life and an EN 847-1 certificate. Return risk: wrong bore or kerf versus the riving knife, maximum speed too low. Fits as a set with the site table saw 11-baukreissaege.

Request from the supplier

  • Request a test report to EN 847-1 (dimensions, runout, tooth fixing) and marking of maximum speed and direction of rotation on the body; the permitted speed must exceed the machine speed (Jifa 73151T about 2,800 rpm).
  • Confirm the dimensions in writing: 315 × 3.2/2.2 × 30 × 48 teeth, bore tolerance and any drive holes; check the kerf against the riving knife thickness of the target machine (body thinner than the riving knife, kerf wider).
  • Ask for the carbide grade, the maker of the tooth tips (listing: Ceratizit) and the tooth form; test tooth breakage and braze joints and compare service life on a sample against the reference blade Optiline Wood (EUR 99).
  • Body: measure tensioning, flatness and runout on the sample; check the expansion slots with copper plugs for noise reduction (per listing).
  • Confirm price and minimum order for the 315 mm: the nearest series blade in the listing is 300 mm (JP300/60); 315 × 30 × 48 teeth is a special order (“Customization available”). Xinsheng is listed on Made-in-China as “Manufacturer/Factory & Trading Company”: ask for a factory audit and copies of the certificates (ISO 9001:2008 per listing).
  • Agree packaging with edge protection in a single carton, labelling with the private brand, EU responsible person (GPSR) and a traceability number.
