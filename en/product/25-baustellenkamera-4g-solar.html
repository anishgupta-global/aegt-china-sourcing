Description

The 4G construction-site camera works without mains power or LAN using a solar panel and battery and monitors storage areas, containers and access roads of demolition and remediation sites …

More

or documents construction progress as a time-lapse. Professional users in Germany expect a weatherproof, vandal-resistant housing (at least IP66), reliable night vision, motion detection with a low false-alarm rate and encrypted transmission to EU servers. Legally decisive are RED conformity with cybersecurity evidence and the GDPR (field of view, signage, retention periods, data processing agreement). Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under RED 2014/53/EU with test reports (radio, EMC, safety) and evidence of the cybersecurity requirements under Art. 3(3) d/e/f RED for the firmware shipped
  • IP66/IP67 test report (EN 60529) and an autonomy test of solar panel and battery in a German winter (e.g. at no more than 3 hours of sunshine per day)
  • Data protection: EU server location, data processing agreement, end-to-end or TLS encryption, no default passwords, documented firmware update process
  • Battery: test report to EN IEC 62133-2 and UN 38.3 test summary; labelling under the EU Battery Regulation (EU) 2023/1542
  • German user manual, German-language support, spare parts (solar panel, bracket, battery), at least 24 months warranty and a named EU responsible person (GPSR)
  • Origin clarification for public clients: agree in advance with the customer on usage restrictions for certain Chinese camera makers (Hikvision, Dahua)
