Description

The electric forklift covers loading, unloading, storing and material handling in strip-out, refurbishment and warehouse logistics and, being free of exhaust fumes, can also be used in closed halls.

More

A professional unit has a welded steel frame with counterweight, a visibility mast (duplex or triplex), an operator restraint system, key switch, horn, warning light and a lithium battery with battery management. Users in Germany expect CE conformity under the Machinery Directive, EN ISO 3691-1, a German manual, spare parts and a service network as well as the annual UVV inspection under DGUV Regulation 68; drivers need written authorisation and instruction under DGUV Principle 308-001.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity and CE rating plate with serial number, year of manufacture, rated load and load chart
  • Test reports from an accredited lab (TÜV, SGS, Intertek) to EN ISO 3691-1, EN 1175 and EN 12895 including tipping stability and load chart
  • Li-ion battery: UN 38.3 report, EN IEC 62619, battery-management documentation, charging and fire-protection instructions
  • German operating manual, logbook for the UVV inspection, proof of the restraint system and driver instructions
  • Written service and spare-parts concept for Germany/EU (e.g. via Hangcha Europe in Flörsheim or Noblelift Europe), response times, parts stock, warranty of at least 12 to 24 months
  • Factory audit (ISO 9001), acceptance with test run before shipment and load-securing record for sea freight
