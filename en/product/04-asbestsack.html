Description

The asbestos bag takes up asbestos-containing construction waste at the workplace and seals it dust-tight before it leaves the black area.

More

Quality product is made of UV-stabilised, tear-resistant film or a woven PP sack with a PE liner and carries the mandatory warning print in several languages. Professional users in Germany expect double packaging, clean seams or welds and labelling in line with TRGS 519 so the waste can be disposed of under waste code 17 06 05*. Consistent film thickness and traceable batches are a prerequisite for documentation towards the authority and the waste handler.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Sample with measurement report on film thickness (µm), dimensions and printing before series release
  • Approved artwork of the warning print in German in line with TRGS 519/CLP (clarify printing-plate costs)
  • Tear-strength and leak-tightness test (drop and tensile test) by an independent body
  • Manufacturer declaration: REACH-compliant film without SVHC, material data sheet
  • Proof of the double-packaging system (bag + liner), delivery form and pallet size
  • Traceable batch number for every delivery for documentation towards the authority and waste handler
