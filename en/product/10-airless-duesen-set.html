Description

The set bundles six reversible airless tips in common sizes with a filter set for gun and inlet.

More

The orifice sets flow rate and fan width; carbide inserts withstand the abrasion of dispersion paints longer than simple steel tips, and the reversing function lets clogged tips be cleared without removal. Professional users expect clean orifice geometry, a dimensionally accurate fit in the tip guard and a fan free of tails. As importer, AE Global Traders is responsible for GPSR, fit testing (compatible with standard tip guards, no third-party brands on product or packaging) and packaging registration.

Request from the supplier

  • Request factory status (own carbide sintering or tip production) and supplier audit proof
  • Drawing and dimensional report for the orifices (211-523) against reference tips; fan-pattern test with dispersion paint
  • Test fit in a standard tip guard and leak-tightness at test pressure (target about 250 bar) on a sample
  • Proof of carbide grade and cobalt content; REACH declaration
  • Neutral marking without Graco, Wagner or RAC brand reference; packaging per VerpackG
