Description

The fastening tool (nail gun) drives nails and pins into concrete, steel and masonry using gas or powder-charge energy and replaces time-consuming drilling and plugging …

More

on site for brackets, cable trays and drywall profiles. Germany applies special safety and approval requirements: a positively controlled trigger safety (firing only under contact pressure), testing to EN 15895 or EN ISO 11148-13 and, for powder-actuated tools, proof of admissibility under German firearms-testing law (PTB type approval) are expected. Tool, fasteners and gas or cartridge supply must be matched as one system, and operators need documented instruction. Therefore, only buy from a manufacturer with complete documentation, spare-parts supply and dangerous-goods-compliant gas-cell delivery.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 15895 or EN ISO 11148-13 from an accredited body and EU declaration of conformity (2006/42/EC)
  • Proof of admissibility in Germany (PTB type approval/approval mark for powder-actuated tools); have the safety officer check this before ordering
  • Safety data sheet and dangerous-goods classification of the gas cells (transport, storage) and approved cell/nail combinations
  • Battery and charger evidence (UN 38.3, EN 60335-2-29, 2014/30/EU, 2011/65/EU)
  • Function video of the trigger safety (contact pressure) and endurance test (shots until maintenance)
  • Operating manual in German, instruction material and spare-parts list
