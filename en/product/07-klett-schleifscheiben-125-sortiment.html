Description

The assortment bundles 50 hook-and-loop sanding discs, dia. 125 mm with 8-hole dust extraction, in several grits from K40 (stock removal, paint and varnish stripping) to K180 (fine sanding before painting).

More

The abrasive is aluminium oxide on a paper backing with a hook-and-loop back; the discs change without tools on common 125 mm random orbital sanders. Professional users expect an accurately punched hole pattern for dust extraction, clear grit marking to the FEPA P scale (ISO 6344), an even grain distribution and an anti-clogging coating that reduces loading on paint and filler. Retail-ready, the set belongs in a bag or box with a German label, a grit overview and EU information under the GPSR.

Per the scan stage (search hits, not re-opened by us) also Kaufland 50-pc sets around EUR 14, OBI Bosch 25-pc and Toolbrothers Makita 50-pc. Light, ships flat, grit mix allows tiered pricing. Competition is high (branded goods plus many marketplace sellers); return risk through hole pattern and hook-and-loop quality – show compatibility (125 mm, 8-hole) clearly in title and image.

Request from the supplier

  • Hole pattern: check the 8-hole pattern (hole diameter, spacing) on samples on common 125 mm random orbital sanders; the listing states neither hole count nor a clear diameter (title 125 mm, field 150 mm)
  • Grit: have the FEPA P grit printed on the back; request a grit test report per grit (the listing contradicts itself: P16-2000 / 36-2000# / 60#)
  • Hook-and-loop back: test holding force and re-use (cycles) on sanding pads from several makers
  • Assortment: split per grit, bag or box, German label with EAN; no third-party brand or competitor logos
  • Compliance: GPSR information with EU responsible person, REACH declaration, VerpackG/LUCID data for the packaging
  • Company: request ISO 9001 certificate and SGS audit report (listing states SGS ID QIP-ASI244816); clarify manufacturer status (shop: factory and trading company); MOQ is 100,000 single discs – negotiate small quantities for assortments
