Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Paper pendants are a stable home trend (Hornbach/Lampenwelt: Lindby Magali, pleated paper shade, EUR 46.30 gross instead of RRP 69.90; OBI: Lindby Valdorin EUR 79.90).

More

Low purchase price at low weight; return risk: creases/moisture on the paper.

Request from the supplier

  • Check the paper for fire behaviour and distance to the light source (EN 60598-1, glow-wire/flammability test); release only LED bulbs
  • Define dimensions, weight and maximum socket power (W) for E27 and have it printed on the product
  • Request origami/folded variants separately; check samples of shade shapes beforehand (listing prices refer to the lantern)
  • Packaging against crushing/moisture (single carton, silica gel), check sea-container transport
  • Plan EU responsible person (GPSR) and WEEE registration
