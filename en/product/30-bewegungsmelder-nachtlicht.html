Description

Small round night lights (Ø 85 mm, plastic, 0.3 W) that switch on only in the dark and on movement thanks to a PIR motion and light sensor.

More

The integrated 800 mAh battery is charged via USB; according to the maker one charge lasts about 2–3 months in sensor mode (maker's claim, not independently verified). Mounting is magnetic, adhesive or loose, without drilling or cables. High-quality sets show a strong magnet, a well-tuned sensor range, a tested Li-ion cell with a UN 38.3 report and the desired charging port (USB-C). For the EU, EMC, RoHS, the Batteries Regulation, ElektroG/BattG marking and an EU responsible person under GPSR must be observed.

A typical add-on to bedside lamps, autumn/winter season (derived); price war from white label, return risk from weak magnets, false triggering and shorter battery life than advertised.

Request from the supplier

  • Ask for a USB-C version: the listing states Micro USB; also cell maker, UN 38.3 report and overcharge/short-circuit protection of the 800 mAh cell.
  • Have the sensor data (range, detection angle, lux threshold, hold time) and the advertised 2–3 month sensor-mode runtime proven by a test.
  • Ask for the luminous flux in lumens: below 60 lm the light source falls outside (EU) 2019/2020, above it EPREL is required.
  • Check magnet holding force, adhesive pad and weight; test samples for false triggering in daylight and for switch-off behaviour; request an EMC report.
  • Clarify the set packing: MOQ 1,000 pcs equals about 166 sets of 6; price for carton/single box, marking, German manual.
  • Registration and marking: EU responsible person (GPSR), VerpackG/LUCID, ElektroG/stiftung ear, BattG symbols (crossed-out bin, chemical symbol).
