Description

The sonic facial cleansing brush has a soft silicone brush head, which is more hygienic than nylon bristles, and a sonic vibration that combines cleansing and facial massage.

More

Per the listing, the device offers three customizable modes and charges on an inductive charging station (about 10 hours). Buyers expect a quiet motor, a grippy, waterproof build and an easy-to-clean head. For the EU market, safety testing to EN 60335-2-23, battery compliance and a German manual are required; claims stay cosmetic.

Request from the supplier

  • Request model JS063 in writing with a confirmed water protection class (the parameter sheet gives none; the company text only mentions 'IPx8 Approval' generically) and cell certificates for the 3.7 V/650 mAh battery; ask about USB-C instead of inductive charging.
  • Request test reports: LVD (EN 60335-2-23), EMC, RoHS, REACH, battery per IEC 62133-2 and UN 38.3, IP test per EN 60529.
  • Silicone quality: check skin-contact suitability, REACH/SVHC, hardness (Shore), colour fastness and cleaning resistance.
  • Samples: vibration frequency, noise, battery life, behaviour on water contact; include replacement heads as additional revenue.
  • Get a second quote from the alternative Foshan Taida (listing: USD 12.00 from 50 pcs, USD 11.70 from 200 pcs, IPX6) and compare.
  • German manual incl. note for sensitive skin; advertising cosmetic only (pore cleansing as care, not therapy).
