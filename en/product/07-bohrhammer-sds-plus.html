Description

The SDS-plus rotary hammer is the standard tool on renovation and strip-out sites for breakthroughs, anchor and dowel holes and light chiselling in concrete and masonry.

More

Professional users in Germany expect an electropneumatic hammer mechanism with several modes (drilling, hammer drilling, chiselling), a safety slip clutch against jamming, an adjustable auxiliary handle with depth stop and effective vibration damping. The machine must be CE-compliant and tested to EN 62841-2-6; vibration and noise values measured to the EPTA procedure belong in the German operating manual. When drilling in mineral substrates, drilling-dust extraction per TRGS 559 is state of the art, so the hammer should work with common extraction attachments.
