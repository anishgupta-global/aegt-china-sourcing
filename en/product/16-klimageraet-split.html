Description

The split air conditioner consists of a wall-mounted indoor unit and an outdoor unit and both cools and heats (heat pump) offices, meeting and break rooms permanently, quietly and with low power consumption.

More

An inverter compressor, R32 refrigerant and energy classes in the A++ range are the market standard; users in Germany expect CE, an EPREL entry with energy label, a German manual and a unit that a specialist firm certified under the German Chemicals Climate Protection Ordinance may install. For site cabins, interim offices and site-management rooms it is the quieter and more efficient permanent solution compared with mobile monoblock units, but it needs refrigerant lines, condensate drainage and a wall opening. The delivery should include indoor and outdoor unit as a matched set with installation accessories and warranty.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity with test reports (EN 60335-2-40, EMC; RED if a Wi-Fi module is fitted) from an accredited body.
  • EPREL registration and energy label with SEER/SCOP and efficiency class per Regulation (EU) 626/2011, plus a measurement report per EN 14511.
  • Photo of the rating plate showing R32, refrigerant charge, voltage and manufacturer; proof of compliance with the F-gas regulation and A2L safety instructions.
  • German installation and operating manual stating installation by an F-gas-certified specialist firm; define installation accessories (bracket, piping, condensate drain) in the scope of delivery.
  • Factory test of every unit (leak tightness, function test), at least 24 months warranty and available spare parts (compressor, PCB, fan).
