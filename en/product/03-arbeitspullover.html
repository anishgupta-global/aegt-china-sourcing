Description

The work pullover is the warm mid-layer on cold sites and is worn daily and washed often.

More

Quality models use a cotton-polyester blend with a brushed inside, shape-retaining rib cuffs, a reinforced collar and covered seams so that the garments do not pill or lose shape after many washes. A professional user in Germany expects correct fibre labelling, fit in standard EU sizes and harmful-substance testing to OEKO-TEX or REACH. As a simple garment the pullover is not PPE; EN ISO 20471 applies only to a hi-vis variant.

Request from the supplier

  • Fibre composition test report (cotton/polyester share) under Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011
  • Test report on pilling (ISO 12945-2), dimensional change after washing and colour fastness (e.g. ISO 105-C06)
  • OEKO-TEX certificate or REACH test report from an accredited lab on azo dyes and formaldehyde
  • Size chart in EU measurements with fit samples in at least three sizes before production
  • Care, size and fibre labelling on the label in German
  • For logo print or embroidery: durability sample after 10 washes at 40 °C
