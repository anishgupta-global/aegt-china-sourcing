Description

The high-visibility T-shirt replaces the hi-vis vest in summer and provides all-round visibility for road works, logistics, landscaping and event services.

More

What matters are the fluorescent colour and retroreflective bands in the required minimum area, a breathable, quick-drying fabric and bands that survive washing. Under EN ISO 20471 the class (1 to 3) must be marked on the garment and evidenced by EU type examination as category II PPE.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EU type-examination certificate to EN ISO 20471 with class and NB number for exactly this model (SFTE06) and check it in NANDO.
  • Require test reports for luminance of the base colour, retroreflection values and wash resistance (e.g. 25 washes).
  • Label: CE, pictogram, class, size, fibre composition, care instruction, manufacturer details; check samples against size measurements.
  • Fix the production site for the order in writing (Wuyi/Zhejiang versus Anhui or Myanmar), inspect the BSCI audit.
  • Confirm price per size/colour and MOQ 2,000 pcs; fix the retroreflective tape supplier (3M or local).
