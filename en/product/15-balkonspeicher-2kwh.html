Description

The balcony storage stores the midday surplus of the balcony power plant and releases it into the house grid in the evening and at night to increase self-consumption.

More

The Marstek VENUS A offers 2.12 kWh of LiFePO4 capacity, four MPPT inputs for up to 2.4 kW of module power and an integrated inverter with up to 1.2 kW output; the capacity can be extended with add-on batteries up to 12.72 kWh. Quality markers are cells with over 6,000 cycles, IP65 protection, backup switchover in 15 ms and a manufacturer warranty of 10 years (manufacturer data per ESS News). Decisive for retail are grid compliance (unit certificate, 800 VA limit for plug-in solar), the Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 and dangerous goods transport.

E-commerce potential

Storage is the upgrade lever in the balcony power plant market: Marstek announced the VENUS A at IFA 2025 for EUR 499 (ESS News), at Basic Solar it costs EUR 489 (0% VAT) …

More

the Marstek counterpart VENUS E Gen 3.0 with 5.12 kWh EUR 1,029; Anker SOLIX Solarbank 3 E2700 Pro (2.68 kWh) and Zendure SolarFlow 800 Pro are around EUR 1,000. Other 2 kWh class units at Basic Solar: Hoymiles HiBattery AC 1.92 kWh EUR 549, conow CBE2000 PRO EUR 655, Marstek B2500-D EUR 309. Demand per kleines kraftwerk: storage is seen as the growth driver, in Q4 demand shifts from modules to storage and discount promotions. Competition: sharp price war and strong brands (Marstek, Anker, EcoFlow). Return risk: medium to high (setup, smart meter connection, 60 kg freight item per shop), shipping as class 9 dangerous goods.

Request from the supplier

  • Clarify the sourcing route: Marstek distributor or factory, minimum quantity, price maintenance, EU warehouse and warranty handling (10 years).
  • VDE-AR-N 4105 unit certificate and confirmation that feed-in can be limited to 800 VA for plug-in solar (the device feeds in up to 1.2 kW).
  • Safety test reports for battery and system (IEC 62619 or IEC 63056), UN 38.3 summary, CE declaration of conformity (LVD, EMC, RED) and cybersecurity evidence (EN 18031).
  • Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542: labelling, battery passport preparation (above 2 kWh), registration with stiftung ear (batteries and ElektroG), EU responsible person.
  • Manufacturing site per model (Shenzhen, Jishou, Luxi or Nantong), factory audit, cell origin, dangerous goods packaging (class 9) and a forwarder with ADR experience.
