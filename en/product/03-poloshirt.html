Description

The polo shirt is the standard shirt for site managers, fitters, service staff and reception because it looks professional with customers and in the office while remaining hard-wearing.

More

Quality models use cotton piqué or cotton/polyester of 180 to 220 g/m² with a shape-retaining collar, double stitching and a secured button placket that withstand 60 °C washes. A professional user in Germany expects clean logo embroidery, colour fastness to washing and perspiration, sizes S to 5XL and freedom from harmful substances under REACH. Normatively the EU textile labelling rules for fibre composition and REACH apply; an OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certificate serves as proof, and there is no protective rating under the PPE Regulation.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report on dimensional change after washing, colour fastness (washing, rubbing, perspiration) and pilling of the piqué
  • REACH declaration of conformity or test report on azo dyes, formaldehyde and heavy metals, plus a valid OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certificate with number
  • Laboratory check of fibre composition and fabric weight (g/m²) matching the label under Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011
  • Embroidery or print sample with logo approval and wash test of the decoration
  • Size chart in EU measurements with fit samples in at least three sizes
  • Social audit of the factory (BSCI/amfori or similar) and country-of-origin statement before production
