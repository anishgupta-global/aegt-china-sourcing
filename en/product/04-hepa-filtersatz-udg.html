Description

The HEPA filter set is the wear part of the negative air machine: the H13 or H14 HEPA filter per EN 1822 separates asbestos and fine dust fibres from the extracted room …

More

air before the air is returned to the work area or released outdoors. The aluminium-frame product, format 610 x 610 x 292 mm, uses a glass fibre medium with aluminium or paper separators, polyurethane sealant and an expanded-metal guard; upstream G4 pre-filters extend its service life. The format must match the unit exactly; the Bersi unit B2000, for example, uses a filter of 610 x 610 x 95 mm. After use in the black area, filters and pre-filters count as contaminated and must be disposed of under TRGS 519. Filters and pre-filters are kept in stock per project as sets.

More

Evidence (as of 2 Nov 2026): AS Luftfilter lists HEPA H13 610 x 610 x 292 mm from EUR 193.89 net (MDF frame) or from EUR 278.88 net (MDF frame with aluminium separators); per the scan, H14 cartridges cost EUR 76.90 net (Stuermer) to EUR 172.00 net (TEKA). The Chinese product is around USD 62 per filter, plus freight, duty and test proof. Competition: unit makers (Deconta, enviro, Dantherm/Heylo) with unit-specific frame sizes and air-filter specialists. Return risk: wrong format, frame material (MDF/aluminium) or missing individual test report; ask for the format by unit type and include the EN 1822 test report with each filter.

Request from the supplier

  • Confirm format, frame material (aluminium/MDF) and sealing groove against the original filter or the negative air machine drawing (Bersi B2000: 610 x 610 x 95 mm).
  • Demand an individual EN 1822 test report per filter (serial number, efficiency, leak test); mandatory for H14; have the test lab named.
  • Fix the initial pressure drop at nominal air flow and the effective filter area in the data sheet.
  • Check PU sealant and frame stability; define transport packaging that protects the pleats (carton, wooden case).
  • Test a sample in the unit before series: pressure drop, seal seating, smoke test.
  • Agree marking (type, class, batch), EU responsible person and packaging details (VerpackG).
