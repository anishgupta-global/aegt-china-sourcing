More

China supplies masks, filters and blowers at a fraction of German prices, but only suppliers with a verifiable EN certificate qualify for demolition and asbestos work (TRGS 519).

Respiratory protection clusters in the Yangtze River Delta (Nanjing: PAPR/SCBA makers TECMEN and KELISAIKE; Suzhou: mask maker Sanical; Ningbo/Shanghai: own-brand masks and trading) and in Hubei (Xiantao and Jingzhou: disposable FFP masks and filters); full-face masks and filters additionally come from large manufacturers in northern China (Taiyuan, Shanxi).

1) Certification: Respiratory protection is category III PPE (EU Regulation 2016/425) and is marketable in Germany only with an EU type-examination certificate from a notified body …
More

(EN 140/EN 136/EN 143/EN 14387, PAPR EN 12941 or EN 12942) plus annual surveillance (module C2/D) and CE marking with NB number; Chinese GB standards (GB 2626, GB 30864) and NIOSH approvals (TECMEN FreFlow, 'P100') are not valid in the EU. Request each supplier's certificate number and NB number and check them in the NANDO database; the standards named in the listings are supplier claims only (Safetree cites EN 140:1998/EN 136:1998, Passion Safety inconsistently cites EN 12942 and EN 12941). 2) For asbestos work (TRGS 519) P3 filters or TM3/TH3 powered systems are mandatory – the P300 PAPR half mask (TM3, EN 12942) is a different device class from the 3M Versaflo hood/helmet system (EN 12941 TH2/TH3) used as the German price reference; Dräger X-plore 5500 is sold without filters. 3) Suppliers: according to the Made-in-China audit Zhejiang Safetree is a trading company with its own brand (11 employees), not a plant – have the factory named; Shaanxi Passion Safety has been on Made-in-China for only 1 year; no own website was found for Shanxi Xinhua and Safetree (contact via Made-in-China shop only), hence no phone/e-mail. 4) Excluded: Shanghai Dasheng (all NIOSH approvals withdrawn in 2021 over quality management). Not verifiable and therefore not listed as priced makers: Shanghai Baoya Safety Equipment (SCBA/PAPR, Interschutz 2026 exhibitor; website unreachable) and Xiamen Safety Plus Group (P3-R filters US$0.48–0.65 from 10,000 pcs, 5 employees per audit); Jingzhou Strong (Hubei) is included only as an unverified enquiry candidate. 5) Duty: EU third-country duty 1.7 % for 9020 00 10 (gas masks/masks with replaceable filter) and 9020 00 90 (breathing apparatus, blower units, filters as parts); disposable FFP masks without mechanical parts fall under 6307 90 98 instead – do not confuse; anti-dumping measures were not checked. 6) Prices are list prices (Made-in-China, mostly FOB) excluding freight, customs clearance, testing costs and import VAT; Alibaba prices could not be confirmed on product pages because of captchas, and the WebSearch quota ran out during research (follow-up research on Baoya recommended). 7) Addition of 30 Oct 2026 (P3 filter, FFP3 mask, ABEK1-P3 combination filter): P3 filter – the CN price from Jingzhou Strong comes from earlier research, the product page was behind a captcha, URL not captured; DE price Galaxus.de Dräger 2-pack EUR 25.24 (EUR 12.62 per filter, promotional price). FFP3 – no FFP3 manufacturer price with URL found (FFP2 reference Xiantao Zhongyi US$0.16–0.22 from 30,000 pcs); DE price Moldex 2555 20-pack EUR 114.36 at Kaufland.de (third-party seller, EUR 5.72 per mask); 3M Aura offers on eBay.de were discarded because of an expired best-before date (07/2025) and unclear unit prices; the customs classification of FFP3 with exhalation valve (9020 00 90 rather than 6307 90 98) must be clarified by a binding tariff information. ABEK1-P3 combination filter – neither CN nor DE price found reliably (Portwest P971 ABEK1P3: EUR 64.99 per 4 pcs per droppe.com, gross/net not identifiable and therefore not used as DE price). New makers: Suzhou Sanical (Jiangsu) as well as Xiantao Zhongyi and Jingzhou Strong (Hubei) – certificates and contact data not verified on their own websites; no further reliable manufacturer found for Zhejiang and Shanxi. 5) New in v3: the FFP2 mask from Xiantao Zhongyi (US$0.16–0.22) is evidenced only via an aggregator (valve model; the Global Fund list names Zhongyi only for Type IIR medical masks) – request the EN 149 certificate. The hood PAPR (Kelisaike KL99) names neither CE nor EN 12941 in the listing and a battery of only > 4 h.
