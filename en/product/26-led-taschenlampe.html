Description

The rechargeable flashlight lights up shafts, basements, ceiling voids and plant rooms where site lighting does not reach.

More

Professional users in Germany expect an anodised aluminium housing, at least splash protection, light output measured to ANSI/PLATO FL1 and a battery with protection circuit instead of invented lumen claims. For importers, CE conformity (EMC, low voltage, RoHS), photobiological safety and the transport approval of the lithium battery are what count. The lamp is not suitable for explosive atmospheres unless it carries an ATEX approval.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity and test reports to EN 60598-2-8, EN 62471 (risk group) and EMC from an accredited lab
  • Test record to ANSI/PLATO FL1 (lumens, beam distance, run time) from an integrating sphere, not only data-sheet values
  • IP test report to EN 60529 and drop test on series samples
  • Battery documents: cell manufacturer, protection circuit, UN 38.3 report and safety data sheet
  • German manual with safety notes, spare battery and spare seals available, warranty in writing
  • Clarify registration under ElektroG and battery law as well as GPSR details on the packaging
