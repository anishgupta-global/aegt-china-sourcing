Description

The floor saw cuts expansion joints, openings and separating cuts in concrete, asphalt and screed floors and is the standard machine when floor slabs are opened for services or demolition sections.

More

The petrol engine of about 7 kW drives a diamond blade of 300 to 400 mm, the cutting depth is about 120 mm, and a water tank binds the dust. Professional users expect a torsion-free frame, a cutting-depth indicator with a direction sight, a safe blade guard and readily available wear parts. To be proven are CE marking under the Machinery Directive, conformity with EN 13862, engine approval under (EU) 2016/1628, the noise declaration under 2000/14/EC and a German manual.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC with CE marking (listing claims CE and ISO 9001; have these proven by certificate)
  • Test report to EN 13862 (blade guard, emergency stop, depth stop, tilt safety)
  • Engine emission approval under (EU) 2016/1628 (Stage V) or engine certificate from the engine maker with type-approval number
  • Sound power level LWA under 2000/14/EC marked on the machine and stated in the manual
  • German manual, spare-parts list (belts, bearings, blade flange, water tank) and at least 24 months warranty
  • Diamond blades supplied with oSa mark or EN 13236; test cut on video before loading
