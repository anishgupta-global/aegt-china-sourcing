Description

On demolition and earthwork sites the GNSS RTK rover delivers centimetre-accurate coordinates for setting out, topographic survey and quantity take-off, and in many cases replaces the classic total station.

More

The IMU tilt sensor allows measuring with a tilted pole, for example at building edges, and speeds up capture considerably. Professional users in Germany expect reception of GPS, GLONASS, Galileo and BeiDou, connection to correction services via NTRIP (e.g. SAPOS), ingress protection of IP67 or higher and survival of a drop from a 2 m pole. The radio module and conformity documents must meet EU requirements; Leica Geosystems is the quality benchmark in the German market.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity (RED, EMC) and proof under ETSI EN 303 413.
  • Test protocol for RTK accuracy under ISO 17123-8, ideally with a comparison measurement against a known control point.
  • Confirmation that NTRIP access to German correction services (e.g. SAPOS) and Galileo reception work.
  • Approval of the UHF radio module (410 to 470 MHz) for Germany and the Federal Network Agency frequency list.
  • German-language manual, field software with German coordinate systems (ETRS89/UTM, DHHN2016) and data export (DXF, CSV).
  • Warranty, calibration service and spare-parts supply (batteries, pole) in the EU.
