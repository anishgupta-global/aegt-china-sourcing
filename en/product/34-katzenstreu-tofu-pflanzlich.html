Description

The tofu litter consists of plant-based granules, clumps quickly, binds odours and is clearly lighter and lower-dust than mineral bentonite litter.

More

It is offered to households that want a natural, biodegradable alternative; the pellets are available in several diameters (1.5/2.0/3.0 mm). Buyers expect low dust, firm clumps, odour control and a traceable raw-material base (soy, cassava, pea fibre). The litter is not feed and is traded as a consumer product; packaging must comply with VerpackG and carry disposal information.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Fressnapf lists 85 cat litter items; about 6 of 43 on page 1 are plant-based (Cat's Best, Benek, Real Nature), tofu litter was not visible there.

More

Zooplus, by contrast, has a dedicated “Tofu litter” subcategory with Tigerino Plant-Based Tofu (4.2/5 from 136 ratings): 7 l (4.6 kg) costs EUR 11.49 (3-pack EUR 29.99), i.e. about EUR 2.50/kg – a clear premium over Cat's Best Original 17.2 kg at EUR 26.99 (EUR 1.57/kg). Demand and search volume must be confirmed by keyword analysis before listing (not evidenced in this scan). Risks: high weight (freight per kg), moisture in the carton, “flushable” claim legally delicate.

Request from the supplier

  • Request raw-material declaration (shares of tofu/soy components, cassava, pea fibre), allergen/GMO statement and safety data sheet (MSDS)
  • Sample test values: dust content, clump strength, absorption, odour control, residual moisture and absence of mould
  • Confirm density and bag weight per 6/7 l, bag material (tear-proof, sealed) and pallet/container loading; set freight per kg against retail price
  • Have origin of the soy documented (check EUDR applicability) and request pesticide/contaminant report
  • Use claims (“flushable”, “biodegradable”) only with robust evidence, otherwise omit (unfair competition law)
  • Prepare packaging design with German disposal notice, GPSR labelling and LUCID registration of the importer
