Description

The magnetic 10,000 mAh power bank clings to compatible smartphones with a magnet ring, charges wirelessly at up to 15 W and via USB-C at up to 20 W Power Delivery …

More

and at about 9 mm thick is much slimmer than a classic promotional power bank. Professional buyers expect an aluminium or high-quality plastic housing, clean magnet alignment to the Qi2 standard, protection against overheating, overcharge and short circuit, foreign object detection and an abrasion-resistant logo finish. Because the device transmits inductively, the Radio Equipment Directive (RED) applies in addition to the battery rules (Regulation (EU) 2023/1542, UN 38.3, EN IEC 62133-2); ElektroG registration and GPSR come on top. The name MagSafe is an Apple trademark and should be replaced by magnetic or Qi2 in the shop.

Anker recommends 10,000 mAh, USB-C and wireless charging for promotional power banks, and Werbezeichen names multifunction promotional items (including multi-function charging cables) as a 2026 trend. The China list price of USD 10.10 to 11.50 is far below the branded price tier, without logo print, duty, freight, test reports and retailer margin. Risks: radio and battery conformity (test reports are expensive), returns because of Qi2 compatibility on Android devices without a magnet ring, trademark law if MagSafe is named.

Request from the supplier

  • Test reports for exactly this model: RED (radio, EMC, safety) from an accredited lab, EN IEC 62133-2 and UN 38.3; a mere CE label is not enough.
  • Have the Qi2 claim substantiated (Wireless Power Consortium certificate) or write only magnetic in the shop; test magnet strength and alignment on samples with common smartphones.
  • Measure capacity and heat generation: discharge test at the cable output, 15 W wireless charging for 30 minutes, document housing temperature.
  • Clarify the stand: the listing names it only in the title; the alternatives (M-Queen PDW518A, Ronc RC-WP037) explicitly list an integrated foldable stand.
  • Logo: define print process (screen, pad, laser), Pantone matching, print area, abrasion test and minimum quantity (Ronc names logo from 100 pieces), approve a pre-production sample.
  • EU documents: EU responsible person under GPSR, ElektroG registration, battery labelling, German manual, dangerous-goods shipping (lithium battery).
