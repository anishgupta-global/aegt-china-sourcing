A premium unit offers a stable tracked undercarriage with retractable tracks, a quick coupler for bucket, hydraulic hammer and grab, and an EU Stage V diesel engine. Professional users in Germany expect full CE marking with a declaration of conformity, a German-language manual, documented noise levels and a secured spare-parts supply. Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity and CE marking for the complete machine, naming manufacturer, type and serial number
  • Stage V type-approval of the delivered engine under (EU) 2016/1628; EPA statements are not sufficient
  • Noise test report for the sound power level under 2000/14/EC; LwA on the rating plate
  • ROPS test certificate under ISO 12117 for cab or roll bar
  • German-language manual, spare-parts catalogue and a written lead time for wear parts
  • Written warranty of at least 12 months and a named service partner in Germany or the EU
