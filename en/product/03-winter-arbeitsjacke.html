Description

On shell construction, civil engineering and outdoor installation work, the jacket decides performance and sick days in winter.

More

Good models combine a water-repellent PU-coated outer fabric with 120 to 200 g/m² polyester padding, taffeta or fleece lining, a detachable hood, wind-stopper cuffs and an adjustable hem. A professional user in Germany expects cuts that allow movement with a pullover underneath, durable zips and a PFAS-free impregnation. Normatively the jacket is workwear under EN ISO 13688; if cold or rain protection is claimed, EN 14058 (or EN 342) and EN 343 must be proven, and the jacket then falls under the PPE Regulation.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 14058 or EN 342 (thermal insulation, air permeability) if cold protection is claimed
  • If rain protection is claimed: test report to EN 343 on water column and water vapour resistance (RET)
  • Data sheet of the padding (weight in g/m², fibre type, down-free) and the lining
  • Declaration of PFAS-free impregnation (DWR) and REACH declaration of conformity
  • Durability test of seams, quilting and zip; sample after washing and cold test
  • Size chart in EU measurements with fit samples, also tested with a pullover layer underneath
