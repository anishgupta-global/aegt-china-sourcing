Site power & lighting

Cables, cable reels, site distribution boards, CEE connectors and LED lighting are the basic supply of every demolition, strip-out and refurbishment site and must meet DIN VDE 0100-704 …

More

DIN EN 61439-4 and the DGUV rules in Germany, or the CE directives (LVD, EMC, RoHS) including ElektroG registration. Chinese list prices are clearly below German retail (e.g. cable reel about 32 USD versus 77.49 EUR gross), but the goods can only be placed on the German market with complete documentation and independent test reports.

Cables cluster in Jiangsu (Yixing: Far East Cable; Jiangsu Grand Cable as listing supplier), CEE plugs, distribution boxes and switchgear in Zhejiang (Yueqing/Wenzhou: Wangheng, Kripal, Chint), LED work lights and cable reels in Zhejiang (Ningbo: Korrectunion, Znpon) and LED lighting in Guangdong (Shenzhen, e.g. Zhide Cable as a distribution-box supplier).
