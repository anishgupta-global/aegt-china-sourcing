The capiz chandelier combines discs cut from windowpane oyster shell with a metal frame and spreads a soft, warm light.

More

Request from the supplier

  • Clarify the model number: product title says WH-MI-07, datasheet WH-MC-07.
  • Request the CE declaration of conformity and test reports (LVD/EN 60598, EMC, RoHS); treat UL/SAA/BSCI/BIS in the listing as claims only.
  • Approve the shell batch by sample: colour, thickness, edge finish, adhesive (formaldehyde/VOC).
  • Drop-test the packaging and have spare shell discs supplied.
  • Get tiered prices from MOQ 5 pcs in writing: the listing shows a placeholder range of US$ 1-500 and only the sample unit price of US$ 28.
