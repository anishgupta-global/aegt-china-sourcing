E-commerce potential

Replacement prisms are mainly sold via marketplaces in Germany (sets of 20 in 38 mm, seen on eBay.de from about EUR 10); a dedicated shop item with clear sizing is thinly served.

More

Return risk: wrong size/eyelet, colour deviation, breakage; a dimension drawing and fit notes are mandatory.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix a dimension drawing per prism (length, width, eyelet/hole) and fit to the intended luminaire
  • Request a lead-free declaration and REACH test report (incl. metal eyelets/plating)
  • Check samples for clarity, facet cut and colour fidelity; agree a tolerance of +/-0.5 mm
  • Breakage-proof packaging (individual compartments/foam) and a maximum breakage rate (e.g. 0.5%) in the contract
  • Agree set make-up (20 pcs, bag/carton, EAN, EU responsible person)
