Description

The stationary 10 kWh battery storage (LiFePO4, 48 V) buffers PV power and provides backup power in the group's yards, offices and existing buildings.

More

A professional user in Germany expects fixed indoor mounting, an integrated BMS with fault shutdown, a documented compatibility list with the inverter and traceable cell quality. The storage can only be placed on the market with CE marking, conformity with the EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542, UN 38.3 transport testing and test reports under IEC 62619 or IEC 63056. Installation is subject to the safety requirements of VDE-AR-E 2510-50, registration with the grid operator and in the Marktstammdatenregister.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • UN 38.3 test report and dangerous-goods transport documents (cell and finished unit)
  • Test report under IEC 62619 and/or IEC 63056 from an accredited lab (e.g. TÜV, VDE, SGS) referencing serial numbers
  • EU declaration of conformity with CE under EU 2023/1542 and 2014/30/EU; clarify the battery passport timeline (from 18 Feb 2027) and registration with Stiftung EAR
  • Cell origin (A-grade, manufacturer) and datasheet with cycle count, depth of discharge and warranty terms
  • Compatibility list and communication protocol (CAN/RS485) for the planned inverter, test report if available
  • Warranty of at least 5 years (capacity >= 70 %) and a named service partner in the EU
