More

The candles run on batteries, so there is no cable tangle and no open flame. Request from the supplier

  • Clarify the remote: infrared or RF; for RF require a RED declaration of conformity and frequency list.
  • Define the battery (type, replaceable); for button cells a child-resistant compartment and warning label, marking per (EU) 2023/1542.
  • Fix the specification for the DE market: set of 20 (here 2 sets of 10), timer, flicker effect, warm-white colour; not stated in the listing.
  • Check the clip holders: grip on the branch, candle tilting, edges and plastic fire behaviour.
  • Require test reports (EMC, RoHS, CE) for the specific model; inspect the factory audit (BSCI per storefront).
  • Packaging: EAN, GPSR label, ElektroG WEEE number and battery symbol; logo printing possible per listing.
