Description

The set combines the three chisel shapes used most often in strip-out: a point chisel for breaking up, a flat chisel for slotting and removing material, and a wide chisel for plaster, screed and tiles.

More

The SDS-max shanks (18 mm) lock into common chisel hammers without tools and transmit the full impact energy. The material is alloyed 40Cr quenched-and-tempered steel with a forged tip, designed for reinforced concrete, masonry and floor coverings. Professional buyers ask for a material and hardness certificate, a dimensionally accurate shank profile that fits all common SDS-max machines, and GPSR-compliant labelling and packaging.

E-commerce potential

Chisels are the number-one wear part of every SDS-max chisel hammer; machines often ship with only a point chisel.

More

Amazon.de shows sets from EUR 21.89 (Einhell, 3-pc, product page checked 31 Oct 2026) to EUR 72.39 (Makita, metal case, scan stage); the Bosch 4-pc set has 4.7 stars from about 1,220 reviews (product page 31 Oct 2026: EUR 42.99), Toolbrothers lists Bosch 3-pc at EUR 35.95 (scan stage). The market is a price ladder from second-tier brands to premium; a no-name set has to win on value (40Cr, documented hardness) and clean labelling. Return risk: wrong shank (SDS-plus instead of SDS-max) and breakage at the shank end, so test samples in a continuous-impact test before a series order. The China price is calculated at about USD 3.45–4.98 per 3-piece set (3 × the piece price of a Jieda flat/point chisel, not a set quote), i.e. roughly 17–24% of the net retail price of the Einhell set, before freight, duty, set packing and selling costs; the margin is therefore only roughly documented.

Request from the supplier

  • Test the SDS-max shank profile (Ø 18 mm, groove and locking-groove lengths) on samples in machines of several brands; dimensional deviations cause returns.
  • Request the 40Cr material certificate and a hardness report (HRC per EN ISO 6508-1) for shank and tip, batch per delivery.
  • Run a continuous-impact and breakage test on at least 10 sample sets in your own chisel hammer before triggering the series order.
  • Clarify set assembly: composition (point + flat 25 + wide 40/50 mm), hard case or banderole, EAN, German labelling and GPSR details with the EU responsible person.
  • Negotiate minimums: the supplier states 1 carton (25 pieces) per article number and, on the WP401410 page, a minimum order value of USD 30,000; ask about leeway for a first order.
  • Request the price list with Incoterm in writing (sales@workprotool.com); per the product page 30% deposit, 70% before shipment.
