Description

The kit consists of a solar panel (9 V / 4 W) and a brushless DC submersible pump (4.5–10 V) that runs directly on sunlight: no battery, no mains connection, no cabling to a socket.

More

According to the listing, model RP-SP004A delivers up to 180 l/h and an adjustable spray height of 60 to 90 cm; the scope of delivery includes a bracket, nozzle heads and decorative leaves. The pump suits bird baths, small ponds, water basins and ornamental fountains. High-quality kits stand out through a weatherproof panel with UV-resistant laminate, a fully potted pump, an intake filter against algae and an honest performance figure at defined irradiance.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the number and shapes of nozzle heads (target product: 9 nozzle heads), approve a photo of the scope of delivery.
  • Request a measurement protocol for flow and spray height at defined irradiance (e.g. 1,000 W/m2), not only maximum values.
  • EMC and RoHS test report for pump, panel and cable; confirm the pump's protection class and the panel's weather resistance in writing.
  • Check endurance run (clogging, dry-run behaviour, intake filter) and UV resistance of the panel.
  • Define cable length, mounting (post/float) and packaging; confirm the standard model has no battery.
  • German manual, WEEE marking, EU responsible person (GPSR) and LUCID registration.
