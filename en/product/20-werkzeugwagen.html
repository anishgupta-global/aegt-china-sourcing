Description

The tool trolley keeps tools, measuring equipment and consumables within reach at the workstation in the workshop, warehouse or on site, and can be rolled directly to the vehicle or machine.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a torsion-resistant steel body with powder coating, ball-bearing full-extension drawers with an interlock against opening several drawers at once (anti-tilt), a central lock and swivel casters with brakes rated for the load. Mandatory are stated load limits per drawer and for the trolley, documented corrosion protection (salt spray test to EN ISO 9227) and a REACH-compliant coating; models with electronics such as a digital lock additionally need RoHS and radio-equipment compliance. Spare parts such as drawer slides, casters and locks and a clear warranty should be secured in the contract.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Data sheet with dimensions, sheet thickness, drawer load and total load, plus photos of the slides and casters.
  • Salt spray test report to EN ISO 9227 and proof of coating adhesion (cross-cut) for the powder coating.
  • REACH declaration for the coating; for models with electronics (digital lock) RoHS and radio-equipment evidence.
  • Sample with endurance test of the drawers (slides, anti-tilt, lock) before series delivery.
  • Spare parts offer for drawer slides, casters and locks; German user instructions.
