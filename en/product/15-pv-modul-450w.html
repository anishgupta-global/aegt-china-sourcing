Request from the supplier

  • IEC 61215 and IEC 61730 certificates from an accredited lab (TÜV, VDE, Intertek or similar) for exactly this model
  • Flash list (Pmax per module) of the delivery and PID test report
  • WEEE registration number (Stiftung EAR) of the distributor, or clarification of who takes over the registration
  • Authorisation letter from the brand manufacturer (for Jinko/Trina/JA goods) and serial number list
  • Written clarification whether the price is per piece or per watt, including incoterm and palletising
  • Product and performance warranty with a contact in the EU, plus data sheet and installation manual in German
