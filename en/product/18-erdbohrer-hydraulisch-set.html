Description

The hydraulic earth auger drive uses the mini excavator's hydraulic circuit and replaces hand augers and petrol earth augers for fence, post, planting and foundation work.

More

A hydraulic motor drives the auger through a planetary gearbox; depending on soil and hole diameter, bits from 100 to 300 mm diameter with different tooth shapes are used. What professional buyers look for: a torque that matches the flow and pressure of the carrier machine, a securely supported output shaft, a robust housing and bearing design and a suitable mounting adapter for stick and quick coupler. Before purchase, oil flow (l/min), working pressure and pin size of the target machine must be matched.

Request from the supplier

  • Define the target machine (model, weight, hydraulic circuit): oil flow (l/min), working pressure (bar) and pin/mounting dimensions on the stick; check the model's torque curve and speed against it (YDH2000: 656–2,270 Nm, 30–75 l/min).
  • Clarify the scope of delivery in writing: the title says 'with Drill', but whether bits (Ø 100–300 mm), hoses, couplings and mounting adapter are included in the price is not stated in the listing.
  • Agree warranty (listing: 18 months gearbox, 1 year motor) and spares (seal kit, auger teeth, bit extension); have the brand of the hydraulic motor named.
  • Require CE marking, EC declaration of conformity and German operating instructions for the attachment (Machinery Directive, from 20 Jan 2027 Machinery Regulation); check guarding at the auger.
  • Test one sample unit in continuous operation: leak-tightness, bearing play, oil heating; request the test protocol at delivery.
  • Check the factory: verify the audit report (TÜV Rheinland, ID MIC-ASI2351426 on Made-in-China), the ISO 9001:2015 certificate and the CE certificates shown in the profile.
