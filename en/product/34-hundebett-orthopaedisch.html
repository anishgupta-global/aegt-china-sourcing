Description

The dog bed combines a memory-foam core that adapts to the body and relieves joints with a soft faux-fur plush cover.

More

The cover is removable and, per the listing, machine-washable, and a waterproof layer protects the foam. The bed is available in several sizes (S, M, L, and Jumbo in the description text) and colours and suits dogs and cats. For premium quality, buyers look for foam density and compression hardness, a non-slip base, clean zippers and tested textiles.

Opportunity: a wide price gap between basic and orthopaedic beds; risks: returns due to size and hygiene, bulky volume (ask for compression packing), pollutant and odour issues with foam.

Request from the supplier

  • Get foam density (kg/m³), compression hardness and layer build (memory foam plus base foam) confirmed in writing; photos and dimensions per size S/M/L.
  • Ask for the price per size: the listing tiers (US$13.65 / 8.65) name no size and point to small formats.
  • Request test reports for REACH/azo dyes and odour/VOC; ask for OEKO-TEX or CertiPUR as proof.
  • Test the cover: zipper quality, seams, wash test at 30-40 °C, shape stability, non-slip base.
  • Clarify the waterproof liner and its material (PU/TPU) and how it behaves when washed.
  • Compression/vacuum packing and carton size for sea freight, sample before series order.
