Description

The microinverter converts the DC of up to two solar modules directly at the module into grid-compliant 230 V AC and limits feed-in to the 800 VA permitted for plug-in solar devices.

More

The Deye SUN-M80G4-EU-Q0 works with two independent MPP trackers, integrated Wi-Fi for yield monitoring, fanless cooling and IP67 protection, which makes it suitable for outdoor mounting on a balcony railing. Discerning buyers look for verifiable certificates (VDE-AR-N 4105 unit certificate, NA protection), the rapid shutdown concept and a reliable manufacturer warranty, which per the datasheet is 10 years (15 years when installed in Germany and Austria). For retail, complete documentation matters as much as the technology: declaration of conformity, radio test report, EU responsible person and ElektroG registration.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the VDE-AR-N 4105 unit certificate and NA protection certificate for exactly SUN-M80G4-EU-Q0 (netzwelt: available from the dealer) and check validity.
  • Have conformity with DIN VDE V 0126-95 (rapid shutdown, 800 VA limit, Schuko operation) confirmed in writing.
  • Radio test report (RED) incl. evidence for EN 18031 and EU declaration of conformity; check firmware with German grid code and 800 VA preset.
  • Clarify the sourcing route: authorised Deye distributor or factory, price maintenance/sales restrictions, warranty handling in the EU (15 years only when installed in DE/AT).
  • Define scope of delivery (5 m Schuko cable, bracket, connectors) and agree packaging/labelling (LUCID, ElektroG, GPSR label).
