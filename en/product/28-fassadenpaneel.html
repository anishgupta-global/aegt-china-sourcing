Description

WPC facade profiles consist of wood or bamboo fibres and HDPE and are mounted as profile boards on a substructure in front of the exterior wall.

More

They are rot-proof, need no paint and come in a wood look with sanded, brushed or grained surfaces. The 173 × 21 mm profile in lengths up to 5.8 m is in the size range of common DIY-store profiles (Konsta Nativo: 178 × 24 × 2,200 mm). Professional buyers check fire behaviour and building-law admissibility, thermal expansion, colour fastness and the fixing system (clips, screws, substructure).

Season: building and garden season from spring to summer; target groups homeowners, garden and facade builders, extension and carport projects. Risks: fire protection and building law for higher building classes, colour deviations between batches, bulky goods (lengths up to 5.8 m), complaints from installation errors (thermal expansion).

Request from the supplier

  • Request test reports to EN 15534-1/-5 (flexural strength, water absorption, swelling, thermal expansion, weathering/UV).
  • Have fire behaviour per EN 13501-1 evidenced for exactly this profile; clarify admissibility under the state building code (building class) with the planner or building authority.
  • Fix the recipe in writing (wood content, HDPE, additives, recycled content) as the listing gives two different compositions; confirm hollow or solid build.
  • Check the fixing system: clips, screws, substructure, expansion gaps and ventilation; request assembly instructions in German.
  • Approve colour samples and colour fastness (weathering test); agree batch control for repeat orders.
  • Agree a third-party inspection before shipment (AQL, dimensions, packing of long profiles).
