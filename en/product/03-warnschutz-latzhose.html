Description

The hi-vis bib trouser protects back and kidneys with its bib and stays where it should when bending, kneeling and lifting, which makes it particularly suited to road building, rail work …

More

sewer and pipeline construction and the colder seasons. Quality models use a polyester blend fabric (benchmark engelbert strauss hi-vis bib trousers e.s.motion: 85 % polyester, 15 % cotton) with elastic braces, 50 mm retroreflective bands on the legs, inner and outer bib pockets, pen compartments, rule and cargo pockets and Cordura-reinforced rear pockets. Professionals in Germany expect class 2 marking with pictogram and standard number, an EU type-examination report (PPE category II) per model and adjustable braces in all sizes. The bib trouser is sold as the warmer alternative to the hi-vis work trouser and combined with knee pads, a hi-vis jacket or vest.

Compared with the work trouser, the bib trouser addresses buyers working outdoors in cold, wind and wet; knee pads are the most important cross-sell, plus hi-vis jacket and base layers. Return risk is medium to high (body height, brace length), so show a size chart with body measurements and brace adjustment. Request from the supplier

  • The Kinglong listing is internally contradictory (attributes 'Cotton', 'Anti-Radiant Heat, Flame Retardant', 'Oekotex/En11612' alongside the description 'EN20471 Class 2'; model number WH505 also used on the winter bib listing): have fabric, standard and model fixed in writing.
  • Request the EU type-examination certificate and EU declaration of conformity (PPE category II) for exactly this article with the notified body number; test report EN ISO 20471 class 2 per size.
  • Retroreflective tape with manufacturer proof (e.g. 3M or equivalent), 50 mm width; have reflectance tested on the sample after the wash count stated on the label.
  • Check brace adjustment range, seam strength at brace attachment and crotch, and zip and button quality on the sample; optionally ask for knee pockets for EN 14404 pads.
  • Approve size and body measurement chart (chest, height, inseam) and label with pictogram, standard number, care symbols and EU contact details.
  • Request comparison samples of Hollydare HD9362 and Great Wall SEF-KM25123 and have the Kinglong price tiers confirmed in writing.
