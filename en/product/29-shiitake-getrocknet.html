Description

Dried shiitake mushrooms deliver the typical umami flavour for soups, wok dishes and sauces and are soaked before preparation.

More

Quality markers are whole, thick-fleshed caps of even size, a short stem share, a residual moisture of at most 13 % and a clean condition without mould or insect infestation. The goods are sold in pre-packed 100 g bags; the pack-size ladder in German Asian retail runs from 25 g to 454 g. As a food it needs no CE mark: the importer is responsible for LMIV labelling including the preparation note (“do not eat raw”), traceability and evidence on pesticides, cadmium and lead.

Return risk is low (long shelf life); risks are residues, moisture and mould, and insect infestation.

Request from the supplier

  • Request proof of operation: business licence, scope and own operation; the supplier is listed as “Manufacturer/Factory” but describes itself in its profile as a coordinator working with “specialised producers”: name the farm or drying plant and verify by audit.
  • Lab report per batch from an accredited laboratory (ISO/IEC 17025): pesticides under Regulation (EC) 396/2005, cadmium and lead under Regulation (EU) 2023/915, and sulphur dioxide if used.
  • Measure or inspect moisture (at most 13 %), mould, insect infestation and foreign matter on goods receipt; sample per cap-size grade before ordering.
  • Specify cap size, stem share, thickness, breakage share and colour in writing; sample of the 100 g retail pack including seal seam.
  • Require a food-grade, resealable inner bag with declaration of conformity (Regulation (EC) 1935/2004), batch code and best-before date; approve the German label with preparation note in advance and register with LUCID (VerpackG).
