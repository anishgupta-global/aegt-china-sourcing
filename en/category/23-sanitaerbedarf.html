Sanitary supplies

Sanitary supplies cover toilet and washbasin ceramics, taps, dispensers, hygiene paper and mobile toilets for AE Global Traders's construction sites, refurbishment projects and staff facilities.

More

Ceramics and taps from China are price-attractive, but Germany applies strict standards (EN 997, EN 14688, EN 817) and drinking-water rules that must be proven by test reports before import.

Sanitary ceramics and taps cluster in Foshan (Guangdong), Tangshan (Hebei) and Xiamen/Quanzhou (Fujian); mobile toilets are reportedly more common in Guangdong/Jiangsu (not verified). New on the map: Hengan (Jinjiang, Fujian) for hygiene paper, Solex (Fujian) for taps and showers, and Dongpeng (Guangdong, Foshan per company profile, not stated on its website) for tiles and sanitary ware.
