Request from the supplier

  • Clarify the material: alabaster (gypsum, title) or marble (material field); approve a sample batch.
  • Verify the GS and VDE certificates: test house and certificate number, otherwise treat as a claim only.
  • Check heat build-up and shade thickness with E27 lamps and prescribe LED bulbs.
  • Have weight and ceiling load of the suspension stated; drop-test the breakage-proof packaging.
  • Get the configuration (size, number of lights) per price in writing: range US$ 60-600 at MOQ 1.
