Description

The nylon universal plug is the standard for light to medium fixings of brackets, cable holders, trims and installations in concrete, solid brick and perforated brick.

More

Its quality lies in the polyamide with defined moisture and temperature behaviour and in an expansion geometry that grips securely in different substrates. German professionals expect ETA and CE-assessed plugs to EAD 330284-00-0604 with stated minimum embedment depth and setting instruction; plugs without assessment are only acceptable for minor purposes. Without pull-out tests in the target substrate no plug is released for load-bearing purposes.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Present ETA/CE document; without ETA mark the plug clearly as 'non-structural'
  • Pull-out tests in C20/25 concrete and solid brick (test report of an independent laboratory)
  • Material proof for PA6 (data sheet) and confirmation of virgin material instead of regrind
  • Dimensional check of outer Ø, length and wall thickness on sample
  • REACH declaration and data sheet on temperature and moisture behaviour
  • Sample of 500 pieces before series release
