Description

The additional solar panel keeps battery and 4G cameras permanently ready for use on site where no site power is available and replaces manual battery changes.

More

Professional buyers expect monocrystalline cells with real output under load, a weatherproof housing (IP65) with UV-stable laminate, a sufficiently long connection cable, a swivelling wall bracket and the connector type that matches the camera model (USB-C, micro-USB, DC 5521). Voltage and charging current of the camera must be clarified beforehand (typically 5-6 V), because a wrongly dimensioned panel does not recharge the battery in the low-light season. Legally, photovoltaic modules count as electrical/electronic equipment (ElektroG/WEEE) with registration at stiftung ear; RoHS, REACH and the GPSR also apply.

Request from the supplier

  • Power measurement under load on the sample (characteristic curve at standard irradiance) with test report; confirm rated power 5/6/8/10 W and match voltage (5 V versus 6 V) to the site camera
  • Fix connector type and cable length per camera model (USB-C, micro-USB, DC 5521) and run a charging test on the camera battery, also under overcast sky
  • IP65 test report (IEC 60529) plus UV and ageing evidence for ABS frame and PET/ETFE laminate; bracket made of UV-stable plastic with stainless-steel screws
  • Check original CE, RoHS and REACH declarations; listing and company profile name CE, TÜV and Germany EPR, request registration number and validity
  • Duties of the party placing it on the market: ElektroG registration (stiftung ear), LUCID for the packaging, GPSR information (EU responsible person)
  • Packaging and logistics: single pack 211 × 172 × 48 mm, carton 58 × 49 × 26 cm, confirm pieces per carton and lead time in writing (manufacturer states 15 working days off-season, up to one month in peak season)
