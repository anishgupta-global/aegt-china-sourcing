Description

Anyone working on roads, storage yards or sites with vehicle traffic needs warning clothing that is reliably seen by day and night; the jacket complements the hi-vis vest when wind, rain and cold are added.

More

Quality models use fluorescent polyester oxford with PU coating, 50 mm silver retroreflective bands, sealed seams, a storm flap and an optionally detachable hood. A professional user in Germany expects proof of hi-vis class 2 or 3 per size, a label with pictogram and standard number, and reflectance values that are retained after the stated number of washes. EN ISO 20471 applies; the jacket is PPE category II under Regulation (EU) 2016/425 and needs an EU type-examination by a notified body, plus EN 343 for the waterproof version.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN ISO 20471 stating the class (1/2/3) per size and cut
  • EU type-examination certificate from a notified body (module B) and production control (module C2 or D) under Regulation (EU) 2016/425
  • EU declaration of conformity and marking (CE, pictogram with class and standard number, care label with number of washes)
  • Certificate of the retroreflective material (retroreflection after washing) and of the fluorescent fabric (colour and light fastness)
  • If rain protection is claimed: test report to EN 343 (water column, water vapour resistance)
  • Series sample with wash test and measurement of reflectance after the stated number of washes
