Description

The 5 kg chisel hammer is the tool for precise removal of tiles, plaster, screed and wall chases in interior fit-out, where a heavy demolition hammer would destroy too much substance.

More

With around 1,100 W and roughly 10 J single-blow energy it works in a controlled, low-fatigue way, including wall and overhead work. Professional users in Germany expect a declared vibration value, an auxiliary handle and readily available chisels – with the 17 mm hex shank in the listing this must be clarified before ordering, as SDS-max is the most widespread system in the German trade. Compliance means CE under the Machinery Directive, a test report under EN 62841-2-6, the sound power level under 2000/14/EC and a German operating manual.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC with a test report under EN 62841-2-6 from an accredited laboratory (TÜV, SGS, Intertek) – the general CE/GS claim in the shop is not a model-specific proof
  • Sound power level LWA under 2000/14/EC on the rating plate and a vibration value under EN ISO 28927-10 (m/s², uncertainty K) in datasheet and manual
  • Confirm the tool holder in writing (17 mm hex per listing or SDS-max version) as well as chisel availability and chisels in the scope of supply
  • German operating manual, safety instructions and spare parts list (carbon brushes, hammer mechanism, handles) before ordering; EU plug and 230 V version
  • Sampling with endurance test, written warranty of at least 24 months, Incoterm and HS code in the quotation (negotiate the MOQ of 500)
