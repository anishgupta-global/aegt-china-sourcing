Description

The three-phase hybrid inverter combines PV modules, low-voltage battery storage and grid in one unit and enables self-consumption and backup power for site containers, offices and refurbished buildings.

More

Professional users in Germany expect an efficiency above 97 %, IP65 protection, a backup output, generator input, monitoring and a multi-year manufacturer warranty with German service. Parallel grid operation is only permitted with a VDE-AR-N 4105 unit certificate, registration with the grid operator and an entry in the Marktstammdatenregister. Dealer goods carrying the Deye brand must be checked for authenticity, EU firmware and warranty handling.
