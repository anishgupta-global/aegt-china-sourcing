Description

The half mask with P3 filters is the standard respirator for dust-intensive site work: strip-out, demolition, cut-off grinding and remediation of contaminated building elements.

More

A soft, skin-friendly silicone mask body, a low-resistance exhalation valve and an adjustable multi-point head harness ensure a secure seal and comfort over long shifts. Professional users in Germany expect EN 140 and EN 143 with CE marking including the four-digit NB number, a German-language manual and available spare parts such as valves and head straps. For asbestos work under TRGS 519 suitability must be demonstrated in the risk assessment; as category III PPE the device may only be placed on the market with an EU type-examination certificate.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU type-examination certificate (module B) for EN 140 and EN 143 with number and name of the notified body – cross-check in the NANDO database.
  • EU declaration of conformity and proof of ongoing production surveillance (module C2/D) with validity date.
  • Filter test reports: P3 penetration (≤ 0.05 %) and breathing resistance to EN 143, per batch.
  • German-language instructions for use, spare parts (valves, straps, gaskets) and written warranty terms.
  • Have the plant named (trader or manufacturer), ISO 9001 certificate and samples before a series order for seal/fit testing.
