Description

The headlamp keeps both hands free when working in basements, shafts or roof frames where site lighting fails or is missing.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a widely adjustable, washable headband, a tilt joint with a firm detent, dust and splash protection and a mount for the safety helmet. The mount must not impair the protection of the helmet to EN 397, so the helmet manufacturer's approval must be observed. As importer, AE Global Traders needs CE conformity, battery transport evidence and a German manual.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity and test reports to EN 62471 (risk group) and EMC from an accredited lab
  • Test record to ANSI/PLATO FL1 (lumens, beam distance, run time) instead of data-sheet values
  • IP test report to EN 60529 and endurance test of tilt joint and headband on series samples
  • Battery documents: cell manufacturer, protection circuit, UN 38.3 report and safety data sheet
  • Have the helmet mount supplied with an approval or compatibility statement for common safety helmets to EN 397
  • German manual, spare parts and warranty in writing, plus GPSR details on the packaging
