Description

The workbench is the standard workstation for assembly, repair and equipment maintenance in the workshop and store of a construction and remediation company.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a welded or bolted steel frame with powder coating, a load-bearing top of beech multiplex or sheet steel, ball-bearing drawers with central locking and height-adjustable feet. Important are a stated load capacity with load test, verifiable corrosion protection and wood-based panels with a tested formaldehyde class. Product safety under ProdSG and the substance requirements of the REACH Regulation must be met, together with German assembly instructions and transport-safe packaging.

Request from the supplier

  • Load test report from an independent laboratory (SGS, TÜV or Bureau Veritas) for worktop and drawers
  • Powder coating test report with film thickness and salt spray test to ISO 9227
  • Formaldehyde proof (E1) for the wooden top and proof of timber origin, taking the EU Deforestation Regulation into account
  • REACH declaration of conformity for coating and fittings
  • Reference sample before series production, German assembly instructions and transport-safe packaging (corner protection, sturdy cartons)
