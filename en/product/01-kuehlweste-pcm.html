Description

The PCM cooling vest absorbs body heat through phase-change material that changes from solid to liquid at the chosen switching temperature (here 21 °C), without power, water or noise.

More

Once the packs have melted they solidify again below the switching temperature (fridge or cool box) and are swapped for a fresh set, so a spare pack set belongs in the kit. The reference vest is offered in one size with a buckle system, made of bio-based materials, non-toxic and, according to the shop, flame-retardant. Professional buyers expect a clear statement on cooling duration, washability and compatibility with coveralls and chemical protective suits. Whether the vest is marketed as PPE under (EU) 2016/425 or as a consumer product under the GPSR must be fixed by the manufacturer.

Request from the supplier

  • Binding classification by the manufacturer: PPE under (EU) 2016/425 (then type examination, category, instructions) or consumer product under the GPSR.
  • PCM data sheet: melting point (21 °C, further levels), enthalpy of fusion, filling (bio-based?), leak protection of the packs and safety data sheet.
  • Measured cooling duration (thermal manikin or subject test) at defined ambient temperature and workload, not only a marketing claim.
  • Flame-retardancy evidence with test standard if advertised; textile testing for REACH substances and azo dyes.
  • Washing instructions, sizing concept (one size) and spare-pack availability including lead time.
  • Factory audit and sample with a field test under a coverall (type 3/4/5/6) before series order.
