Description

The street broom is the standard tool for daily site cleaning: it sweeps rubble, sand, grit and dust from access roads, scaffold bases and pavements and helps meet the duty to keep the site safe for traffic.

More

The wooden broom head carries stiff plastic bristles bundled to 400 mm width that withstand coarse dirt and wet conditions, and it is joined to the 140 cm handle by a metal ferrule. Professional users in Germany expect a smooth, splinter-free handle, a handle seat that stays firm and bristles that neither shed nor bend. Imported goods must comply with the EU General Product Safety Regulation 2023/988 and REACH; wooden parts must also be checked against the EU Deforestation Regulation.
