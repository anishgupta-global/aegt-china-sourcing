Surveying & testing

Surveying and testing equipment from China ranges from builder's levels, rotary and line lasers and laser distance meters to total stations …

More

FOB prices for the standard devices are roughly 10–30 % of the gross German prices of comparable Bosch devices (excluding freight, duty, import VAT and testing costs). For AE Global Traders, levels, line/rotary lasers and distance meters are sensible entry products for demolition and strip-out, while total stations and plate load devices should only be used with proven calibration and acceptance by testing bodies.

GNSS surveying and total stations cluster in Guangzhou (Hi-Target, South) and Shanghai (CHCNAV); rotary lasers and simple automatic levels come from the laser/optics cluster in Changzhou (Jiangsu: Laisai, Mount Laser, Skyvictor) and from Shanghai (Apekstool), while light weight deflectometers are built in Nanjing (Jiangsu: T-Bota) and in the testing-equipment cluster Cangzhou (Hebei: Gaotiejian) – not in Zhejiang. New on the map: Suzhou FOIF (total stations) and Nantong Zhicheng (line lasers) in Jiangsu, and Sndway (laser distance meters) in Dongguan, Guangdong.

The static plate load test per DIN 18134 is a different device (hydraulic press with counterweight) and not part of the SKU. 2) Rotary lasers: the laser class to EN 60825-1 must appear on the device and datasheet (aim for class 2 like the Bosch GRL 400 H; from class 3R the duties under OStrV/TROS Laser Radiation apply). CE conformity (EMC, RoHS, Li-ion battery UN 38.3 plus BattG registration, remote control under the Radio Equipment Directive) is borne by AE Global Traders as importer; the Mount Laser listing mentions neither laser class nor CE – request test reports before ordering.
