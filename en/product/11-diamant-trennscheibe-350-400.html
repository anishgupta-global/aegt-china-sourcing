Description

The laser-welded diamond blade is the wear part for cut-off saws, joint cutters and masonry table saws and cuts asphalt, concrete and green concrete wet or dry.

More

The steel core carries diamond segments plus additional protective segments that are meant to shield the blade from core wear in abrasive asphalt. Professional users expect a blade that runs true with an exact 25.4 mm bore, clear marking of diameter, segment height and maximum permitted speed, and a segment bond matched to the material: softer for asphalt, harder for abrasive concrete. Required are conformity with EN 13236, ideally an oSa mark, GPSR-compliant labelling with an EU responsible person and German packaging and usage information.

The market is crowded with Bosch, Goelz and HDV; a private label needs proven tool life and an EN 13236 proof. Return risk: wrong bore (20 instead of 25.4 mm), wrong bond for the material, machine speed limit; a data sheet with material recommendation is mandatory. The China purchase price (Sunny, 350 mm: USD 20–38 from 10 pcs per the Made-in-China listing) is far below all German references, but it is an unbranded blade without a tool-life proof.

Request from the supplier

  • Request an oSa certificate or EN 13236 test report and maximum-speed marking on the core; the listings name no certificates.
  • Confirm dimensions per variant in writing: Ø 350 mm segment 40 × 3.2 × 12 mm (the page elsewhere states 10/12 mm), Ø 400 mm 40 × 3.6 × 10 mm, bore 25.4 mm, segment count including protective segments.
  • Have bond recipe and diamond grade per material (asphalt/green concrete, concrete/reinforced concrete, universal) proven with a cutting-performance test protocol (metres to wear-out).
  • Check the laser weld: destructive test (segment pull-off, core cracks) and measure run-out and flatness of the core on the sample.
  • Ask for price and MOQ ex Xiamen with tiers (samples 1–5 pcs, series from 100 pcs); the listing names T/T and 7–15 days lead time for small orders.
  • Agree private-label printing, single packaging and German labelling (GPSR details, EU responsible person, LUCID number) in advance.
