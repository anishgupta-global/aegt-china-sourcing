Description

The tracked mobile crushing plant moves itself around the site and crushes rock and construction waste on the spot without a concrete foundation.

More

The listing states a 600x900 mm feed opening, an 18-48 mm product size, a dual drive (diesel/electric) with 280 kW and CE and ISO claims. For EU use, a Stage V engine, CE conformity, dust and noise protection and the emission-control permit for operation must be clarified.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the YG-series model and have the jaw crusher configuration confirmed (listing mixes jaw and impact crushers); request a data sheet with operating weight, throughput (t/h) and transport dimensions.
  • EU Stage V engine (Reg. (EU) 2016/1628) for diesel drive; electric drive at 400 V/50 Hz with EU control cabinet (EN 60204-1).
  • CE declaration of conformity, technical file, German operating manual; check guards and emergency stop.
  • Clarify the operating permit under BImSchG and dust and noise protection at the site.
  • Wear parts (jaw plates), spare-parts supply and service in DE/EU; transport by low-loader/container, Incoterm.
