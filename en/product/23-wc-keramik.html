Description

The two-piece floor-standing toilet in white sanitary porcelain is the robust standard solution for staff toilets, sanitary containers and refurbished existing buildings.

More

A washdown pan with elongated bowl, self-cleaning glaze and dual flush keeps water consumption low and cleaning easy. A professional user in Germany expects dimensional accuracy per EN 33, tested ceramics per EN 997, a cistern per EN 14055 with backflow protection, plus spare parts and a German installation manual. Before import, test reports and drinking-water compliance of the flush valve must be proven.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report per EN 997 and EN 33 (water absorption, glaze quality, flushing performance, connecting dimensions) from an accredited laboratory
  • Cistern test certificate per EN 14055 incl. fill valve with backflow protection (EN 1717) and drinking-water suitability
  • Dimensional drawing for the German market (horizontal outlet DN 100, water inlet G 3/8) and sample before series production
  • Manufacturer's declaration of conformity, REACH declaration for the glaze and the factory's ISO 9001 certificate
  • Pre-shipment inspection (AQL 2.5) and breakage-protection packaging (corner protection, palletising)
  • German installation manual, spare parts (seals, fill valve, flush actuator) and a warranty of at least 2 years
