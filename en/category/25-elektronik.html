Electronics

Rugged smartphones, tablets and laptops, construction-site cameras, dashcams and power banks are in daily use on demolition and remediation sites and must withstand dust, water and drops.

More

Chinese manufacturers offer much lower landed prices here, but CE/RED conformity including cybersecurity evidence, the Battery Regulation and GDPR-compliant camera use decide whether the goods can be placed on the German market at all.

Rugged smartphones, rugged tablets, power banks and dashcams cluster in Shenzhen/Dongguan (Guangdong: Ulefone/Gotron, Doogee, Blackview, Baseus), video surveillance and construction-site cameras around Hangzhou (Zhejiang: Hikvision, Dahua); Anker is based in Changsha (Hunan). Added (v3): Emdoor (ONERUGGED, rugged laptops and tablets), Compro Electronics (power banks) and Cwell (rugged laptop listing) are also based in the Shenzhen area (Guangdong).
