Demolition & Breaking Equipment

Demolition and breaking equipment from China is most interesting for AE Global Traders in electric breaker hammers, core drilling rigs, wire saws and compressors, where list prices sit well below German dealer prices.

More

What decides the purchase is the EU paperwork (CE, Outdoor Noise Directive 2000/14/EC, Stage V engine), without which the machines cannot be used on German sites.

Electric chisel and rotary hammers cluster in Jiangsu (Qidong/Nantong) and Zhejiang (Yongkang/Wenzhou, among others Baichun), towable diesel screw compressors in Zhejiang (Quzhou) and Fujian (Quanzhou/Xiamen), diamond wire saws and core drilling technology in Fujian (Quanzhou, Luojiang District with Huada and ToolWits) and Zhejiang (Hangzhou), exoskeletons in Shanghai (ULS Robotics, Crimson Dynamics) and Shenzhen.

(hand-held concrete breakers/demolition hammers are Annex I machines with a limit value and conformity assessment by a notified body) and vibration declarations (2002/44/EC) – cheap goods without these proofs cannot be used; the Hornbach price (Pattfield 1700 W / 14.2 kg: EUR 169 incl. VAT = EUR 142.02 net, incl. flat/pointed chisel, auxiliary handle and trolley case) is below the CN listing (USD 175-185 per piece ≈ EUR 157-166, machine only, MOQ 300) – the relation is real, not a unit error; own import only pays off with volume and a private label; 30 mm hex chisels are less common in Germany than SDS-max. 2) Compressors: diesel engines of mobile machines need EU Stage V type approval (Reg. (EU) 2016/1628) – standard Chinese engines mostly do not meet it; in addition Pressure Equipment Directive 2014/68/EU, noise marking 2000/14/EC and road approval of the chassis; the CN listings (USD 4,300-4,700) state neither engine approval nor CE – import only with a Stage V engine (Kubota/Yanmar/Deutz/Cummins EU); the DE comparison is a private eBay sale (EUR 14,210, 5 m³/min / 8 bar, no VAT shown), branded machines (Atlas Copco/Kaeser) only on request. 3) Core drilling rigs: the DE price field is already occupied by Chinese import brands (VEVOR EUR 273.90 gross = EUR 230.17 net, set with stand, 3200 W); the CN listing (Hangzhou Shenghua, motor DBM22H 2200 W) contains no stand – the SKU price of USD 565-820 is the sum of motor (USD 350-590) and stand VKP250 (USD 215-230) from two listings of the same supplier, without drill bit, and is clearly above VEVOR (different power and quality class; ask the supplier for a set offer); wet drilling requires a PRCD safety switch (DGUV) – the motor listing mentions PRCD, other CN goods usually do not include it; classification 8467 21 99 is an assumption (stand-guided rigs may be classified differently) – obtain a binding tariff information. 4) Duty: third-country duty rates TARIC (as of 28.10.2026, origin CN): 8467 29 85 = 2.7%, 8467 21 99 = 2.7%, 8414 40 90 = 2.2%; no anti-dumping measures found; plus 19% import VAT. 5) Exoskeletons: ULS Robotics has no EU office, CE/ISO is evidenced only via a third-party source (Exoskeleton Report) and not on its own website; active exoskeletons fall under machinery law (from 2027 Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230); Hypershell (Shenzhen) is consumer goods and was not checked. 6) Excluded/not verified: Hongwuhuan (website hongwuhuan.com only via JavaScript, no contact data reachable), KEN (not checked), Liutech (Atlas Copco subsidiary, not a Chinese manufacturer in the sense of the brief);
