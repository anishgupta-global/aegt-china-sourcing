Description

The day set bundles the consumables for a short asbestos job in one carton: three each of coverall type 5/6 (category III), FFP3 masks with valve …

More

nitrile gloves and overshoes for three entries, plus one asbestos bag (120 l, marked), cloth tape for taping gloves and overshoes and one asbestos warning sticker. The structure follows the set principle common in the market (Otto Fischer 'day set easy XL' with 15 pieces, Vandeputte/SINGER asbestos kit, OEG 5-piece). All components are category III PPE and must each hold an EU type examination. The set replaces neither the risk assessment nor competence under TRGS 519; depending on the procedure a full-face mask or powered respirator may be needed. It suits short jobs with low fibre release (maintenance, repair), not large-scale remediation.

Request from the supplier

  • Request certificates per component individually (EU type examination certificates for coverall type 5/6 and FFP3, test report for gloves); include only certified components in the set.
  • Clarify assembly: packing of the sets at the supplier on request or assembly in Germany from the catalogue items; packing list per carton.
  • Asbestos bag: check printing and marking to specification and film thickness (80 µm and above) on a sample.
  • Define size split (coverall M-XXL, gloves 8-10) and minimum quantities per size.
  • Check batch and shelf-life details (masks, gloves), PPE marking, EU responsible person and GPSR details.
  • Check warning sticker (size, German text) and carton marking.
