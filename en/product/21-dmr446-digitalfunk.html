Description

DMR446 uses the digital DMR standard (Tier I) on the PMR446 frequencies and delivers clear speech without noise up to the edge of range, which helps amid machine noise on demolition sites.

More

Like PMR446 it can be used in Germany without application or fees if the antenna is fixed and no more than 0.5 W ERP is transmitted; DMR Tier II devices in licensed bands, by contrast, need a frequency assignment from the Bundesnetzagentur. A professional device offers IP67, a display with channel and group indication, group and individual calls and a headset connector. AE Global Traders must demand CE/RED, a German manual, firmware updates and an EU service address, and ensure the device cannot be programmed to foreign frequencies.

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under RED 2014/53/EU with test reports (radio, EMC, safety) from an accredited lab; notified body number if harmonised standards were not fully applied
  • Written confirmation that the delivered model is DMR446/Tier I with a fixed antenna and max. 0.5 W ERP and does not allow programming to foreign frequencies
  • Interoperability test with existing DMR446/PMR446 devices from other manufacturers on samples
  • IP67 test report and drop test
  • Firmware update policy, programming software in English or German, spare parts for at least 5 years
  • German manual, warranty of at least 24 months, EU service partner and WEEE/battery registration of the importer
