Description

The cross-line laser projects horizontal and vertical reference lines in rooms and suits drywall, tiling, façade substructures and installation work after strip-out.

More

The 12-line design (3 × 360°) provides plumb and level in one device; a pendulum with transport lock self-levels the lines within ±3–4°. Green light is much more visible to the human eye than red; in the German trade, laser class 2, a pulse mode for receivers, 1/4″ and 5/8″ tripod threads and a rubber-protected housing are standard. CE conformity (EMC, RoHS), UN 38.3 tested batteries and the importer's WEEE registration are mandatory.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report on laser class to EN 60825-1 (class 2) with a photo of the warning label and power output in mW
  • EU declaration of conformity with EMC (EN 61326-1) and RoHS test reports; the listing mentions neither CE nor FCC nor RoHS
  • UN 38.3 test report and battery datasheet; details on battery and WEEE registration
  • Accuracy record with serial number (line deviation at 5 m and 10 m, pendulum transport lock)
  • German manual, target plate and accessories in a case; negotiate a 24-month warranty instead of 1 year
  • Binding tariff information (BTI/vZTA) on classification (90153010 or 90314990)
