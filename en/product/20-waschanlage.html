Description

The gantry wash system cleans vans, cars and small trucks of the company fleet after site use and keeps dust, clay and concrete residue away from the yard.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a robust stainless steel or galvanised gantry structure, a PLC control with German user interface, a protection rating of at least IP55 on the nozzle gantry and a clean separation of water, chemical and electrical areas. Decisive is conformity under the Machinery Directive, the Low Voltage Directive and the EMC Directive, with the electrical equipment tested to EN 60204-1. In Germany, wastewater treatment, oil separator and the water-law permit must also be clarified before the system is ordered.

Request from the supplier

  • EC declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC, 2014/35/EU and 2014/30/EU stating model and serial number
  • Electrical test report to EN 60204-1 (protective earth, insulation) and circuit diagram with parts list
  • Proof of the IP55 protection rating on the nozzle gantry and of the corrosion resistance of frame and nozzle lines
  • German user interface and operating manual, data on water, wastewater and chemical connections for the permit
  • Spare parts list (pumps, nozzles, frequency converters), service commitment in Germany and a warranty of at least 24 months
