Description

Ground control points anchor aerial images and scan data in the coordinate system: they are laid out before the flight, measured in with the RTK rover and used as reference in processing.

More

A high-quality set is printed matt and glare-free, carries a high-contrast black-and-white pattern with an unambiguous centre and clear numbers, can be pegged down windproof through eyelets, and stays flat and legible after transport and rain. The market example from ArduSimple is made of polyester mesh with an anti-reflective coating, measures 61.5 × 61.5 cm and contains ten markers numbered 0 to 9. In sourcing, print quality, dimensional accuracy and UV resistance matter more than the unit price.

Request from the supplier

  • Identify the supplier by enquiry first: no verified GCP factory was found in this research; obtain samples from at least two suppliers.
  • Define the print file (centre point, contrast, numbers, size about 60 cm) and approve a sample print with a matt, glare-free finish.
  • Measure dimensional accuracy and flatness after transport and rain on a sample; agree the tolerance in writing beforehand.
  • Eyelets reinforced and tear-resistant? Test pegging on the sample.
  • Have UV and colour fastness and the anti-reflective finish documented (e.g. test report to ISO 4892-2).
  • Field test with a drone flight and RTK measuring-in: are the markers detected reliably in the processing software? Appoint an EU responsible person under GPSR and clarify packaging for LUCID.
