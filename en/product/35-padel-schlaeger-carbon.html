Description

The padel racket has a carbon-fibre face (3K, 12K or 18K selectable) with a soft EVA foam core and an impact-resistant frame and weighs 360 g +/- 10 g according to the maker.

More

The listed OEM model B05 is available with custom EVA colour, decals and an anti-slip grip, samples from 2 pcs. Discerning buyers check weight and balance tolerance, EVA density, hole pattern, grip tape and surface finish and, only if claimed, conformity with FIP rules. For EU sales, GPSR information and a REACH-compliant grip (PAH) are decisive.

E-commerce potential

Padel is growing strongly in Germany: Padelfinder counts more than 2,000 courts at almost 600 locations according to Statista (09/2026) versus 130 courts in early 2023 (figure from the category scan).

More

In February 2026 Decathlon promoted entry rackets at EUR 74.95-79.99 (NOX X-HERO EUR 74.95, KUIKMA Hybrid Metal EUR 79.99) and pro models up to EUR 209.90 (STIGA AXE 3; Bullpadel Vertex 05 Comfort 26 EUR 199.99), a wide band for private labels. Brands such as NOX and Bullpadel dominate; private label suits clubs, hotels and the entry segment. Return risk moderate (weight/balance preferences); carbon quality claims (12K/18K) must be provable.

Request from the supplier

  • Written weight and balance tolerance per batch (listing: 360 g +/- 10 g), measurement protocol per lot.
  • Have the EVA specification (density/hardness; clarify '17 deg' in the listing) and carbon grade (3K/12K/18K) documented.
  • PAH/REACH test report for grip tape, coating and plastic parts.
  • FIP conformity only if advertised: request dimensions and approval proof.
  • Two samples (possible per listing) with impact, shock and surface-adhesion tests before series release.
  • Define OEM elements (decal, logo, cover, carton) and EU marking under GPSR.
