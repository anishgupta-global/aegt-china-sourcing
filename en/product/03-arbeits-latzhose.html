Description

Bib overalls protect chest and back from dust, draughts and mortar splashes and are the alternative to work trousers on shell construction, drywall and demolition jobs with a lot of bending and kneeling.

More

Quality models use cotton canvas or cotton/polyester blend of 260 to 320 g/m², with adjustable straps and clip buckles, an elastic back panel and Cordura-reinforced knee-pad pockets. A professional user in Germany expects sizes 44 to 64, triple stitching at stress points, a durable YKK zip and freedom from harmful substances under REACH. Normatively the bib overalls are workwear under EN ISO 13688; with inserted knee protection EN 14404 also applies.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report on tensile strength, seam strength, dimensional change after washing and colour fastness of the fabric
  • REACH declaration of conformity or test report on azo dyes, formaldehyde and heavy metals
  • For knee-pad pockets: declaration of conformity of the knee protection to EN 14404 and Regulation (EU) 2016/425
  • Size chart in EU measurements with fit samples (regular, short and long sizes) before production
  • Durability test of strap buckles and zip, plus proof of origin of trims (YKK, Cordura)
  • Series sample with wash test (5 washes at 60 °C) for dimensional change and seam stability
