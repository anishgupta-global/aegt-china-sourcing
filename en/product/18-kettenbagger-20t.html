Description

The 20 t class crawler excavator is the standard machine for building pits, sewer and pipe trenches and bulk excavation in civil engineering.

More

A premium unit offers an EU Stage V diesel engine, a stable tracked undercarriage, a hydraulic quick-coupler system with auxiliary hydraulic circuits for hammer and grab, and a comfortable cab with automatic climate control and rear and side cameras. Professional users in Germany expect full CE marking with a declaration of conformity, ROPS/FOPS-tested operator protection, documented sound power levels and a German-language manual with a secured spare-parts supply. At AE Global Traders it covers earthworks and excavation on larger sites where mini and compact excavators reach their limits.

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity and CE marking for the complete machine, naming manufacturer, type (e.g. SY215C) and serial number
  • Stage V type-approval of the delivered Cummins engine under (EU) 2016/1628; EPA statements are not sufficient
  • Noise test report for the sound power level under 2000/14/EC; LwA on the rating plate (not stated on the SANY Europe page)
  • ROPS/FOPS test certificate of the cab under ISO 12117-2 and ISO 10262 (not stated on the product page)
  • German-language manual, spare-parts catalogue and a written lead time for wear parts
  • Written warranty of at least 12 months and a named service partner in Germany or the EU
