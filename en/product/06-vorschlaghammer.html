Description

In demolition and strip-out, the sledge hammer is the tool for wall breakthroughs, breaking up screed and tiles and driving chisels and stakes.

More

The head is drop-forged from 45# carbon steel, hardened on the striking faces and securely fixed in the eye with a wedge and adhesive; the hickory handle absorbs shocks, while the fibreglass handle is splinter-free and weather-resistant. Professional users in Germany expect design and testing to DIN 1042, an immovable head fit, burr-free and crack-free striking faces and the GS mark. Imported goods must meet GPSR and REACH and be labelled with an EU responsible person.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to DIN 1042 including head-fit test (pull-off and impact test) and GS certificate from an accredited body
  • EN 10204 type 3.1 material certificate for the 45# steel (C45) and hardness record of the striking faces
  • Proof of head retention (steel wedge plus adhesive) and a sample cut through the handle eye
  • REACH/PAH test report for lacquer and handle; for hickory, check timber origin (FSC/PEFC) and the EU Deforestation Regulation
  • GPSR details: EU responsible person with address, batch number, German instructions for use and safety
  • Approval sample with piece-weight tolerance (± 3 %) before series production
