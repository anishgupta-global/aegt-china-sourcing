Description

The support frame takes the weight of the demolition hammer and guides it for floor and wall work, so that arms, shoulders and back are relieved and the vibration load falls.

More

With a hammer of the 30 kg class it shifts most of the load into the frame and makes long, even chiselling practical in the first place. Professional users in Germany expect a stable steel frame, a mount that fits the hammer neck and clear information on permissible hammer types and load capacity. Compliance means a documented risk assessment under EN ISO 12100, a CE-compliant design as machine accessory and a German instruction manual.

Request from the supplier

  • Written release of the permissible hammer types and hammer weights (manufacturers and models) with a dimensioned drawing of the mount
  • Proof of load capacity and stability (load test of the frame) as well as material data for frame and welds
  • Risk assessment under EN ISO 12100 and CE-compliant marking as machine accessory
  • German instruction manual with notes on set-up, working position and vibration reduction
  • Sampling with your own hammer, warranty of at least 12–24 months, Incoterm, MOQ and HS code in the quotation
