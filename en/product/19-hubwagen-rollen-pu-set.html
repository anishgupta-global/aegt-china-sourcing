Description

The load roller (fork roller) carries the rated load of the hand pallet truck at the fork tip and is the most common wear part: polyurethane tread, size 80 x 70 mm, bearing seat 47 x 14 mm …

More

(corresponds to the 6204 deep-groove ball bearing with 20 mm bore). PU runs quieter and is more floor-friendly than nylon and wears less, whereas polyamide is more heat-resistant (per tente). Quality markers are a firmly bonded tread that does not separate from the core, a precisely made bearing seat and dimensional accuracy in diameter and width, because neighbouring sizes such as 82 x 80 or 85 x 80 do not fit. Commercial buyers expect a load rating per roller, REACH compliance and a dimensional drawing; most trucks use four rollers (tandem) or two rollers, the steering wheels (180 or 200 x 50 mm) are a separate part.

Request from the supplier

  • Clarify exact dimensions per pallet truck model: dia. x width (80 x 70, variants 82 x 80, 85 x 80), bearing seat 47 x 14 mm (bearing 6204, 20 mm bore) and installation width; test a sample on the original pallet truck (manufacturer noblelift in the catalogue: 19-handhubwagen).
  • Clarify bearings: with or without (the listing says “Without Bearings”), two 6204 bearings per roller, branded quality, sealing; request the price separately.
  • Tread and core: specify PU hardness to ISO 868, have core material (steel, cast iron, polyamide) and PU-to-core adhesion (separation test) documented.
  • Load rating per roller: the listing states 800 kg, another manufacturer only 400 kg for 80 x 70 mm; demand a test report with load class. The listing also names DOT/ECE certificates, which do not apply to rollers.
  • Rolling test under load: noise, floor protection, wear after a trial run; REACH test report (SVHC) for the PU material.
  • Assembly and labelling: set of four in a bag with dimensions, GPSR details, LUCID registration; approve a production sample before release.
