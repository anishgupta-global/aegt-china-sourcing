Description

The pull-up bar mounts in the door frame without drilling and allows pull-ups, hanging and core exercises at home.

More

It is made of steel with foam and TPR elements on grips and contact surfaces. Decisive are the stated load capacity (target: approx. 150 kg), a secure locking mechanism, protection of the door frame and a German safety guide. Because a fall can cause injury, load testing and GPSR documentation are a prerequisite for sale, not a nice-to-have.

Request from the supplier

  • Request a static load test report with a test load well above the rated load (target rated load 150 kg)
  • Check locking and fixing: slip/rotation test, welds, door frame protection pads
  • Define mounting type (clamping or hooking) and suitable door frame dimensions (width, depth, trim height)
  • German safety and mounting instructions with warnings (door frame condition, load limit) and GPSR labelling
  • Confirm plant location by audit (company address Hefei, Anhui; plant 550 m2 per profile); clarify product liability insurance
