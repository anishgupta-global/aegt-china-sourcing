Description

The Aoro M6 is a rugged Android satellite phone with Tiantong-1 and BeiDou connectivity plus DMR and GSM, intended for use without mobile coverage.

More

With IP68 and MIL-STD-810G it would in principle suit harsh construction and demolition conditions. However, the Tiantong satellites (3 GEO) only cover Asia-Pacific and the device needs a China Telecom SIM, so there is no satellite reception in Germany. A professional user in Germany expects CE/RED conformity, a German manual and service, for which no EU proof was found; for sites without network, Iridium or Thuraya from German specialist dealers is the right choice.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under RED 2014/53/EU with test reports from an accredited lab (do not place on the market without them)
  • Written confirmation of the satellite service for Europe (Tiantong is Asia-Pacific only); do not order without it
  • Check radio approval and frequency use in Germany (Bundesnetzagentur) for the Tiantong/BeiDou bands
  • Lab test reports for IP68 and MIL-STD-810G, not just the manufacturer's claim
  • German or English manual, warranty of at least 24 months, spare battery/parts availability and WEEE registration of the importer
