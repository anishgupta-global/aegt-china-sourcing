Description

The 10 kg chisel hammer bridges the gap between the light class and the 15 kg demolition hammer: it breaks concrete, masonry and thick screed quickly and can still be guided well above shoulder height.

More

A 1,500 W motor and a hammer mechanism in the class of around 16–17 J are built for shift operation in strip-out, and the SDS-max chuck ensures broad chisel availability in Germany. Professional users expect effective vibration damping, a declared vibration value and available spare parts for carbon brushes, hammer mechanism and handles. Compliance means CE under the Machinery Directive, a test report under EN 62841-2-6, the sound power level under 2000/14/EC and a German operating manual.

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC with a test report under EN 62841-2-6 from an accredited laboratory (TÜV, SGS, Intertek) for the specific model DZG10
  • Manufacturer datasheet with weight, single impact energy and tool holder (confirm SDS-max) – so far only dealer data seen

  • German operating manual and spare parts list (carbon brushes, hammer mechanism, handles); contact person/distributor with spare parts shipping to Germany
  • Sampling with endurance test, written warranty of at least 24 months, Incoterm, MOQ and HS code in the quotation
