Description

The hydraulic breaker is mounted on the excavator via the quick coupler and breaks concrete, masonry and screed during demolition and strip-out.

More

A premium unit has a robust hammer body sealed against dust and mud, a pressure accumulator for consistent impact energy, blank-firing protection and replaceable tools (chisels, moil points). Professional users expect a mounting matched to the excavator with the right oil flow, sound power levels marked under EU law and a German manual with a maintenance plan. When working on components contaminated with hazardous substances, such as asbestos, TRGS 519 applies in addition.
