A replacement battery extends the operating time of a radio fleet and replaces worn-out packs without buying new radios.

More

Professional buyers require a UN 38.3 report, the origin of the cells, a documented capacity measurement and proper labelling under the EU Batteries Regulation. More

per the market scan a commercial eBay seller offers a third-party battery for Hytera at EUR 16.39 (page not retrievable, not re-checked). The China price (US$19.60-20.99) is therefore close to the third-party selling price, so margin only arises through volume, private label or bundles (battery + charging station). Risks: fit and compatibility returns, trademark law, dangerous-goods shipping (Li-ion) and the registration and labelling duties of the Batteries Regulation.

Request from the supplier

  • Request a UN 38.3 test report, safety data sheet and proof of the 1.2 m drop test; clarify dangerous-goods shipping as UN 3480/3481 in advance.
  • Require the cell origin (cell brand, maker) and an IEC 62133-2 report; re-measure capacity on a sample (target: >= 95% of 2,000 mAh).
  • Trademark law: packs must not carry Hytera logos; use 'compatible with' only as a notice. Test fit and contacts on the original radio (BL2006 vs BL2008/BL2503).
  • Batteries Regulation: CE/capacity/separate-collection marking, manufacturer and importer details, EU responsible person, registration with stiftung ear.
  • Fix a 12-month warranty and RMA process by contract; agree batch traceability and a cycle test (e.g. >= 300 cycles).
