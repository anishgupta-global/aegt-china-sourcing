HVAC, Heating & Water Pumps

Air conditioners, construction heaters, dehumidifiers and pumps keep demolition, strip-out and refurbishment sites dry, heated and workable, above all after water damage …

More

during screed and plaster drying and in winter construction. China supplies solid series products at much lower purchase prices, but CE conformity, electrical safety (ingress protection, testability under DGUV Regulation 3) and, for burners, flue-gas routing must be proven before ordering.

Pumps cluster in Zhejiang (Wenling/Taizhou), air conditioners in the Pearl River Delta (Foshan and Zhuhai in Guangdong), and construction heaters in Shandong (Qingdao) and Zhejiang (Ningbo). Added as Zhejiang neighbours: Ningbo Keycool (air conditioners), Ningbo SingFun (diesel heaters) and Zhejiang Deye (dehumidifiers), each taken from marketplace or quotation listings; neighbours in Guangdong and Shandong have not been researched yet.
