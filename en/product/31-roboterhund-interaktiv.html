Description

The interactive robot dog walks on four legs, reacts to touch on the head and to sounds, barks, plays songs and can be programmed with simple command sequences via the remote control.

More

All functions run offline on the device – no app, cloud, camera or data transfer – a clear advantage for children's toys and data protection. Discerning buyers look for protected pinch and trap points on the legs, a safe battery compartment with USB charging, volume-limited sound and robust test reports to EN 71 and EN 62115. The item complements robot, STEM and camera toys in the range as a gift from about EUR 35.

Request from the supplier

  • Request EN 71-1/-2/-3 and EN 62115 test reports from an accredited lab for the exact model (OC0363705); the certificates named in the listing (incl. EN 60825, HR4040) are manufacturer claims and must be backed by report numbers.
  • Confirm offline operation: no app, no Bluetooth/Wi-Fi, no cloud; voice control local only (microphone without data transfer) – otherwise RED/cybersecurity and GDPR/child data protection apply.
  • Moving parts: check pinch and trap points on legs/joints to EN 71-1; prove age grading from 3 years, small parts and sound pressure level.
  • Radio remote: RED test report (2.4 GHz, EMC, cybersecurity) or proof that the remote uses infrared only.
  • Battery and charger: IEC 62133-2, UN 38.3 report, CE/LVD charger; clarify Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542, ElektroG (stiftung ear) and LUCID.
  • Private label and contract: logo/packaging in German, GPSR marking (EU responsible person, batch); anticipate the new EU Toy Safety Regulation (applies from 1 Aug 2030: digital product passport, PFAS/bisphenol bans) contractually.
