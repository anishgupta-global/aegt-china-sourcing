More

Professional buyers look for a radio-compliance file (RED), battery and charger evidence, a robust ABS build and clean edge finishing. Return risk medium to high (battery/charger faults, radio range, flip mechanism), so sample endurance testing and battery quality decide.

Request from the supplier

  • Request test reports for KSL736600/-601: EN 71-1/-2/-3, EN 62115 and RED (EN 300 328 plus EMC) from an accredited lab; check the declaration of conformity.
  • Battery: demand cell maker, chemistry (NiMH or Li-ion), protection circuit, IEC 62133 report and UN 38.3 evidence for sea freight; charger with CE/LVD.
  • Test waterproofing on the sample (title says amphibious), plus endurance run, flip test and 2.4 GHz radio range.
  • Plan registrations: ElektroG (stiftung ear), battery under BattG, VerpackG/LUCID; name manufacturer and EU responsible person (GPSR) on the carton.
  • Approve German manual and warnings (charging, water, age grade); pre-shipment inspection per AQL with a function test of every sampled unit.
