Request from the supplier

  • Reconcile the piece list: the listing states 18 pieces in the title but 19 in the text; have a photo and dimensions per item confirmed.
  • Confirm the stainless-steel grade (e.g. 304) in writing and require a migration report to LFGB / Regulation (EC) 1935/2004 plus metal release (CM/Res(2013)9) from an accredited lab.
  • Handle material (silicone / plastic): obtain heat resistance in °C and testing to Regulation (EU) 10/2011 or the BfR recommendation.
  • Inspect the case: aluminium frame, latches, lining; drop test and weight per set.
  • Agree packaging with German labelling (GPSR, EU responsible person, cleaning advice) and the tariff classification of the set.
