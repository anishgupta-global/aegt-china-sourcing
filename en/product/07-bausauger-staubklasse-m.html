Description

The dust class M construction vacuum is the minimum requirement on German sites for extracting mineral dust and other hazardous dusts with an occupational exposure limit from 0.1 mg/m³.

More

It is typically operated with rotary hammers, sanders, long-reach sanders and cut-off grinders and switches automatically with the tool via the tool socket. Professional users expect testing to EN 60335-2-69 with a separation efficiency of at least 99.9 %, automatic filter cleaning, low-dust emptying (disposal bag/Longopac) and a consistently sealed filter system. For asbestos and class H substances, a separate H-class unit with HEPA filter to EN 1822 remains necessary.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 60335-2-69 with stated dust class M (separation efficiency ≥ 99.9 %) from an accredited body; class M on the rating plate
  • Filter test report (main filter, filter bag) and replacement filter part numbers
  • Evidence of low-dust emptying (disposal bag/Longopac) and of automatic filter cleaning
  • EU declaration of conformity (2014/35/EU or 2014/30/EU, 2011/65/EU), 230 V version with Schuko plug (CEE 7/7)
  • Written confirmation of the manufacturing site and of serial-number/batch marking
  • Spare-parts and service commitment in the EU (filters, hose, motor unit)
