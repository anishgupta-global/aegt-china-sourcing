Description

The hydraulic demolition shear cuts steel beams, profiles, reinforcement and pipes in the strip-out of halls, bridges and industrial plants without an open flame …

More

a clear advantage for fire protection and hot-work permits. As an attachment it works via quick coupler and the excavator's auxiliary hydraulics; cutting force, jaw opening and own weight must match the lifting capacity and hydraulics of the carrier. Professional users in Germany expect wear-resistant, replaceable cutting blades, a traceable sizing for the carrier, line-burst protection and complete documentation under the Machinery Directive. Compliance means a CE declaration of conformity for the interchangeable equipment, a German operating manual and test proofs for the hydraulic lines.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • CE declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC for the demolition shear as interchangeable equipment with risk assessment (EN ISO 12100) – CE and ISO 9001 in the listing are only self-declarations
  • Written carrier assignment (excavator weight, oil flow, operating pressure, quick-coupler type) with a datasheet on cutting force, jaw opening and own weight
  • Material certificates for cutting blades and shear jaw (steel grade, hardness) as well as availability of spare blades
  • Pressure test of the cylinder and test proofs for the hydraulic hoses; line-burst protection
  • Test-cut video or factory acceptance on a steel beam (e.g. IPE/HEA) stating the profile
  • German operating manual, warranty of at least 12–24 months, Incoterm (FOB/CIF) and HS code in the quotation
