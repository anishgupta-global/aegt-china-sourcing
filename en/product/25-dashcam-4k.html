Description

The dashcam documents trips of vans and trucks to the site and can provide evidence in accidents and damage disputes; the version with rear camera and 12/24 V input also fits tipper and container trucks.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a supercapacitor instead of a battery (heat resistance in the vehicle), stable recording from -20 to +70 °C, GPS and G-sensor, and continuous operation with loop recording. Legally, permanent recording is delicate: under BGH VI ZR 233/17 permanent recording without cause violates data-protection law, although recordings may still be usable in civil proceedings; short ring buffers with accident lock are standard and employees must be informed. The device must also be RED-compliant and e-marked for vehicle use (UN ECE R10).

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under RED 2014/53/EU with test reports (Wi-Fi/GPS, EMC, safety) for the exact model variant
  • E-mark or test report to UN ECE R10 for vehicle EMC; evidence for 12/24 V operation and voltage transients (ISO 7637-2) and temperature testing (ISO 16750)
  • Energy storage: proof of supercapacitor or, for a battery, test report to EN IEC 62133-2 and UN 38.3 test summary (fire risk in a hot vehicle)
  • Data-protection functions: short ring buffer, accident lock, switchable microphone, no mandatory cloud, GDPR-compliant app; agree on use with the works council or inform employees
  • German user manual, at least 24 months warranty, replacement memory card (high endurance) and a named EU responsible person (GPSR)
