E-commerce potential

Stiftung Warentest tested 48 electric toothbrushes in November 2025 (headline: thorough cleaning for less than 50 euros, https://www.test.de/elektrische-Zahnbuersten-im-Test-4621863-0/) …

More

Notino lists Oclean (Flow S from EUR 46.50) next to Philips Sonicare 5300 (EUR 82.90), and at Saturn the Oclean Flow S costs EUR 49.90. The price anchor of about EUR 15-50 leaves room for a private label; replacement heads drive repeat purchase. Risks: brand trust (Oclean, Philips, Oral-B), medical claims are off limits, quality variance in battery and motor.

Request from the supplier

  • Confirm model D67 and scope of delivery (number of brush heads, travel case, charging cable) in writing; the listing states neither.
  • Clarify the contradiction: page title '15 mode', description '5 Cleaning Modes'.
  • Request test reports: CE (LVD/EMC), RoHS, REACH, IPX7 per EN 60529, battery per IEC 62133-2 and UN 38.3; FDA/FCC are irrelevant for the EU market.
  • Brush head compatibility and bristle material: bristle hardness, end-rounding, colour indicator; buy own replacement heads at a separate price.
  • Samples: vibration frequency, noise, battery discharge after 30 days, water-tightness test after 100 charge cycles.
  • Limit claims to cosmetic oral care, no therapeutic/gum promises (medical device boundary).
