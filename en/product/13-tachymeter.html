Description

The reflectorless total station combines angle and distance measurement for setting-out, as-built survey and quantity measurement in demolition and strip-out: façade points …

More

ceiling heights and cut edges can be captured without a prism and without scaffolding. Professional instruments offer a dual-axis compensator, 2–5″ angular accuracy, IP66 protection and field software with setting-out, free-stationing and measurement programs. In Germany, clients and testing bodies expect traceable calibration, a test report to ISO 17123-5 and a laser classification to EN 60825-1, because the reflectorless measuring laser usually reaches class 3R. For official surveys such as building registration, the publicly appointed surveyor (ÖbVI) remains responsible; own instruments serve internal setting-out and quantity take-off.

Request from the supplier

  • Factory calibration and test report to ISO 17123-5 with serial number; counter-check by a DAkkS-accredited body in Germany
  • Test report on laser class to EN 60825-1 (measuring laser, sighting laser) and note on OStrV duties for class 3R
  • EU declaration of conformity with RED, EMC and RoHS reports; statement of the radio frequencies used
  • German manual and German-language field software (setting-out, free stationing, DXF/CSV export)
  • Service commitment in Europe (calibration, spare parts, firmware) – CHCNAV has an EU headquarters in Budapest, Hi-Target, FOIF and South do not
  • Warranty of at least 24 months, spare batteries and a statement on the tariff code (BTI/vZTA)
