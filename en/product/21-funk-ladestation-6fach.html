Description

The 6-bay charging station charges a complete radio fleet overnight: six radios are simply placed in the bays …

More

and every position charges Li-ion-appropriately at 4.2 V and 500 mA. The pockets are individually replaceable and the power supply (5 V / 4 A) has an exchangeable plug for international use. For construction sites, events, hotels and warehouses it is the standard complement to the radio set because it saves charging cables, mix-ups and single power supplies. Professional buyers look for an exact pocket fit for the radio series, a CE-compliant power supply with ecodesign evidence and secured spare-part supply for the bays.

E-commerce potential

Add-on sale with high basket value in sets: the market scan shows Retevis 6-packs with a 6-bay station from EUR 164.99 (Kaufland.at marketplace) …

More

and at Onedirect the 6-pack G15 PRO + multi-charger for EUR 1,047.70 gross. Singly, the Retevis station C9059H costs EUR 79.00 gross in the German Retevis shop, the Midland station C1251 EUR 237.94 (Onedirect, 2 Nov 2026). Opportunity and risk: the station is model-specific and fits only RT48/RT648; it sells in a bundle with the radio or with a clear compatibility list. The margin depends on dealer/OEM prices because the web-shop list price (US$54-88) is close to the German selling price.

Request from the supplier

  • Check the pocket geometry on a radio sample (RT48/RT648): contacts, latching, charging all six positions at once, housing temperature.
  • Power supply: CE/LVD declaration of conformity, EN 62368-1 test report, ecodesign evidence (EU) 2019/1782, EU plug (type C/F).
  • Clarify dealer price and MOQ (web-shop ladder: 1 unit US$87.99, 10 units US$54.10 each), private label/OEM label and packaging.
  • Have the protection features per datasheet (overcharge, short circuit, over-temperature) documented with the charging curve of a series sample.
  • GPSR details: EU responsible person, type/batch marking; spare-part commitment for individual bays (pockets).
