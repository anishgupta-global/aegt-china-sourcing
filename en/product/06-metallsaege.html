Request from the supplier

  • Check blade length (300 mm), frame adjustment and blade tension on a sample; run the tightening knob in an endurance test
  • Define the supplied blade (material, teeth per inch) and order spare blades with it
  • Check die-cast aluminium handle and paint for REACH conformity (Cd, Pb, Ni release)
  • Confirm the unit price in writing (listing: USD 1.38 from 48 pcs, USD 1.34 from 480 pcs)
  • Clarify GPSR labelling (manufacturer, EU responsible person) and German packaging details
