Description

The cordless 48 W nail lamp cures UV and LED gels, builder gels and top coats within seconds.

More

The auto sensor starts the cycle as soon as a hand is inserted; the removable base plate creates room for toenails and pedicures. The built-in battery frees the device from the wall socket, which matters for mobile nail artists, travel and home use. At premium level, discerning buyers look for even curing without heat spikes, dual wavelength (365 + 405 nm), a cleanly finished reflector chamber and complete CE/EMC/RoHS documentation including photobiological testing.

Request from the supplier

  • Request test reports (LVD/EMC, EN 62471, EN 60335-2-27) for exactly model ES-H16 from an accredited lab (SGS/TUV/Intertek); do not accept listing certificates unverified.
  • Battery: obtain capacity (mAh), cell type, UN 38.3 report and MSDS for transport (Li-ion contained in equipment).
  • Fix timer steps, sensor response, LED count/wavelengths and lifetime in the data sheet (not stated in the listing).
  • Order samples (MOQ 2): curing with common gels, heat build-up, base plate, finish; confirm tier price from 200 pcs.
  • Clarify location: company text says Foshan, FAQ mentions a plant near Jiangmen; verify the CTI audit report (ID QIP-ASI261510) on the CTI website.
  • Packaging with German manual and GPSR details; ensure the importer's ElektroG/battery/LUCID registrations.
