Description

Wooden cutlery belongs to every site, topping-out and canteen catering order, since single-use plastic cutlery has been banned in the EU. Good cutlery is sanded splinter-free …

More

tastes neutral, does not break when cutting soft food and is packed dry and mould-free. Professional buyers in Germany expect FSC- or PEFC-certified wood with traceable origin and evidence of food contact compliance under Regulation (EC) 1935/2004. Coatings or wax are only permitted if they are not plastic within the meaning of the Single-Use Plastics Directive.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Declaration of conformity under Regulation (EC) 1935/2004 and test report from an accredited lab on food contact (wood, wax or coating explicitly named).
  • Request in writing the wood species (birch), country of origin and evidence of legality and deforestation freedom (EUDR/FLEGT check per TARIC).
  • Valid FSC or PEFC chain-of-custody certificate; verify the licence number in the database.
  • Declaration that no plastic coating is applied, so that the goods do not fall under the plastic cutlery ban.
  • Check the sample for splinter freedom, breaking strength when cutting, odour, moisture content and mould.
  • Manufacturer status (factory or trader), hygiene certificate (e.g. ISO 22000) and German labelling on the packaging.
