Description

The toilet cabin made of factory-prefabricated EPS sandwich panels covers sanitary needs on demolition and refurbishment sites where no fixed connection exists.

More

A steel frame, metal-faced panels on both sides and a lockable door provide a stable, easy-to-clean room. A professional user in Germany expects a documented fire class of the panels (EN 13501-1), testing of the cabin per EN 16194, electrical installation per DIN VDE 0100-704 and equipment per ASR A4.1. Note: this is a prefab EPS cabin, not the common HDPE type; the applicable standard and tariff position must be clarified bindingly before ordering.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report per EN 16194 or written clarification of which standard applies to the prefab product
  • Declaration of performance/CE marking of the sandwich panels per EN 14509 with fire class per EN 13501-1 (check the EPS core critically)
  • Electrical conformity: CE (low voltage, EMC), IP rating and connection per DIN VDE 0100-704
  • Dimensional drawing, lifting and transport evidence (crane eyes, load capacity) and wind-load/stability data
  • Leak test and drinking-water suitability of the fresh and waste water tanks, if included
  • German assembly and operating manual, spare parts (lock, ventilation, lighting) and warranty
