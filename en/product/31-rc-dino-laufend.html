Description

The remote-controlled dinosaur walks across smooth floors, roars, lights up and releases water mist at the touch of a button – a toy with a visible show effect that demonstrates well in videos.

More

High-quality models feature robust ABS joints, a rechargeable battery with USB charging, a sealed water tank with cleanly separated electronics and edge-free, contaminant-tested surfaces. As an electric toy with radio remote control it falls under the Toy Safety Directive, EN 62115 and – at 2.4 GHz – the Radio Equipment Directive (RED); warnings and instructions belong on the product or packaging in German. An original design without copying licensed film dinosaurs is essential.

Request from the supplier

  • Request EN 71-1/-2/-3 and EN 62115 test reports from an accredited lab for the exact model (RAC5284296); 'EN71/ASTM' in the listing is a manufacturer claim only, ASTM is not valid for the EU.
  • Clarify the control type (2.4 GHz radio, infrared or button only): for radio a RED test report incl. cybersecurity and EMC; the listing says 'Spray-controlled', remote control is not explicit.
  • Water module: separation of water and electrics (IP protection), tank sealed and safely fillable by children; check age grading 6+/8+.
  • Clarify power supply (Li-ion battery or cells): for a battery IEC 62133-2, UN 38.3, CE charger, Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542; ElektroG (stiftung ear) and LUCID.
  • Design freedom: no copy of licensed film dinosaurs; check shape, joints and pinch points (EN 71-1) on the sample.
  • New EU Toy Safety Regulation (in force since 1 Jan 2026, applies from 1 Aug 2030: digital product passport, PFAS/bisphenol bans) to be anticipated in the supplier agreement. GPSR marking (EU responsible person, batch) in German.
