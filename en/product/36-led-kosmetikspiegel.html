Description

The stand mirror combines a normal view with 3x magnification for details such as eyebrows, eyeliner and lips; the dimmable LED light is operated by a touch sensor.

More

The built-in 2,000 mAh battery makes it cordless, and the 360-degree rotation allows free positioning. The maker describes a waterproof construction for bathroom use; no IP class is stated. High-quality mirrors are characterised by distortion-free glass, a natural light colour with high colour rendering, a stable joint and clean edge finishing. Request from the supplier

  • Request test reports (LVD/EMC, EN 62471, if applicable EN 60598-1) for F001-007; the listing names CE, EMC, LVD, ERP, WEEE, RoHS, REACH but no reports.
  • Clarify applicability of Ecodesign (EU) 2019/2020 and energy label (EU) 2019/2015 to the LED light source (listing mentions 'ERP').
  • Fix the 5x variant, light colour/colour temperature, colour rendering (CRI) and brightness steps; check mirror glass for distortion.
  • 2,000 mAh battery: UN 38.3 report, MSDS, charging port (USB-C?) and charging time; advertise an IP class only with a test report.
  • Reconcile company data: Made-in-China shows establishment 2019 and 22 staff, the text says 'since 1995'/18 years; MOQ 10 pcs in the listing vs 50 pcs in the company profile; verify audit ID QIP-ASI251419 on the CTI website.
