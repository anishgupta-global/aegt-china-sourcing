Description

The P2 particle filter is the standard replaceable filter for reusable half masks and protects against solid and liquid particles, for example when sanding, sawing or spraying water-based paints.

More

It retains at least 94 % of the test aerosols (P2: penetration max. 6 %); the R mark means reusable up to the consumption limit. Professional users expect filters that fit the bayonet or thread of their own mask, with CE marking including the notified body number and batch and expiry marking; the filter-and-mask combination must be approved.

E-commerce potential

Add-on to reusable half masks: Hornbach sells the 3M particle filter 5925PRO10 (P2/FFP2, 20 pcs) for EUR 49.95, i.e. EUR 2.50 per filter, and the P3 filter 5935PRO2 for EUR 17.95 per pair.

More

Filters are tied to the mask brand (fit), so the opportunity lies more in universal bayonet and bundles with a mask.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EU type-examination certificate to EN 143 with NB number and cross-check it in NANDO; the listing only says “EN143:2006” and gives no certificate.
  • Fix the connection system (bayonet/thread) and mask compatibility in writing; test samples for seal on your own mask model.
  • Require a batch test report: penetration (P2 max. 6 %), breathing resistance, activated carbon content for combination filters.
  • Single sealed packs with batch number, expiry date and German instructions; ask for a P1 variant and the price per pair/piece.
