Gadgets

Gadgets covers the small everyday and promotional items that are needed constantly on construction sites and in the office: power banks, USB flash drives, lanyards, multitools and flashlights or headlamps.

More

These items can be sourced from southern China with a company logo at low cost, but battery products and lamps are subject to strict EU requirements that must be secured with test reports before every order.

Promotional gadgets cluster in Shenzhen (USB flash drives, power banks) and Dongguan (lanyards) in the Pearl River Delta in Guangdong. The flashlight brands Nitecore and Olight are also based in Guangdong. The two ballpoint pen suppliers evidenced here (Seven Star, Becol) are based in Ningbo (Zhejiang).
