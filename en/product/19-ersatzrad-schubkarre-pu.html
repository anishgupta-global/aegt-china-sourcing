Description

The flat-free wheelbarrow wheel replaces the pneumatic wheel without modifying the barrow: a solid tyre of foamed polyurethane sits on a plastic rim with a ball bearing …

More

(20 mm axle bore) and measures 400 mm in diameter (tyre size 4.00-8). It can neither burst nor lose air, which makes it the answer for building sites, gardens and rental fleets where punctures drive downtime. Quality markers are an even foam density, a cleanly moulded tyre free of voids, a bearing without play and dimensions that match the axle of the target barrow (hub length, bore). Commercial buyers expect a documented load rating, a REACH proof for the tyre material and GPSR-compliant labelling with an EU responsible person.

The price band of flat-free 400 mm wheels is approx. EUR 28-40 against an FOB price of USD 6.20-6.80 (MOQ 200). Seasonal goods (March to June), small, parcel-friendly and a wear part with repeat purchase. The main risk is fit: axle bore, hub length and tyre width must match the barrow, otherwise returns; a dimension table and compatibility notes for the target barrows reduce the rate. Competition is strong (DIY chains, Amazon, eBay); the advantage for specialist retailers lies in a set with matching axle and screws.

Request from the supplier

  • Load-rating proof (static and dynamic) for the version ordered: the listing states 100 kg (plastic rim) or 150 kg (metal rim), the market standard is 200 kg. Request a test report with rim type, tyre hardness and serial number.
  • Fit: match diameter, tyre width, hub length and axle bore (20 mm) against the target barrows and test a sample on 19-schubkarre-stahl.
  • REACH test report (SVHC) for tyre and rim material; the manufacturer names ISO 9001, BSCI as well as REACH, PAH and CE tests, the reports have not been seen.
  • Tyre quality: even foam density, no voids or cracks, tyre-to-rim adhesion (pull-off test), specify hardness to ISO 868, have cold behaviour at -10 °C tested.
  • Bearing: ball bearing instead of plain bushing, sealing and greasing; measure bearing play on the sample.
  • Packaging and labelling: single pack with dimensions, GPSR details (manufacturer, EU responsible person), LUCID registration; approve a production sample before release.
