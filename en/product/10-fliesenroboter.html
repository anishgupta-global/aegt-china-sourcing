Description

The autonomous paving robot lays large-format tiles in thin-bed mortar on level sub-floors by itself and spares the tiler from lifting heavy slabs over large areas.

More

The reference unit is the Partner Robotics P900 with up to 18 m²/h, joint deviation below 0.5 mm and a 180 Ah battery for more than six hours of operation. Professional users in Germany expect execution to DIN 18157 and DIN 18352, a sub-floor flat to DIN 18202, CE conformity under the Machinery Directive and a German manual. At around 640 kg it suits load-bearing new-build and large refurbishment floors, not small rooms.
