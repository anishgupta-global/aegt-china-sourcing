Description

On strip-out, refurbishment and asbestos sites the mobile tower offers a large, safe work platform for ceiling, wall and installation work and can be assembled and moved without tools.

More

Frames of aluminium profiles, platforms with trapdoor, toe boards, guardrails and lockable castors provide stability and low weight in transport. Professional users in Germany expect components to DIN EN 1004-1 with manufacturer marking, a German assembly and use manual per EN 1298 and an instructed person for erection and dismantling. A GS mark and test reports from an accredited body are usual for branded goods and should also be demanded from an importer.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to DIN EN 1004-1 (stability, platform and frame loads) from an accredited test body
  • German assembly and use manual per EN 1298 with permitted configurations and heights
  • Permanent component marking (manufacturer, year, EN 1004-1) on frames, platforms and outriggers
  • Function test of castors, brakes, platform latching and trapdoor on a sample
  • Material certificate for aluminium alloy and connectors, spare parts supply commitment
  • GS certificate or third-party surveillance by a test body, and warranty terms
