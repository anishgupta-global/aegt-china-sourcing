Description

The dust-barrier door is attached to the door frame with tape without a frame and separates the work and living area dust-tight.

More

The transparent PE film (100 µm) with a sewn-in zipper operable from both sides allows passage without removing the film; the size 220 x 120 cm fits standard doors. It is a disposable item for renovation, sanding and painting work and complements PE protective film, cover sheeting and cloth tape. For work with hazardous substances it is no substitute for an airlock under TRGS 519 (assessment). Quality markers are even film thickness, a durable zipper seam and clean packaging.

Standard stock in DIY ranges at a low price point: Hornbach lists the film door 220 x 120 cm for EUR 8.95 and the non-woven door for EUR 9.95, Triuso 212 x 112 cm (100 µm) for EUR 10.99 …

More

Hinrichs 2-pack for EUR 19.99 and a 10-piece dust-protection set for EUR 9.99 (as of 2 Nov 2026); also OBI, Kaufland (2-pack, complete sets with telescopic pole about EUR 65-160 per search snippet), Lidl (Parkside) and baustoffshop.de (ReinWerk). Season: renovation in spring and autumn (assessment). Competition is price-driven (own brands, marketplace); differentiate via multi-packs, included double-sided tape and sets with a dust wall. Return risk: torn zipper, film too thin; state film thickness and mounting instructions clearly.

Request from the supplier

  • Re-measure film thickness on the sample (100 µm, tolerance at most +/-10 %) and check size 220 x 120 cm and zipper length.
  • Zipper: cycle test (at least 500 openings), pull quality, jamming; confirm double pull.
  • Ask for a version with pre-applied adhesive strips (self-adhesive) or enclosed double-sided tape and test adhesion on wallpaper and paint.
  • Packaging: 1 pc per poly bag with band; check suffocation warning for film.
  • GPSR: name and address of the EU responsible person, article and batch number; agree VerpackG/LUCID registration.
  • Clarify price tiers at 1,000 and 5,000 pcs and lead time (supplier states one month); samples before series.
