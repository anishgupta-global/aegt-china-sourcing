Description

The 10,000 mAh power bank keeps smartphones, tablets, radios and handheld meters ready for use even without site power; at 22.5 W it charges modern devices quickly.

More

Professional users in Germany expect real rather than inflated capacity figures, a sturdy housing, short-circuit, overvoltage and over-temperature protection, and safe behaviour under impact and heat in the company vehicle. Legally the power bank counts as a lithium battery: it needs CE (low voltage, EMC), UN 38.3 transport testing, labelling under the EU Battery Regulation and registration of the importer for battery and waste-electronics take-back. Cell quality and rated capacity must be proven by a test report, not by what is printed on the case.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN IEC 62133-2 from an accredited lab for the cell and the finished power bank (model and capacity identical to the offer)
  • UN 38.3 test report incl. test summary from cell and pack manufacturer (prerequisite for air, sea and road transport)
  • CE declaration of conformity (low voltage, EMC, RoHS) with exact model designation
  • Discharge test on a sample batch: real usable capacity at 5 V/9 V compared with the printed mAh figure
  • Clarify importer duties: labelling under (EU) 2023/1542, registration with Stiftung EAR (WEEE) and a battery take-back scheme; at least 24 months warranty
