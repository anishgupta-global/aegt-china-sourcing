Description

The calendar holds 24 small surprises made of soft TPR, from squishies to fidget minis, in a printed carton box with numbered doors.

More

It picks up the squishy trend and gives children a small gift on each of 24 days without confectionery. Commercial buyers look for a varied content pattern without duplicates, a sturdy carton design and a clear age grading. Every content item is a toy subject to CE marking; age grading and warnings follow the weakest part, and the packaging must comply with the German Packaging Act (VerpackG).

Request from the supplier

  • EN 71-1/-2/-3 test reports for each of the 24 content items and the complete pack; ask for a matrix content to report, limit the number of articles
  • Fix age grading and small-parts warning by the weakest content item; German warning texts in a legible font size on the box
  • Exclusion list: no water beads, magnets, button cells; pure toy calendar without confectionery (ban on food combined with toys)
  • Carton box: mono-material, printing inks, disposal notes, LUCID registration and system participation; approve a sample of the carton design
  • Fix content mix per door and weight; agree samples and carton concept on the Yiwu trip, fix the order window May-July 2027
  • GPSR labelling: EU responsible person, batch and German instructions on the carton and, where possible, on the contents; anticipate the new Toy Safety Regulation in the supplier agreement
