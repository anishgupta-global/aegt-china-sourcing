Description

The aluminium mounting stand carries framed PV modules on a flat roof, garage roof, in the garden or on the balcony parapet and sets the tilt angle (15-25 degrees at the supplier) for more yield.

More

The construction in aluminium with small parts in aluminium or stainless steel stays corrosion-resistant and needs no roof penetration when ballasted. Quality markers are a traceable structural proof (manufacturer data: wind up to 80 m/s, snow 1.4 kN/m2), clamps for common frame heights and a clear statement of how many modules a set covers. For the German market, installation instructions, ballast data and GPSR labelling also count.

E-commerce potential

With about 49% four-module systems in 2026 (kleines kraftwerk), mount demand per customer rises.

More

Prices in DE: Hopergy stand M1C-H360 EUR 22.90 per module (Basic Solar, 0% VAT), Basic Stand flat roof set for 1 module EUR 33 instead of EUR 49, Sunshare Ray flat roof EUR 35, Profinal balcony mount with structural proof EUR 89; at Kaufland generic aluminium sets are EUR 52.99-72.99 per search hits (page returned 403), tink lists EcoFlow mounts from EUR 19.90 to EUR 132.90. Light (Hopergy set 1.9 kg) and easy to ship; the main risks are structural proof and wind load as well as fit to frame height (many sets for 30 mm), hence returns for wrong module size. Season: installation mainly in spring, but orders run all year with the installed base growth.

Request from the supplier

  • Define the set scope in writing: how many legs/supports and clamps belong to one piece, for how many modules does the ENF price apply (panel number 2)?
  • Request a structural and wind load proof for the specific configuration (tilt, building height, ballast) with a calculation report per Eurocode.
  • Material and surface proof: alloy (e.g. AL6005-T5), anodising layer thickness, stainless steel grade of the screws (A2), salt spray test.
  • Clarify suitable module clamps for frame heights of 30 to 35 mm or 28 to 50 mm; test a sample with a German module.
  • Agree packaging, German installation instructions, GPSR data and CBAM data availability (emission data of the aluminium profiles) with the supplier.
