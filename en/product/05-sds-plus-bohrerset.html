Description

With five bits from 5 to 10 mm the set covers the typical diameters for dowels and concrete screws and fits any SDS-plus rotary hammer.

More

Carbide tips with four cutters drill cleanly with little tendency to wander, and the flute removes drilling dust reliably and protects the bit from overheating. The set is the obvious accessory to concrete screws, frame and nylon dowels: matching drill and fastener come from one source, and the hole fits the manufacturer's specification. Buyers in Germany expect clean tip brazing, concentric running, legible diameter and length marking and a REACH-compliant carbide grade.

Drills are wear parts with repeat purchase and fit as a bundle with concrete screws and dowels; return risk lies in confusing SDS-plus with round shank and in quality scatter of the carbide tip. Request from the supplier

  • Fix the set composition in writing (e.g. 5/6/6/8/10 mm with lengths 110 or 160 mm), SDS-plus shank with four grooves per drawing
  • Evidence carbide grade and brazing: material certificate (Co content, hardness), brazing quality, run-out and diameter tolerance per batch
  • Drill test in a concrete test block (e.g. C20/25) against Bosch and HiKOKI sets: drilling progress, service life, hole diameter, wander
  • Check certificates (ISO 9001, CE/RoHS per listing are self-declared): obtain copies with number and validity
  • Marking on each bit (diameter, length), German labelling of the box, EAN, EU responsible person (GPSR), LUCID
