Power tools

Power tools are the daily working equipment in demolition, strip-out and renovation – from angle grinders, rotary hammers and reciprocating saws to site vacuums.

More

For AE Global Traders the unit price matters less than CE/GS conformity to EN 62841, German documentation and spare-parts supply, so brands with an EU subsidiary (FLEX, Kress/Worx) take precedence over plain OEM imports.

Power tools cluster in the Yangtze Delta: Jiangsu (Nanjing/Chervon, Suzhou/Positec, Qidong-Nantong/Dongcheng – also home to Nantong Change), Zhejiang (Yongkang/Jinhua as the hardware capital for OEM angle grinders, rotary hammers and vacuums – home to Wuyi Krain; Ningbo/Yuyao with Goldsen; Hangzhou with GreatStar) and Shanghai (KEN).
