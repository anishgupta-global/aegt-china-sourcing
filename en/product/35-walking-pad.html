Description

The walking pad is a flat, foldable treadmill that slides under a desk or sofa and is designed for everyday walking.

More

The listed model M4538 uses a 0.75 HP continuous-duty motor, a diamond-pattern belt with anti-slip side tracks and an LED display for speed, distance, time and calories; it can be controlled via app. Discerning buyers look at continuous motor power, noise level, maximum user weight, running surface and a robust test report to EN ISO 20957-6. EU sales require low-voltage, EMC and RoHS compliance, ElektroG registration and an EU responsible person under GPSR.

On testbedarf.de the listed Amazon.de offers range from about EUR 80-195 (no-name with incline) up to about EUR 450 for the WalkingPad C2; the bestseller THERUN sits at EUR 89.99. The China list price (USD 74.99) is already in the range of that price point before freight, duty (2.7%) and VAT, so without brand, service and EU warranty the margin is thin. Cross-sell to the height-adjustable desk (cat. 28). Return risk: bulky shipping, noise complaints, motor and belt claims; plan for a test report and EU spare parts.

Request from the supplier

  • Request a test report to EN ISO 20957-1/-6 plus LVD/EMC/RoHS reports for model M4538; the supplier's 'EN957' claim is outdated. Verify the TUeV Rheinland audit ID MIC-ASR2431394 on the TUeV site.
  • Datasheet: continuous and peak motor power, maximum user weight, running surface, noise level in dB, standby consumption.
  • EU power adapter/plug with CE, German manual and warnings, GPSR details (EU responsible person, type/serial number), clarify ElektroG registration.
  • Samples with endurance run, folding and belt tests; check app and data protection (GDPR, server location).
  • Put spare parts (belt, control board, remote) and EU warranty handling in writing (motor 2 years per supplier).
  • Compare the 0.8-6 km/h variant (USD 59.99 per Ypoo listing) and competing suppliers.
