Description

The hand saw is the all-round saw for carpenters, drywall fitters and DIY users: the long blade cuts structural timber, battens, boards and panel materials quickly and with direct control of the cut.

More

Professional users expect a flat-set spring steel blade of about 0.9 mm, hardpoint teeth (extra-hardened, preferably ground on three sides) that stay sharp in softwood and chipboard, an ergonomic plastic handle with a non-slip surface and a clean tooth guard in the packaging. Imported goods need GPSR-compliant labelling with an EU responsible person and a REACH-compliant handle (PAHs, phthalates).

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the teeth: ground on three sides, hardpoint/induction-hardened, 7 TPI pointed teeth (the listing says 'ground on two sides') – sample and cutting test in softwood and chipboard
  • Blade: 0.9 mm #65Mn, hardness 54–58 HRC with record to ISO 6508-1, flatness and set (no warping), rust protection (oil film or coating)
  • Handle: ABS + TPR, proof of PAH and phthalate freedom (REACH), firm riveting or screw fixing to the blade
  • Packaging: tooth guard or blister, 24 pieces per carton, GPSR details (EU responsible person, batch), German notices, VerpackG/LUCID
  • Factory proof: the business type of Ningbo Jingyan is not shown in the profile – check manufacturer and trading status and SGS audit report no. QIP-ASI2331692, and order a sample before series
