Description

Made of cold-rolled steel, this shop shelving is a modular system of perforated uprights, back panel and adjustable shelves.

More

Per the listing it is supplied single-sided as a wall unit or double-sided as a free-standing gondola. The European 'Tegometall-style' build is meant to accept common brackets, hooks and accessories; compatibility must be checked on a sample. Professional buyers look for the shelf load rating per EN 16121, stability (wall anchoring or base feet), an even powder coating and a complete parts list with assembly instructions. Width, height, depth and colour can be customised.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Demand comes from shop openings and refits (kiosks, supermarkets, pharmacies, specialist retail).

More

German specialist dealers start at EUR 204 net for a 1 m × 2.10 m wall bay at GKK Technik and from EUR 439.95 for a Tegometall gondola on ladenbau.de. Buyers decide on compatibility with existing systems, lead time and bulky-goods freight; competition is specialist shopfitting dealers with configurators. Return risk: bulky freight and fit deviations, so check a sample first. 'Tegometall' is a third party's brand name and may only be used as a compatibility hint, not as a product name or logo. Distinction: not a duplicate of 28-schwerlastregal (heavy-duty warehouse shelving).

Request from the supplier

  • Request a test report to EN 16121/16122: shelf load, upright load and stability (tip test) for the ordered configuration.
  • Compatibility: order a sample upright, shelf and bracket and test against existing parts on the 50 mm pitch (hole pattern, hook dimension).
  • Fix in writing the thickness of upright, back panel and shelf (mm), powder coat thickness (µm) and salt-spray hours.
  • Clarify the unit of the quoted price: what is a 'set' (number of uprights, shelves, back panels, base feet)? Get a quote per bay in a defined configuration.
  • Require a parts list, part marking and German assembly instructions including wall-anchoring advice; approve RAL colour samples.
  • Agree a third-party inspection before shipment (AQL, count and dimension check, packing for bulky goods).
