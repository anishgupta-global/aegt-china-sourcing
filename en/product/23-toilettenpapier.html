Description

The two-ply toilet paper in bulk packs reliably supplies site toilets, sanitary containers and staff rooms with hygiene material.

More

A basis weight of 17 to 20 g/m² per ply, clean perforation and a well-fitting core ensure tear strength and smooth dispensing in the holder. A professional user in Germany expects raw-material evidence (FSC/PEFC), origin documentation under the EU Deforestation Regulation, testing per EN ISO 12625 and German labelling. For site toilets with a waste tank, the paper's dispersibility must also be checked.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Laboratory test report per EN ISO 12625 (basis weight, tensile strength, absorbency, sheet count/length) for the sample and each batch
  • FSC/PEFC chain-of-custody certificate of the factory and EUDR due-diligence statement (pulp origin, geolocation)
  • Declaration of safety (REACH, dyes and additives, skin compatibility)
  • Evidence of dispersibility for site toilets with a waste tank
  • Packaging sample (film, carton, EU pallet size) with moisture protection for the sea container
  • German-language labelling (plies, sheet count, length, content) and binding delivery date
