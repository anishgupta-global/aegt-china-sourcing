Description

Marking spray makes cut, drill, demolition and service lines as well as survey points clearly visible on concrete, masonry, screed and asphalt.

More

Inverted spray valves allow marking downwards without tilting the can, and the fluorescent colours stay legible in dust and poor light. A professional user in Germany expects a fast-drying acrylic paint with a clear durability statement (temporary or permanent), a CLP label and a safety data sheet in German. Aerosols are dangerous goods (UN 1950), so transport and storage are subject to their own rules.
