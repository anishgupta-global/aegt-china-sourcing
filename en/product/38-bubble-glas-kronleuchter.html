E-commerce potential

Glass-globe cluster pendants are a visible part of Westwing's range; the comparison model Grover (EUR 229) shows 387 reviews there (as of 3 Nov 2026).

More

High return risk from glass breakage and size deviations, so exact dimensions and shock-proof packaging are key.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EN 60598-1 (LVD) and EMC test report of an accredited lab for exactly this model
  • Approve a pre-series sample: dimensions, weight, finishes, wiring, strain relief, suspension
  • Agree label and German manual incl. EU responsible person (GPSR) and WEEE symbol
  • Validate the packaging with a drop test (transport damage is the most common return reason)
  • Approve glass quality (bubbles, inclusions, wall thickness) and colour samples in writing; fix fixture size and number of lights (not stated in the listing)
