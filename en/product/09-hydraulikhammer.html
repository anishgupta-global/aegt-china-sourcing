Description

The hydraulic breaker is the standard tool for demolishing concrete, foundations and masonry and runs on the carrier without extra drives.

More

A professional user in Germany expects a forged, wear-resistant hardened tool, a gas-accumulator impact mechanism with a balanced ratio of impact energy to blow rate, and a tight, easily replaceable seal kit. Oil flow, working pressure and mounting must match the carrier exactly, otherwise overheating and breaker damage follow. It must be delivered with CE conformity under the Machinery Directive, a measured sound power level under 2000/14/EC and a German operating manual.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • CE declaration of conformity with serial number and a test report on the sound power level under 2000/14/EC.
  • Written confirmation of fit to the carrier: oil flow (l/min), working pressure (bar), mounting dimensions, pin diameter and weight.
  • Material certificate (EN 10204, type 3.1) for the tool and intermediate piece plus hardness data.
  • German operating manual, spare-parts list and a seal kit as well as a spare tool in the scope of delivery.
  • At least 12 months of warranty in writing; clarify warranty and return terms for defects.
  • Test-run video showing blow rate and pressure measurement before shipment.
