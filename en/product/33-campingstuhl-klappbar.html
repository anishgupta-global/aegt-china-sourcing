Description

The DS4003 folding chair combines a steel-tube frame (19 mm, wall thickness 0.7/0.8 mm) with a two-layer 600D Oxford seat with PP foam padding, cup holder and side pocket.

More

Set up it measures D 48/60 x W 60/88 x seat height 47 / overall height 96 cm; in its carton it weighs 3.6 kg net. It is the typical mid-range design for camping, festivals, fishing and the garden, clearly more comfortable than the basic angler chairs at EUR 15. For discerning buyers a documented load rating to EN 581, galvanised or powder-coated tubes, clean rivet joints without pinch points and a tear-resistant, UV-stable fabric are what matter.

Request from the supplier

  • Request an EN 581-2 test report from an accredited lab (load, endurance load, tipping stability); print the load rating on product and packaging.
  • Check tube wall thickness, galvanising or powder coating, rivet joints and pinch points (salt-spray test, open/close cycle test).
  • Fabric: 600D Oxford with coating, REACH/azo/PAH report, colour fastness and UV resistance.
  • The supplier has been listed on Made-in-China for only about 1 year: check audit/BSCI/SGS reports, agree reference sample, AQL 2.5 and pre-shipment inspection.
  • Label and packaging with manufacturer/EU responsible person, lot number, maximum load and German instructions; LUCID registration.
  • Clarify the optional carry bag (target product mentions one) and fix colour variants/logo.
