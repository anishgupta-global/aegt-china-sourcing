Description

The S3 safety shoe protects on site against falling loads, nails and sharp objects as well as water penetration.

More

Grained leather, a steel toe cap (200 J), a penetration-resistant midsole and a profiled, slip-resistant outsole are standard; breathable lining and an anatomical footbed are decisive for all-day use. Professional users in Germany expect marking to EN ISO 20345:2022 with additional codes (e.g. SRC, ESD) and an EU type-examination. Penetration protection is indispensable especially during strip-out and demolition.

Request from the supplier

  • EU type-examination certificate (Cat. II) from a notified body for exactly this model, cross-checked in NANDO.
  • Test report to EN ISO 20345:2022 (toe cap 200 J, penetration, slip resistance, water penetration) – listings sometimes cite older editions.
  • Chromium VI test report (REACH, ≤ 3 mg/kg) for the leather.
  • Marking inside the shoe (manufacturer, model, size, S3, date of manufacture, standard) and German user manual.
  • Samples with incoming inspection (sole bonding, size dimensions) and EU declaration of conformity.
