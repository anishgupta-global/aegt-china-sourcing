Description

The 14-inch rugged laptop of the ONERUGGED/Emdoor line (N14M) brings Windows 11 workstation performance to places where office notebooks fail: shell construction …

More

demolition and remediation, where dust, water jets and drops are routine. The housing is protected to IP65, the 1,000 cd/m² display stays legible in bright daylight, and the interfaces (COM, 2x LAN, USB-C, HDMI) allow connection of measuring devices, scanners and site networks. Two batteries (1,300 + 4,850 mAh) and GPS support mobile site supervision, defect reports and quantity take-off with specialist software such as CAD, tendering/costing tools or BIM viewers. Professional users in Germany expect CE conformity under RED including cybersecurity evidence, independent IP and MIL test reports instead of self-declaration, a QWERTZ keyboard, German-language documents and warranty and spare-parts service.

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under RED 2014/53/EU for exactly this model variant with test reports from an accredited lab (radio, EMC, safety per EN 62368-1) and evidence of the cybersecurity requirements (e.g. assessment to EN 18031-1/-2/-3) for the firmware shipped
  • Independent test report for IP65 (EN 60529) and MIL-STD-810H (drop, vibration, temperature tests) naming the lab and test scope instead of a bare listing claim
  • Battery: EN IEC 62133-2 test report, UN 38.3 test summary and labelling under the EU Battery Regulation (EU) 2023/1542; availability of replacement batteries
  • USB-C charging interface per (EU) 2022/2380 (laptops from 28 Apr 2026) and a CE-compliant power supply with ecodesign information
  • German keyboard layout (QWERTZ), Windows 11 licence as a legal OEM licence with proof of authenticity, display brightness (1,000 cd/m²) with a measurement report
  • German-language manual, named EU responsible person (GPSR), at least 24 months' warranty and spare-parts and repair service in the EU
