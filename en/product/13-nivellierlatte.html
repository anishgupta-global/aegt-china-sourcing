Description

The telescopic levelling staff transfers heights from the level or rotary laser to the measuring point: up to 5 m extended, carried retracted in the supplied bag.

More

The E-graduation can be read at a distance, the bubble vial helps keep the staff plumb, and spring-loaded square locking buttons secure the sections. Professional buyers expect an even, abrasion-resistant graduation, sections that run with little play and locking without height offset; for setting-out work to DIN 18202, a graduation error in the millimetre range matters. For EU sales, GPSR details and a traceable proof of graduation accuracy are part of the package.

Request from the supplier

  • Test report of the graduation against a reference scale on the sample, measured over all sections (listing states 1 mm, unverified)
  • Record the number of sections and the retracted length in writing (not given in the listing); test locking without height offset
  • Bubble vial: check adjustability and plumb accuracy, confirm the carrying bag is in the delivery
  • Test the graduation print for abrasion (wipe and rub test), request aluminium wall thickness and a REACH declaration
  • GPSR details with an EU responsible person and German manual; allow for bulky freight (carton 128 × 23 × 17 cm, 20 kg)
  • Order a sample for counter-checking (listing: USD 18 per sample) and have the lead time of up to 15 working days confirmed
