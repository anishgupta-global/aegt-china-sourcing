Description

The PMR446 handheld radio is the standard tool for communication between site management, demolition crews and machine operators when mobile coverage fails in buildings or basements.

More

In Germany PMR446 may be used without registration and without fees, provided the device has a fixed antenna and transmits at no more than 0.5 W ERP. A professional device is at least splash-proof, better IP67, with a robust Li-ion battery, a charging dock and a connector for a headset or speaker microphone. The importer must prove CE/RED conformity, a German manual and ElektroG and battery compliance.

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under RED 2014/53/EU with test reports to EN 300 296 and EN 301 489-5 from an accredited lab
  • Proof of the PMR446 parameters: fixed antenna, max. 0.5 W ERP, channels 446.00625 to 446.19375 MHz; do not buy devices with a detachable antenna or freely programmable frequencies (Baofeng type)
  • IP test report (IP54/IP67) and drop test, not just the catalogue claim
  • Battery with UN 38.3 transport test and battery/ElektroG registration of the importer
  • German manual, charging dock with CE-marked EU power supply, warranty of at least 24 months and spare battery availability
