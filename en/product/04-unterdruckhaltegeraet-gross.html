Description

The large negative air machine keeps remediation areas with a high air volume, such as halls, attics or several rooms at once, under negative pressure and removes asbestos and mineral fibres in the HEPA stage.

More

Quality units are three-stage filtered (coarse, fine and HEPA main filter H13/H14 to DIN EN 1822), continuously adjustable and fitted with a differential pressure monitor and an operating-hours counter. German specialist contractors expect proven filter testing, a leak-tight filter seat, CE conformity for the mains connection and a robust, decontaminable design. Only then can the required air exchange under TRGS 519 be maintained reliably and documented towards the authority and the client.

Request from the supplier

  • Test report of the HEPA main filter to DIN EN 1822 (H13/H14) with serial number
  • Measurement report of the air flow (m³/h) with the HEPA filter fitted; do not accept figures without filter
  • CE declaration of conformity (2006/42/EC, 2014/35/EU, 2014/30/EU) for the supplied voltage version
  • Proof of leak-tightness of filter seat and housing and testing to DGUV Grundsatz 309-012 (dust class H) for use in Germany
  • Differential pressure display, operating-hours counter and operating manual in German
  • Decontaminable surfaces; availability and price of replacement filters (G4, F7–F9, H13/H14)
