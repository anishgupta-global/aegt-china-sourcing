Description

Cloth duct tape is the all-round tape on a strip-out site for fixing cover sheeting and protective fleece, sealing sheet joints, airlocks and dust walls, and bundling pipes and cables.

More

Quality tape has a PE-coated cotton/polyester cloth with rubber adhesive, tears straight by hand and still peels off with little residue after weeks. A professional user in Germany expects data-sheet values to EN 1939 for adhesion, consistent roll quality and water-repellent behaviour; for containment in hazardous-substance removal (TRGS 519) a tight, durable bond of sheet joints is what counts. The adhesive must be solvent-free and REACH compliant.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Technical data sheet with adhesion, tensile strength and elongation stating the test method (EN 1939)
  • REACH/SVHC declaration and confirmation that the adhesive is solvent-free
  • Sample per batch: re-measure width, thickness and cloth count, peel test after 4 weeks on concrete, plaster and sheeting
  • Proof of manufacturer (business licence, production site) rather than a trading company
  • Carton and roll label with batch, dimensions, manufacturer and German labelling
