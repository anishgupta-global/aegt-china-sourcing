Description

The cross traverse is mounted at the foot of the ladder and widens the stance so that the ladder stands more stably.

More

The part is light, durable and not a consumable; what matters is the fit to the ladder model, because after mounting the ladder must still meet the requirements of EN 131 (as stated by BG BAU in its requirements for premium-eligible ladder accessories). The Chinese listings offer stabiliser bars for aluminium telescopic ladders (sample: balance bar 63 cm); cross-section, fixing and foot caps must be matched to each ladder model. For retail the part works as an add-on to the telescopic ladder, not as a universal part without a compatibility list.

Request from the supplier

  • Define the fit: specify target ladder (tube size, stile spacing, telescopic stage), bar length, fixing and foot caps in writing; listing dimensions such as 63 cm are not enough for a universal release.
  • Tip and load test with the traverse mounted on the target ladder by a laboratory; the ladder must continue to meet EN 131-1/-2 after retrofitting.
  • Check material and wall-thickness evidence (aluminium alloy, surface) and slip-resistant foot caps on the sample.
  • Compatibility list, mounting instructions and warnings in German; GPSR information (EU responsible person) on the packaging.
  • Confirm price and MOQ (Leader EUR 1.35 to 1.80, MOQ 50 pcs; comparison listing Dexin EUR 3.58 to 3.94, seller not verified), ask in writing for model number and dimensions (the listing gives none) and request a sample.
