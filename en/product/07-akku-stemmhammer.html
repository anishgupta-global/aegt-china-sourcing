Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Evidence (as of 2 Nov 2026): billiger.de lists the Makita HM001GZ02 (without battery, with case) from EUR 396.58 and the HM001GM201 version from EUR 749.98; searching HM001G returns 133 results from 40 shops.

More

Cordless chipping hammers are a premium segment with competition from HiKOKI (36 V, 6.8 J per search hit) and DeWalt (DCH892, 54 V). Add-ons: batteries and fast charger, SDS-max chisel sets, dust extraction kit. Competing on price is hard because battery-platform loyalty (Makita XGT) drives the purchase; return risk through battery compatibility and power claims. Note: the China variant uses 2 x 20 V instead of a platform and is therefore better suited to own-brand/OEM sale than to platform re-purchase.

Request from the supplier

  • Sample and datasheet: measure SDS-max holder, weight including batteries (target <= 7.7 kg), impact energy (8 J) and impact rate – the listing states neither weight nor holder
  • Clarify price basis: whether batteries, charger, case and side handle are included in the USD 57.60-58.60 (listing unclear, price probably tool only)
  • Battery: cell supplier, capacity (Ah), UN 38.3 test report, BattG/Batteries Regulation marking; charger to EN 60335-2-29 with EU plug
  • Vibration and noise: total vibration value to EN 62841-2-6 (reference HM001G: 6.5 m/s2, K 1.5) and guaranteed sound power level to 2000/14/EC as a test report
  • Conformity: CE declaration (machinery, EMC, RoHS), ElektroG/WEEE registration, German manual, manufacturer information under GPSR
  • Company: Yongkang Suntec – 20 employees per company profile, listing claims 500,000 units capacity: check plausibility by factory audit; verify CTI audit ID QIP-ASR2432542; MOQ 500 pcs
