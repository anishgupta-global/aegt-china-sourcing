Description

The crystal-growing kit combines experiment and crafting: from a salt solution, children grow their own crystals within a few days and can then display them.

More

The reference listing (PIN International, model 0010) describes a STEM kit made of chemical and plastic components with a package size of 7 x 7 x 9 cm; the exact contents (salt, moulds, dyes) are not broken down there. Quality markers are salts classified and labelled under CLP without borax/boric acid, child-resistant packaging, clear German instructions with warnings and safety notes (do not swallow, eye protection) and an age grading that fits the chemistry; the German reference sets at Mueller are graded 8+ or 10+. For the EU market the Toy Safety Directive, EN 71-3 and, depending on the contents, EN 71-4 or EN 71-5 apply.

Request from the supplier

  • Request EN 71 test reports (EN 71-3 plus EN 71-4 or EN 71-5 depending on contents) and the CLP classification of salts and additives from an accredited lab; the listing only names "ASTM F963-96A, EN 71, CE" without reports.
  • Require a contents list with CAS numbers (salt, dye, moulds) and a safety data sheet; exclude borax/boric acid and REACH SVHC substances.
  • Clarify the age grading: the listing names 3-8 years, for a chemistry set 8+ or 10+ is usual per the Mueller reference sets; define age grading, warnings (do not swallow, eye protection, supervision) and German instructions.
  • Function test on the sample: growth time, crystal size, colour fastness and success rate (complaint risk); sample per listing US$50 per piece, 7-15 days.
  • Define packaging and labelling: manufacturer, EU responsible person (GPSR), type/batch number, CLP pictograms if needed, VerpackG/LUCID; anticipate the new EU Toy Safety Regulation (PFAS/bisphenol bans, digital product passport) contractually.
  • Clarify the role of PIN International: Ningbo storefront as factory and trading company, production of the chemical and plastic parts is not evidenced; request factory and audit reports (BSCI, SMETA per FAQ).
