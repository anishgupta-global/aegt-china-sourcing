The 55-inch digital signage display (landscape, wall mount) shows menus, advertising and information in catering, retail, hotels and reception areas.

More

A built-in Android player (Android 11 or higher, Windows optional) plays content via USB stick or network, 500 cd/m2 and a 178 degree viewing angle ensure legibility in bright interiors. Commercial buyers expect a release for continuous operation (24/7), a known panel origin, CMS integration and remote maintenance as well as documented stability of the wall mount. The electronics need CE (LVD, EMC, plus RED for Wi-Fi/Bluetooth), RoHS and registration as a B2B electrical device with stiftung ear; energy efficiency follows the ecodesign regulation for electronic displays.

Request from the supplier

  • Confirm resolution, brightness and contrast of the 55-inch model TYL-HGM55BA(N)04 in writing; the listing's parameter table covers six sizes and is not unambiguously assigned.
  • Contractually fix panel maker and type (e.g. BOE, LG, AUO, HKC), pixel-fault class and a release for continuous 24/7 operation together with warranty (min. 24 months, replacement instead of repair).
  • Clarify Android version (11 or higher), update policy, CMS integration (own cloud yes/no), server location and GDPR conformity of the pre-installed software.
  • Request test reports: EN 62368-1, EN 55032/EN 55035, RoHS, for Wi-Fi/Bluetooth RED with EN 18031; declaration of conformity with EU responsible person (plus GPSR for consumer sales).
  • Check the wall bracket in the scope of supply for load rating and VESA pattern; document the stability of the mounting.
  • Packaging and freight: wooden crate with corner protection, drop test, transport insurance; WEEE B2B registration (stiftung ear) before the first import.
