Description

The extension cable connects solar modules, microinverters or charge controllers and consists of a 6 mm2 photovoltaic cable with pre-assembled MC4 plug and socket.

More

The large cross-section keeps line losses low, the cross-linked insulation (XLPE) is UV and weather resistant, and the connectors reach protection class IP68 per the listing. Quality shows in a fine-stranded, tinned copper conductor, a tested crimp with high pull-out strength, test marks to EN 50618 and connectors compatible with existing MC4 plugs. For sale in Germany, labelling, an RoHS/REACH declaration and GPSR data are required.

E-commerce potential

Light, recurring accessory with a high margin that is sold along with every module and inverter purchase.

More

Prices in DE: Offgridtec 2 m 6 mm2 EUR 5.33 gross (EUR 4.48 at 0% VAT; German assembly with original MultiContact connectors), Hornbach Bleil 2 m EUR 12.60 and 5 m EUR 21, tink solar extension cable 3 m EUR 29.90 (2-pack EUR 39.95). The driver is the installed base growth (about 49% four-module systems in 2026, kleines kraftwerk). Competition: many no-name sets on marketplaces; the quality difference lies in connector origin and crimping. Return risk low, liability risk high with incompatible connectors (contact resistance, fire hazard), so sell only with test certificates.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the TUV certificate for the cable (EN 50618 or 2PfG 2642) with certificate number and cross-check it in the test body directory.
  • Clarify the connectors: original MC4 (Staubli/MultiContact) or MC4-compatible with an IEC 62852 test report; avoid mixing different makes.
  • Define the conductor build: fine-stranded (class 5), tinned copper, 6 mm2 cross-section, conductor material and insulation in the datasheet (the listing contradictorily states a solid conductor).
  • Have the crimp quality proven: pull-out strength, contact resistance, 100% final test, sample report per lot.
  • Define lengths and colours (red/black) as a set; agree labelling, RoHS/REACH declaration, GPSR data and packaging (LUCID).
