Demolition Robots & Demolition Attachments

Remote-controlled demolition robots and hydraulic attachments (breakers, shears, pulverizers, sorting grapples) are the core equipment for strip-out, deconstruction and asbestos remediation.

More

From China, mainly the attachments are available at a fraction of German prices, while demolition robots are only an option with documented CE conformity, radio-remote test reports and service inside the EU.

Hydraulic demolition attachments (breakers, shears, grapples) cluster around Yantai in Shandong, while the demolition-robot listings found are scattered across Shandong and Hebei. Three more Yantai makers (Hemei, Jisan, Zhuoxin) were added; no further demolition-machinery maker could be verified in Hebei. As of v3: the three robot classes come from Pailien (Dezhou, Shandong), the five attachments from Yantai Jiwei.
