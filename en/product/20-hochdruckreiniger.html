Description

The high-pressure cleaner cleans company fleet vehicles, construction machinery, workshop floors and the yard in continuous use, and 200 bar working pressure is designed for stubborn dirt as well.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a slow-running four-pole three-phase motor, an axial piston pump with brass head, a bypass and safety valve, total stop and a robust frame with wheels and hose holder. Mandatory are CE conformity (low-voltage, EMC and machinery law) with testing to EN 60335-2-79, sound power marking under 2000/14/EC and a German operating manual; liquid jet machines must be inspected by a competent person at least once a year under DGUV Regel 100-500 chapter 2.36. Washing is only permitted on a paved wash area with wastewater treatment (WHG, AbwV), and spare parts such as seal kits, valves and high-pressure hoses should remain available long term.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • CE declaration of conformity with a test report to EN 60335-2-79 and the sound power level under 2000/14/EC.
  • Order the 400 V / 50 Hz version explicitly (listing states 380 V); photo of the motor name plate with kW, voltage and protection rating before shipment.
  • Pump data sheet (make, plunger material, pump head) and proof of 200 bar and 15 l/min in a test-run video with pressure gauge.
  • German operating manual and spare parts list (seal kits, valves, high-pressure hose, gun, lance).
  • Inspect a sample before series delivery and agree a pre-shipment inspection.
  • Verify the manufacturer's certificates and factory status (Zhengmao: website and contact data not seen in the listing).
