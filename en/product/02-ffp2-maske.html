Description

The FFP2 mask is the standard protection for drywall work, grinding, sawing, sweeping and clean-up …

More

where dust levels stay below the asbestos and mould-remediation tier and a reusable half mask would be disproportionate. Multi-layer polypropylene fleece with an electret filter layer, an adjustable nose clip and soft head or ear bands ensure a secure fit at low breathing resistance; a variant with a climate valve relieves the wearer in heat and over long wearing times. Professional users in Germany expect EN 149:2001+A1:2009 marked FFP2 NR D, CE marking with a four-digit NB number on every mask, individual packaging with expiry date and German instructions. KN95, N95 and GB 2626 are no substitute in the EU; wearing time follows DGUV Regel 112-190 (for FFP2 typically 75 minutes at a stretch, then 30 minutes of recovery).

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU type-examination certificate to EN 149:2001+A1:2009 stating class FFP2 with NB number – the number must be printed on every mask and confirmed in NANDO and directly with the body (many forged FFP certificates circulate).
  • Batch test report: penetration (NaCl, paraffin oil) ≤ 6 %, total inward leakage ≤ 8 % and breathing resistance to EN 149.
  • Written clarification that the goods are tested to EN 149 and not as KN95, N95 or to GB 2626 – these standards are no substitute in the EU.
  • Individual packaging marked FFP2 NR D with expiry date, batch number and German instructions; valve variants only with proven exhalation resistance.
  • Have samples tested independently before a series order (fit test); require proof of plant, ISO 9001 and production surveillance (module C2/D) – the Xiantao Zhongyi listing is evidenced only via an aggregator, so confirm company, price and MOQ directly.
