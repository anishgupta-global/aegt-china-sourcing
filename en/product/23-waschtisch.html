Description

The wall-hung washbasin in glazed sanitary porcelain permanently equips staff rooms, sanitary rooms and refurbished bathrooms with a hygienic washing facility.

More

The smooth, low-porosity glaze is easy to clean and resists common cleaning and disinfecting agents. A professional user in Germany expects accurate connecting dimensions per EN 31, a load-tested body per EN 14688 and fixing material for safe wall mounting. Test reports, samples and a glaze-related REACH declaration should be demanded before import.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report per EN 14688 (water absorption, load, glaze defects, overflow) from an accredited laboratory
  • Dimension sheet per EN 31 with tap-hole, fixing and outlet dimensions; sample before series production
  • REACH declaration for the glaze (lead/cadmium) and manufacturer's declaration of conformity
  • Pre-shipment inspection (AQL 2.5) with visual check for glaze defects and hairline cracks, plus breakage-protection packaging
  • Fixing set, German installation manual and warranty
