Description

The protective mat of cast polyurethane is bolted under the base plate of the plate compactor and protects paving stones, concrete slabs and natural stone from scratches …

More

scuff marks and chipped corners during compaction. Compared with the standard rubber mat, trade users expect higher abrasion resistance, work without marking on light surfaces and a dimensionally exact fit to plate size and hole pattern. The mat must be cut exactly to the plate compactor model; hardness and abrasion data and a material declaration belong to the delivery. In the trade it is sold as model-specific accessory, from DIY stores to specialist dealers.

Request from the supplier

  • Have a drawing with dimensions, hole pattern and fixing type approved per plate compactor model (first the two catalogue machines, 90 kg and 400 kg)
  • Test report on hardness (ISO 868) and abrasion (ISO 4649) plus material data sheet and REACH declaration
  • Sample with field test on light concrete paving: no marking, no detachment from the steel insert after several operating hours
  • 'Vulkollan' is a trademark (Covestro): in the shop use 'PU protective mat' or 'Vulkollan type' only descriptively
  • Price tiers per model, MOQ, packaging (flat), warranty of at least 12 months
