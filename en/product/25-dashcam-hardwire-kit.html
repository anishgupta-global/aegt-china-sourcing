Description

The hardwire kit powers the dash cam permanently from the vehicle electrical system, so parking mode and impact detection also work while the vehicle is switched off.

More

The built-in converter turns 12 V (cars, vans) and 24 V (trucks, construction machinery) into 5 V USB-C; an adjustable low-voltage cut-off switches the dash cam off before the starter battery is discharged too deeply. Professional buyers expect a documented input range up to 30 V, a reliable cut-off threshold with minimal standby current, a sufficiently long cable (here 3 m) and a connector that matches the dash cam model. This version plugs into the OBD-II port without touching the fuse box; for vehicles whose OBD permanent plus switches off after parking, the fuse-tap version remains the alternative. As vehicle accessory the kit must be EMC-compliant (EN 55032/55035) and withstand vehicle supply transients (ISO 7637-2, ISO 16750-2).

E-commerce potential

Parking mode only works with permanent power, so the kit is a typical must-have accessory for a dash cam.

More

On MediaMarkt.de 70mai hardwire kits cost 27.36 EUR (UP03), 32.71 EUR (OBD-II kit) and 41.95 EUR (UP06), each from marketplace sellers. A small, light part with a wide margin against the China listing price (1.50 USD per listing, to be secured by a quotation). Return risk comes mainly from wrong connectors (USB-C, mini-USB, micro-USB), too low output current (5 V/2 A) and OBD permanent plus that some vehicles switch off; a compatibility list per dash cam model reduces returns. Sell it as a bundle with the dash cam (25-dashcam-4k) and a high-endurance card (25-microsd-high-endurance).

Request from the supplier

  • EMC test report (EN 55032/55035) and RoHS declaration for the exact variant; check the original of the FCC/CE certificates named in the listing
  • Measure the cut-off voltages of the three levels and the standby current after cut-off on a sample (12 V and 24 V vehicle supply), then run an endurance test with the real dash cam in parking mode for at least 48 hours
  • Match the output current: 5 V/2 A may be too tight for a 4K dash cam with rear camera, GPS and Wi-Fi; fix a version with 5 V/3 A or a connection matching 25-dashcam-4k
  • Clarify connector type (USB-C, mini-USB, micro-USB), cable length and vehicle suitability: OBD-II pin 16 does not supply permanent power in all vehicles, in that case source a fuse-tap version separately (no verified maker yet)
  • Have the listing price (1.50 USD at 50 pieces, sample 10 USD) confirmed in writing by quotation and fix the model designation, as the listing gives no article number
  • German installation manual, at least 24 months warranty, GPSR information (EU responsible person) and ElektroG/LUCID registration of the party placing it on the market
