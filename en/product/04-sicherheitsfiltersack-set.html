Description

The safety filter bag sits in the dust container of the safety vacuum and catches the extracted fine and hazardous dust, so that emptying and disposal are possible without dust escaping.

More

On dust class H vacuums the bag is part of the tested system: only a bag type released by the vacuum manufacturer for the specific unit may be sold as a class H bag. The reference on the German market is the Nilfisk safety filter bag for ATTIX 33/44 (art. no. 107413549, 5 pcs, tested to IFA rules according to the retailer). The sourceable factory product (Nanjing Blue Sky, model BS1404) is a non-woven bag with plastic collar, 650 x 450 mm, white, customisable under OEM in colour, packaging and filtration grade. Buyers are remediation, demolition and construction firms under TRGS 519 that use several bags per job; the 5-pack is small, light and cheap to ship.

Consumable with predictable repeat purchases: every job uses bags.

More

Evidence (as of 2 Nov 2026): the Nilfisk safety filter bag ATTIX 33/44 (5 pcs) costs EUR 134.49 (Werkzeugstore24) to EUR 135.14 (Profi-Store24) at retailers, up to EUR 172.55 per search snippet (Fritzweg); standard non-woven bags for the same vacuum are EUR 36.65 per 5 at Hornbach, Karcher NT 30/1 non-woven bags (5 pcs) EUR 23.95. The safety bag is therefore a premium accessory; own-brand bags must prove release within the vacuum system. Competition: unit makers (Nilfisk, Karcher) and B2B specialists (Jungheinrich Profishop, Profi-Store24, Fritzweg). Return risk: wrong fit; force vacuum selection and a compatibility list in the shop.

Request from the supplier

  • Request an original bag from the vacuum maker as a sample and check the fit (collar, sealing disc, closure); third-party bags only after release by the unit manufacturer.
  • Demand a test report on the separation efficiency of the non-woven material and proof of suitability within the tested system (IFA/DGUV); do not advertise as 'class H' without proof.
  • Check the closure: collar with self-closing seal or closure flap; test tightness after removing the full bag.
  • Check tear strength and seam quality (drop test, fill samples with fine dust).
  • Marking on bag and carton: unit type, article and batch number, disposal note (TRGS 519), GPSR details with EU responsible person; 5 pcs per poly bag.
  • Clarify price per 5-pack and tiers (listing: MOQ 1,000 bags = 200 sets); samples before series.
