Description

The clamshell box packs hot lunches for crews and site management who bring food from the caterer into the site cabin.

More

A high-quality box closes tightly, is stackable, does not go soft with sauce and can be filled hot and reheated in the microwave. Since the EU ban on food containers made of expanded polystyrene, the German trade expects fibre-based boxes with an EN 13432 certificate and proven PFAS freedom. They are packaging within the meaning of the German Packaging Act (registration with LUCID and system participation) and the PPWR (EU) 2025/40.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Valid EN 13432 certificate (e.g. DIN CERTCO, TUV Austria) for exactly this box and this manufacturer; verify the number in the test body's database.
  • PFAS test report (targeted PFAS analysis and total fluorine) as evidence for the PPWR limits.
  • Declaration of conformity under Regulation (EC) 1935/2004 with migration and heavy-metal test report from an accredited lab.
  • Leak, grease and hot-fill test on the sample with the lid closed, stacking and drop test, documented with photos.
  • Confirmation that no plastic coating (PE, PLA) is applied; otherwise clarify single-use plastics obligations and fund payments.
  • Clarification of Packaging Act obligations (LUCID, system participation) and German labelling on the carton.
