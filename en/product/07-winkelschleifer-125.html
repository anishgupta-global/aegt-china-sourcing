Description

The 125 mm angle grinder is the small, agile complement to the 230 mm unit and the standard tool for cutting, rough-grinding and deburring in metalwork as well as for tile and joint work.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a metal gearbox housing, a tool-free adjustable guard, soft start, restart protection and a spindle lock for fast disc changes. What matters is that the no-load speed matches the rated speed of the cutting discs (EN 12413, oSa mark) and that vibration data are stated cleanly per EN 62841-2-3. For concrete and masonry, a dust-extraction hood and a dust class M site vacuum belong to the mandatory scope.

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 62841-1 and EN 62841-2-3 from an accredited body and EU declaration of conformity (2006/42/EC, 2014/30/EU, 2011/65/EU)
  • Measurement record for no-load speed, guard test and vibration value (three-axis measurement)
  • Evidence of soft start and restart protection (function video or test record)
  • Statement of permissible disc rated speed (≥ 80 m/s) and use of discs with oSa mark only
  • GS certificate (ProdSG Sec. 20) with certificate number – no GS logo without a valid certificate
  • Sample with H05RR-F/H07RN-F cable and EU plug, spare-parts list (carbon brushes, guard, handle)
