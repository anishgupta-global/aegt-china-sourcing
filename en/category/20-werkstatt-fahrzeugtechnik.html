To my knowledge vehicle lifts are listed in Annex IV of the Machinery Directive, so clarify the conformity assessment before ordering. Operation in Germany requires inspection before first use and recurring inspections by competent persons (BetrSichV/DGUV); explicitly order the 400 V/50 Hz version, because some offers state 380 V.
