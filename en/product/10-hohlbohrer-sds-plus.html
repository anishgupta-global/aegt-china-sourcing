Description

The hollow drill bit removes drilling dust at the point of origin through the hollow shank into the connected extractor and keeps quartz dust from concrete and masonry out of the breathing air.

More

It is used with an SDS-plus rotary hammer and a dust class M site vacuum, together with a suction connector. Professional users expect a carbide cutting head with a clean centring tip, a true-running shank to the SDS-plus standard and a continuous suction channel without constrictions. As importer, AE Global Traders is responsible for GPSR, packaging registration and checking dimensional accuracy and extraction performance on a sample.

Request from the supplier

  • Request factory status (own carbide brazing and grinding line) and reference customers in drill bits
  • Dimensional report for SDS-plus shank (Ø 10 mm, grooves) and run-out on a sample, drawing of the suction channel
  • Have extraction performance measured on a rotary hammer with a dust class M extractor (residual dust at the hole)
  • Test tool life in C20/25 concrete against a reference bit; proof of carbide grade and brazing temperature
  • Clarify connector compatibility and scope of delivery (bit alone or with connector); neutral marking without fischer reference
