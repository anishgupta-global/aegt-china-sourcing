Description

Soft hard-floor castors replace the hard standard castors fitted at the factory and prevent scratches, marks and rolling noise on parquet, laminate, vinyl and tiles.

More

The set of five fits common swivel chair bases with an 11 mm push-in stem; the PU tread glides quietly and lets the chair start moving without jerking. Safety-minded buyers look for a load rating of at least 50 kg per castor, clean concentricity and a tested holding force of the stem. As a wear part with repeat purchase and an accessory to the office chair, the set suits office, home office and site-cabin office; braked variants secure chairs on smooth floors.

Per the scan research, bueromarkt-ag lists over 30 five-piece sets from EUR 14.99 to EUR 44.99 and shows them with floor mat, footrest and cable tray; Kaufland and OTTO have 11 mm sets from approx. EUR 12-14.

More

Checked myself on 2 Nov 2026: Hornbach sells the Walteco set (5 pcs, dia. 50 mm, 60 kg per castor, PU, 11 mm stem) for EUR 15.65, single castors with 11 mm stem from EUR 6.35. Repeat purchase, small parcel, but high fit risk on stem diameter (11 mm versus 10 mm and M10 thread) and on floor type (soft for hard floors, hard for carpet); make both clear in title and variant selection.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the stem size in writing: 11 mm (length approx. 22 mm), ring/friction stem, tolerance and holding force; test a sample on the reference base (e.g. mesh chair zebai).
  • Request load per castor (at least 50 kg) and a test report to EN 12529 or castor testing to EN 1335; the SY-OC listing states only 30 kg.
  • Test tread hardness (soft for hard floors) and wear pattern on parquet; demand hydrolysis resistance of the PU and a marking/discolouration test.
  • Obtain a REACH declaration (plasticisers, PAHs, heavy metals) and material statement for tread, housing and stem.
  • Packaging as set of five in a bag with carton, printed with fit size (11 mm), 'for hard floors' notice, EU responsible person and LUCID number.
  • Braked variant: check brake function and load release; secure the MOQ of 1,000 pcs per castor at Benyu with a sample and batch inspection.
