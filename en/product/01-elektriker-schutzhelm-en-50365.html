Description

The electrician's safety helmet combines the impact and penetration protection of an industrial safety helmet with an electrically insulating …

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Demand comes from electrical contractors, grid service firms and wholesalers; the Hornbach branded helmet uvex pheos with EN 50365 sells at 13.65 EUR gross, while specialist traders …

More

(Hoffmann Group, 3M) ask roughly 17-50 EUR net depending on version. Liability and return risk are high: without robust EN 50365 evidence the helmet must not be advertised as an electrician's helmet.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EU type-examination certificate (module B) from a notified body and the test report per EN 397 AND EN 50365 for model CG-06I; the listing's certification line only shows CE/ANSI.
  • Protection against electrical hazards will probably need the highest PPE category (III) with production monitoring - confirm with the test body.
  • Check that the shell is unvented and that the test covers the combination of shell, suspension and chin strap that is sold.
  • Check marking (standard, voltage rating, production date, service-life note) and a German user manual.
  • Independent sample test of the insulation properties before the first shipment.
