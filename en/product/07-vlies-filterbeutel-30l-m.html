Description

The non-woven filter bag captures the fine dust produced by drilling, grinding and sawing on site already in the bag and thereby protects the motor and main filter of the vacuum.

More

Model BS1404 consists of non-woven fabric with a plastic collar and is designed as a replacement for the Karcher bag 2.889-154.0 (NT 30/1 Tact Te L/M, NT 30/1 Ap Te L/Ap L). For businesses that need dust class M this matters: the class applies to the system of appliance and filter, so the replacement bag must demonstrably fit the appliance and the separation efficiency must be proven by a test report. Bags with a collar can be changed with little dust; packs of five match the usual consumption rhythm on site.

E-commerce potential

Price anchor: Hornbach sells the fleece filter bag Karcher NT 30/1, 5 pcs, explicitly 'dust class M, 30 litres' for EUR 23.95 gross (item no. 6629474); Starmix fleece bags 5-pack cost EUR 21-29 at Hornbach.

More

Per scan research: Lidl Parkside 4 pcs EUR 22.99, Kaufland compatible 10-packs. Consumable with repeat purchase, high margin between Chinese goods and original. Return risk through appliance compatibility: show model numbers in the title, dimensions and a photo of the holder plate; no Karcher logos, 'suitable for' only descriptive. Use the claim 'dust class M' only with a test report.

Request from the supplier

  • Request a test report on separation efficiency (suitability for dust class M in the appliance per EN 60335-2-69 Annex AA); without a report no 'dust class M' claim in the shop.
  • Have the layer structure (at least three layers), basis weight per layer and non-woven supplier documented; check tear strength and seam quality.
  • Test fit on the original NT 30/1 appliance: collar, seal at the suction port, bag size; cross-check on a second 30 l class appliance.
  • Fill test with concrete and gypsum dust (no breakthrough, no blow-out at the ring); check the collar against deformation.
  • Brands and logos: unbranded goods without Karcher or Starmix marks, compatibility statement only descriptive; packaging and label with EU responsible person under GPSR.
  • Define pieces per pack (5) and film bag; agree a sample and a pre-shipment inspection (AQL).
