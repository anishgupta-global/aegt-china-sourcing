Description

Mineral wool is the standard for non-combustible thermal, acoustic and fire-protection insulation in roofs, walls, ceilings and drywall and is installed in large quantities in fit-out and refurbishment.

More

Professional users in Germany expect Euroclass A1, a declared thermal conductivity of 0.035 W/(m·K), dimensionally stable boards or uniform rolls without thickness variation, a biosoluble fibre under Note Q of the CLP Regulation and a declaration of performance under the Construction Products Regulation. For facades and fire walls, additional proof of suitability (e.g. general building approval) must be observed. Imported goods should be tested batch by batch because density and thickness can vary.

Request from the supplier

  • Declaration of performance (DoP) and CE marking under Reg. (EU) 305/2011 with test report of a notified body (EN 13162, λD, thickness)
  • Fire classification A1 to EN 13501-1 with test report
  • Proof of biosolubility or exemption under Note Q (e.g. EUCEB certificate)
  • Batch test per container (density, thickness, thermal conductivity) and traceability
  • Proof of suitability for the intended use (e.g. general building approval for facades) and moisture-protected packaging
