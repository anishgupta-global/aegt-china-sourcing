Description

The two-post lift raises vehicles of the company fleet, meaning cars, vans and small trucks, to working height for servicing, brake and chassis work in the company's own yard.

More

Professional users in Germany expect corrosion-protected columns, an automatic locking latch on both sides, synchronous lifting by steel cable or chain, and a 400 V drive unit with limit switch. Mandatory are the CE declaration of conformity under the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC with a test report to EN 1493, a German operating manual and clear foundation requirements; operation requires inspection before first use and recurring inspection by competent persons (BetrSichV, DGUV). Spare parts such as seal kits, cables and slide blocks and a clear warranty should be secured in the contract.

Request from the supplier

  • EC declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC stating manufacturer, model and serial number, plus technical documentation with risk assessment
  • Proof of the conformity assessment procedure including the test report to EN 1493 and the notified body number (CE claims on marketplaces are seller statements only)
  • Written confirmation of the 400 V/50 Hz drive version (some offers state 380 V)
  • German operating manual, installation and foundation requirements, maintenance plan and inspection logbook for inspections under BetrSichV/DGUV
  • Spare parts list with delivery time for seal kits, cables and slide blocks and a written warranty of at least 24 months
