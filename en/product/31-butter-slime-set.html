As a private-label item with tub label and optional scent or glitter, the set suits toy retail, drugstores, gift and party-favour ranges.

Request from the supplier

  • EN 71-3 test report from an accredited lab for the exact formulation (incl. boron) plus safety data sheet; confirm the formulation needs no borax activator kit.
  • Request the preservative system, fragrance and allergen list (Annex II Part III) and a microbiology test (shelf life, mould).
  • Fix the age grade; EN 71-1 for tubs and packaging; check leak-proof closure and seal.
  • Glitter/charms: microplastics declaration (REACH no. 78) and small-parts check of add-ons.
  • Check samples: texture, smell, drying after 4 weeks open, colour bleed, separation in transit; pre-shipment inspection.
  • Own look without imitating known brands (Gui Gui, Kawaii Slime Co.); EU responsible person and German warnings on pack.
