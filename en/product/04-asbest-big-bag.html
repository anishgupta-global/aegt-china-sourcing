Description

The sheet bag takes up asbestos-cement corrugated sheets, façade panels and other rigid asbestos-containing components at full length so they can be disposed of without …

More

being broken up. Quality product is made of coated woven PP, is dust-tight and carries the asbestos warning print in several languages plus four reinforced lifting loops for crane or forklift handling. German specialist contractors expect a stated safe working load, traceable seams and labelling in line with TRGS 519 so the waste can be handed over under waste code 17 06 05*. Batch traceability and test certificates are prerequisites for documentation towards the authority and the waste handler.

Request from the supplier

  • Test report on load capacity: SWL 1,500 kg with safety factor 5:1 (load and drop test per ISO 21898)

  • Proof of coating and dust-tightness, material data sheet and REACH declaration
  • Clarification of carriage requirements (UN approval/ADR) where transport demands them
  • Traceable batch number for every delivery for documentation towards the authority and waste handler
