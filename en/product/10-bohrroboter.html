Description

The ceiling drilling robot drills holes for dowels and anchors in ceilings and walls under BIM control, precisely and with integrated dust extraction – the overhead work that strains crews the most.

More

The benchmark in the German market is the Hilti Jaibot, offered as a service or rental model without a public purchase price. Professional users expect millimetre-range positioning by total station, class M dust extraction with attention to TRGS 559 (quartz dust) and suitability for drilling holes to ETA anchor specifications. CE conformity under the Machinery Directive, a safety controller to EN ISO 13849-1 and a German manual are mandatory.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity and technical file under the Machinery Directive incl. safety controller (PL d to EN ISO 13849-1) and risk assessment
  • Proof of dust extraction class M (EN 60335-2-69) and dust measurement to TRGS 559
  • Accuracy report of the hole positions (set vs. actual, total-station link) and a test drilling in C20/25 concrete with drill diameter / depth
  • Approval by the anchor manufacturers (ETA) for holes drilled by the robot; tool wear and drill-bit change intervals
  • German manual, EU service commitment, spare parts (drill bits, batteries) and 24-month warranty
