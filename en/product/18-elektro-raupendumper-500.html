Description

The battery tracked dumper carries rubble, screed, concrete and material up to about 500 kg over ramps, courtyard access and soft ground without releasing exhaust fumes indoors.

More

The tracked undercarriage provides traction, the tipping skip empties hydraulically or manually, and the quiet, low-emission electric drive is a clear advantage in refurbishment of occupied buildings. Professional users expect a lithium battery with proven cell safety (UN 38.3), CE marking, a German manual, plus emergency stop and dead-man control. Important: simple consumer models from DIY stores often do not meet the requirements of a commercial construction site.
