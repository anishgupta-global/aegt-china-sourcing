Description

The stirrer made of light-coloured wood (birch) turns cup and lid into a complete coffee-to-go set and replaces the plastic stirrer, whose placing on the market has been banned since 3 July 2021.

More

Good quality shows in a smoothly polished, splinter-free surface, rounded ends, even length and neutrality of taste and odour; the specialist trade expects an uncoated surface and FSC evidence. Delivery form is the carton with several thousand pieces or, as a hygiene variant at a surcharge, the individually paper-wrapped version. As a wood product from a third country it is subject to due diligence under the Deforestation Regulation, and food contact requires conformity under Reg. (EC) 1935/2004.

Request from the supplier

  • Have the wood species (birch) and the origin of the raw wood proven in writing (country, region, geolocation for the EUDR due-diligence statement); for birch from Russia or Belarus check the EU sanctions situation for wood products.
  • Have the FSC certificate with licence code and chain-of-custody evidence presented and check it in the FSC database; without valid evidence no FSC claim.
  • Declaration of conformity under Reg. (EC) 1935/2004, migration test report (hot water) and odour/taste test; confirmation that neither wax nor plastic is applied.
  • Check samples for freedom from splinters, edge rounding, dimensional accuracy (140 mm, tolerance) and swelling in 90 °C hot drink, photo-documented.
  • Ask for the individual wrap as a paper variant (no plastic) at a surcharge and define pieces per carton.
  • Obtain a binding quote: the listing shows USD 0.001 (rounded) at MOQ 10,000; ask for tiers for 10,000, 100,000 and 1 million pieces.
