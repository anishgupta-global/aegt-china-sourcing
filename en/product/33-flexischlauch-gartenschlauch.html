Description

The expandable garden hose combines an elastic latex/rubber inner tube with a woven polyester jacket: under water pressure it lengthens to about three times its length …

More

and contracts to roughly one third when the pressure is off. The 50 ft version (5 m resting length, 15 m working length) weighs only about 0.44 kg, the 75 ft version (22.5 m) about 0.72 kg; the hose hardly kinks or twists and fits in a bag. The set includes two couplings and a hand-held spray gun with seven spray patterns for watering, car washing and patio cleaning. Quality markers discerning buyers look for are brass fittings with cleanly crimped hose ends, a documented burst pressure and EU-compliant material (REACH, phthalates); these points must be fixed with the supplier in writing.

Request from the supplier

  • Request burst-pressure and endurance test reports (EN ISO 1402) plus an expansion cycle test (fix the number of cycles in writing).
  • Fix the fitting material in writing: brass (lead/nickel content) instead of plastic, 3/4 inch and 1/2 inch threads, seals, clean crimping.
  • REACH/RoHS test report (phthalates, PAHs, heavy metals) for inner tube, jacket and fittings.
  • Test samples for tightness under sustained pressure, UV/frost resistance and jacket abrasion.
  • State lengths unambiguously (resting vs. expanded length) on packaging and product page (risk of warning letters).
  • Ensure German manual, EU responsible person on the packaging (GPSR) and LUCID registration.
