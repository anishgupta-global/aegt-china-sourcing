Request from the supplier

  • Define the delivery scope in writing: tripod (height, material, stability), phone holder (clamp width), remote control, power supply/USB cable, bag; the listing only says 'Tripod Stand'.
  • Request test reports: CE declaration, EMC (EN 55015/61547), photobiological safety (EN 62471) and RoHS/REACH; have the IP65 claim substantiated.
  • Check the sample: flicker at low dimming level (with smartphone slow motion), colour temperatures, even illumination, tip resistance of the tripod with a smartphone.
  • Define logo and packaging (colour carton, German manual, WEEE symbol, name and address of the EU responsible person); confirm tier prices at 500 and 1,000 pcs.
  • Clarify EU duties: GPSR responsible person, stiftung ear registration (ElektroG), VerpackG/LUCID.
  • Quality agreement: AQL sampling at final inspection and third-party inspection before shipment; note the minimum order of 100 pcs.
