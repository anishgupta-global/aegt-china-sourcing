Description

The core drilling rig makes openings for cables, ventilation and building services in reinforced concrete and masonry without shaking the structure the way a chisel hammer does.

More

The motor with three-speed gearbox and the rigid drill stand are built for shift operation in wet drilling, and the cooling water supply protects the drill bit and reduces dust. Professional users in Germany expect a PRCD safety switch for wet drilling, a smooth-running stand with secure anchoring and common drill bit connections. Compliance means CE under the Machinery Directive with testing to EN 62841-3-6, testability under DGUV Regulation 3 and a German operating manual.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC with a test report to EN 62841-3-6 from an accredited lab
  • PRCD safety switch with test mark to DIN VDE 0661 included, EU plug and 230 V version (not only 220/110 V)
  • Sample test of the drill stand: play, concentricity and anchoring (anchor bolts or vacuum), water supply and water collection ring
  • Confirm the drill bit connection thread (1 1/4 inch UNC) in writing; specify drill bits separately and by segment quality
  • German operating manual, spare parts list (carbon brushes, gearbox) and at least 24 months warranty
