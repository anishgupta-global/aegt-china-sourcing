Description

The ball rolls on its own and avoids obstacles, triggering hunting behaviour and keeping indoor cats busy when nobody is available to play.

More

The ribbed silicone shell with a transparent protective housing is bite-resistant and easy to clean; the braided rope tail is detachable. It charges via USB, and operation is quiet according to the listing. In the EU it counts as a pet product with electronics and a battery: GPSR, EMC, RoHS and battery/electrical-equipment rules are relevant, but not the Toy Safety Directive for children's toys.

More

MORE app-controlled Mouse Racer EUR 31.99; plus PetSafe automatic toys (prices not collected). The price band for own brands is therefore roughly EUR 16-32. Season: Christmas trade and indoor cats all year. Risks: some cats ignore the toy, battery/electronics defects raise the return rate, small parts and silicone quality are safety-relevant.

Request from the supplier

  • Battery: ask in writing for type, capacity, protection circuit, UN 38.3 report; charging port (USB-C instead of micro-USB) and runtime per charge.
  • Silicone: material proof (REACH, migration test), bite safety of the housing; rule out swallowed small parts from tail/closure (pull and drop test).
  • Housing welded or screwed, waterproofness/cleanability, measure noise level in dB on the sample.
  • Request EMC test report and RoHS declaration for electronics and charging cable; prepare documentation for ElektroG/BattDG.
  • Test obstacle detection and endurance (rolling time, auto shut-off); agree defect rate and warranty.
  • German manual, safety notes ('use under supervision only') and disposal notes for packaging and carton.
