Description

The axe with a 1,250 g head is the all-round tool for timber work on site, at the yard and in forestry: splitting firewood and stakes, removing branches, dressing structural timber.

More

The head is drop-forged from carbon steel, hardened at the edge (manufacturer states HRC 47–53) and painted; the fibreglass handle with a soft grip zone absorbs shocks and, unlike wood, does not warp in wet weather or frost. Professional buyers expect a head that sits without play (pull-out and bending tests), an evenly ground edge with edge protector, clean dimensions and weight to DIN 7287 and PAH-tested grip materials. Imported goods need GPSR-compliant labelling with an EU responsible person, German safety instructions and, for retail, ideally a GS certificate.

E-commerce potential

Evidence (Hornbach/OBI look-ups, 2 Nov 2026): the 1,250 g class is established in German DIY stores.

More

Fibreglass versions cost EUR 21.99 (OBI 'Rheinische Axt 1250 g', DIN 7287, plus EUR 4.95 shipping, seller OBI E-Commerce GmbH) and EUR 23.16 (BGS axe fibreglass handle 1250 g, Hornbach marketplace, Werkzeugstore24 GmbH, delivery about 2–3 weeks); the ash-handle for_q safety axe 1250 g is EUR 32.49, branded goods such as the Fiskars Solid A19 splitting axe with fibreglass handle (65 cm) EUR 49. Hornbach lists 'making firewood' as an autumn project; demand is seasonal (autumn/winter). Opportunity: a DIN-compliant import at a shelf price of about EUR 22 whose FOB price is well under a third of that (before freight, 1.7% duty and VAT). Risks: price-aggressive no-name competition, product liability if the head loosens, the edge needs shipping protection. Return risk is low if weight and length are exact.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix model and weight in writing: A666 with a 1,250 g head (listing shows 1,000–1,800 g), overall and handle length and head shape (splitting axe or Rhenish pattern to DIN 7287) by drawing and sample; the parts list A66601–03 does not include 1,250 g, ask for the article number
  • Hardness record per batch: edge HRC 47–53 (ISO 6508-1), steel grade 45# with material certificate; impact and bending test of the handle and pull-out test of the head-to-handle joint with proof load and report
  • Grip material: PAH test report from an accredited laboratory for the PP+TPR grip (REACH Annex XVII entry 50) and SVHC declaration; the listing's 'PAHs and REACH approved' alone is not enough
  • Check the TÜV/GS certificate: number, test body, validity and model reference (the listing names TUV/GS without a certificate number); paint finish with salt spray test to ISO 9227 (hours stated) and lead-free paint
  • GPSR and packaging: EU responsible person, batch number, German safety instructions (safety glasses, edge protector, check the head fit), carton with edge protection, VerpackG/LUCID registration of the importer
  • Factory and terms: MOQ 500 pcs, about 1 month lead time, FOB Qingdao, T/T; inspect the BV audit report (ID MIC-ASI2551558 per storefront), factory visit, paid sample (listing: US$ 7 per piece)
