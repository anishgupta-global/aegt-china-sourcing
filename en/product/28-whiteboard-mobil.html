Description

The mobile whiteboard 180 × 90 cm turns every meeting room and project area into a workspace: writable on both sides, magnetic and freely movable on castors.

More

Professional buyers expect a scratch-resistant, low-ghosting sheet-steel surface (painted or enamelled), a torsion-resistant anodised aluminium frame with plastic corners, a continuous pen tray and a tip-resistant base with lockable castors. Required are proof of stability (based on EN 14434), REACH conformity of the paint or enamel layer and GPSR-compliant marking. Ohsung's offer (model AS09) names steel wheels of 40 or 60 mm diameter; for office floors, soft twin castors with brakes should be agreed.

E-commerce potential

Mobile whiteboards are a recurring need in offices, schools and workshop rooms and fit as a bundle with the conference table.

More

Schaefer Shop sells the same design at 1,200 × 900 mm from €189 and at 1,800 × 900 mm for €249 net (€229 from 3 pieces); branded systems such as Nobo Move & Meet (900 × 1,800 mm) start at €879 net (retrieved 2 Nov 2026). On the Hornbach marketplace, 120 × 90 cm models with stand range from €134.90 (HOMCOM) to €238.95 (Vivol) gross. The price gap between imported goods and brands is large; bulky shipping and transport damage to frame and board are the main risk.

Request from the supplier

  • Define the surface: painted or enamelled steel, thickness (0.2–0.4 mm per manufacturer range), test magnetic hold and ghosting resistance on a sample
  • Stand and stability: fix tube size and wall thickness, tilt test in the erected state, check the rotation mechanism with lock
  • Castors: soft PU twin castors with brakes instead of steel wheels, proof of floor compatibility
  • Have the 90 × 180 cm size confirmed in writing and a unit price per size stated in the quotation (the listing shows only the overall range)
  • Packaging for bulky freight (edge/corner protection, pallet, drop test), scope of delivery incl. magnets, markers, eraser
  • REACH/SVHC declaration for paint or enamel, German manual, GPSR label with EU responsible person, warranty in writing
