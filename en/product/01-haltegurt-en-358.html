Description

The lanyard holds the user in working position and keeps them from approaching the fall edge; it is not a fall-arrest system and does not replace a full-body harness.

More

A polyester kernmantle rope with aluminium rope grab lets the length be adjusted; it is supplied in lengths of 1.25 to 1.8 m (max. 2 m). As category III PPE it needs EU type-examination and production control by a notified body.

More

High liability and return risk: category III requires notified-body certificates and may not be sold without a valid EU type-examination.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EU type-examination certificate (module B) of the notified body and the module C2/D proof with the body's number
  • Clarify which standard is actually certified: the listing names both EN 358 and EN 354, the attribute table shows only CE/EN 354
  • Have rope adjuster, breaking strength and rope diameter destructively tested on a sample
  • Require marking (serial number, year of manufacture, notified body) and German instructions including inspection intervals
