Description

The frame pool set with Ø 366 cm and a 76 cm wall height holds about 8 m3 to the brim by calculation and is the classic entry size for families in the garden.

More

It consists of a powder-coated steel-tube frame that is plugged together without tools, a multi-layer PVC composite liner (triple layer with fabric insert) and a cartridge filter pump; depending on the set, a cover and ladder are included. Assembly takes a few hours, and in winter the pool can be dismantled and stored. Discerning buyers judge quality by tube wall thickness and corrosion protection, welded liner seams, a CE-compliant pump with EU plug and clearly visible warnings to EN 16582.

Request from the supplier

  • Specify the target model exactly (Ø 366 x 76 cm, 12 ft x 30 in, with cartridge filter pump, cover) and request a quotation with a price per set; the listing found only shows 16 ft x 48 in.
  • Test reports: EN 16582-1 and -3 (stability, warnings, wall strength), pump CE/LVD/EMC with EU plug; water/leak test of the liner.
  • Liner: layer structure, tear and seam strength, cold flexibility, REACH report (phthalates, PAHs, heavy metals).
  • Frame: tube diameter and wall thickness, corrosion protection (salt-spray test), joints without pinch points, stability when fully filled.
  • Calculate palletisation, weight and freight route (bulky goods); secure spare parts (liner, frame parts, cartridges) contractually.
  • German manual with warnings (drowning hazard, supervision), EU responsible person (GPSR), LUCID registration.
