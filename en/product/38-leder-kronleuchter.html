More

Return risk: colour deviation and leather odour.

Request from the supplier

  • Clarify the leather type in writing: genuine leather or PU/faux leather (the listing only says 'leather').
  • Check REACH Annex XVII: chromium(VI) in leather (entry 47) and azo dyes (entry 43); request a test report.
  • Confirm factory status by audit: the business type is not stated on the storefront.
  • Request the EU version (230 V, socket, cable) and LVD/EMC/RoHS test reports.
  • Approve colour and odour samples; minimum order 2 pcs, get tier prices in writing.
