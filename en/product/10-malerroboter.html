Description

The spraying robot puttys and coats interior walls and ceilings up to 3.2 m working height in successive passes (putty, drying/sanding, latex coat) with filler and latex paint …

More

replacing physically demanding manual application on large areas. The reference is the Weibuild PNP-3000iC with 200 m²/h putty and 300 m²/h latex output, 4 h battery life and app remote control. In Germany a uniform surface to DIN 18363, a masking concept for windows, doors and floors and execution to EN 1953 for spraying equipment are required. CE marking incl. radio, EMC and battery conformity is mandatory, and an ATEX assessment as well for solvent-based coatings.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity and technical file under Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC or (EU) 2023/1230; proof of the CE assessment (a manufacturer announcement alone is not enough)
  • EMC and radio (RED) test reports for the app / remote control; battery with UN 38.3 report and safety data sheet
  • Proof to EN 1953 for the spraying system; ATEX classification if solvent-based paints are used
  • Factory acceptance test (FAT) with putty and latex samples on a German plasterboard / plaster wall: area output, layer thickness, surface quality
  • German manual, maintenance plan for pump / nozzles, spare-parts commitment and 24-month warranty
