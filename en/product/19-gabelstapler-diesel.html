Description

The 3.0 t diesel forklift is the robust solution for loading, unloading and material handling outdoors, on gravel, chippings and concrete yards where electric trucks reach their limits.

More

A professional unit has an overhead guard to EN ISO 6055, super-elastic or pneumatic tyres for uneven ground, a hydrodynamic drive with direction switch and an engine meeting EU Stage V emission rules. Users in Germany expect the EU declaration of conformity under the Machinery Directive, proof of the engine type-approval, a German manual, secured spare-parts supply and the annual inspection under DGUV Regulation 68 by a competent person. In enclosed spaces it may only run with a particulate filter under TRGS 554 and only be operated by trained drivers with written authorisation.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC with serial number, test report to EN ISO 3691-1 and a German manual
  • Proof of the engine type-approval to Stage V (Reg. (EU) 2016/1628) naming the engine maker, plus the data plate
  • Load-capacity chart for the mast and attachments and a stability verification to EN ISO 22915
  • Noise measurement (LwA) under 2000/14/EC and vibration measurement at the driver's seat
  • Test certificate of the overhead guard to EN ISO 6055 and the restraint system (seat belt)
  • Spare-parts catalogue, EU service partner and written warranty; factory acceptance record per serial number
