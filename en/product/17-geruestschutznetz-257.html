Description

The net made of UV-stabilised HDPE is supplied as a roll of 2.57 × 10 m (25.7 m²) and fixed to the scaffold with cable ties or clips through edge eyelets.

More

It retains dust, plaster and paint residue and small parts, reduces wind load and provides privacy on the facade. Commercial users look for edge reinforcement with closely spaced eyelets, weight per area, UV resistance and fire behaviour, because requirements for flame-retardant nets may apply to scaffold sheeting depending on the building and state law. The net is not a safety net to EN 1263-1 and must not be advertised as fall protection.

Risk: price-driven competition and questions on fire behaviour that cannot be answered without a test report.

Request from the supplier

  • Define the specification: width 2.57 m, roll 10 m, 50 g/m², colour green, edge reinforcement with eyelets at the sample spacing; weigh the roll on the sample (about 1.3 kg by calculation).
  • Prove UV stabilisation and tear strength by test report or weathering test; spot-check weight and mesh size per lot.
  • Fire behaviour: demand a test report to DIN 4102-1 (B1) or EN 13501-1 if the net is advertised for scaffold sheeting; the listings only say 'flame retardant' or NFPA 701 (Jiacheng), no EU standard.
  • Raw material: confirm in writing 100 % virgin HDPE (no recyclate), request a REACH declaration on UV stabilisers and colourants.
  • Packaging: roll in PE film or carton, label with article number, dimensions, weight and GPSR information; LUCID registration for the packaging.
  • Confirm price per weight class and roll in writing; clarify MOQ (listing from 100 m², custom production per FAQ from 3,000 kg) and lead time.
  • The listing text advertises protection of persons against falling from height: do not carry this claim into shop copy (a scaffold debris net is not a safety net to EN 1263-1).
