Description

The excavator plate compactor replaces the hand-guided vibrating plate in the trench: it is guided on the excavator arm, compacts gravel, sand and fill also on slopes and at manholes …

More

and nobody has to climb into the trench. The researched model Jisan JSC08 is specified for excavators of 17 to 23 t, weighs 900 kg and needs 120–170 l/min oil flow at 120–180 bar; the frequency is around 1,800–2,000 rpm, the manufacturer states an impact force of 15 t. Branded references are the SIMEX PV 700 and PV 850 (Profi-Bau-Shop) and the NPK C series (C-8C: excavators 14–34 t, 1,056 kg, 108 kN, 37 Hz, Schlueter Baumaschinen). Professional users look for low-maintenance bearing of the eccentric mass (oil bath), well-damping rubber mounts, a mounting and hydraulic data matching the excavator, and CE conformity under the Machinery Directive.

Request from the supplier

  • Request CE marking, EC declaration of conformity and German operating instructions before ordering (Machinery Directive, Machinery Regulation from 20 Jan 2027)
  • Have the JSC08 data sheet confirmed in writing: impact force (t or kN), frequency, oil flow, pressure, plate size; the listing contradicts itself on unit, pressure and plate size
  • Have the fit to the excavator confirmed: carrier weight and tipping stability with 900 kg attachment weight, oil flow and pressure of the auxiliary hydraulics, case-drain line, mounting (pin pattern or quick coupler)
  • Ask for rubber mounts (45° inclined dampers), eccentric bearing and hydraulic motor (make, oil bath); order rubber mounts and seals as spare parts
  • Request a test-run video and a noise statement (sound power level) before shipment
  • Contractually fix pre-shipment inspection, warranty (1 year per listing) and EU spare-parts supply
