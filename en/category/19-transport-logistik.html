Transport & Logistics / Material Handling

This category covers AE Global Traders's internal logistics, from the construction wheelbarrow and pallet trucks to forklifts and transport crates.

More

Powered equipment (pallet trucks, forklifts, electric barrows) counts as machinery under the EU Machinery Directive and must be supplied CE-compliant, with UN 38.3-tested lithium batteries and inspectable under DGUV Regulation 68, while manual products fall under product safety law (GPSR/ProdSG). Extended by the diesel forklift, sack truck and roll-off container for material flow and waste logistics on site.

Forklifts and warehouse equipment cluster in Zhejiang (Hangzhou/Anji: Hangcha, EP Equipment; Huzhou-Changxing: Noblelift; Ningbo: Xianda) and Anhui (Hefei: Heli); simple wheelbarrows cluster around Qingdao (Shandong: Asiana, Hongyutools). Hand trucks and wheelbarrows also come from Qingdao (Huatian, Jinmaorun); roll-off containers from Yangzhou (Jiangsu, Hero Equipment) as well as Henan and Shandong.
