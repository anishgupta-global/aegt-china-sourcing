Description

Glass noodles (fensi, Longkou vermicelli) are drawn and dried from mung bean starch or blends with pea and corn starch; after a short soak or boil they are glassy, firm to the bite and absorb sauces well.

More

Buyers look at the starch base (pure mung bean starch counts as premium, blends are common), cooking stability without sticking, a bag with a clean seal and batch code and, for a “gluten-free” claim, a laboratory proof. The top region is Longkou/Zhaoyuan (Yantai, Shandong); listed here is the Longkou vermicelli of Qingdao Sunrise New (main ingredient mung bean starch, certificates BRC, IFS, HACCP per listing). The German price anchor is Tian Tan 500 g at Asiafoodland (4.89 EUR).

Steady demand in the Asian range, sold via volume tiers; a private label scores with a price ladder, recipe card and, only with laboratory proof, “gluten-free”. Light, 36 months shelf life per listing and few returns, but price pressure from brands and marketplace wholesalers. Note: Yantai Yanliang names “Tiantan” as one of its Longkou brands; whether this relates to the Asiafoodland item Tian Tan is not evidenced.

Request from the supplier

  • Recipe: have the share of mung bean starch in %, other starches (pea, corn, potato) and additives or processing aids (e.g. sulphites) confirmed in writing.
  • A gluten-free claim only with laboratory analysis (below 20 mg/kg) and proof of line separation from wheat noodles.
  • Batch laboratory report: pesticides, heavy metals, aflatoxins, moisture and insect infestation.
  • Confirm the manufacturer: Sunrise calls itself a manufacturer, its office address is in Qingdao; have the producing factory named in writing and verified by licence, certificate and audit checks; approach Zhaoyuan Sanlian as the factory alternative.
  • Cooking test on samples (sticking, breakage, water uptake); bag with seal and batch code; confirm the 500 g format and price in writing, as the listing states 250 g × 50.
  • Secure the tariff code: binding tariff information (BTI); the assumed code 1902 19 10 carries a mixed duty that belongs in the calculation.
