Description

The sack truck moves cement bags, tile cartons, equipment and crates of up to 200 kg across ramps and level surfaces on site, without a forklift and with little effort.

More

A professional unit has a welded, powder-coated tubular-steel frame, a deep-drawn toe plate, pneumatic wheels with ball bearings and non-slip handles. Users in Germany expect a clearly marked load rating, a GS mark or equivalent proof under product-safety law, and a REACH-compliant coating and tyres free of lead, cadmium and PAHs. As work equipment it is included in the risk assessment under the BetrSichV and checked regularly for frame, tyres and handles.

Request from the supplier

  • Load-test report for 200 kg (static and dynamic) with test date and serial number
  • GS certificate or test report from an EU-accredited body; named EU responsible person under GPSR
  • REACH/PAH test report for handles, tyres and coating (lead, cadmium, PAHs)
  • Salt-spray test to ISO 9227 stating the hours achieved for the powder coating
  • Sampling record for weld seams, wheel bearings and tyre pressure per lot
  • German instructions with the load rating marked on the unit and a batch or lot number
