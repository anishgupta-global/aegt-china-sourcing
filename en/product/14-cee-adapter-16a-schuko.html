Description

The 16 A CEE adapter with a 5-pin plug (3P+N+E, 6 h) and one Schuko socket turns any three-phase site outlet into a 230 V socket for hand tools, lights and chargers within seconds.

More

Professionals expect an impact- and UV-resistant PA housing, IP44 when mated (the PCE reference item has a self-closing flap), brass contacts with clean contact pressure and a keyed design to EN IEC 60309 on the CEE side and EN 60884-1 / DIN VDE 0620-1 on the Schuko side. On German sites DIN VDE 0100-704 applies: the feeding CEE outlet must be RCD-protected; the adapter does not replace a site distribution board with its own RCD. From China the item can only be placed on the market with a test report from an accredited lab, a German-language declaration of conformity and an EU responsible person under GPSR.

Request from the supplier

  • Test report from an accredited lab (TUEV, VDE, SGS, Intertek) for exactly model G02P: EN IEC 60309-1/-2 for the CEE plug, EN 60884-1 for the Schuko socket, temperature rise at 16 A
  • CE declaration of conformity (2014/35/EU, 2011/65/EU) referencing the model and manufacturer; accept the certificates named in the listing (SEMKO, VDE, KEMA etc.) only with a certificate number and cross-check them in the test house database; the listing also cites EN 50520 and ISO 9001:2008, have the meaning clarified
  • Material evidence: flame-retardant PA66 (glow-wire test), brass contacts (cross-section, contact pressure), RoHS/REACH reports
  • Check samples: insertion and retention force, early-make of the earth contact, 6 h keying, fit on Mennekes, PCE and ABL outlets, IP44 when mated
  • Marking and documents: type, ratings, manufacturer and EU responsible person (GPSR) on product or packaging, German safety instructions
  • Third-party inspection before shipment (AQL) and written confirmation of MOQ, lead time (per the FAQ of a Znpon listing: 20 to 45 days) and payment terms (per FAQ: up to USD 1,000 100 % in advance)
