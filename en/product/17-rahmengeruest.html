Description

The frame scaffold is built from prefabricated steel frames that are combined with decks, diagonal braces and guardrail parts into facade, plastering and working scaffolds …

More

allowing regular scaffold surfaces to be erected quickly on refurbishment and demolition sites. The strength of the system lies in a few standardised components; what matters, however, is tube quality, weld quality and galvanising, which must be proven for every batch. German contractors and scaffolders expect a scaffold to EN 12810/12811 with a general building-authority approval (abZ), erection and use instructions and documented load classes. Without an abZ, use in Germany is possible only with an additional proof of usability, so system compatibility must be clarified before the first import.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EN 10204 type 3.1 mill certificate for tubes (wall thickness, yield strength) per production lot
  • Test report to EN 12810/12811 (load class, stability) from an accredited body, e.g. TÜV
  • Proof of hot-dip galvanising (coating thickness to EN ISO 1461) and weld inspection
  • Clarification of the abZ (Z-8.1-...) or a proof of usability for Germany; German erection and use instructions
  • Parts list with frame dimensions and proof of compatibility with the AE Global Traders stock; samples before series delivery
  • Original ISO 9001 and CE certificates claimed on the manufacturer page (issuer, number, scope)
