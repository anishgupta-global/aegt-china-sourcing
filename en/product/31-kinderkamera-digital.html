Description

The digital kids camera puts real photo and video capture into children's hands: 2.4-inch IPS display, selfie lens, a silicone case for shock protection and a rechargeable Li-ion battery with USB charging.

More

It works fully offline – no Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, app or cloud – so it can be sold without radio-equipment or data-protection overhead. Discerning buyers look for a soft, edge-free silicone housing, a securely screwed battery compartment, German-language menus and robust test reports to EN 71 and EN 62115. Request from the supplier

  • Request EN 71-1/-2/-3 and EN 62115 test reports for the exact model (incl. silicone case) from an accredited lab (e.g. SGS, TÜV, Intertek); any certificate claims in the listing would be manufacturer statements only.
  • Age grading and small parts: prove suitability from 3 years (small-parts test, cable/lanyard, screwed battery compartment, swallowing protection for the memory card).
  • Chemistry: EN 71-3, REACH Annex XVII (PAHs in silicone/rubber), phthalates and RoHS declaration.
  • Battery and charger: IEC 62133-2, UN 38.3 report, CE/LVD conformity of the USB charging; marking under Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542; clarify ElektroG registration (stiftung ear).
  • Check the sample: image performance and feature set (X3 listing: 5 MP sensor, up to 44 MP interpolated, display 320×240, 1080p video; title '2.0 Inch IPS', description '2.4 inches' and model numbers X3/C62 contradictory), memory card in the box, silicone case and selfie lens (possibly the C62 dual lens); confirm that there is no Wi-Fi, Bluetooth or app (otherwise RED and GDPR apply).
  • Supplier status and contract: factory visit/audit (factory address Shenzhen, Bao'an, Licheng Industrial Park; office in Yiwu), clarify manufacturer status (name 'E-Commerce Firm', account since 2022, profile text looks copied); EU responsible person and GPSR marking in German; anticipate the new EU Toy Safety Regulation (in force since 1 Jan 2026, applies from 1 Aug 2030: digital product passport, PFAS/bisphenol bans) contractually.
