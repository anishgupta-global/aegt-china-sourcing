More

Remote and manual control plus a load-sensing hydraulic system adapt output to mining, construction waste, asphalt and topsoil. Commercial buyers expect a Stage V engine certificate, CE with a German operating manual, a radio remote control compliant with RED and dependable service for screen media and hydraulics.

E-commerce potential

Quote-based business for recycling, gravel and civil-engineering firms: in Germany e.g. Kleemann (Wirtgen Group) offers mobile screening plants, new prices on request only.

More

The opportunity is the price gap; risks are the thin evidence for the model, the Stage V engine certificate and service. Offer only with a written confirmed configuration and a deposit.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the Stage V engine certificate (engine maker, type, power) and the EU type approval of the engine
  • Have the configuration confirmed in writing (tracks, engine, screen decks, conveyors): the model is evidenced only via reseller and aggregator listings
  • CE declaration of conformity, German operating manual and RED conformity of the radio remote control
  • Get spare parts (screen media, hydraulics, track chains) and service technicians in DE/EU confirmed
  • Fix transport dimensions and weight (approx. 32 t) as well as loading (low-loader/container) in the quote
