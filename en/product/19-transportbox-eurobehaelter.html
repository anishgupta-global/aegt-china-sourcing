Description

Euro stacking containers in the modular 600 × 400 mm size keep tools, consumables and small parts organised on site, in service vehicles and in the store and combine into stable stacks on Euro pallets.

More

Professional containers are made of impact-resistant polypropylene (PP) or HDPE, have reinforced bases and walls, integrated handle recesses and anti-slip feet, and stay cold-resistant down to about −20 °C. Users in Germany expect dimensional accuracy to ISO 3394, defined stacking loads, REACH-compliant materials and, where food contact is intended, conformity with Regulation (EU) No 10/2011. For long-term use, virgin material instead of regrind and consistent dimensional and colour quality are decisive.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Dimensional report against ISO 3394 and stacking test (stacking load over 24 hours) on the sample
  • Material proof: virgin PP/HDPE (no regrind) with datasheet and REACH declaration (SVHC)
  • For food contact: declaration of conformity under Regulation (EU) 10/2011 and migration test report from an accredited lab
  • Drop and cold-impact test on the sample (e.g. drop from 1 m at −20 °C) with test report
  • Export packaging: nested or stacked on Euro pallet, container loading plan (FCL), labelling
  • For private label: fix mould costs, logo embossing or hot stamping, colour samples and MOQ in writing in advance
