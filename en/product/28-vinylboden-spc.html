Description

SPC (stone plastic composite) vinyl flooring has a rigid core of PVC and limestone powder, topped by a decor layer and a clear wear layer with UV lacquer.

More

The floor is dimensionally stable and waterproof and is installed floating with a glueless click system. The 4 mm version with a 0.3 mm wear layer is the usual entry class for residential and light commercial floors. Professional buyers check the CE declaration of performance to EN 14041, the fire class, phthalate freedom, emission values and the licence situation of the click system. An IXPE or EVA underlay can be added at the factory.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Hornbach lists many SPC click vinyl floors: 4 mm at EUR 12.95–16.95/m², 5 mm at EUR 19.95–26.95/m² gross (volume prices included).

More

Growth driven by water resistance and easy installation; target groups are renovators, tradespeople and property managers. Competition: DIY stores, flooring specialists and marketplaces with many decors; differentiation via use class 23/33, bevel and sample shipping. Return risk: colour deviation between batches and screens, remainders on pack goods (about 2.2 m² per pack), weight and freight forwarding. Plan samples and batch approval.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the CE declaration of performance (DoP) to EN 14041 and a fire-class test report (EN 13501-1) for exactly this decor and thickness.
  • Require a REACH test report on phthalates (DEHP, DBP, BBP, DIBP) and lead in the PVC; have '100 % virgin material' confirmed in writing.
  • Require proof of licence for the click system (Unilin/Välinge); without it, selling in the EU risks patent infringement.
  • Measure wear-layer thickness (0.3 mm) and thickness tolerance on a sample; have use class 23/33 and slip resistance (R class) evidenced in the report.
  • Request emission testing (AgBB/EN 16516) or labels (e.g. EMICODE, Blue Angel) and a formaldehyde test.
  • Fix batch and colour approval before production, pre-shipment inspection (AQL) and pack content (m² per pack).
