Description

The mini prism system combines a 25.4 mm prism in a robust holder with an adjustable circular bubble, a 5/8 inch connection and a prism pole for stakeout and layout work with a total station.

More

Professional users expect a defined prism constant (offset), a cleanly centred prism, an adjusted bubble and a torsion-resistant pole. For total-station-guided layout robots a 360-degree prism is decisive; this version is not stated on the manufacturer pages checked and must be requested from the manufacturer. As importer, AE Global Traders is responsible for GPSR and packaging registration and should offer it only with a test certificate for the prism constant and a compatibility list for Leica, Trimble and Topcon tracking.

Request from the supplier

  • Have prism constant (offset) and centring error confirmed by test certificate (measured against a reference prism)
  • Confirm 360-degree version and ATR/tracking compatibility with Leica, Trimble and Topcon total stations in writing
  • Check reflective coating (silver/copper) and tightness of the holder; test bubble adjustment
  • Check pole connection, torsional stiffness and 5/8 inch thread on a sample
  • Neutral marking without Leica brands or part numbers; fix scope of delivery per set
