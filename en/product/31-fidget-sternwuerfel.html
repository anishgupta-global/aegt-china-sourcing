Description

The star cube, also sold as Euclidean cube or galaxy cube, is a transforming geometric puzzle with a 5.5 cm edge that can be folded into different shapes and serves as a fidget toy, brain teaser and small gift.

More

The manufacturer offers it in the "Marine Animal" motif series; a comparable variant sells in the German trade as "Ozean-Serie". Quality shows in a smooth mechanism without sharp burrs, colour-fast print and tested plastics. If magnets are fitted, the magnet requirements of EN 71-1 apply; the listing names no magnets.

A cheap impulse buy and add-on to puzzles and brain games; the price gap to the Shashibo brand leaves room, but property rights must be respected. Risks: small parts, ingestion hazard if magnets are used, mechanism breakage per reviews.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EN 71-1/-3 test report for TH04-0098; if magnets are fitted, explicitly require the magnet test under EN 71-1.
  • Check property rights: the manufacturer names Shashibo as its first OEM product (end of 2018); avoid Shashibo copies and trademark-protected names, and clarify the design-right and patent position of your own design.
  • Check samples for mechanism (smoothness, endurance), burrs, print quality and colour fastness.
  • Define the target age (small parts below 36 months) and agree age and warning notices for the packaging.
  • Clarify labelling: CE, EU responsible person under GPSR, batch number, VerpackG/LUCID for the colour box.
  • Have tier price, MOQ (150 pieces) and Incoterm confirmed in writing.
