Workwear

Workwear for demolition, strip-out and renovation must be robust, wash-resistant and, depending on use …

More

standards-compliant: hi-vis clothing to EN ISO 20471 and rain protection to EN 343 are PPE and need an EU type-examination, while trousers, pullovers and T-shirts are consumables where fit and durability count. Chinese manufacturers supply hi-vis, trousers and basic textiles in volume; what matters is proof of conformity, a size chart in EU measurements and pre-production sample approval.

Hi-vis and workwear are concentrated in Jiangsu (Nanjing/Suzhou), Fujian (Fuzhou/Quanzhou), Zhejiang and Guangdong, with flame-resistant protective clothing additionally in Henan (Xinxiang). HollyDare and Outstar are both based in Fuzhou, and Quanzhou (Xinli) belongs to the same Fujian cluster.
