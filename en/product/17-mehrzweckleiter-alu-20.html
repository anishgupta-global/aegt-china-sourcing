Description

The multi-purpose ladder covers several uses with one device: as a step ladder in a room, as a leaning ladder on facades and shafts, and as a stair or scaffold ladder where levels differ.

More

On changing strip-out and refurbishment sites this saves space and purchase cost compared with several single ladders. Professional users in Germany expect joints with proven fatigue strength per EN 131-4, latching locks, slip-resistant feet and clearly legible user instructions. An EN 131 test report and compliance with the usage rules of TRBS 2121 Part 2 must be proven before purchase.

Request from the supplier

  • EN 131 test report (incl. EN 131-4 hinge test, fatigue strength) from an accredited body
  • Confirmation of the real step count, type and 150 kg rated load in the test report of the delivered model
  • Hinge and lock sample for your own function and load test before series order
  • Material certificate for the aluminium alloy and rail wall thickness
  • German user information and warnings per EN 131-3
  • Warranty on hinges and locks, spare parts (feet, hinges)
