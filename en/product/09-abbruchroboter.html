Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • CE declaration of conformity with serial number and technical file under 2006/42/EC (or (EU) 2023/1230); do not accept marketplace CE logos without documents.
  • Test reports for the radio remote control (RED/EMC) and proof of emergency stop (performance level per EN ISO 13849-1).
  • Written data sheet with model, actual operating weight, motor power, reach and tool circuit (l/min, bar); the listing states no weight.
  • German operating manual, maintenance plan and spare-parts catalogue; availability of wear parts and a service technician in the EU confirmed.
  • Factory visit or video audit and a test run with acceptance protocol before the final payment; ISO 9001 certificate of the maker.
  • At least 12 months of warranty in writing; prove manufacturer status (factory rather than trader) with the business licence.
