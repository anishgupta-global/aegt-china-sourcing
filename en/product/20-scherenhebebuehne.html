Description

The scissor lift raises cars, vans and company fleet vehicles to working height with no columns in the work area, which makes it suitable for tyre, brake, chassis and bodywork in the company's own yard.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a torsion-resistant double scissor with synchronisation protection, a mechanical locking latch at several height steps, a lowering-safety valve and a 400 V drive unit with limit switch and emergency stop. Mandatory are the CE declaration of conformity under the Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC (from 20 January 2027 the Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230) with a test report to EN 1493, a German operating manual and clear requirements for installation type and foundation. The lift must be inspected by a competent person before first use and at recurring intervals (BetrSichV, DGUV Regel 100-500 chapter 2.10), and spare parts and warranty should be secured in the contract.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • CE declaration of conformity (2006/42/EC or (EU) 2023/1230) and a test report to EN 1493 from a notified body, with the serial number of the delivered lift entered.
  • Data sheet with confirmed capacity (3.5 or 4 t), lifting height, installation type (surface or in-ground) and foundation drawing.
  • Order the 400 V / 50 Hz version explicitly and demand a photo of the motor name plate before shipment.
  • German operating manual, inspection log and spare parts list (seal kits, cylinders, safety valves).
  • Factory test under load (lifting/lowering test, locking latch) by video or pre-shipment inspection.
  • Clarify installation, acceptance, service and warranty in Germany, for example via LAUNCH Europe GmbH in Kerpen.
