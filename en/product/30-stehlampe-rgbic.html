Description

The slim RGBIC floor lamp is 150 cm tall and lets individual LED segments glow in different colours, creating flowing gradients instead of a single colour; it emits light all around (360°) without dark zones.

More

According to the listing, model DW-FL-150 from Shenzhen Dowell Lighting is controlled by IR remote and by the Tuya app via Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, and offers chasing, water-flow and music-sync effects. The lamp ships as a roll-up body in a compact carton (18 x 13 x 13 cm, 0.8 kg), which saves freight and storage space; colour, packaging and branding can be customised as OEM/ODM. Discerning buyers look for stability, even segment colours without a colour cast, flicker-free driving, an EU-compliant power supply with test reports and a RED-compliant radio module with cybersecurity proof.

Request from the supplier

  • Request a sample plus test reports from an accredited lab: LVD (EN 60598-1/-2-4), EMC (EN IEC 55015/61547), RED including EN 18031 proof, RoHS; the reports must name model DW-FL-150 and the supplied power supply and radio module (the listing only shows CE/RoHS without report numbers).
  • Fix the power supply: the listing names 5/12/24 V; have the EU plug, protection class and ecodesign for external power supplies (EU) 2019/1782 including marking confirmed.
  • Clarify radio and app: Tuya white label or own app, get Alexa/Google compatibility confirmed in writing (not named in the Dowell listing), GDPR-compliant cloud (EU data centre) and firmware updates.
  • Measure a production sample: segment colours and brightness along the length, flicker, stability (tip-over test), joints and bonding of the roll-up body; record batch tolerances in the quality agreement.
  • Clarify pack unit and price tiers in writing (1 pack = 1 lamp?), MOQ 18 packs, OEM branding, German manual and GPSR details (EU responsible person, address) on product and carton.
  • Have the BSCI certificate with validity, the audit report and the patent claimed in the listing for the design verified; run a design/patent search in the EU market before import (similar designs from Govee or Briloner).
