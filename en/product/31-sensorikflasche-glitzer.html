Description

The timer consists of a transparent acrylic body in which oil and liquid separate: when turned, drops or bubbles travel slowly downwards – a calm visual stimulus for office …

More

classroom and children's room, used as an hourglass substitute and calm-down aid. Professional buyers look for a permanently sealed housing, a clear, low-bubble liquid with a consistent run time, colour-stable pigments and a filling with a test report to EN 71-3 and REACH. A glitter effect is conceivable as a customisation but must be clarified beforehand because of the microplastics restriction (REACH Annex XVII entry 78). The maker's listing shows the item as an acrylic hourglass for decoration (company profile: sensory and therapy toys) without an age grading; classification as a toy and age grading must be clarified for EU distribution.

Standards & marks (Germany/EU)

Request from the supplier

  • Request filling formulation and safety data sheet (oil type, viscosity, aspiration risk) plus an EN 71-3/REACH test report from an accredited lab; ASTM/EN71 in the listing are maker statements only.
  • Tightness: have drop and leak tests to EN 71-1, the leakage rate per batch and the method (ultrasonic welding or gluing) documented.
  • Clarify classification: toy or decor item (the listing says decoration, the company profile sensory/therapy toys, no age stated); this drives CE marking, age grading and test scope.
  • Clarify price, size and packaging: compare the listing model (13.5 cm) with the market-standard 14-15 cm timer, confirm tier prices from 100 pcs (product page US$ 1.00/0.86, category list US$ 1.2) and calculate against the German unit price of about 4 EUR gross; check wall thickness and leakage in the sample.
  • Release the glitter variant only with proof of the glitter material (microplastics restriction) and a test report.
  • Clarify factory site and audit: company seat in Yiwu (Yiwu day Sat 28 Nov), factory address per profile in Yancheng (Jiangsu); request the ISO 9001 evidence, file GPSR data.
