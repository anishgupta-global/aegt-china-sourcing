Home & smart lighting

Home and smart lighting is an impulse and seasonal range with low unit prices, high volumes and a current technology shift (Matter radio in lamps and luminaires) that replaces installed products.

More

The selection is for AE Global Traders and independent e-commerce sellers who sell private labels via Amazon, OTTO, Kaufland or their own shop and need manufacturers with EU compliance experience (EPREL, RED, GPSR). Deliberately distinct from category 14 (site lighting): this is consumer lighting for living space, desk, garden and terrace.

Zhongshan (Guzhen town, called the 'lighting capital of China' since 2002: ceiling, floor, table and decorative luminaires; the autumn edition of the Guzhen International Lighting Fair is export-oriented) · Shenzhen (Bao'an/Longgang: smart bulbs, LED strips, Matter/Tuya modules, light bars) · Dongguan and Huizhou (rechargeable lamps, LED strips, drivers) · Yiwu/Zhejiang (string lights and festive lighting) · Ningbo/Zhejiang (solar and outdoor lights, electronics OEMs). Only Guzhen is backed by the listed sources; the other clusters are industry knowledge and the factory location must be verified per supplier.

Check exemptions (e.g. by luminous flux, non-removable light sources) and light-source replaceability requirements per product. 2) Mains-powered products: LVD 2014/35/EU, EMC 2014/30/EU, RoHS; radio (Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, Zigbee, Thread): RED 2014/53/EU including the cybersecurity requirements of Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/30 (to our knowledge applicable since 1 Aug 2025, please cross-check); Matter certification via the CSA per model. 3) Rechargeable/solar lamps: Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 + BattG, UN 38.3 for shipping. 4) GPSR (EU) 2023/988: EU responsible person and traceability for all online sales; ElektroG registration (stiftung ear) and VerpackG/LUCID. 5) Sourcing: cheap sets (solar, string lights, strips) show inflated lumen/battery claims and heavy price pressure in the market; plan for sample testing in an EU lab before volume orders.
