Description

The pneumatic breaker is the rugged standard tool for breaking concrete, asphalt and masonry where no mains connection exists or where high blow power is needed in continuous use.

More

With around 20 kg own weight, a working pressure of 5 to 6.3 bar and at least 16 Hz blow frequency, it connects directly to the site's mobile diesel compressor and needs no electronics. Professional users in Germany expect effective vibration damping, a declared vibration value, a silencer and available spare parts for piston, valve and chisel retainer. Compliance means CE under the Machinery Directive, testing to EN ISO 11148-4, sound power level under 2000/14/EC and a German operating manual.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC with a test report under EN ISO 11148-4 from an accredited laboratory (TÜV, SGS, Intertek); Sanrock states CE and ISO 9001:2015 in the listing – request certificates with model reference
  • Sound power level LWA under 2000/14/EC (notified body, state the number) on the rating plate; silencer in the scope of supply
  • Vibration value under EN ISO 28927-10 (m/s², uncertainty K) in datasheet and manual
  • Datasheet with air consumption (m³/min), connection thread/coupling and chisel shank; matching to the diesel compressor (08-diesel-schraubenkompressor)
  • German operating manual and spare parts list (piston, valve, chisel retainer); endurance test on the sample with pressure regulation to the permissible working pressure
  • Written warranty of at least 24 months, Incoterm, MOQ and HS code in the quotation
