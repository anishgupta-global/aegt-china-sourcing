Description

The demolition tong is the attachment for deconstruction in confined areas: it nibbles away concrete and masonry, cuts reinforcing steel and, with a mini excavator, can be used indoors and in backyards.

More

The researched model CTHB-S17 is specified for excavators of 1.7 to 4 t, weighs 210 kg, opens 197 mm and, according to the manufacturer, reaches 47 t crushing or cutting force at 210 kg/cm² (around 206 bar). The manufacturer describes a single-cylinder design with replaceable blades. Professional users look for replaceable blades of wear-resistant steel, a sturdily supported slewing unit, a mounting that matches the excavator exactly and a declaration of conformity under the Machinery Directive. Because of the weight, the lifting capacity and tipping stability of the mini excavator must be calculated before ordering.

Request from the supplier

  • Request CE marking, EC declaration of conformity and German operating manual before ordering (Machinery Directive, Machinery Regulation from 20 Jan 2027)
  • Have fit to the mini excavator confirmed in writing: carrier weight, oil flow and pressure of the auxiliary hydraulics, mounting (pin pattern or quick coupler), ports
  • Check lifting capacity and tipping stability of the excavator with 210 kg attachment weight, demand the manufacturer's release
  • Demand EN 10204 3.1 material certificates for housing and blades plus a hardness report, order replaceable blades as spare parts together
  • Clarify the rotation function (mechanical or hydraulic, motor type) and request a test-run video before shipping
  • Fix pre-shipment inspection, warranty (1 year per listing) and EU spare-parts supply contractually
