Description

On site, the multitool replaces a trip to the toolbox for small jobs such as stripping cables, cutting wire, loosening screws or slitting film.

More

Professional users in Germany expect hardened, corrosion-resistant steel, a play-free pliers joint and blades with a secure lock instead of thin budget sheet metal. On import, weapons law (carry ban for one-hand and locking knives, exception for a legitimate interest) and the trademark and design rights of well-known makers must be considered. A simple version suits promotional or equipment use; for daily use, material and workmanship should be proven by a material certificate.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Material certificate with steel grade and hardness measurement (HRC) of blades and pliers jaws
  • Salt spray test report (at least 48 h) to DIN EN ISO 9227 or equivalent
  • Technical drawing with blade length and locking mechanism for weapons-law classification before import
  • GPSR details, German operating and safety instructions and importer address on the packaging
  • Endurance test of the pliers joint (cycles) and a functional sample from the series, not only a hand sample
  • Intellectual-property check: no copy of protected designs or trademarks (e.g. Leatherman, Victorinox)
