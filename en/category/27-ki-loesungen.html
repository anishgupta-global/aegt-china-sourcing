AI Solutions

This category bundles AI-assisted construction-site technology from China: surveillance cameras with PPE detection, surveying and inspection drones, walking robots …

plus 3D scanners and GNSS surveying for as-built capture and demolition. Beyond unit price, buyers should focus on EU conformity (CE, RED, class marking, cybersecurity and data protection), because connected devices with cameras, radio or cloud links are scrutinised far more strictly in Germany than plain hardware.

AI cameras and robotics cluster around Hangzhou (Hikvision, Dahua, Unitree), drones and hardware electronics around Shenzhen (DJI, suppliers in Guangdong).
