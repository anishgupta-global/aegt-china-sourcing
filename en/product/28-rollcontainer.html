Description

The three-drawer steel mobile pedestal sits under or beside the desk, keeps files, pens and personal documents under lock and, thanks to its castors, moves easily between office, meeting room or site cabin.

More

It calls for cold-rolled steel of at least 0.7 mm wall thickness, an epoxy powder-coated finish, telescopic full-extension runners with drawer stops and a lock with central locking. Professional users in Germany expect a test report to EN 16121/16122, documented castor quality (EN 12529), German assembly and safety information and GPSR-compliant marking. Belivio's offer states 0.7 mm standard thickness and 30 kg load per drawer; however, the dimensions of 400 × 500 × 500 mm are smaller than the German market size (about 420 × 600 × 570 mm) and should be adapted before ordering.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

The mobile pedestal is a classic add-on to desk and filing cabinet and sells well in an office bundle.

More

In its mobile-pedestal category alone, Schaefer Shop lists models between €129 and €339 net (retrieved 2 Nov 2026), with the entry model Pure 333 on promotion at €129 instead of €185; marketplace sellers on Hornbach.de show simple two-drawer units from about €47 gross. Price pressure in the entry segment is high, so differentiation comes from steel thickness, lock, test evidence and delivery assembled. Return risk is medium (fit under the desk, transport damage on bulky freight); no reliable sales figures are available.

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 16121 / EN 16122 (tilt safety, drawer loading, drawer stops) for the model actually supplied
  • Material certificate: fix a wall thickness of at least 0.7 mm in the contract, request powder-coat adhesion and salt-spray records
  • Adapt dimensions to the German market (about 420 × 600 × 570 mm) and check clearance under the desk
  • Castors: define number, material and brakes (soft castors for hard floors, proof to EN 12529)
  • Lock concept: central locking, at least 2 keys per pedestal, spare-key service
  • Factory audit before a volume order (supplier only on Made-in-China since 2026), sample, packaging with edge/corner protection, German manual, GPSR label and warranty terms
