Description

The trolley jack lifts vans and cars of the company fleet for wheel changes, brake maintenance and underbody work without tying up a vehicle lift.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a steel frame with clean welds, a quick-lift pump, an overload valve, rubber-faced saddles and smooth-running castors. The jack is intended for lifting only: the vehicle must always be additionally secured with axle stands, which belongs in every German operating instruction. Evidence under the Machinery Directive and EN 1494 and a GS mark are the minimum for commercial use, complemented by an annual inspection by a competent person.
