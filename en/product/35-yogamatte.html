Competition is very high (Otto, Amazon, Kaufland with many own brands); differentiation only through material and test reports, packaging and bundles (band, roller). Return risk: odour, inconsistent thickness claims (listings state 6 mm or 0.6 cm) and slip resistance.

Request from the supplier

  • Request a REACH/PAH test report (SGS or similar) for exactly this material and batch
  • Specify thickness, density and recovery in writing and measure on the sample
  • Odour and slip test (dry/wet) on the sample; approval sample before series start
  • Fix carry strap, packaging and label with German text, GPSR details and EU responsible person in the contract
  • Confirm the Zhangjiagang (Jiangsu) factory by audit; the company seat is in Yueyang (Hunan)
