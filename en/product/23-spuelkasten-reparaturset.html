Description

The kit replaces the worn internal mechanism of a WC cistern: drain valve with dual-flush push button (full and eco flush) and side-connected inlet valve with float.

More

It restores leaking cisterns, running water and defective seals without replacing the ceramic and lowers water consumption. Buyers look for exact fit (drain opening, 3/8 inch inlet connection, installation height), tight rubbers, corrosion-free fixing and adjustable flush volume. As a part in contact with drinking water, the inlet valve must meet the German requirements for plastics in contact with drinking water.

More

OBI the Siamp universal fill valve for EUR 17.99 and Geberit Unifill for EUR 29.99; according to the scan stage, the universal fill valve costs EUR 7.90 at Kaufland and EUR 12.90 at Bauhaus, OBI lists seal sets from EUR 1.99, and eBay offers dual-flush repair kits (a search hit shows about EUR 14.99). Wear parts are bought year-round as repair items and cross-bought with the WC or renovation. Risk: fit (inlet, thread, installation height) is the main cause of returns, so the shop needs a dimension and compatibility table; mains-water parts carry a liability risk in case of leaks.

Request from the supplier

  • Fit table from the supplier: drain opening (2 or 3 inch), installation height, inlet thread 3/8 inch, compatibility with the Huida close-coupled cistern in the catalogue (reference product 23-wc-keramik).
  • Test report on tightness and flush volume (dual flush, EN 14055) and endurance test of push buttons and seals.
  • Proof for drinking-water contact of the inlet valve: KTW/UBA conformity of plastics and rubbers, backflow prevention per EN 1717.
  • Packaging as a set with German installation instructions, parts list, GPSR labelling and LUCID registration; clarify the unit (1 mechanism or 2 parts per piece; the listing says 'Piece' and 'Two Piece').
  • Company proof: Honglei states 107 employees and 3,000 sqm; the product page names a 1996 founding, the profile 2014; clarify the discrepancy and request a factory audit.
  • First sample batch and incoming inspection (tightness, gap, spring force), only then the series order.
