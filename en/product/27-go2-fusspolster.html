Description

The foot pads sit at the foot tips of the Go2 and are the part that takes ground, grit and stair edges: they cushion the touchdown impulse and provide grip, but wear down with distance walked.

More

Anyone using the robot regularly outdoors, in teaching or in demonstrations needs replacements that fit exactly, stay non-slip and can be swapped without special tools. On the market the pair is offered at EUR 65.95; other Go2 accessories such as batteries and chargers range from EUR 71 to 1,049. The part is light, suitable for letter or parcel shipping and not dangerous goods, unlike the battery.

Request from the supplier

  • Identify the supplier by enquiry: no verified manufacturer or price for Go2 foot pads was found in this research; original parts come from Unitree (Hangzhou), replicas from rubber/silicone mould makers (e.g. around Dongguan, Shenzhen, Ningbo).
  • Check the 3D sample and dimensional drawing on a real Go2: fit, hold, abrasion, slip resistance on tiles, grass and gravel.
  • Define and document material and hardness (Shore A, abrasion, temperature range); prove REACH conformity (PAHs, SVHC) with a test report.
  • Define the unit and scope of delivery (pair or set of four pads, attachment).
  • Request tooling cost and MOQ for a dedicated mould if no standard part is available.
  • Sell only as a compatible part without Unitree logo; appoint an EU responsible person under GPSR and clarify packaging for LUCID.
