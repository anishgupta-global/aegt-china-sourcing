Description

The snap-off utility knife is the daily cutting tool on a strip-out site for sheeting, insulation, carpet, floor coverings, cardboard and sealant.

More

A high-quality version has a metal body of die-cast aluminium or zinc, an auto-lock that stops the blade giving way while cutting, and a compartment for spare blades in the handle. A professional user in Germany expects 18 mm blades of hardened carbon steel with defined break points that snap off cleanly without splinters, and clear product-safety marking. Because of the risk of cuts, a safety knife with a self-retracting blade is also recommended.

Request from the supplier

  • Material and hardness proof for the blade (steel grade, HRC 58 to 62) and cutting test on 20 sample knives
  • Check the break points: blades snap off cleanly without splinters; dimensions 18 mm / 0.5 mm fit common replacement blades
  • Declaration of conformity / product safety under ProdSG or GPSR (EU) 2023/988 with a named EU responsible person
  • REACH/PAH declaration for plastic and soft-grip parts of the handle
  • Packaging with German warning and safety notice; warranty and return agreement for rejects
