Description

The petrol cut-off saw cuts concrete, stone, asphalt, pipes and steel independently of a power connection on demolition and strip-out sites and reaches cutting depths of …

More

about 125 mm with a 350 mm diamond or abrasive blade. Professional users expect a two-stroke engine with anti-vibration damping, a water connection for dust suppression, a sturdy blade guard, easy belt re-tensioning and spare parts for carburettor, starter and ignition. Compliance covers CE marking under the Machinery Directive, testing to EN ISO 19432, engine approval under Regulation (EU) 2016/1628 and a German manual. In enclosed rooms petrol operation is not permitted without sufficient ventilation because of carbon monoxide; electric or battery machines are to be chosen there.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC with CE marking (listing claims CE, EPA and Euro V; have these proven by certificate)
  • Test report to EN ISO 19432 (blade guard, speed, throttle lock, brake/run-out)
  • Engine approval under Regulation (EU) 2016/1628 (Stage V) with approval number on the engine plate
  • Sound power and vibration values to EN 12096 in the German manual
  • Spare-parts package (carburettor, ignition coil, starter, drive belt, air filter) and at least 24 months warranty
  • Blade release: maximum permissible speed matched to diamond blades with oSa mark or EN 13236
