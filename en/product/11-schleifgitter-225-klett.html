Description

Hook-and-loop sanding mesh Ø 225 mm is the consumable for long-neck and drywall sanders (“giraffe” sanders) and removes filler, plaster and paint from walls and ceilings.

More

The open mesh lets the sanding dust escape directly through the machine pad into the extraction, so the grit clogs less and the room air is less polluted. Professional users expect an evenly coated grain to the FEPA P grit scale (ISO 6344), a hook-and-loop backing that still holds firmly after many changes, and clear marking of diameter and grit. The mixed set K80–K180 covers everything from coarse removal to fine sanding; properly packed, it ships in a 25-piece bag with a grit colour code, EU responsible person and GPSR labelling.

Return risk: hook-and-loop backing or hole pattern does not fit the machine pad, grit mix-ups in mixed sets (colour code on the backing). A natural bundle partner for long-neck sanders (“goes with”).

Request from the supplier

  • Request samples of each grit (K80/K100/K120/K150/K180) and test sanding performance and service life against the Menzer reference mesh (Hornbach 8490696) on the target machine; fix the abrasive grain (aluminium oxide or silicon carbide) and backing in writing.
  • Check the hook-and-loop holding force (pull and shear, repeated peeling and re-applying) and the Ø 225 mm fit on the target machine (e.g. Flex GE 5/GE 6 or the dws01 wall sander); test dust extraction through the pad with the machine running.
  • Confirm price and minimum order in writing: the listing states 3,000 sheets in the price line, 10,000 pieces per grit in the description and 1,500 m² as an attribute; the price of USD 0.04–0.05 per sheet is far below competing listings (USD 0.16–0.45).
  • Ask for a grain size distribution test report to ISO 6344 (FEPA P) and batch traceability; agree assembly as a 25-piece mixed set with a grit colour code, single-grit bags and retail packaging (EU responsible person, GPSR).
  • Request a REACH declaration (SVHC) and the safety data sheet for resin bond and adhesive.
  • Verify maker status: Made-in-China lists Zhongtai as “Manufacturer/Factory & Trading Company” (factory in Zhijiang, Hubei); ask for copies of the certificates (per listing ISO, BSCI, FSC, MPA, SEDEX, no evidence shown), and run a factory audit or third-party inspection before a series order.
