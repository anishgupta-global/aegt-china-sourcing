Description

The Z-line pre-filter sits in the intake of the negative pressure unit and catches coarse dust and fibres before they reach the high-grade H13 HEPA filter.

More

The pleated synthetic medium in a moisture-resistant, die-cut full cardboard frame offers a large filter area at low initial pressure drop and is quickly changed in the black area. G4 is the common filter class, in standard formats such as 610 × 610 × 48 mm; the format must match the unit type or pre-filter holder. After use in the contaminated area, used pre-filters count as contaminated and must be disposed of in line with TRGS 519. The pre-filter is replaced more often than the downstream HEPA filter set and is therefore kept in stock by project in packs.

E-commerce potential

Recurring consumable: pre-filters are replaced per project and can be cross-sold with every negative pressure unit and HEPA filter set.

More

German filter specialists list Z-line G4 filters in a cardboard frame at roughly EUR 17-21 each (filter-shop24.de, as of 31 Oct 2026: 592 x 592 x 48 mm EUR 21.30 gross; smaller formats EUR 16.90-20.23). Competition comes from filter specialists and from unit makers (Deconta, enviro) with unit-specific formats; returns risk arises almost only from a wrong format, so the shop should ask for the unit type when choosing the size.

Request from the supplier

  • Check the installation size (length x width x depth, 47 vs. 48 mm) and frame overhang on the unit or pre-filter holder; order samples first
  • Ask for a filter-class test report (G4 per EN 779 / ISO 16890 Coarse) from an independent lab, not just a self-declaration
  • Cardboard frame moisture-resistant and dimensionally stable (humidity test), medium firmly bonded, no fibre shedding
  • Fix initial and final pressure drop and nominal air flow in the data sheet (reference approx. 42 Pa / 250 Pa)
  • Dust-tight packaging in bundles (e.g. 10 or 20 pcs) with a label for format and class; clarify packaging registration (LUCID)
