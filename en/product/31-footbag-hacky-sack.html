Description

A footbag (hacky sack) is a small granulate-filled bag made of faux leather, suede imitation or crochet yarn that is kept in the air with feet and knees.

More

Quality models show cleanly stitched panels (typically 2 to 32), tightly set seams, leak-proof filling granulate and even weight. Custom colours, logo embroidery and printing make the footbag a popular promotional item and party favour. Depending on positioning it counts as a toy (6+, Toy Safety Directive with EN 71) or as sports equipment for teens and adults (GPSR).

Request from the supplier

  • Clarify the material (PVC, faux leather, textile) and request samples to specification; resolve the contradiction in the listing (PVC vs. suede/cotton) in writing.
  • EN 71-1/-3 test reports from an accredited lab; have filling granulate and seam strength checked with a leakage test.
  • Chemical tests for PVC/PU and dyes: phthalates, PAH, azo dyes; chromium(VI) for leather.
  • Fix age grading and warnings (toy 6+ or sports equipment 14+); no licensed or brand prints.
  • Clarify maker and production site: check suppliers at the route locations (Yiwu Sat 28 Nov, Shenzhen) for in-house footbag production by audit or video audit; samples in 3-7 days per listing, measure weight and diameter per piece.
  • GPSR documents: EU responsible person, batch marking, German warnings; packaging registration (LUCID).
