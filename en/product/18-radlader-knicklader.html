Description

The compact wheel loader loads rubble, excavated soil and demolition material into containers and onto trucks and moves material across site yards and ramps.

More

Articulated steering and hydrostatic four-wheel drive make it manoeuvrable and capable off-road; with pallet fork, grab bucket or sweeper it becomes a multi-purpose machine. A premium unit offers a ROPS/FOPS cab, an EU Stage V diesel engine, a standardised quick-attach interface and, for road travel between sites, an operating permit under German law. Professional users also expect CE with a declaration of conformity, a German manual, documented noise levels and a secured spare-parts supply.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity and CE marking for the complete machine (type, serial number, manufacturer)
  • Stage V type-approval of the delivered engine under (EU) 2016/1628
  • Proof of rated and tipping load under ISO 14397-1 with rating plate
  • ROPS/FOPS test certificates of the cab (ISO 3471, ISO 3449) and noise test report for the sound power level under 2000/14/EC
  • Documents for road approval (operating permit, COC or ability to pass individual approval) for travel between sites
  • German-language manual, spare-parts catalogue, written warranty and a service partner in Germany or the EU
