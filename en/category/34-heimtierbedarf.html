Pet supplies

Pet supplies are a stable online market with high repeat purchase: pets live in 43% of German households and, according to IVH, online retail generated just over EUR 1.5 bn in 2025 …

The range bundles non-food products (furniture, smart devices, travel gear, grooming) that are made in China in large volumes and sold in Germany via marketplaces, D2C brands and specialist retail. It is aimed at AE Global Traders and independent e-commerce sellers who want to build own brands; consumables such as filters and litter add recurring revenue.

Typical sourcing areas (orientation only, verify the factory location per supplier in the product stage): Shenzhen and Dongguan (Guangdong) for smart devices, GPS trackers, feeders and fountains; Ningbo and Cixi (Zhejiang) for injection moulding, small appliances and grooming items; Yiwu (Zhejiang) for leashes, harnesses and small accessories; Shantou-Chenghai (Guangdong) for toys including interactive pet toys; Foshan and Dongguan (Guangdong) for furniture such as cat trees; bag clusters such as Guangzhou, Quanzhou (Fujian) and Baigou (Hebei) for carrier bags.
