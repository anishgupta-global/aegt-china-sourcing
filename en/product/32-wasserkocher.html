Description

The cordless kettle holds 1.7 litres and brings water to the boil quickly at high power.

More

Auto shut-off and boil-dry protection are the safety-critical core functions; for a mains-powered device a cleanly finished rust-free water path, a limescale filter and a stable 360-degree base also count. Demanding buyers expect CE conformity (low voltage, EMC), RoHS, ElektroG registration and food-contact compliance of the water path as well as a German manual. As a mass-market small appliance the model suits an own brand competing on price and reliability.

The segment is crowded and price-driven; differentiate via finish (stainless steel instead of plastic), noise level and warranty. Return and warranty risk from electrics and limescale; autumn and winter and Black Friday promotions support sales (assessment).

Request from the supplier

  • Request the CE declaration of conformity with test reports under EN 60335-1 and EN 60335-2-15 plus an EMC report for exactly this model (not only series certificates).
  • Confirm the water-path material (304 stainless steel, BPA-free plastics) and request an LFGB report for inner parts and lid.
  • Measure power, boil-dry protection and shut-off (Strix controller or equivalent) on the sample; Schuko plug with German manual.
  • Clarify the importer's ElektroG registration and WEEE number before selling; GPSR label and EU responsible person.
  • Confirm the plant: BSCI/ISO audit, test-lab accreditation, warranty and spare-part agreement (wear, heating element).
  • Have limescale filter, lid hinge and base endurance-tested (e.g. 3,000 boil cycles).
