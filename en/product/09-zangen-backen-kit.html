Description

Jaws, blades and teeth are the wear parts of the demolition shear and pulverizer and determine cutting performance and service life.

More

Competing products use exchangeable wear plates with teeth and blades made of Hardox 400; professional users expect wear-resistant steel with proof of hardness, mounting and bolt pattern matching the machine exactly, and fastening material with tightening torques. The kit is model-specific and is ordered only with the model designation or drawing of the shear or pulverizer. Because of the high weight it ships as palletised freight.

Request from the supplier

  • Model and serial number of the shear or pulverizer plus drawing of jaws and blades (mounting, bolt pattern, bolt size) before quotation.
  • Material certificate EN 10204 3.1 for Hardox 400 or NM400-equivalent steel and hardness report (HBW) per lot.
  • Parts list with item numbers: number of blades, teeth and bolts per kit, tightening torques.
  • Define weight per kit, pallet dimensions, packaging and Incoterm before ordering.
  • Fit check of the first set on the machine, then series delivery; 12 months warranty (listing).
  • Batch marking and traceability of the wear parts.
