Description

The mobile construction fence (Bauzaun) is a 3.50 m wide and 2.00 m high panel of a galvanised steel tube frame with welded wire-mesh infill and is set up without foundation on concrete or plastic feet.

More

Professional users expect a hot-dip galvanised frame (reference: vertical tubes dia. 40 x 1.1 mm, horizontal tubes dia. 28 x 1.1 mm, wire 3 mm), clean weld points, matching feet and clamps for seamless connection of the panels and optional support braces under wind load. The system is modular: one foot per panel, clamps hold the panels together, and banners or privacy screens raise the wind load and require additional anchoring. From China the item can be placed on the market with GPSR information, an EU responsible person and packaging registration; for steel products, the current EU import rules must also be checked.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix tube diameter, wall thickness, wire gauge and mesh size in writing (target values like the reference: dia. 40/28 x 1.1 mm, wire 3 mm) and weigh a sample (reference 15 kg per panel).
  • Prove the zinc coating: hot-dip galvanising to EN ISO 1461 with a coating-thickness record or salt-spray report; explicitly separate pre-galvanising with powder on weld points.
  • Test the fit of feet, clamps and support braces with the systems common in Germany (sample set-up with at least three panels, check tube spacing and clamp geometry).
  • Spot-check dimensional accuracy (3,500 x 2,000 mm), weld-point strength and deburring; fix the scope of the set (panel, foot, clamp) in writing.
  • Clarify palletising, container loading (panels per 40 ft container) and transport protection; check GPSR labelling, EU responsible person, LUCID and current EU import rules for steel goods (tariff classification, steel measures, CBAM) before ordering.
