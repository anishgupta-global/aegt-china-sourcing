Description

The steel locker provides separate, lockable storage of employees' work and private clothing in changing and break cabins.

More

Expect a torsion-resistant body of powder-coated sheet steel, deburred edges, ventilated doors and a cylinder or coin-deposit lock. Professional buyers in Germany require compliance with the requirements for changing rooms and wardrobes under ASR A4.1, stability tested to EN 16121/16122 and a spare-key service. Supplied knocked down, the locker saves freight; supplied welded, it saves assembly time on site.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 16121 / EN 16122 (stability, door loading, door-opening endurance test)
  • Material certificate: body ≥ 0.7 mm, door ≥ 0.8 mm, deburred edges (cut protection) and powder-coat adhesion to EN ISO 2409
  • Locking system: cylinder lock with at least 2 keys, optionally coin-deposit or master-key variant; proof of lock quality
  • Compare compartment dimensions and fittings (hat shelf, clothes rail, ventilation) with ASR A4.1 and the needs of the site
  • Fix the delivery form (knocked down or welded), palletising and pack dimensions; German assembly instructions for knocked-down goods
  • Warranty and spare parts (doors, locks, keys) and a pre-shipment sample to check edges and door gap
