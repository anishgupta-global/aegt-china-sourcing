Description

The submersible clear-water pump moves clean to slightly turbid water without coarse solids and suits transferring from tanks and cisterns, draining pre-filtered water and irrigation on site.

More

A stainless-steel or impact-resistant plastic housing, float switch, thermal motor protection and IPX8 protection are the standard for continuous duty. Users in Germany expect CE, conformity with EN 60335-2-41, an H07RN-F cable with earthing-contact plug and a German operating manual. On construction sites, operation on a site distribution board with RCD protection per DIN VDE 0100-704 and regular inspection under DGUV Regulation 3 are part of the package; for sludge and solids the dirty-water pump remains the right tool.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity with test reports (EN 60335-2-41, EMC) from an accredited body; Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC and, from 20 Jan 2027, Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230 addressed.
  • Proof of IPX8 (immersion depth, test duration) and functional test of float switch and thermal motor protection per lot.
  • Pump curve (Q/H) to ISO 9906 and photo of the rating plate showing 230 V, 750 W, immersion depth, CE and manufacturer.
  • H07RN-F supply cable with earthing-contact plug (VDE/HAR marked) and strain relief; sample for DGUV V3 inspection before series delivery.
  • German operating manual, at least 12 months warranty and available spare parts (impeller, mechanical seal, float).
