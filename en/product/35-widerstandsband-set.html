Description

The set comprises natural latex bands in five resistance levels (X-Light to X-Heavy, 15-300 lbs per maker) plus a door anchor for pulling exercises and pull-up assistance.

More

It replaces dumbbells for full-body training, stretching and rehab and stores in very little space. Buyers look for even wall thickness, tensile strength per level, colour-coded levels and a natural latex notice (allergy warning). For EU marketing, GPSR details, a REACH/PAH test report and a German-language exercise guide are recommended.

Wear item: plan for tear and odour complaints.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix number and dimensions of bands per set and level marking in writing
  • Require a REACH/PAH test report; the RoHS/CE claims in the listing are not relevant for sports bands
  • Tensile test per level (force at elongation, breaking load) and endurance test on samples
  • Natural latex notice and German exercise guide on packaging; ask for a latex-free option
  • Check door anchor for load and edge protection; factory audit (plant in Yangjiang, Guangdong)
