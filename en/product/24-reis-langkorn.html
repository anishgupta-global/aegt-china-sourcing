Description

White long-grain rice (jasmine aromatic rice) in 5 to 25 kg sacks for site catering, canteens and repacking.

More

Uniform, polished grains with a low share of broken kernels and no impurities, insects or off-odour are expected. In the EU, maximum levels apply to inorganic arsenic and cadmium in rice (Reg. (EU) 2023/915), and rice from China is, in practice, checked more intensively for unauthorised genetically modified lines. Traceability, German labelling under the FIC Regulation and lab reports per batch are mandatory.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Valid ISO 22000 / BRCGS / IFS certificate of the mill and packing site.
  • Lab report per batch on inorganic arsenic, cadmium, mycotoxins and pesticide residues.
  • Proof of GMO freedom (GMO test) and clarification with customs of the current rules on intensified import controls for rice from China.
  • Batch traceability back to producer or harvest (vintage, growing region) and a specification sheet.
  • Declaration of compliance of the sack material for food contact under Reg. (EC) 1935/2004 and a German label draft under the FIC Regulation.
  • Sample with cooking and odour test and certificate of origin before series release.
