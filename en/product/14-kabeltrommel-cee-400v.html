Description

The 400 V CEE cable reel supplies three-phase loads such as building dryers, negative-pressure units …

More

concrete mixers and extraction systems over longer distances and is part of the basic equipment of every strip-out and refurbishment site. German professionals expect an impact-resistant, UV-stable housing, an H07RN-F 5G2.5 mm² rubber cable with thermal protection for the wound-up state, CEE connectors rated IP44 and a sturdy crank drum with stand. The reel must be CE-compliant, meet DIN VDE 0100-704 for construction sites and pass the inspection cycle under DGUV Regulation 3; brands such as Brennenstuhl or AS-Schwabe set the benchmark. Imported reels from China are only marketable with a test report to EN 61316 and EN 60309 and traceable cable quality (HAR/VDE marking).

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 61316 and EN 60309-1/-2 from an accredited lab, including temperature rise with the cable wound up
  • Cable evidence: HAR/VDE marking H07RN-F 5G2.5, conductor resistance and copper content
  • Proof of the thermal cut-out (trip test) and of IP44 protection to EN 60529
  • CE declaration of conformity (2014/35/EU, 2014/30/EU, RoHS) and ElektroG/WEEE registration of the importer
  • Sample test: strain relief, crank and drum strength, drop test at low temperature
