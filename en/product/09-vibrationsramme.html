Description

The vibro driver drives sheet piles, beams, pipes and sections into the ground by vibration and extracts them again, without hammer blows, with much less noise and shock than an impact piling hammer.

More

It is carried on the excavator boom or stick and supplied through the existing auxiliary hydraulics, no separate power pack is needed. The researched model Jisan JSP250 is specified for excavators of 18 to 25 t, with 265 kN centrifugal force, 36 Nm eccentric moment, 2,800 rpm, 155 l/min and 300 bar at about 2,300 kg total weight including the extension boom. Branded references are the NPK sheet pilers HP-7SX (excavators 17–21 t, 1,050 kg, 10.6 t) and HP-10L (25–35 t, 1,250 kg, 17.6 t) from Schlueter Baumaschinen and the Ammann APA range for excavators of the 10–20 t class. Professional users look for clamp jaw and clamping force, rubber damping to the boom, hose routing including case-drain line, slewing unit and conformity under the Machinery Directive and EN 996.

More

Schlueter Baumaschinen lists the NPK sheet pilers HP-7SX and HP-10L without prices; Ammann names the APA range for 10–20 t with 72 kN centrifugal force in product data sheets, likewise without prices. A public EU new-unit price for the 18–25 t class was not found, so there is no price comparison. Chinese listings are around USD 9,500–24,000 for 14–30 t units. The unit is a niche product with few purchase occasions but high goods value; return risk with wrong oil output or mounting, freight weight about 3.5 t gross. No bestseller rank data found.

Request from the supplier

  • Request CE marking, EC declaration of conformity and German operating instructions before ordering (Machinery Directive, EN 996; Machinery Regulation from 20 Jan 2027)
  • Have the JSP250 data sheet confirmed in writing: weight, pressure and rotation angle, because the listing contradicts itself (table 2,300 kg, 300 bar, 180° versus attribute field 2,600 kg, 280 bar, 270°)
  • Have the fit to the excavator confirmed: carrier weight and tipping stability with about 2,300 kg on the boom, oil flow 155 l/min at 300 bar, case-drain line, mounting, control circuit of the clamp jaws
  • Ask for clamp jaws (profile type: sheet pile, beam, pipe), clamping force, rubber dampers and eccentric bearing; order clamp jaws and seals as spare parts
  • Request a test-run video with clamping and vibrating and a noise statement before shipment; clarify vibration load for the excavator operator
  • Contractually fix pre-shipment inspection, warranty (1 year per listing) and EU spare-parts supply
