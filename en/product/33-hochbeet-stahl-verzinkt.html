Description

The bottomless raised bed is made of galvanised or Aluzinc-coated sheet steel and assembled as a modular system of panels and connectors.

More

The open base allows contact with the soil and soil life; wall heights of up to 82 cm reduce bending, and the 100 × 40 × 77 cm balcony variant fits a terrace or loggia. Quality markers are an even zinc or Aluzinc layer, rolled or rounded edges against cuts, a documented sheet thickness and a wall that withstands the earth pressure of a full fill. For retail, clean GPSR labelling, a compact flat pack and spare connectors and screws matter.

Competition is high and price-driven, return risk medium (bulky parcel, cut edges, rust complaints); differentiate through sheet thickness, rounded edges, size variants and spare parts.

Request from the supplier

  • Request sheet thickness (mm), zinc coating weight (g/m²) or Aluzinc class and mill certificate to EN 10346.
  • Require a salt-spray test report (EN ISO 9227) stating hours to red rust.
  • Check edge protection on the sample: rolled or rounded, deburred, no sharp corners.
  • Quote the custom sizes 240 × 80 × 82 cm and 100 × 40 × 77 cm separately (panel height, connectors, flat-pack size and weight).
  • Have German assembly instructions, spare parts (connectors, screws), GPSR label and the tariff code incl. CBAM applicability confirmed.
