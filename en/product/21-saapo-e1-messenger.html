Description

The Saapo E1 satellite messenger is a compact communication device that, according to the listing, exchanges text messages over the Iridium network …

More

(Short Burst Data) and determines its position via GNSS (GPS/BeiDou), so it works where there is no mobile coverage. For sites in open terrain, lone working and crews away from the networks it is an inexpensive emergency and status channel, but it does not replace a satellite phone because voice is not supported and Iridium airtime must be booked on top. The dealer listing names neither protection rating nor battery nor certifications, so robustness and runtime must be checked on a sample before ordering. A professional user in Germany expects CE conformity under the RED, a proven authorised Iridium connection, a German manual and a service partner in the EU; the reference product in this segment is the Garmin inReach Messenger.

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under RED 2014/53/EU with test reports from an accredited lab (radio in the Iridium band, EMC to EN 301 489-20, safety to EN 62368-1); do not place on the market without them
  • Written proof that the device is approved for the Iridium network, including airtime provider and costs; the listing claim 'Brand New 100% Original' must be backed by a manufacturer declaration
  • Clarification of who manufactures the Saapo brand (Maojiexin may only be a dealer or agent), with factory address and business licence
  • Lab test report for the ingress protection (IP) plus data on battery capacity, runtime and UN 38.3 transport testing (not named in the listing)
  • Practical test of a sample in Germany: message delivery, GNSS accuracy (the 10 m claim in the listing is not confirmed in the attribute field) and the emergency/SOS path, if any
  • German or English manual, warranty of at least 24 months, service point in the EU and WEEE/battery registration of the importer
