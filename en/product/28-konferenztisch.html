Description

A conference table of 3.2 m length and 1.5 m depth gives eight to ten participants ample working surface and is the centrepiece of every meeting room.

More

What matters is a torsion-resistant construction, a robust melamine top with cleanly bonded edges, a stable base with levelling feet and, for office use, an option for cable cut-outs. Professional buyers in Germany expect dimensions and mechanics to EN 527-1/-2, strength proof to EN 14074, formaldehyde emission within the new REACH limits (applicable since 6 August 2026) and a well-thought-out assembly concept for knock-down delivery. Esun's offer is a configurable standard model (KT002) that does not state top thickness or base material; both must be fixed by contract.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Conference tables are project-driven goods (office fit-out, relocation, coworking) with a high share of bulky freight and assembly.

More

In Germany, system suppliers and specialist retailers dominate: Schaefer Shop lists rectangular tables of 3,500 × 1,000 mm for up to 12 persons between €2,499 and €3,099 net (retrieved 2 Nov 2026). In the entry segment direct importers push prices down: a search hit for Kaufland.de names tables of 240 × 120 cm 'for 10 persons' from €346.90 (per search-result summary, page not retrievable). The opportunity lies in B2B project business with chairs and whiteboard as a bundle; return risk in B2C is high (size, transport damage).

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the panel build in the contract: melamine MFC with thickness ≥ 25 mm, PVC/ABS edge banding, formaldehyde test report (new REACH limits)
  • Base: specify material, wall thickness and powder coating, levelling feet, load test to EN 14074 and dimensional check to EN 527-1/-2
  • Agree the configuration for 8–10 persons (3,200 × 1,500 mm) with optional cable cut-outs and power-outlet recess
  • Packaging and assembly: knock-down delivery with corner protection, German assembly instructions, assembly video, spare fittings kit
  • Timber origin and due diligence (EUDR): request supply-chain evidence for the panels
  • Sample before a volume order, payment plan (30 % deposit per listing), 5-year warranty in writing and spare-parts supply
