Description

The welder's protective jacket protects the upper body and arms against weld spatter, brief flame contact and UV radiation as generated in arc and gas welding and cutting.

More

Essentials are flame-retardant fabric made up with covered closures, flap pockets and a high-closing collar; under EN ISO 11611 a distinction is made between class 1 (lower spatter exposure) and class 2 (higher exposure) and flame spread A1/A2. As category II PPE the jacket needs an EU type examination, and the manufacturer must state standard, class and care instructions (wash durability of the finish) on the label.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EU type-examination certificate to EN ISO 11611 (class, A1/A2) with NB number for exactly this model; the listing only names NFPA/GB/AS-NZS.
  • Clarify the fabric: 100 % cotton or Nomex/aramid blend; evidence weight, flame-retardant finish and wash durability (number of washes).
  • Require test reports on flame spread, spatter resistance (number of drops), heat transmission and tensile strength.
  • Check workmanship: flap pockets, covered buttons/zip, high collar, seam strength with flame-retardant thread.
  • Have tier prices (200/500/2,000 pcs) and the manufacturer status of the Changping/Dongguan plant confirmed in writing.
