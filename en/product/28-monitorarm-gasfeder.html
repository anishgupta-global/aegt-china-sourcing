Description

The monitor arm lifts the screen to eye level and frees the desk surface, a classic accessory for sit-stand workstations.

More

The gas spring adjusts to monitor weights of 1 to 9 kg, so the screen can be raised, lowered, tilted and swivelled with one hand and stays in any position. The slim metal frame with cable routing and rotation stop looks tidy in meeting and reception areas as well. Discerning buyers in Germany expect a clean VESA mount of 75 x 75 and 100 x 100 mm, top-side installation without crawling under the desk, documented endurance tests and a German manual. Gas spring and joints should be covered by spare parts or a warranty.

More

single arms start at approx. EUR 39.99 at Kaufland and a Smartzone review (9 Jan 2026) calls monitor arms indispensable for ergonomics, neither re-checked here. Small, light parcel (approx. 2.9 kg gross in a 41.5 x 20.5 x 11.5 cm carton) with a price ladder up to approx. EUR 139 for premium dual arms. Returns are mostly caused by wrong monitor weight (arm too strong or too weak) and missing VESA mount on curved monitors, so state the weight range and VESA notes prominently in the listing. Strong add-on sale to the height-adjustable desk.

Request from the supplier

  • Request an endurance test report for arm and gas spring (cycle count, load range 1-9 kg, test lab) and GS/TUV/CE certificates quoting model LDT62-C012.
  • Have the following confirmed in writing: VESA 75 x 75 and 100 x 100, screw kit incl. spacers for curved monitors, clamp range (desk thickness) and grommet size.
  • Check a sample: gaps, spring return at 1 kg and 9 kg, squeaking, paint and anodising quality, cable routing.
  • Obtain REACH and RoHS declarations, a salt-spray test of the finish and the material statement (aluminium/steel/plastic).
  • Neutral packaging with German manual, EU responsible person (GPSR) and LUCID number; confirm carton 41.5 x 20.5 x 11.5 cm and unit weight 2.3 kg net.
  • Put warranty period and spare-parts supply (gas spring, clamp) in writing; sample first, then a first order from 500 pcs (MOQ).
