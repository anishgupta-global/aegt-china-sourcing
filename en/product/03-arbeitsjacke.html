Description

The waistband jacket is the counterpart to work trousers and the classic top for construction, installation and the trades, because the waistband keeps out draughts and nothing snags on scaffolding or ladders.

More

Good models use 260 to 300 g/m² cotton or cotton/polyester with triple stitching, flap chest pockets and a full-length zip with a cover placket. A professional user in Germany expects a reliable size chart from 44 to 64, colour fastness after washing at 60 °C and freedom from harmful substances under REACH. Normatively the jacket is workwear under EN ISO 13688; without a stated protective function it is not PPE, and the fibre composition must appear on the label under EU textile labelling rules.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report on tensile strength, seam strength, dimensional change after washing and colour fastness of the fabric
  • REACH declaration of conformity or test report on azo dyes, formaldehyde and heavy metals
  • Laboratory check of the fibre composition (e.g. cotton/polyester 65/35) matching the label under Regulation (EU) No 1007/2011
  • Size chart in EU measurements with fit samples in at least three sizes before production
  • Durability test of the zip and snap buttons, plus proof of origin of trims (YKK)
  • Series sample with wash test (5 washes at 60 °C) for dimensional change and seam stability
