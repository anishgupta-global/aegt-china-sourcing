Description

The 12-inch balance bike without pedals trains balance, coordination and steering and prepares children for later cycling.

More

Good models have a cleanly welded steel-tube frame, height-adjustable saddle and handlebar, grippy handles with end caps and puncture-proof tyres. The model listed here, NQ-B003, states a carbon-steel frame with argon welding, a front basket and a CE statement. Tyre type (EVA or pneumatic), saddle height and age statements are inconsistent in the listing and must be clarified before ordering.

Request from the supplier

  • Clarify classification: toy (EN 71, saddle height/target weight) or bicycle (EN ISO 8098); request a test report from an accredited lab for NQ-B003.
  • Resolve listing contradictions: age 3-8 years, load over 50 kg, tyre type (EVA or pneumatic), saddle and handlebar adjustment range.
  • Check frame and fork strength, welds, grips and end caps on the sample (edges, pinch points, small parts).
  • Have the paint/powder coating tested for EN 71-3 and REACH.
  • Calculate freight (bulky carton, CKD options) and check transport packaging; labelling with CE, EU responsible person (GPSR), German assembly instructions.
  • Obtain a binding tariff information (toy 9503 or bicycle 8712) and plan a manufacturer audit (employee figures contradict each other).
