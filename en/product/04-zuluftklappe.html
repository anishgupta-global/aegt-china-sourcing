Description

The supply-air flap forms the controlled make-up opening of the black area: as soon as the negative pressure unit starts, the louvres open even at low air flow …

More

if the negative pressure drops, they close by themselves and prevent contaminated air from escaping. The 305 x 305 mm format suits small enclosures and lock walls; larger set-ups use several flaps or formats up to 610 x 910 mm. Depending on the version, frame and louvres are made of aluminium or weather-resistant plastic; an optional pre-filter holder makes the flap combinable with standard pre-filters. Smooth surfaces and few joints ease decontamination during dismantling.

More

Evidence (as of 2 Nov 2026): the ASUP online shop publishes DECONTA supply-air flaps at EUR 124.04 net (305 x 305 mm) and EUR 199.02 net (610 x 610 mm); the maker lists the 305 x 305 mm flap as item no. BO6713. Several flaps are needed per containment, so it sells well as a set with the negative pressure unit and pre-filter holder (cross-sell). Competition comes from manufacturer solutions by Deconta/enviro; a low-cost aluminium gravity louver from China is the price alternative. Returns risk: wrong opening direction or opening pressure too high.

Request from the supplier

  • Define the opening direction (supply air = opening inwards) and test the opening pressure at low differential pressure on a sample
  • Have leakage in the closed state proven (test per EN 1751 or comparable)
  • Louvres and frame smooth, without gaps and wipeable without tools (decontamination); edges deburred
  • Agree the 305 x 305 mm frame size including mounting frame and seal for foil or panel walls
  • Check the fit with the pre-filter holder set and 305 x 305 pre-filters
  • Document powder coating and aluminium alloy (RAL colour, coating thickness, REACH declaration)
