Description

The Qunfeng QF800 forms hollow blocks, pavers, permeable pavers and kerbs up to 300 mm in height.

More

According to the maker, a percussive vibration system and arch-beam demoulding give precise products (0.2 mm tolerance), and the gearbox-driven feeding cart shortens the cycle to 11–25 seconds. The maker states CE and ISO; buyers should explicitly request certificates, moulds (tooling), pallets and German documentation.

E-commerce potential

Quote-based business for concrete plants and landscaping suppliers: specialists such as MASA offer block production in Germany project by project only.

More

The machine alone is not enough, it needs moulds, pallets, concrete preparation, curing and a foundation. Sell only with a clearly defined scope of supply and service plan.

Request from the supplier

  • Define the scope of supply: moulds (tooling) for each block format, pallets, concrete mixer, conveying and curing equipment; the price covers the machine only
  • Inspect the CE declaration of conformity (EN 12629) and ISO certificates; German operating manual
  • Request missing data: motor/hydraulic power, weight, pallet size, supply voltage (400 V/50 Hz)
  • Get spare parts (vibrators, seals, sensors, hydraulics) and service technicians in DE/EU confirmed
  • Check transport dimensions (main machine 3.0 × 2.09 × 3.0 m) and the foundation plan for installation
