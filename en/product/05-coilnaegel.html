Description

Wire-collated coil nails are the consumable for pneumatic coil nailers in formwork, roofing …

More

pallet and timber-frame work: one magazine holds hundreds of nails and the high firing rate saves a great deal of time on site. What matters is wire grade, pull-out resistance of the ring shank, head shape and a collation that fits the nailer exactly (15°, wire spacing), otherwise jams and misfires result. German professionals expect data to EN 14592 for structural timber connections, a zinc coating thickness suited to the service class and compatibility with branded nailers such as DeWALT DPN75C/DPN90C, Paslode, Senco or Bostitch. Without a magazine fit test and a pull-out test no carton is released.
