Description

The Go2 Air is a compact quadruped with 4D LiDAR that AE Global Traders can test as an entry into site robotics, for example for walk-throughs of shell structures and plant rooms without staff in the danger zone.

More

As an education and consumer product it is only conditionally suited to demolition, strip-out and asbestos remediation with dust and moisture and should be used as a pilot device. Professional users in Germany expect CE marking under machinery and radio equipment law, a German-language manual and clarity about radio and cloud connectivity. For use in contaminated areas, decontaminability under TRGS 519 must be clarified separately.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity (Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC or, from 20.01.2027, Machinery Regulation, RED, EMC) naming the manufacturer.
  • Ingress protection and dust resistance confirmed in writing; for use in asbestos areas, decontaminability under TRGS 519.
  • Description of radio and cloud connections and the option of offline operation (data protection, cybersecurity).
  • German-language manual, warranty terms and spare-parts supply (batteries, joint motors) in the EU.
  • Battery test report (UN 38.3 / IEC 62133-2) and proof of compliance with the Batteries Regulation.
