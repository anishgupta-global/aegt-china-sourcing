Request from the supplier

  • Fix steel grade and hardness (HRC) in writing (e.g. 1.4116 or a 7Cr17MoV equivalent) and request the mill certificate.
  • Request LFGB and Regulation 1935/2004 test reports for blade, handle and block from an accredited lab (SGS, TUeV, Intertek) referencing the model.
  • Wood species and origin of the block and, if FSC is claimed, the certificate with licence number; have lacquer or oil checked for food suitability.
  • Confirm the exact composition of the 7 pieces and test samples for sharpness, corrosion (salt spray) and handle fixation.
  • Confirm the plant: manufacturer or trader, own production, audit (BSCI/ISO) before ordering.
  • Plan the GPSR label, EU responsible person and German packaging data (LUCID).
