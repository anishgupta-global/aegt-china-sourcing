Description

The set seals tilt, turn or roof windows around the exhaust hose of a portable air conditioner and stops warm outside air from flowing back into the room – no drilling, so it also works in rented flats.

More

The 100 % polyester fabric is waterproof, UV-resistant and washable; a zipper takes the hose, hook-and-loop tape and fixing material hold the set to the window frame. Demanding buyers look for a perimeter of about 400 cm, clean seams, hook-and-loop tape that does not fray and a smooth-running zipper. As a light, flat-packable accessory it suits being added to every portable air conditioner as an extra or bundle.

More

the HT800 has 54 ratings averaging 3.1 of 5 stars there – a hint of fit or quality problems (our reading of the rating count, review texts not read) and thus room for a better-made set. Per the scan research Bauhaus, Klimaworld, eBay and ManoMano also list such sets (about EUR 14-47). Strongly seasonal (May-August), very light and ships flat; return risk from differing window sizes – include a size chart and fitting instructions.

Request from the supplier

  • Request a sample with zipper and hook-and-loop at about 400 cm perimeter; the Jjpro listing names 3 m × 39 cm as an option and gives the material as 'Plastic' in one place and 'polyester fabric' in another – get fabric and coating confirmed in writing.
  • Ask for a REACH test report (SVHC, azo dyes) and, if wanted, OEKO-TEX Standard 100.
  • Define seam strength, zipper cycles and hook-and-loop adhesion in the specification and test them on the sample.
  • Clarify MOQ: the Global Sources listing states 1,000 units, the description text 2,000 pcs; the alternative Moonstar supplies from 100 pcs.
  • OEM points: private label, German fitting instructions, label with fibre composition, EU responsible person (GPSR).
