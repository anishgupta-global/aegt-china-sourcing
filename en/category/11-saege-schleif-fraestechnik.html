11 · Construction supplies

Cutting, grinding & milling

Floor grinders, scarifiers, masonry saws, cut-off saws, floor saws and wall sanders are the machines that run daily in demolition, strip-out and renovation, and branded units quickly cost four to five figures.

More

Chinese makers offer large price advantages here, but only CE-compliant machines with a German manual, proven dust extraction and spare-parts supply are suitable for AE Global Traders.

Floor grinders and diamond tools cluster in Quanzhou/Jinjiang and Xiamen (Fujian), stone and wire-saw technology in the Quanzhou stone-machinery cluster (Luojiang/Nan'an/Shuitou), scarifiers mainly in Changzhou (Jiangsu) and Guangzhou (Guangdong). Added by the research of 30 Oct 2026: portable petrol cut-off saws from Wuxi (Jiangsu, next to Changzhou), floor saws from Shanghai and wall sanders from Hangzhou/Ningbo (Zhejiang); these three price sources lie outside the Fujian core. Added in v3 (30 Oct 2026): wood construction-site table saws come from Zhejiang (Jifa, Yiwu/Jinhua) and Shandong (Allwin, Weihai), stone table saws from Anhui (Ludwig, Hefei); both lie outside the Fujian core.
