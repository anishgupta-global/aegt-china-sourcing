Description

The site cable reel brings power flexibly to every workplace, for example for hand tools, site lights and extraction units during strip-out and refurbishment.

More

Professional users in Germany expect an impact-resistant plastic or sheet-steel reel with IP44 splash protection, sockets with hinged lids, thermal overload protection and an H07RN-F cable that stays flexible in frost and wet conditions. The permissible load must be stated for the cable unwound and wound, the reel must be tested to EN 61316 and checked regularly under DGUV Regulation 3. Branded products from Brennenstuhl, HEDI or As-Schwabe are the benchmark; no-name reels with thin cable overheat when wound.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 61316 and EN 60529 (IP44) from an accredited lab; aim for the GS mark.
  • Cable proof: H07RN-F with VDE/HAR print, 1.5 mm² pure copper (measure conductor resistance), no CCA.
  • Trip test of the thermal overload protection and stated permissible load for unwound/wound cable.
  • EU declaration of conformity (LVD, RoHS), German manual and marking with manufacturer and address.
  • 100 % routine test (high voltage, protective-conductor continuity) with record, plus spare parts (sockets, crank) and 24-month warranty.
