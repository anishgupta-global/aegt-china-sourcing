Description

The speed jump rope combines anodised, knurled aluminium handles with a PVC-coated steel cable that does not tangle and can also be used outdoors.

More

The length is adjusted to body height without tools via clamp screws. High-quality ropes for adults have ball-bearing handle ends for smooth rotation, a feature that must be explicitly agreed and tested with the maker. Logo and packaging are customisable; for EU marketing, GPSR details and clear positioning as adult sports equipment are required.

Request from the supplier

  • Agree ball bearings in the handle ends in writing (bearing type, quantity) and test rotation on the sample
  • Check cable break/endurance test (number of jumps) and how the cable is attached to the handles
  • Request a PAH/REACH report for handles and PVC coating (incl. cadmium)
  • Define spare cable and accessories (key, pouch) and retail packaging with German text
  • Confirm plant location: company address Hefei (Anhui), plant 550 m2 per profile; factory audit
