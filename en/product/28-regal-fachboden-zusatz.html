Description

An extra or replacement shelf board turns an existing boltless shelf back into a fully usable storage system without having to buy a whole add-on bay.

More

The sheet is galvanised, folded at the edges and hung into the uprights with four shelf supports in a fixed grid (typically 25 mm); the shelf load is stated for evenly distributed weight, typically 150 kg and 330 kg. Professional users expect an exact fit to the shelving system, clean deburring, even galvanising and a documented shelf-load proof. The board is bulky and is best offered specifically as an accessory to the matching shelf, not as a universal part.

More

incl. 4 shelf supports for EUR 20.90 net (EUR 24.90 gross), the 150 kg board for EUR 14.20 net, next to add-on bays from approx. EUR 116 net as the step up. Per the scan, Hornbach and toom show the shelf without any accessory logic, which is the gap for an independent shop. Weaknesses: bulky, sellable only with a matching grid (high return risk on a wrong purchase) and an ongoing EU anti-dumping proceeding on steel shelves from China (registration since 12 Sep 2026, see customs classification): clarify before adding to the range.

Request from the supplier

  • First clarify grid, size and hook shape of shelf 28-schwerlastregal (maker gdlt) and have the boards made to the same drawing; check a fitting sample on the existing upright.
  • Request a shelf-load proof per variant (150 kg and 330 kg evenly distributed) with test report and deflection limit; have shelf supports supplied in the set.
  • Demand the mill certificate of the sheet (grade, thickness, zinc coating, Cr(VI)-free passivation) and a salt-spray test.
  • Check edges and corners for burrs, cut-injury risk and folding accuracy; measure the hole-pattern tolerance on the sample.
  • Clarify the customs situation: check the scope of the anti-dumping registration (Regulation (EU) 2026/2023) for single boards with supports via a binding tariff information before ordering.
  • Define packaging for bulky goods (edge protection, pallet), EU responsible person (GPSR), LUCID number and a load label in the carton.
