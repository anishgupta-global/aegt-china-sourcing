Description

The heavy demolition-robot class of around 5 tonnes is designed for high-volume concrete demolition, foundation and industrial deconstruction and work in tunnels and on bridges …

More

where smaller robots fall short on power and reach. It carries heavy hydraulic breakers and shears and works by radio remote control from a safe distance, keeping the operator outside the dust, noise and collapse zone. Because of its dead weight, floor load capacity, low-loader transport and stability must be checked before every job; a robust steel build, emergency stop, quick change for attachments and a seamless spare-parts and service chain in the EU are expected. It must be delivered CE-compliant under Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC with a German operating manual, a radio-remote test report and a sound power statement under 2000/14/EC.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • CE declaration of conformity with serial number and technical documentation under 2006/42/EC (or (EU) 2023/1230) for model PLE-500; do not accept marketplace CE logos without documents.
  • Test reports of the radio remote control (RED/EMC) and proof of the emergency stop (performance level per EN ISO 13849-1).
  • Written datasheet for model PLE-500: drive type, mains connection (voltage/current), operating weight, reach, transport dimensions, ground pressure and oil flow/pressure (the listing states neither reach nor dimensions); proof of stability and outrigger/support design.
  • Proof of manufacturer status (business licence, video factory audit): the listing says manufacturer/trading company and gives inconsistent company data (founded 2015, over 20 years of manufacturing experience).
  • German operating manual, spare-parts catalogue and maintenance plan; availability of wear parts and a service technician in the EU confirmed in writing.
  • Binding quotation with Incoterm, scope of supply (breaker, quick coupler, remote control) and freight costs (the listing shows a deposit-only quotation); test run with acceptance protocol before final payment, at least 12 months' warranty.
