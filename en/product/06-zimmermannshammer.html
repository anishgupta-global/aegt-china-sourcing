Description

The carpenter's hammer (roofing hammer) with a 600 g head is the standard tool for roof trusses, formwork and timber construction: the milled face prevents slipping when nailing …

More

the claw pulls nails and battens, and a magnet holds nails in position. The head is made of drop-forged, hardened carbon steel and sits inseparably on a handle of wood, fibreglass or all-steel. Professional users in Germany expect balanced striking behaviour, a non-slip grip zone and a head seat that does not loosen under sustained use. Imported goods need test evidence (GS), REACH/PAH compliance of the grips and GPSR labelling; wooden handles are also subject to the EU Deforestation Regulation.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • GS certificate from an accredited body and test report on head seating (pull-off and impact test)
  • EN 10204 type 3.1 material certificate and hardness record of striking face and claw
  • Proof of head retention (wedge plus adhesive, or one-piece forging) with a sectioned sample
  • REACH/PAH test report for grip and lacquer; for wooden handles origin proof under the EU Deforestation Regulation
  • GPSR details: EU responsible person with address, batch number, German instructions
  • Approval sample with weight tolerance (+/- 3 %) before series production
