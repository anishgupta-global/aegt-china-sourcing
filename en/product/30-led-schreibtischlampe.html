Description

Compact, foldable desk lamp with a clamp base for table tops and shelf edges; brightness is controlled by a touch sensor and the colour temperature is chosen from three steps (2700 / 4000 / 6000 K).

More

With a maximum of 5 W it works as supplementary task lighting for a height-adjustable desk. High-quality models show flicker-free dimming, a play-free joint, a stable clamp and a power supply with a CE record. For EU distribution CE (LVD/EMC), RoHS, an EU responsible person under GPSR and, if a battery is fitted, the Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542 must be observed.

A fitting add-on to the height-adjustable desk; return risk from flicker, wobbly clamps and unclear power supply.

Request from the supplier

  • Clarify the power supply: USB/DC adapter (CE proof, ecodesign for external power supplies (EU) 2019/1782) or battery (then UN 38.3, Batteries Regulation (EU) 2023/1542, BattG).
  • Request flicker values (Pst LM, SVM), CRI and lumens per colour temperature plus a blue-light assessment under EN 62471 as a test report.
  • LVD/EMC test reports from an accredited lab that refer to model WDF132-2401; have the CE/RoHS claims in the listing proven.
  • Check the mechanics: clamp range (max. table thickness), joint endurance test, tipping stability, cable length, touch response also with damp fingers.
  • If 2700–6500 K is wanted: ask for a 6500 K variant instead of 6000 K (the listing states 6000 K).
  • Samples and OEM options (colour, logo, German manual), EU responsible person on the pack (GPSR), VerpackG/LUCID, ElektroG/stiftung ear.
