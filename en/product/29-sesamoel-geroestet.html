Description

Toasted sesame oil is pressed from roasted sesame seeds and is added at the end of cooking or used cold: a few drops give noodle dishes, soups, dips and marinades the nutty, roasted aroma of Chinese cooking.

More

According to the listing, the chosen supplier's product is a pure sesame oil in a transparent glass bottle (270 ml, approx. 250 g fill weight) with a bright golden colour, strong sesame aroma and 24 months of shelf life. What matters for the German market is purity (100 % sesame, no admixture of other oils), batch-by-batch analytics for ethylene oxide, PAH and mycotoxins, and a German label with the allergen sesame highlighted. With BRC, HACCP, ISO 22000, Kosher and Halal certificates (manufacturer claims) and private-label filling, the oil can be positioned as an own brand next to soy sauce and glass noodles.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Sesame oil is the classic add-on purchase to soy sauce and noodles.

More

Evidence: in its oils and fats category Asiafoodland lists ten sesame oil items (incl. Oh Aik Guan, Yeo's, Amoy, Ottogi, LKK) with base prices from 19.98 to 56.19 EUR/l (regular prices; one 150 ml oil is on a time-limited offer at 1.00 EUR); Oh Aik Guan costs 3.99 EUR (150 ml), 7.99 EUR (360 ml) and 12.99 EUR (650 ml), Yeo's 7.49 EUR (150 ml) to 24.99 EUR (640 ml). The LKK oil is, according to its product title, blended with soybean oil, so a pure 100 % sesame oil can be set apart from the mass product. REWE also has its own sesame oil category, so competition extends into the supermarket. Risks: glass breakage and leakage in shipping, price pressure from branded goods and high residue-testing requirements for sesame. Recommended as a cross-sell in a bundle with soy sauce and glass noodles, not as a traffic driver. Sales figures are not evidenced.

Request from the supplier

  • Prove purity in writing and by analysis (fatty acid profile, sesamin/sesamolin): 100 % sesame oil without admixture; clarify beforehand the title addition 'Blended' in two Kwangna listings
  • Per batch: analyses for ethylene oxide/2-chloroethanol and pesticides (Reg. (EC) 396/2005), PAH/benzo[a]pyrene for roasted oil, aflatoxin B1 and heavy metals
  • Request BRC/IFS certificate with scope (sesame oil, filling site); as Kwangna is a trading company, ask for the filling plant's address and audit report
  • Glass bottle, closure and liner: declaration of compliance under Reg. (EC) 1935/2004; breakage and leak test in the shipping carton (24/48 pack)
  • Check the German label: allergen sesame highlighted, best-before date, importer/responsible operator, fill quantity; sample approval before series production
  • Clarify the customs classification (CN 1515 50 19 crude vs. 1515 50 99 refined) by binding tariff information before calculating prices
