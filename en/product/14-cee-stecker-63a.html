More

container units and sub-distribution boards on larger sites and connects the main board with the sub-distribution in demolition and strip-out projects. Professional users expect a robust, impact- and UV-resistant housing, watertight IP67 protection with a coupling ring, silver- or tin-plated contacts that stay cool under continuous load and a strong strain relief for cross-sections up to 25 mm². The product must be CE-compliant and meet DIN VDE 0100-704 and the inspection duties under DGUV Regulation 3; brands such as Mennekes, ABL or PCE set the benchmark. Imported goods are only marketable with a test report to EN 60309-1/-2 including a temperature-rise test and a German declaration of conformity.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 60309-1/-2 from an accredited lab (e.g. TÜV, VDE, SGS), including temperature rise at rated current
  • CE declaration of conformity (2014/35/EU, 2014/30/EU, RoHS) referencing the exact model and manufacturer
  • Proof of IP67 protection to EN 60529 on a test sample (mated and unmated)
  • Material evidence: contact material (copper alloy, tin- or silver-plated) and UV- and flame-resistant housing
  • Sample test of mating compatibility and 6 h coding against Mennekes or ABL counterparts
  • Routine testing and traceability (batch number, production date) plus a take-back clause for serial defects
