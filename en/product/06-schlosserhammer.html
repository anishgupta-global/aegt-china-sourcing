Description

The engineer's hammer is the standard hand hammer for metalwork, assembly and workshop use; the face and pein serve for straightening, riveting and driving.

More

Professional buyers expect a forged, fully heat-treated head with a polished striking face, a firmly wedged ash handle and the DIN 1041 head shape. The supplier states manufacture to German safety standards for its hammer line; conformity must be checked on a sample.

E-commerce potential

Perennial seller in online tool retail: in Hornbach search, 500 g engineer's hammers range from EUR 9.45 (no-name, ash handle) to EUR 16.05 (Picard, hickory).

More

Price pressure from marketplace goods at about USD 2 ex-works (trader in Yiwu); differentiate via the DIN 1041 reference and handle quality; check handle fit and hardness before series launch to limit returns.

Request from the supplier

  • Check hardness (Rockwell) of face and pein and head dimensions on a 500 g sample against DIN 1041
  • Check handle fit and wedging (pull-out/torsion test), ash wood quality and varnish (REACH)
  • Request a binding 500 g price in writing from MOQ 24 pcs (listing shows only a USD 10-20 band)
  • Have the hammer production site named (storefront shows a Shanghai office, plant not stated) and plan an audit/visit
  • Clarify GPSR labelling (manufacturer, EU responsible person) and LUCID registration of the packaging
