Risks: pump noise and durability (main return reason), filter availability and limescale/cleaning effort.

Request from the supplier

  • Request test report for lead/cadmium release of ceramic and glaze plus migration report (LFGB or (EC) 1935/2004) for all water-carrying parts
  • CE documents (LVD/EMC), declaration of conformity and test report of power supply/pump; confirm EU plug
  • Pump: test noise level (dB), endurance test (e.g. 1,000 h), dry-run protection and low-water warning
  • Replacement filters: secure availability, price per set (retail target EUR 5–10) and minimum quantities in the contract
  • Ceramic: breakage resistance in transit (packaging drop test), watertightness and glaze defects (define sampling AQL)
  • Agree GPSR labelling, German manual (filter change, cleaning) and maintenance intervals
