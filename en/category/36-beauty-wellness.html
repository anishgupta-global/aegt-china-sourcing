Beauty & wellness devices

Hair dryers, stylers, sonic toothbrushes, facial and massage devices are small electrical appliances with high margins, repeat accessory sales and a strong Q4.

More

Asian D2C brands (e.g. Laifen, Oclean, Dreame, Renpho, SKG) appear in the listings of Notino and MediaMarkt and in the Stiftung Warentest toothbrush test, and occupy the middle segment between premium (Dyson, Philips, Braun) and anonymous marketplace goods. Target group: DE/EU retailers with a private-label strategy who want to sell devices together with accessories and consumables.

Typical clusters (each supplier to be verified in the maker step): Guangdong with Shenzhen and Dongguan (electronics, sonic toothbrushes, LED masks, massagers, high-speed dryers, nail lamps), Guangzhou (beauty and nail devices, cosmetics ecosystem), Zhongshan (small appliances); Zhejiang with Ningbo, Cixi and Yuyao (small appliances, hair dryers, shavers and trimmers) and Yiwu (mirrors, beauty accessories). Many makers work as ODM/OEM for brands; actual MOQs and private-label terms must be clarified per factory.
