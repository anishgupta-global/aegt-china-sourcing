Description

The chrome-plated brass single-lever mixer is the standard solution for washbasins in staff rooms, sanitary areas and refurbished bathrooms.

More

A ceramic cartridge provides drip-free, precise control of temperature and flow, and the aerator limits the flow rate. A professional user in Germany expects brass that meets the 4MS/UBA requirements for drinking-water contact, testing per EN 817, noise group I and, where required, a DVGW certificate. Before import, test reports, material certificates and evidence of drinking-water suitability must be demanded.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test reports per EN 817 and EN 248 (tightness, pressure, endurance, coating) from an accredited laboratory
  • Material certificate of the brass (CW number, 4MS conformity) and lead/nickel migration test
  • Proof of drinking-water suitability for Germany (UBA metals assessment, KTW/DVGW) for body, cartridge and seals
  • Noise test certificate per DIN EN ISO 3822 (noise group I)
  • Cartridge endurance test (at least 500,000 cycles) and 100 % factory leak test
  • German installation manual, spare cartridges/aerators and warranty
