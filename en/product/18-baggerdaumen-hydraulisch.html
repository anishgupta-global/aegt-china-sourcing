Description

The hydraulic excavator thumb sits on the stick and clamps material against the bucket, so that a mini excavator can grab and sort timber, pipes, boulders and demolition debris.

More

Compared with the mechanical version with a top link, it can be opened and closed from the operator's seat. Quality markers are structural steel of class S355 or Q355, clean welds, coarse teeth for a secure hold, a hydraulic cylinder with hose connection and a hole pattern that allows the thumb to be set to the bucket geometry. The thumb is matched to stick and bucket width; dimensions and mounting method should be checked against the target machine before ordering.

The hydraulic version is the higher-priced variant for buyers who want to grab timber, pipes and stones. High return risk from fit to the stick (pins, bucket width) and missing hydraulic hoses or quick couplers; a dimension sheet per machine model and a clear scope of delivery lower the rate. Season: landscaping from spring to autumn (own assessment, not measured).

Request from the supplier

  • Define the target machine (make, model, stick dimensions, pin size, bucket width) and compare the thumb drawing with the original; clarify mounting (weld-on or pinned).
  • Record the scope of delivery in writing: hydraulic cylinder, hoses, quick couplers, pins, fixing material; paint finish (e.g. unpainted or black).
  • Request a 3.1 material certificate for Q355/S355 steel, weld inspection and cylinder pressure test (observe the nominal pressure of the mini excavator).
  • Require CE marking and EC declaration of conformity for the attachment (Machinery Directive, from 20 Jan 2027 Machinery Regulation) and operating instructions in German.
  • Load-test one sample and check cylinder tightness and absence of cracks before ordering a series; agree warranty (listing: 12 months) and spare parts (seal kit).
  • Check the factory: plant area (profile: 4,180 m²), audit report and business type; fix packaging and Incoterm.
