Description

The Ø 125 mm flap disc with zirconia flaps removes steel, stainless steel and weld seams quickly and blends the surface in the same pass …

More

the flaps renew themselves in use and therefore last considerably longer than a conventional abrasive sheet. With grit K60 and a flat shape (type 27) it suits edge breaking, deburring and weld-seam work on an angle grinder with a 22.23 mm bore. Professional users in Germany expect a disc to EN 13743 with the oSa mark, a clearly legible maximum speed (12,200 rpm at 125 mm) and a glass-fibre backing plate. Without a test record per batch the goods are not put on sale; the 10-pack is meant as a repeat-purchase consumable next to the inox cutting disc.

Request from the supplier

  • Request a test report to EN 13743 (spin/burst speed) and an oSa certificate with registration number for exactly this type and diameter, verify authenticity
  • Print on every disc: manufacturer, Ø 125 x 22.23, grit, maximum speed 12,200 rpm / 80 m/s, type, production date, German safety instructions
  • Fix the construction in writing: number of flaps, zirconia content, glass-fibre backing plate, adhesive; REACH declaration for it
  • Test samples (at least 1 carton) against FORUM ZK 60 and Bosch Best for Metal: stock removal, service life, run-out, flap break-out
  • Pack of 10 in shrink wrap with EAN, EU responsible person (GPSR) and LUCID-ready packaging; customs code 6805 10 00 on the shipping papers
