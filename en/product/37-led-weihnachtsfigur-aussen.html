Request from the supplier

  • Require a low-voltage version (24 V) with a CE/GS-tested power supply (e.g. EN 61558-2-6) and an IP44/IP65 test report for outdoor use.
  • Fix the size at 140-150 cm and the weight; check stability with ground spikes or base plate and wind load.
  • Clarify the power rating (listing states 80 W and 150 W), and approve LED count, warm-white colour and timer on a sample.
  • Define the supply cable (at least 5 m) and outdoor cable with plug; verify certificate numbers (VDE/GS/CE) for authenticity.
  • Check whether an exemption from the Ecodesign Regulation (EU) 2019/2020 applies to the light sources; plan ElektroG registration and a bulky-goods carton.
  • Request a price for the consumer variant (rope light, 140-150 cm) from the alternatives Link House and Toprex and compare it with the project model.
