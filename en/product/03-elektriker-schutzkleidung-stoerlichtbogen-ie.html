More

VAT per piece; at MODYF the multinorm work jacket (arc protection, antistatic) costs from EUR 104.66 and the trousers from EUR 92.30, listing ex works USD 38.50-42.50. Demand comes from electrical trades, grid operators and industrial maintenance (B2B with certificate obligation); without classification to IEC 61482 the coverall cannot be sold to this target group and carries liability risk.

Request from the supplier

  • Request a test report to IEC 61482-1-2 (APC 1/2) or IEC 61482-1-1 (ATPV/EBT) for fabric and finished garment including seams, zip and reflective tape; 'Cat 2' in the US system is no substitute.
  • Require an EU type-examination certificate from a notified body (category III) and an EU declaration of conformity; main markets per profile are North America and China, a European reference is missing.
  • Logo print or embroidery and reflective tape can reduce the protective effect: require a test report for the delivered version.
  • Clarify the number of washes after which the class is confirmed and the care labelling (FR treatment vs inherent).
  • Plant audit in Suzhou before a series order (capacity figures contradictory, production possibly external); test the USD 100 sample in Germany.
