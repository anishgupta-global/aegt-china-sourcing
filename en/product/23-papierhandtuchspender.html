Description

The paper towel dispenser in impact-resistant ABS supplies washrooms on construction sites, in welfare containers and workshops with folded paper towels in a hygienic way.

More

The lockable housing with inspection window protects against vandalism and shows the fill level at a glance. A professional user in Germany expects a sturdy wall mounting, handling of common Z/C-fold towels, material compliance under REACH and clean product labelling under the GPSR. Before import, material declarations, an endurance test of the lock and a sample with the towel paper to be used later must be checked.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • REACH/material declaration for the ABS and accessories (no SVHC above the limit) plus a test report on impact resistance
  • Endurance test of lock and housing (e.g. 10,000 opening cycles) with test report
  • Pre-series sample: test with common Z/C-fold towel paper from Germany (dispensing, inspection window, wall bracket)
  • Name of the EU responsible person and product labelling under Regulation (EU) 2023/988; for sensor versions also CE, RoHS and an EMC test report
  • Scope of supply per carton: mounting material, 2 keys, multilingual manual; commitment on packaging and breakage rate
