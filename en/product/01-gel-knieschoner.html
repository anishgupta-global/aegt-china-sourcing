Request from the supplier

  • EN 14404 test report (type, performance level, puncture) for K103 and CE declaration of conformity with technical documentation
  • Gel material and rubber: REACH/PAH test report (skin contact), gel leakage safety
  • Clarify unit: price per piece or per pair; adjustment range of the straps
  • Sample: durability of straps/hook-and-loop, wearing comfort, stitching at gel chamber
  • OEM packaging with German instructions, EU contact address (GPSR/PPE) and brand
