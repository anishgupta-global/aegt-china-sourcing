Description

The light chipping hammer with SDS-plus chuck is the tool for jobs where a rotary hammer is too heavy and a demolition hammer oversized: removing tiles and plaster, chasing slots for cables, reworking masonry.

More

The unit has no rotation, only impact; this saves weight and makes it easy to guide. The Mingpu model MP0407 weighs 3.5 kg per listing, has a chisel position adjustable in twelve steps, a 360-degree auxiliary handle, an anti-vibration system and a lock-on switch, supplied with two SDS-plus chisels 14 x 280 mm in a plastic case. The reference Makita HK1820 offers 550 W, 3.1 J single-blow energy and 3.3 kg. For the EU market documented conformity, a German manual with vibration and noise values and spare-part supply count.

Request from the supplier

  • Require the declaration of conformity and test reports (EN 62841-1/-2-6, EMC, RoHS) from an accredited laboratory for exactly the model MP0407; clarify the contradiction in power (750 vs. 950 W) and impact energy (5 vs. 7 J).
  • Have noise and vibration values measured (sound power level per 2000/14/EC, total vibration value when chiselling); require a German manual with the values.
  • Endurance test of the impact mechanism (greasing, carbon brushes, heating) and drop test of the case; spare-part list and warranty terms in writing.
  • Check mains cable and plug (Schuko/CEE 7/7, cable type H05RR-F, length) for EU conformity; document protective insulation and insulation measurement.
  • Chisels: check SDS-plus shank, hardness and heat treatment; 2 chisels in the scope of supply, ask for the accessory assortment.
  • Rating plate with manufacturer/importer, EU responsible person, serial number and WEEE symbol; clarify ElektroG registration; sample and pre-shipment inspection (AQL).
