Hazardous material remediation

Hazardous material remediation (asbestos, man-made mineral fibres) requires consumables and equipment that safely contain fibres and dust and are documented in line with TRGS rules.

More

China mainly offers bags, big bags, tarpaulins and air scrubbers at a fraction of German prices, but Class H equipment and decontamination units can only be used in Germany with proven test reports.

Equipment (industrial vacuums, air scrubbers/negative air machines) clusters in the Yangtze Delta (Suzhou, Wuxi, Ningbo, Hangzhou) and the Pearl River Delta (Dongguan/Shenzhen); printed PE bags cluster in the Tongcheng bag cluster and in Guangdong (Jiangmen, Shenzhen). PE tarpaulins/films and FIBC big bags sit in Shandong (Jinan, Yantai, Qingdao, Binzhou): Jinan Sheng Hui, Yantai Yjm, Well Merit and Shandong Shuangsheng are direct neighbours there; a tarpaulin/tent maker such as Jinan Sheng Hui is also the obvious OEM candidate for personnel decontamination units.

bags only from MOQ 5,000 pcs, big bags from 500 pcs, tarpaulins from 3 t. 2) Negative air machines: Chinese 'negative air machines' are mostly US units (CFM, 115 V/60 Hz, e.g. Preair AF650, H13); the A-One listing states no filter grade. In Germany, asbestos work (TRGS 519) uses units with an H13/H14 main filter (DIN EN 1822) tested to DIN EN 60335-2-69 (dust class H); BG BAU only funds air cleaners with a Class H main filter. Demand 230 V/50 Hz, H14, CE and a test report as OEM specification; DE reference Deconta G 100 SE EUR 3,064 net. 3) Industrial vacuums: Dust class H to DIN EN 60335-2-69 Annex AA is mandatory; TRGS 519 Annex 7.1 requires an exhaust connection to the outside for units above 1.2 kW. No CN listing names EN 60335-2-69; per search snippet Bersi shows an SGS certification for TS1000 and AC150H (test report not seen), the Villo HEPA series has no test evidence - request a test report from a recognised body, otherwise unusable in Germany. On list prices there is no China advantage (CN EUR 1,462 per unit vs. DE EUR 856 net; EUR 1,044 applies only from 10 pcs). 4) Bags: TRGS 519 requires labelled, tight packaging (double packaging); the German standard is a woven PP sack with PE liner and 4-language print (Cimco EUR 4.19 net), while the CN listing is a single-layer LDPE bag in 60 x 90 / 80 x 120 cm and 70 x 110 cm is a custom size (printing-plate costs); with double packaging the CN price per unit doubles. A factor of about 11 (vs. EUR 2.96 from 5,000 pcs) to 15 (vs. EUR 4.19) is not a pure price advantage. 5) Excluded: Guangdong Sunho Plastics (contradictory thickness data), Ningbo A-One (Alibaba profile only, no website - price reference only), Shandong Aishe Packaging, Wuhan Healthwell/Medi-Crown. 6) Villo: Tier A rests on its own German company Villo Tech GmbH (Oberasbach, since 2019); a stock-exchange listing is not evidenced. 7) Gaps: Huakin and Jinan Sheng Hui have no own website; the Preair standard unit only delivers 250-500 CFM. 8) Method: Contorion, Kaufland, Bauhaus, Baustoffshop and Made-in-China partly blocked retrieval (403); DE prices therefore come from Mercateo, Hornbach and eBay search snippets. 9) New 2026-10-30: KMF big bag: the CN listing (1 t FIBC without skirt and certificate, Yantai Yjm USD 1.35-2.95, MOQ 500) is not equivalent to the German KMF sack (150 kg, skirt, print; eBay macbag24 EUR 6.47 gross per search snippet); no CN listing with UN approval was found for asbestos big bags. Personnel decontamination unit: neither CN nor DE price found, prices deliberately left empty; Jinan Sheng Hui as OEM is only a hypothesis. Cover tarpaulin: the CN price is listed per tonne (Well Merit USD 1,700-2,400/t, MOQ 3 t) and converted arithmetically to 3.6 kg per tarpaulin; the Hornbach DE price of EUR 46.30 gross is a consumer price. HS codes of the new products (63053290, 63062200, 63061200) and duty rates are not verified against the tariff; duty rates are deliberately left empty - check in EZT / binding tariff information before ordering. New makers are evidenced only by Made-in-China showrooms, without contact data. 10) Images: image URLs were only taken from Alibaba/Made-in-China listings; product images of German shops/manufacturers (Starmix, Deconta, Cimco, Hornbach etc.) were not captured (retrieval blocked or research budget exhausted; clear image rights before use). 11) Unit reconciliation: Asbestos bag: both prices are per 1 bag but not for the same construction; the earlier factor of about 38 (EUR 0.109 vs. 4.19) was overstated and is about 11-23 after correction. Industrial vacuum: the CN list price for 1 unit (EUR 1,462.03) is above the DE single price (EUR 856.30 net, Hornbach Starmix H-1635 Safe Plus); DE asbestos versions are more expensive (Starmix H-1235 Asbest EUR 1,091.77 net at Mercateo; Kaiserkraft ECODust 1200 H-Asbest about EUR 3,650 net). Lead: bersivac.com names an SGS Class H certification to EN 60335-2-69:2016 for TS1000 and AC150H (1,200 W, 220-240 V) (https://www.bersivac.com/ts1000-one-motor-single-phase-hepa-13-dust-extractor-product/); request it from Bersi.
