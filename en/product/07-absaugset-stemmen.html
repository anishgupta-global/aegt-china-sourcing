Description

Chiselling and chipping in concrete, masonry and plaster generates respirable quartz dust, which operators must capture at source under hazardous-substances rules (TRGS 559).

More

The dust extraction kit collects dust directly at the chisel through an elastic collar and leads it through a hose into a connected vacuum. The reference Makita 197148-8 (collar diameter 37/44 mm) fits SDS-max chipping hammers such as HM1317CB, HM1812 and HM004G and needs a separate dia 22 mm connection adapter. Professional users expect a tight, wear-resistant elastomer collar, low-loss suction routing, matching adapters for dust class M/H vacuums and tool-free fitting. Important: the kit controls dust at the tool; a tested class M or H vacuum remains a prerequisite.

Request from the supplier

  • Fit: test collar dia (reference 37/44 mm), SDS-max chisel shank and connection (35 mm port on the China sample vs. dia 22 mm adapter on the Makita) on a sample with at least three common chipping hammers
  • Clarify the version: listing HTDDS-35 is a drilling dust hood (max dia 70 mm); a chisel-capable variant (elastomer collar around the chisel) has to be requested as OEM development - ask for tooling costs and MOQ
  • Separation: dust measurement in a practical test with a class M/H vacuum; check collar sealing and hose routing
  • Material: wear-resistant elastomer collar (abrasion, temperature), REACH/SVHC declaration, no third-party brand logos
  • Packaging and law: carton with German label, EAN, compatibility list, GPSR information and LUCID data; request the CE/ISO claim shown in the listing (HTDDS-35) as a document
