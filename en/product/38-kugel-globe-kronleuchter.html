Request from the supplier

  • Confirm in writing the number and diameter of balls, overall size and light source (E14/E27 or LED) for this model.
  • Glass quality: check bubbles, scratches, wall thickness and fastening; document breakage in a drop test.
  • Request test reports (LVD, EMC) and the CE declaration of conformity for the exact model; the listings only state CE claims, no reports were seen.
  • Before selling: ElektroG registration (stiftung ear), LUCID packaging registration and an EU responsible person under GPSR.
  • Confirm the factory location via business licence and an audit/visit appointment (travel route: Zhongshan).
