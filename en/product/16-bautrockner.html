Description

The construction dehumidifier removes moisture from screed, plaster, masonry and water-damaged rooms, shortening drying times and downtime on site.

More

A sturdy housing with castors, humidistat, automatic defrost and continuous drain or condensate pump belong to the equipment of a professional unit. Users in Germany expect CE, conformity with EN 60335-2-40, a refrigerant compliant with the F-gas regulation (preferably R290) and a German manual. The unit must also be designed for 24-hour continuous operation and be testable under DGUV Regulation 3.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity with test reports (EN 60335-2-40, EMC) from an accredited body.
  • Test report for dehumidification capacity per EN 810 (30 °C / 80 % RH) including power consumption.
  • Refrigerant declaration (R290 or R134a) with charge, GWP and safety documents for flammable refrigerants.
  • Proof of continuous-operation suitability (24 h endurance test) and automatic defrost at low temperatures.
  • German manual; compressor brand (e.g. rotary compressor) and spare-parts list.
  • At least 12 months warranty, sample for DGUV V3 inspection before series delivery.
