Description

The levelling system of clips, wedges and assembly pliers pulls neighbouring tiles to a common level while the adhesive bed cures and keeps the joint width constant …

More

decisive for large-format tiles, where any lippage is immediately visible. Professional tilers expect dimensionally accurate clips with a clean break point, wedges with precise serration, pliers with noticeable leverage, and variants for joint widths of 1 to 3 mm and tile thicknesses of roughly 3 to 12 mm. The set must be delivered with German instructions, GPSR labelling and full REACH documentation for the plastic and metal parts; clips and wedges should be available individually as refill packs. As a companion to tile profiles and laying materials it is a consumable with clear repeat purchase.

Lidl sets the price anchor with the Parkside set (1 pliers, 100 wedges, 600 clips, 50 levelling parts) at EUR 7.99, currently sold out online. The driver is the trend to large-format tiles, season spring to autumn. Competition is dense; differentiation runs through set scope (several joint widths) and refill packs. Return risk is low (flat, light parcel), complaint risk with wrong joint width or tile thickness. No Chinese purchase price exists for the set, only a bag price without pliers (see cn_note).

Request from the supplier

  • Samples per joint width (1 / 1.5 / 2 / 3 mm) and tile thickness with a dimensional report; the break point of the clips breaks cleanly without chipping
  • Material data sheets for PE/PP and a REACH declaration (SVHC below 0.1 %) for clips, wedges and pliers; rule out cadmium and nickel on the pliers
  • Field trial with a large-format tile (e.g. 60 x 120 cm): tensile strength of the clips, spring-back of the wedges, scratch resistance of the base
  • German instructions, GPSR labelling (EU responsible person, batch number) and packaging data for LUCID
  • Price tiers incl. refill packs (clips, wedges, pliers individually), OEM packaging with EAN, carton drop test
