Description

The piston compressor supplies impact wrenches, tyre inflators, breakers and chisel hammers as well as spray guns in the workshop and yard with compressed air.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a robust, belt-driven two-stage compressor with a three-phase motor, a pressure vessel protected inside and outside with a drain valve and safety valve, and a traceable rating plate. Mandatory are CE conformity under the Machinery Directive and the Simple Pressure Vessels Directive, a stated sound power level under the Outdoor Noise Directive 2000/14/EC and the inspectability of the vessel under BetrSichV. Air quality, residual oil content and a German-language spare parts service for valve plates, belts and pressure switches are decisive for continuous operation.

Request from the supplier

  • EC declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC and 2014/29/EU stating model and serial number
  • Manufacturer certificate of the pressure vessel with pressure test, vessel number and rating plate (year of manufacture, number, max. pressure) for the inspection under BetrSichV
  • Measurement report of the sound power level under 2000/14/EC and proof of the stated free air delivery
  • Statement of air quality and residual oil content (class per ISO 8573-1) and a note on required downstream filters
  • German operating manual, spare parts list (valve plates, belts, pressure switch) and a warranty of at least 24 months
