Description

The liner is made of food-grade silicone, sits in the air fryer basket, catches fat, marinade and crumbs and replaces disposable parchment.

More

The round, collapsible design with divider allows two dishes at once and saves storage space. Discerning buyers look for platinum-cured silicone without fillers, low-odour material, dimensionally stable wall thickness and complete test reports under LFGB or BfR Recommendation XV. As a small, light accessory with repeat-purchase potential it suits bundles with air fryers.

The market is price-driven with many marketplace sellers; the main risks are returns due to wrong fit (round/square, basket size) and odour complaints - state dimensions and compatibility clearly.

Request from the supplier

  • Request a current test report under LFGB §30/31 or BfR Recommendation XV (migration, volatile components) from an accredited lab for exactly this material.
  • Get written confirmation: platinum-cured silicone, no fillers (no white stress marks when stretched).
  • Fit: match dimensions against common basket sizes of target devices; check samples in 2-3 sizes before series release.
  • Heat test: 30 minutes at 220 °C, assess odour, discolouration and dimensional stability.
  • Confirm in-house production (supplier is Manufacturer/Factory & Trading); request audit report and business licence.
  • Packaging with EU responsible person (GPSR), batch number, care/temperature notice; clarify LUCID registration.
