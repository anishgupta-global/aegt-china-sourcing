Description

The padded chest harness distributes pull across chest and back instead of the neck; soft mesh padding and nylon webbing provide comfort, reflective elements provide visibility at dusk.

More

The matching leash turns the set into a complete everyday offer. Commercial buyers look at breaking load of buckles, D-ring and seams, clean workmanship, stepless adjustability and a reliable size chart. Colours and materials must be REACH-compliant (azo dyes, heavy metals in metal parts).

E-commerce potential

Fressnapf lists 196 dog harnesses (EUR 9.96–230.90); volume sits at EUR 10–60 (Max & Molly from EUR 9.96, Lionto EUR 18.95).

More

Reflective padded models cost EUR 15–55: Dogs Creek Norwegian harness Control with integrated reflectors and neoprene padding EUR 33.99 (size S), Pathfinder with 3M Scotchlite EUR 47.99. Cross-sell: leashes, collars, safety in the dark, GPS tracker. Main risk: high returns because of fit – size chart and measuring guide are decisive; very many competitors.

Request from the supplier

  • Request REACH/azo-dye and PAH test report plus nickel/cadmium evidence for buckles and D-rings (metal parts)
  • Have breaking load of webbing, buckle, D-ring and seams tested per size (minimum values per buyer specification, e.g. ISO 13934-1)
  • Retroreflective material: have manufacturer, reflection value and wash resistance documented
  • Validate size chart (chest and neck circumference, weight) with samples on dogs of different breeds
  • Leash: have length, material, snap-hook breaking load and matching colour offered binding as a set package with price tier
  • Define GPSR labelling, single-unit packaging with size print and batch number, and sampling AQL
