Description

The try square with a 300 mm blade is used by carpenters, joiners and drywall fitters to mark right angles, check workpiece edges and align components …

More

the fixed stock sits against the edge and keeps the angle repeatable. The blade is made of stainless steel with an easy-to-read graduation, the stock of zinc alloy. Professional users expect a documented squareness (accuracy grade per DIN 875), plane-parallel blade faces, a play-free joint between blade and stock and an abrasion-resistant scale. As an imported product the square needs GPSR labelling with an EU responsible person, a REACH-compliant surface and registered sales packaging.

In German trade 'Zimmermannswinkel' in our assessment usually means the large steel square; the version listed here is a 300 mm try square. Return risk medium (assessment: professionals re-check squareness), parcel small and light.

Request from the supplier

  • Approval sample GWS-15A in 300 mm: squareness (mm over blade length) by measurement record, define the accuracy grade per DIN 875, blade thickness and stock length by drawing (the listing states neither)
  • Stainless steel grade, plane-parallelism of the blade faces, play-free riveting or screw joint, abrasion test of the scale
  • Zinc die-casting and coating: REACH report (heavy metals, SVHC), salt spray test EN ISO 9227
  • Request the certificates named in the listing (ISO 9001:2015 per company profile, BSCI audit report); no CE directive is apparent for a mechanical square (assessment), do not treat 'CE' as proof of conformity
  • GPSR details (EU responsible person, batch, German instructions), packaging (blister/carton) with LUCID registration
  • MOQ 1,200 pcs, lead time, factory audit; secure the classification (9017 80 10 or other) with a binding tariff information
