Description

The set bundles the connection of a washbasin below the tap: a chrome-plated brass bottle trap 1 1/4 inch as odour seal and two brass angle valves to shut off the supply lines.

More

Tradespeople and DIY users save the single-part search, and for the retailer it is the typical add-on to basin and tap sales. Buyers expect pore-free chrome plating, clean threads (G 1/2 inch outside, 3/8 inch outlet), tight seals and low-lead brass suitable for drinking water. Connection hoses and rosettes can be added as an option. For parts in contact with drinking water, Germany applies the Drinking Water Ordinance and the UBA assessment basis for metals.

The set is sought as an add-on to every basin and tap purchase and is small and light. Main risks: fit (trap size, wall distance, 3/8 versus 1/2 inch outlet), tightness and the lead content of the brass; an unsuitable set causes water damage, hence product liability cover and incoming inspection before launch.

Request from the supplier

  • Request material proof for the brass: alloy (e.g. CW617N or low-lead), lead content and conformity to the UBA positive list or a DVGW mark for the angle valves.
  • Dimension drawing and thread gauge test: trap 1 1/4 inch x 32 mm, angle valve G 1/2 inch with 3/8 inch outlet (not 1/2 inch), seals and cone washer included.
  • Pressure and leak-tightness test for angle valves (test report) and check of the chrome plating (salt spray test, adhesion) on the sample.
  • The WaterMark certificate in the listing does not replace EU testing; the company profile shows no factory address (only an office address in Taizhou), so clarify factory address and audit.
  • Define the set composition: rosettes, fixing kit, optional connection hoses, single packaging with German instructions and GPSR labelling.
  • Order a first sample batch and run incoming inspection (tightness, threads, chrome) before the series order.
