Description

The KMF big bag takes up large quantities of old mineral wool, insulation and, in the correspondingly labelled version, asbestos-containing rubble, and is moved by crane or forklift.

More

Quality product is made of UV-stabilised woven PP with skirt, closing straps, closed bottom and cleanly sewn lifting loops. Professional users in Germany expect printing in line with TRGS 521 (or TRGS 519 for asbestos), a tested load capacity to ISO 21898 and, for dangerous goods, a UN approval. This allows safe transport and disposal under waste code 17 06 03*.
