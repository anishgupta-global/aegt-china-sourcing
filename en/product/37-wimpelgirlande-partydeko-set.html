Request from the supplier

  • Request a test report on REACH conformity of printing inks and paper (heavy metals in colourants)
  • Clarify labelling: name and address of manufacturer and EU responsible person, type or batch number, 'not for children under 3' notice for small parts (GPSR)
  • Ask the supplier about fire behaviour (B1) if customers use the garlands in catering or daycare
  • Fix motifs, lengths and colour mix per set in a sample catalogue; check batch colour deviation of tissue paper
  • Evidence factory status: have Chenfei Paper Industry confirmed as own production and review the audit report (SGS ID in the listing)
