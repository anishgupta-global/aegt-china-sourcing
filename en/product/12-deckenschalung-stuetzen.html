Description

Slab formwork with props carries the fresh concrete and reinforcement of in-situ slabs via formwork beams and height-adjustable steel props and is the usual system for floor slabs …

More

beams and slab renewals in new build and refurbishment. Professional users in Germany expect props with a verified load class to EN 1065, dimensionally stable H20 timber beams to EN 13377, a verifiable structural design of the whole system to EN 12812, clean corrosion protection and assembly and use instructions in German. The system must be delivered with proof of conformity (test report to EN 1065), material certificates and proof of timber origin. As the importer, AE Global Traders bears responsibility for conformity and for safe use on site.

Request from the supplier

  • Random load test and weld inspection per batch and a written warranty
