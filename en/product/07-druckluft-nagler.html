Description

The pneumatic nailer speeds up formwork, carpentry and fit-out work considerably compared with hand nailing and drives nails to an even depth with low strain on the operator.

More

Professional users expect a light aluminium/magnesium housing, adjustable drive depth, a safety trigger (single-shot and contact actuation only with safety), a quick coupler to the European standard and exhaust air directed away from the operator. From China, a CE declaration under the Machinery Directive, testing to EN ISO 11148-13 and noise and vibration data must be demanded. Cordless nailers (battery or gas drive) are an alternative without a compressor; for AE Global Traders the pneumatic version remains the robust, low-maintenance base with no battery care or gas resupply.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN ISO 11148-13 from an accredited body and EU declaration of conformity (2006/42/EC) with EU authorised representative
  • Measurement record for noise (EN ISO 15744) and vibration (EN ISO 28927-13)
  • Function proof of the safety trigger (video test) and permissible operating pressure on the rating plate
  • Nail compatibility: approved nail types, lengths and diameters (magazine and nail drawing)
  • Endurance test (shots until maintenance) and spare-parts/seal-kit list
  • Operating manual in German, quick coupler to the European profile
