Description

The Holstein shovel is the classic concrete shovel of the German building trades: a wide, slightly curved steel blade that takes up concrete, sand, gravel and mortar in large quantities …

More

and a straight 130 cm wooden handle for low-effort work standing up. Professional users expect a blade of sheet steel or manganese steel (retail reference: 2.1 mm sheet steel at 26 cm working width), a firmly pressed socket connection, a low-knot ash or beech handle with a smooth finish and a corrosion-resistant powder coating or lacquer. Imported goods need GPSR-compliant labelling with an EU responsible person, a REACH-compliant coating and a handle connection that does not loosen even under concrete load.

Request from the supplier

  • Shape and dimensions: approve a sample of the Holstein shovel (handle 130 cm, working width about 25–26 cm, blade 2.1 mm) – the Renhe listing 5511Y states neither blade shape nor handle length
  • Steel and hardness: Mn steel (50Mn), hardness record per batch to ISO 6508-1, bending and impact test on the blade
  • Coating: powder coat or lacquer, salt spray test to ISO 9227 and adhesion test; REACH report (Pb, Cd, PAHs) for lacquer and handle
  • Handle: fix the wood species (ash/beech) in writing, straight grain, low knots, residual moisture 8–12% (ISO 13061-1); pull-out test of the socket connection
  • Packaging and labelling: bundled with edge protection (bulky goods 130 cm), GPSR details (EU responsible person, batch), German notices, VerpackG/LUCID
  • Factory proof: audit of Renhe (check ISO 9001/14001/45001 per listing), first sample before series, negotiate MOQ 600 and tiers 2,000/4,000
