Description

PoC network radios transmit speech by push-to-talk over the LTE network and are therefore not limited by radio range, so site management, fleet and several Berlin sites can be connected in one group.

More

They need no frequency assignment of their own, but a SIM card with a data plan and a PoC service platform, usually run as a subscription by the provider. Professional devices are IP67-protected, have GNSS positioning for lone-worker safety and withstand shock and dust. PoC does not work in a basement or shaft without network, where PMR446 or DMR446 remain necessary; the importer must prove RED conformity with cybersecurity proof and a GDPR-compliant server location.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under RED 2014/53/EU with test reports (LTE bands, EMC, safety) and cybersecurity proof under EN 18031
  • Written confirmation of the LTE bands for Germany (e.g. B3/B7/B20) and a test with a sample SIM on the Telekom, Vodafone and O2 networks
  • PoC platform: server location in the EU, GDPR data processing agreement, licence price per device and month, availability commitment; the device must not be tied to a cloud server outside the EU
  • IP67 test report and UN 38.3 test report for the battery
  • German manual, dispatcher software, warranty of at least 24 months, EU service partner and WEEE/battery registration of the importer
