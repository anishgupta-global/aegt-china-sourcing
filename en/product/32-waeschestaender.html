Description

The foldable steel drying rack takes a full laundry load and stores flat after use.

More

Quality is decided by tube strength, stability under load, the joints and a dense powder coating that does not flake even in humid rooms. Demanding buyers check drying length, load capacity and edge protection at the tube ends and expect a salt-spray corrosion test and a product without harmful substances in grips and paint. As a bulky article the pack size has to enter the calculation early.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix drying length (approx. 18 to 20 m), tube diameter and wall thickness, wing form and weight in writing for the OEM sample and measure them.
  • Request a salt-spray test (e.g. 48 or 96 hours) of the powder coating and adhesion test (cross-cut); clarify the material (steel or stainless steel).
  • Load test: define load capacity (e.g. 15 kg), stability and tip-over safety as well as a joint endurance test (opening and closing) yourself.
  • Confirm pack size, carton weight and container loading (flat pack) for the freight calculation; transport damage test (drop test).
  • REACH declaration for grips and paint, GPSR label, EU responsible person and LUCID registration.
  • Confirm the plant (profile: Factory & Trading), ISO 9001 certificate and audit.
