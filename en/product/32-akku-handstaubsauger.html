Strong brand competition (Kaercher, Einhell, Bosch) and price pressure below EUR 40; differentiate via suction, spare battery, wall mount and accessories. Return risk from runtime and suction claims; battery compliance is demanding.

Request from the supplier

  • Request UN 38.3 and IEC 62133-2 battery test reports plus cell maker and capacity (Ah, Wh) in writing; clarify dangerous-goods shipping (air/sea).
  • Measure suction (kPa) and runtime under load on the sample; clarify the contradiction between 15 and 23 kPa and between 12 to 40 and 30 to 60 min.
  • CE test reports under EN 60335-1 and EN 60335-2-2, EMC and RoHS for exactly this model; confirm HEPA filter and accessory scope (nozzles, wall mount).
  • Align EU Batteries Regulation requirements (labelling, replaceable battery) with the maker and prepare registrations (ElektroG, battery).
  • Confirm the plant: audit (BSCI present per profile), own production, warranty and spare-battery agreement.
  • GPSR label, EU responsible person, German manual and disposal notices.
