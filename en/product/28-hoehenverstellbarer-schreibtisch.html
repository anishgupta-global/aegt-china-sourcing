Description

The 160x80 cm sit-stand desk brings ergonomic working to site management offices, planning rooms and cabin offices: at the touch of a button the height changes smoothly between sitting and standing.

More

A steel frame with dual motor delivers quiet, even lifting, and memory presets store the preferred heights. Professional users in Germany expect an E1-grade board with a scratch-resistant melamine surface, a frame with collision protection and documented conformity with EN 527 as well as CE (low voltage, EMC, RoHS). A German manual, an EU plug and available spare parts for motor and controller are standard.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity with CE marking (Low Voltage Directive 2014/35/EU, EMC 2014/30/EU, RoHS 2011/65/EU) plus a test report from an accredited lab (e.g. TÜV, SGS, Intertek) for motor, controller and power supply
  • Test report to EN 527-2 (crushing and shearing points, stability, endurance lifting) and, if advertised, a GS certificate with a verifiable certificate number
  • Board material: formaldehyde class E1 (EN 717-1) and, for a wooden top, a supply-chain declaration for the EU Deforestation Regulation (EU) 2023/1115
  • German assembly and operating manual including safety instructions; EU plug (type F) and mains cable with approval mark
  • Endurance test record of the lifting system (cycle count, load) and a written warranty of at least 3 years on motor and frame
  • Spare-parts commitment (motor, controller, hand switch) with lead time to the EU, pre-shipment sample and pre-shipment inspection (AQL)
