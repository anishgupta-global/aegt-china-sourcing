Description

The mini crane lifts masonry units, precast concrete parts, windows, glass and panels in tight courtyards, halls and through building openings and replaces manual handling and forklifts there.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a verified load chart with load moment limiter, hydraulic outriggers with tilt indicator, radio remote control, a tested steel structure with documented welds and spare parts and service in Europe. Market references are masonry cranes such as Böcker MK 300 / MK 400 and Lissmac LMK 400 with approx. 300-400 kg at 5 m radius. The machine must be delivered with CE marking, declaration of conformity, load chart and German operating manual; cranes in Germany are subject to inspection under DGUV Regulation 52.

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity, CE marking, risk assessment and load chart with model and serial number
  • Calibration and test record of the load moment limiter and cut-off functions
  • Proof load test (static and dynamic) with acceptance record per machine
  • Material certificates 3.1 to EN 10204 and weld documentation of the steel structure
  • Radio conformity of the remote control (Directive 2014/53/EU), German operating manual, spare parts list and written warranty
