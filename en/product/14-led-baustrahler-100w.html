Description

The LED site floodlight on a tripod provides glare-free, even work light for strip-out, demolition and interior fit-out wherever no permanent lighting exists.

More

Professional users in Germany expect an impact-resistant aluminium housing rated IP65, a stable height-adjustable tripod and an H07RN-F connection cable with earthing-contact plug. What matters is a documented luminous flux (lumens, not just watts), low-flicker driver electronics, CE conformity (Low Voltage, EMC and RoHS directives) and ElektroG registration; brands such as Brennenstuhl, Makita or Hilti show the quality benchmark. Cheap floodlights without a test report and with inflated wattage claims have no place in procurement.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test reports to EN 60598-1/-2-5, EN 62471 and EN 55015 from an accredited lab, not just a dealer CE sticker.
  • EU declaration of conformity (LVD, EMC, RoHS) whose model name matches the ordered type exactly.
  • Measured luminous flux and power consumption (LM-79 / integrating-sphere test) instead of manufacturer claims; flicker measurement.
  • IP65/IK08 proof and inspection of the mains cable (H07RN-F, VDE/HAR marking) and tripod stability.
  • Clarify ElektroG/WEEE registration number and packaging registration (LUCID); commit to a German manual and spare parts (driver, tripod).
