Description

The robot levels and trowels fresh screed and concrete under laser height control and replaces manual screeding and troweling with hand machines on large floor areas.

More

It keeps flatness to DIN 18202 reproducibly and relieves the crew in the tight time window while the material stiffens. Chinese references are Bright Dream's BDR-1 troweling robot (Red Dot project), Fangshi's laser screed and troweling robots and Partner Robotics' mortar levelling robot. Required are a battery-electric drive (Stage V emissions if combustion), a laser-classified height control to EN 60825-1, emergency stop and CE under the Machinery Directive.

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity and technical file under Machinery Directive 2006/42/EC or (EU) 2023/1230 incl. risk assessment to EN ISO 12100
  • Laser classification of the height control to EN 60825-1 and operator protection (emergency stop, tilt protection, obstacle detection)
  • Flatness proof from a reference project or factory acceptance test (FAT) to DIN 18202 on fresh screed / concrete
  • For combustion engines: Stage V type approval under (EU) 2016/1628; for battery drive UN 38.3 / IEC 62619 proofs
  • German manual, cleaning and maintenance plan, EU spare-parts commitment and 24-month warranty
