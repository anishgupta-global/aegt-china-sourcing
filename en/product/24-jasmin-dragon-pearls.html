Request from the supplier

  • Check certificates in the original (ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, BRCGS or IFS; the listing names ISO 9001, ISO 22000, Kosher, CGMP and organic as self-declaration): certification body, validity and scope covering tea.
  • Lab report per batch from an accredited laboratory (ISO/IEC 17025): pesticides under Reg. (EC) 396/2005, heavy metals and pyrrolizidine alkaloids under Reg. (EU) 2023/915.
  • Proof of origin for the tea garden and the processing site of the jasmine scenting (classically Fujian/Guangxi; the supplier is based in Hunan) and confirmation that only natural blossoms without added flavourings are used.
  • Declaration of compliance for tin, bag and inner bag under Reg. (EC) 1935/2004; check aroma and light protection of the packaging.
  • Organic claim only with an EU-recognised control body and a certificate of inspection under Reg. (EU) 2018/848; an “Organic” note in the listing does not replace this.
  • Sample with infusion test and a German label draft under the FIC Regulation before series release; register the importer as food business operator and register the packaging under VerpackG (LUCID).
