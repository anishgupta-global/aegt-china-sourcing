Description

The three-piece folding set, a 220 cm table with two benches, furnishes break and meeting areas in site cabins and accommodation units without any assembly: unfold, lock the frames, ready for use.

More

The job calls for solid fir or spruce tops with a weather-resistant clear lacquer and a steel frame with a secure locking mechanism that withstands damp cabin conditions and frequent set-up and take-down. German site managers and safety officers expect stability evidence under EN 581-3 or EN 15372, proven timber origin under the EU Deforestation Regulation and a check that the benches meet the break-room requirements of ASR A4.2 (backrest). Folded flat, the set stores compactly in the cabin and moves on to the next site.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report from an accredited laboratory (SGS, TUV, Intertek) to EN 581-3 for the table and EN 16139 or EN 15372 for the benches, covering exactly model F1019 and the ordered dimensions.
  • Timber origin: species (Cunninghamia/Chinese fir or spruce), country of origin and geolocation of the harvest areas for the EUDR due diligence statement; FSC or PEFC certificate if available.
  • Frame: steel grade, wall thickness (28 × 28 × 2.5 mm or Ø 25 × 1.5 mm), coating process and salt spray test to ISO 9227 or a coating test report; sample with folding and locking test.
  • Written confirmation that the price covers the complete 3-piece set (table + 2 benches), including Incoterm (e.g. FOB Qingdao), palletising, units per 40-ft container and sample fee (listing: US$ 60).
  • Lacquer and coating: REACH/SVHC declaration and moisture resistance (cabin climate) by test report; for benches with backrest, additional proof of backrest strength.
  • Production site and audit: Welhome is a manufacturing and trading company, so have the production site named, and agree an audit report (BSCI, ISO 9001 or third-party inspection) and a pre-shipment AQL inspection.
