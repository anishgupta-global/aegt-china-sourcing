Request from the supplier

  • Request EN 71-1/-3 and EN 62115 test reports for BL046861; clarify whether solar cell and motor make it electrical equipment under ElektroG/WEEE and plan registration with stiftung ear if needed.
  • Specify solar cell (output, voltage) and motor and measure on the sample in daylight and window light; require at least one working variant under room light.
  • Check parts list, count per set and moulding quality (burrs, connector tolerances) on the sample; tool warnings if a screwdriver is included.
  • Define German manual, warnings (small parts, age grade), CE, manufacturer and EU responsible person (GPSR) and packaging under VerpackG/LUCID.
  • Commission a pre-shipment inspection per AQL with a function test under standard light; clarify business type and factory site by audit.
