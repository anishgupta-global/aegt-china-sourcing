Description

A stable telescopic tripod lifts floodlights up to 3 m and gives more even, lower-glare lighting of work areas, as DIN EN 12464-2 aims for outdoor workplaces.

More

Professional users expect telescopic stages that lock steplessly and do not slip under load, a wide stance with non-slip feet, a stated load rating (reference Brennenstuhl ST 301: 10 kg) and a crossbar for two lights. The imported version from Guangyun (model GY-SJJ-S) is offered in 1.6, 2 and 3 m, weighs 3.65 kg in the 3 m version, folds in 3 sections and can be supplied with a single or double head; the mount must mechanically fit the floodlights in the range. Stability and load rating must be proven on a sample before the item is sold as an accessory to floodlights.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Brennenstuhl sells the ST 301 (115 to 300 cm, 10 kg load, plastic-coated steel tube, with carrier part for two floodlights) at BAUR for EUR 77.21 gross; Kaufland and eBay carry loose telescopic tripods.

More

The import price of USD 6.50 from 300 pcs refers to an unspecified variant of the listing and is far below; the 3 m version with double head must be quoted separately. The tripod is a sensible upsell to the floodlights in the category, but bulky and heavy (3.65 kg); freight cost and returns due to stability or wrong mount are the main risks. Season: site lighting picks up with the short days from October (assumption, no source).

Request from the supplier

  • Order a sample 3 m tripod with double head and test: load with two floodlights, tipping stability also in wind, slip resistance of the clamps after repeated extension and retraction
  • Material evidence: tube alloy or steel grade, wall thickness, surface coating (REACH report), non-slip feet
  • Check mount and crossbar mechanically against the floodlights of articles 14-led-baustrahler-100w and 14-led-akkustrahler (spigot, screw, tilt)
  • Price per height and head variant in writing: the listing shows only a single price (USD 6.50) for 1.6/2/3 m and single/double
  • Have the customs classification fixed in writing (aluminium or steel) and state the HS code in the quotation
  • Marking: max. load, warning (overhead lines, wind), German instructions, EU responsible person (GPSR); ask for carton dimensions and freight per pallet
