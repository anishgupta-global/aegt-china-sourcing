Description

Schuko rubber plugs and couplings (16 A, 230 V, IP44) are the backbone of single-phase supply on site: hand tools, vacuum cleaners, lighting and extension leads are connected with splash protection.

More

Expected are solid-rubber or TPE housings that survive drops, frost and oil splashes, kink protection with strain relief, earthing contacts with reliable contact pressure and, for couplings and sockets, a hinged lid for IP44 protection. In Germany, connectors to DIN VDE 0620-1 and DIN 49440 are the benchmark; they must be CE-compliant, meet DIN VDE 0100-704 for construction sites and pass the regular DGUV V3 inspection cycle. Brands such as ABL, Brennenstuhl or as-Schwabe set the standard; imported goods can only be used safely with a test report and preferably a GS or VDE mark.

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to DIN VDE 0620-1 (EN 60884-1) from an accredited lab, including temperature rise at 16 A
  • CE declaration of conformity (2014/35/EU, 2014/30/EU, RoHS) referencing the exact model
  • Proof of IP44 protection to EN 60529 for plug and coupling (hinged lid)
  • Dimensional conformity to DIN 49440 (contact spacing, earthing clips) by sample measurement
  • Material evidence for rubber/TPE (cold, oil and UV resistance) and copper content of the contacts
  • GS mark or equivalent certification (ProdSG) from an independent test house
