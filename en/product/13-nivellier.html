Description

The automatic level transfers heights on site quickly and with little maintenance: excavation depths, formation levels and foundation tops are checked with a levelling staff, without batteries or radio modules.

More

An air-damped compensator settles the crosshair within seconds, the 32x telescope with a 42 mm objective gives a bright, high-contrast image, and the IP66 housing withstands dust and water jets in demolition work. Professional users in Germany expect a factory test report to ISO 17123-2, adjustment after transport and traceable service, because without these proofs readings are not reliable for acceptance. The importer remains responsible for product safety (ProdSG), marking and the German manual.
