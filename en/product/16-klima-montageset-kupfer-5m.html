Description

The installation kit consists of a copper pipe pair factory-insulated with PE foam (liquid line 1/4 inch …

More

suction line 3/8 inch) in 5 m length and connects the indoor and outdoor unit of a split air conditioner. Soft-annealed copper can be bent and flared on site; the insulation protects against condensation and heat loss. Specialist installers look for pipes to EN 12735-1, the fire class of the insulation, the right pipe size for the unit data sheet and clean, capped pipe ends. Important: the refrigerant connection may only be made by certified personnel; condensate hose, control cable and wall bracket are separate items depending on the kit.

High margin versus purchase cost (about USD 19-21 per 5 m per listing), but bulky, and pipe diameters must match the unit (return risk, show data sheet/compatibility list).

Request from the supplier

  • Have pipe dimensions (1/4 + 3/8 inch), wall thickness and copper quality (EN 12735-1) confirmed with a material certificate; the Tatsukin listing does not state wall thicknesses.
  • Have the fire class of the insulation (Bl-s1,d0 / M1) proven with a test report to EN 13501-1.
  • Clarify what the 5 m set contains: flare nuts, pre-flared ends, capped pipe ends, condensate hose, control cable – the Jiahao listing mentions flare nuts only.
  • Have factory leak/pressure testing and pipe cleaning (moisture-free, capped) documented.
  • Ask for a binding price per set: the listings give only metre prices (Jiahao USD 3.80-4.20/m) or USD 10-100 per roll (Tatsukin); clarify tiers and MOQ per size.
  • Check customs status: for CN 7411 10 90 TARIC shows a registration of imports from China (see customs note) – involve a customs agent.
