Description

Paper carrier bags with a logo are the group's visible presence on meal packs, client gifts and topping-out events.

More

A professional bag carries its load safely: the base is glued and reinforced, the handles are fixed inside with a reinforcement patch, and the print does not rub off, even when damp. German professional buyers expect paper from demonstrably legal forestry (FSC or PEFC) and packaging that meets the PPWR; whoever imports the bags counts as the first distributor and has to settle registration in the LUCID packaging register and system participation under the VerpackG. If the bag is used for baked goods or take-away, proof for food contact is added.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Manufacturer's FSC or PEFC licence number with chain-of-custody certificate, verifiable in the FSC database, plus origin data for EUDR due diligence.
  • Load and handle pull test on the sample (e.g. 8 kg for 24 hours), documented with a test report or video.
  • Pre-production sample with print proof, colour rub and wipe test, and a written colour tolerance for the logo.
  • Heavy-metal test report (sum of Pb, Cd, Hg, Cr(VI) at most 100 mg/kg) for paper, ink and handle.
  • For food contact: declaration of conformity under Regulation (EC) 1935/2004, BfR XXXVI evidence and ink conformity; otherwise a note that the bag is not intended for loose food contact.
  • Weight per bag in grams and material data for the LUCID report; have manufacturer status (factory or trader) evidenced by business licence and factory photos.
