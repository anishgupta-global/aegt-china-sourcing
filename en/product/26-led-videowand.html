More

from whole cabinets any wall size can be assembled in a 64 x 48 cm grid. Request from the supplier

  • Sample cabinet with test reports to EN 62368-1 and EN 55032/EN 55035 from an accredited lab and EU declaration of conformity for exactly this model (listing names 'CE, CCC, ETL')
  • LED chip manufacturer, binning and calibration (brightness and colour per module) in writing; spare modules from the same batch, at least 2-3% of the quantity
  • Clarify control: receiving card, power supply brand and lifetime, sending card/controller; EU mains cable and 230 V operation
  • Prove refresh rate and grey scale with a camera test (flicker); 800 cd/m2 is high, check dimmability indoors
  • Packaging and freight: modules are fragile, flight cases or cartons with edge protection; 2-year warranty, regulate claims and spare parts process by contract
  • Importer duties: ElektroG registration, WEEE symbol, type plate with importer address, LUCID for packaging; clarify structural analysis and fixing for fixed installation with the installer
