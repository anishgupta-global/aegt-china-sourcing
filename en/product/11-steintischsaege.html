Description

The masonry table saw cuts bricks, calcium-silicate blocks, aerated concrete, paving stones and slabs precisely on site in a wet cut; the water tank binds the dust and cools the blade.

More

The 400 mm diamond blade and 2.2 kW motor reach cutting depths well above 100 mm (reference Gölz MS400: 137 mm). Professional users expect a stable, corrosion-protected tray, a smooth-running carriage with a parallel fence, a safe blade guard and IP-protected electrics for wet job sites. The machine is permitted only with CE marking under the Machinery Directive, testing to EN 12418, a 230 V connection and a German manual.
