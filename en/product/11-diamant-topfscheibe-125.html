Description

The cup wheel with a single row of diamond segments grinds concrete, screed and natural stone on an angle grinder.

More

Suited to edges, burrs and levelling; a dust shroud and extraction are required due to silica dust. Fix segment height and mount (22.23 or M14) with the supplier.
