Description

The random orbital sander delivers the even, swirl-free finish needed on filled surfaces, timber components and paintwork in fit-out.

More

Professional users expect a small orbit of 2.5–3 mm, a pre-selectable oscillation speed, an effective extraction port, a hook-and-loop pad with extraction hole pattern and a low-vibration, ergonomic design. Because sanding creates hazardous fine and wood dust, operation with a dust class M vacuum (TRGS 553 wood dust, TRGS 559 mineral dust) is standard. From China, CE conformity under EN 62841-2-4 and proof of the vibration values must be demanded.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 62841-1 and EN 62841-2-4 from an accredited body and EU declaration of conformity (2006/42/EC, 2014/30/EU, 2011/65/EU)
  • Measurement record for vibration (ah) and oscillation speeds plus statement of the orbit diameter
  • Dust measurement to EN 50632-2-4 or proof of extraction performance with a class M vacuum
  • Extraction port compatible with common hose diameters (27–36 mm) and pad hole pattern matching standard sanding paper
  • GS certificate (ProdSG Sec. 20) with certificate number
  • Spare-parts list (sanding pad, carbon brushes, dust box) and sample with EU plug
