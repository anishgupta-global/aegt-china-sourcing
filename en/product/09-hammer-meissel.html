Description

The chisel is the wear tool of the hydraulic breaker and transfers an impact energy of around 5,200 J into concrete, masonry and rock.

More

It is made of quenched and tempered 42CrMo steel; the supplier states a hardness of 58-62 HRC. Professional users expect a uniformly hardened tool with clean collar and retaining-pin groove geometry that fits the tool bushing of the breaker exactly, plus an EN 10204 3.1 material certificate. Diameter, length and tip shape are model-specific and must only be ordered with the model designation or a drawing. It is delivered as palletised freight and always used with chisel paste.

Competition: OEM originals and specialist dealers, warranty question with third-party chisels.

Request from the supplier

  • Material certificate EN 10204 3.1 (42CrMo4, heat number, chemical analysis) and a hardness test report per lot; have the listing hardness of 58-62 HRC confirmed in writing, excessive hardness raises the risk of breakage.
  • Drawing or model confirmation for diameter, length, collar dimension and position of the retaining-pin groove for the HMB SB81 breaker; fit check with a sample chisel before the pallet order.
  • Define tip shape (moil or flat), quantity, weight per piece and pallet dimensions; calculated over 100 kg per chisel, budget freight accordingly.
  • Crack test (magnetic particle or ultrasonic) and dimension report on a sample; proof of heat treatment.
  • Price for 140 mm dia. in writing (listing: USD 30-100 for all diameters), Incoterm (FOB Qingdao or Yantai), lead time (listing: 5-25 working days), 12 months warranty.
  • Rust protection (oil), palletising or wooden packing per ISPM 15.
