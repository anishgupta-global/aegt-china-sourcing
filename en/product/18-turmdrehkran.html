Description

The flat-top tower crane TCT6013-8 (also listed as QTZ80) is a top-slewing crane for residential, commercial and industrial construction.

More

The model code 6013-8 stands for a 60 m jib and 8 t maximum capacity; the lifting height of 50 to 100 m depends on the tower configuration. For use in Germany the load chart, foundation and anchor loads, EN 14439 conformity and the inspection before commissioning are decisive.

Request from the supplier

  • Request load chart/capacity table, CE declaration of conformity, German operating and erection manual under 2006/42/EC / (EU) 2023/1230.
  • Foundation, ballast and anchor loads with foundation plan; have the structural design accepted by a German checking engineer.
  • Fix the configuration in writing (tower height, 60 m jib, counterweight, electrics 400 V/50 Hz with EU control cabinet).
  • Clarify erection service, competent persons, spare parts and safety devices (load moment limiter, anemometer) in Germany.
  • Loading (container/flat rack), weight per component, Incoterm; inspection before commissioning under DGUV Regulation 52.
