Description

The long-reach sander (drywall giraffe) sands filler, plaster and drywall surfaces on walls and ceilings from the floor during renovation and fit-out …

More

saving scaffolding and ladder set-up. Professional users in Germany expect a lightweight design with a foldable long neck, stepless speed control, a tilting hook-and-loop sanding head, work light and a dust port for connection to a site vacuum. The machine must be CE-compliant and tested to EN 62841; because sanding mineral filler creates fine dust, combining it with a dust class M vacuum per TRGS 559 is standard. Operating manual and declaration of conformity are supplied in German, and a 230 V version is mandatory.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Test report to EN 62841-1 and the relevant part -2-x from an accredited body with model and serial number of the sample
  • GS certificate from an EU test house (listing states GS, CE, EMC, RoHS – request certificate number and test house, as the listing also mentions UL)
  • EU declaration of conformity (2006/42/EC, 2014/30/EU, 2011/65/EU) with EU authorised representative; explicitly order the 230 V/50 Hz version with Schuko plug (CEE 7/7)
  • Check extraction port diameter against site vacuums (e.g. 35 mm) and the 225 mm hook-and-loop pad with standard sanding sheets
  • German operating manual, spare-parts list (sanding pad, carbon brushes, hose) and at least 24 months warranty; document the supplier's maker status
