Description

More

spanners and bits are held sorted in inlays and can be distributed over the drawers. Discerning buyers in Germany expect chrome-vanadium steel with cleanly chrome-plated, heat-treated surfaces, dimensionally accurate sockets, reversible ratchets with a fine swing angle (72 teeth), engraved wrench sizes and foam or plastic inlays in which every part has a marked place. The chosen assortment has 216 pieces in a blow-moulded case, can be ordered from 1,000 sets with own branding and, per the listing, carries CE, RoHS, CCC and GS (self-declared); the parts list and composition must be fixed before ordering, and inlay dimensions for the drawers of the target trolley must be specified as an OEM variant. For EU online sales, GPSR labelling, REACH compliance (chromium, nickel, plasticisers in grips) and packaging registration are required.

Request from the supplier

  • Detailed parts list (sizes and quantity per drive size) matching the 216 pieces; count check before shipment
  • Material and hardness test (Cr-V, HRC) for sockets, ratchets (torque breakage test) and spanners; dimensional check to ISO 2725-1 and ISO 691
  • Surface: chrome plating and corrosion protection (salt-spray test), REACH proof for chromium(VI) and nickel, plasticisers in grips
  • Inlays and trays: fix the dimensions of the target trolley's drawers (e.g. 1/3 inlay) and the material (EVA, ABS), position labelling
  • Request CE, GS and RoHS statements from the listing as test reports (so far self-declarations); for non-powered hand tools CE is usually not mandatory, the GPSR duties remain
  • Retail packaging with own brand, EAN and German labelling including a 24-month warranty; VerpackG registration; sample before series release
