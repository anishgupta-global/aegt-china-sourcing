Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Brass-coloured metal-arm chandeliers are crowded in the mid-range: Westwing lists Monarch (Ø 70 cm, height-adjustable) at EUR 219 (as of 3 Nov 2026).

More

Price pressure from many competitors; differentiate via glass quality and finish.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EN 60598-1 (LVD) and EMC test report of an accredited lab for exactly this model
  • Approve a pre-series sample: dimensions, weight, finishes, wiring, strain relief, suspension
  • Agree label and German manual incl. EU responsible person (GPSR) and WEEE symbol
  • Validate the packaging with a drop test (transport damage is the most common return reason)
  • Approve globe material (glass/plastic), gold tone and socket type by sample; ask for the real unit price since the listing range of USD 1-500 is only a placeholder
