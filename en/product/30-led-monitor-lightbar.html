Description

The monitor light bar is fixed to the screen edge with a counterweight clamp and lights the work surface through asymmetric optics without reflecting in or dazzling the screen.

More

According to the listing, model YA-001S from Hangzhou Yaohonghuo Lighting (aluminium/ABS, in 42 and 50 cm) uses a full-spectrum LED with Ra > 95 and up to 800 lx, five brightness and five colour-temperature levels (3,000-7,150 K) by touch, and an hourly break reminder. Power comes via USB (5 V, at most 5 W); the delivery includes a USB cable and manual, no power adapter. Professional buyers check flicker values (Pst LM, SVM), measured illuminance and colour rendering, glare-free behaviour on glossy panels and clamp stability on thin and curved monitors; the ambient-light sensor with auto-dimming that marks the premium class is not stated for the listed model and has to be requested as an OEM variant.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

BenQ sorts monitor lamps into three price classes: budget usually EUR 30-40, mid-range EUR 60-100, premium from EUR 100 with an auto-dimming sensor (benq.eu, retrieved 2 Nov 2026).

More

At HORNBACH a USB monitor lamp with colour temperature 2,900-6,200 K costs EUR 24.95 (marketplace seller Enovatek, 2 Nov 2026); the BenQ ScreenBar Halo 2 with automatic brightness has an RRP of EUR 179 and is listed from about EUR 152-177 according to search hits (Geizhals, Alza, Amazon.de). Between EUR 25 and 150 there is room for a mid-range own brand with higher colour rendering; as an upgrade over the simple desk lamp, a sensor variant can be offered as an upsell. Competition is dense (brands and marketplace no-name); returns arise mainly from clamps that do not fit thin or curved monitors, glare and USB ports that are too weak. Demand is to be expected at year end (gifts) and at the start of the year (home-office setups).

Request from the supplier

  • Ask for an auto-dimming variant: request in writing the ambient-light sensor as an OEM option with model, price and minimum quantity; the listed model YA-001S has manual touch levels only.
  • Request test reports from an accredited lab: LVD (EN 60598), EMC (EN IEC 55015/61547), photobiology (EN 62471), RoHS; the reports must name the model number (the listing shows CE/FCC/RoHS without report numbers; the 'Model NO.' field reads YA-001S, the description text A-001S).
  • Measure light and flicker on three production samples: Pst LM and SVM, illuminance at a defined distance, Ra/R9, colour temperature per level.
  • Test mechanics: clamp on thin and curved monitors, hinge and surface durability (553 g / 652 g), current draw (1 A) on notebook USB ports.
  • Fix MOQ and price tiers in writing: the listing shows MOQ 10, the description text 20 pcs; tiers from 500/1,000 pcs, OEM logo, colour box and German manual.
  • Check capacity: the profile shows an annual output value below USD 1 million; secure delivery capability, ISO 9001 certificate and retention samples before a large order.
