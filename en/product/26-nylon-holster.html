The supplier is a maker of tactical bags: use neutral wording in the shop (belt sheath, tool, outdoor).

Request from the supplier

  • Specify a dimension drawing per target device (multitool sizes S/M/L, lamp diameter) and test samples for fit with a real multitool and lamp; the listed model 20LT042 is a 33 cm lamp holster.
  • Define the fabric: denier (1000D or 1680D), coating (PU/PVC), weight per area, colour fastness; request declarations on azo dyes, PAHs and SVHC.
  • Have seam and belt loop tested: pull test of the loop (hang test), seam reinforcement (bar tack), cycle test of the hook-and-loop or snap closure.
  • Metal parts (snap, clip): have nickel release to EN 1811 and corrosion resistance confirmed; design edges and lining so the tool is not scratched.
  • Logo and label: embroidery, woven label or rubber patch, Pantone matching, minimum quantity; approve a sample before production (samples 7-14 days per listing).
  • GPSR details on label and packaging (manufacturer, EU responsible person), batch traceability, and check odour/off-gassing on the sample.
