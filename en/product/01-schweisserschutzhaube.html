Description

Head protection of fireproof cotton (10 oz per title) with a tight cuff, worn under the welding helmet to protect hair and scalp from sparks and weld spatter.

More

The listing names heat insulation, antistatic and scratch-resistant properties as well as RoHS and CE; a test to EN ISO 11611 is not named. As PPE against thermal hazards it may be placed on the EU market only with an EU type-examination (category II).

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Welder's caps are a low-cost add-on to helmet and apron (Hornbach: STAHLWERK welder's cap, 21.99 EUR).

More

The standards proof (EN ISO 11611) decides whether the cap can be sold to businesses; margin comes from bundles with helmet, apron and gloves.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EU type-examination certificate (category II) and EN ISO 11611 test report for the fabric (flame spread, heat transfer).
  • Have it confirmed in writing whether the flame retardancy is permanent (after washing); ask for the fabric weight in g/m2.
  • Define hood (head and neck) or cap: the listing shows a cap with a cuff, 'Schweißerhaube' in the German market often means the head hood.
  • Define sizing, marking (CE, manufacturer) and German user instructions; test samples under the helmet.
