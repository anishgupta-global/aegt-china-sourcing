Description

The Class H industrial vacuum extracts asbestos-containing fine dust and mineral-fibre dust directly at the source and is the standard unit for fine cleaning in the black area.

More

Quality units have a HEPA main filter with a separation efficiency of at least 99.995 %, a sealed housing, a safe bag change and a clear H marking. Professional users in Germany require testing to EN 60335-2-69 Annex AA by a recognised body, CE conformity and, for units above 1.2 kW, an exhaust connection to the outside. Service, spare filters and a German manual are indispensable for day-to-day site operation.

Request from the supplier

  • Test report from a recognised body to EN 60335-2-69 Annex AA (dust class H) for the delivered model
  • EU declaration of conformity (CE) under Low Voltage and EMC Directives
  • Filter test certificate HEPA H13/H14 to EN 1822 with separation efficiency of at least 99.995 %
  • Dust-tight disposal system (bag/Longopac) and exhaust connection for units above 1.2 kW
  • German manual, spare parts (filters, motor) and service in Germany (e.g. Villo Tech GmbH)
  • 'H' marking on the rating plate and manufacturer instructions for regular leak testing
