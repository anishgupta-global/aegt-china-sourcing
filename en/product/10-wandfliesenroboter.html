Description

The wall tile paving robot sets wall tiles in thin-bed adhesive on prepared interior walls and spares the tiler the physically demanding, repetitive work in bathrooms and kitchens.

More

The reference unit is Bright Dream Robotics' wall tile paving robot (墙砖铺贴机器人), which according to the manufacturer catalogue bonds formats of 300 × (450–600) mm in thin-bed adhesive; laying rate and working height are not published and must be requested in writing before ordering. Professional users in Germany expect execution to DIN 18352 with tile adhesive to DIN EN 12004-1, a substrate to DIN 18202, CE conformity under the Machinery Directive and a German manual. Because the catalogue shows no CE statement, the declaration of conformity and risk assessment must be demanded before purchase.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Signed EU declaration of conformity with technical file and risk assessment to EN ISO 12100 (2006/42/EC, from 20 Jan 2027 (EU) 2023/1230)
  • Written data sheet with tile formats, laying rate in m²/h, maximum wall height, adhesive type and joint accuracy (measured values, not marketing claims)
  • Live or video proof of wall laying with your tile format and adhesive, including a flatness check to DIN 18202
  • Battery test summary UN 38.3 / IEC 62619 and EMC/radio test reports (2014/30/EU, 2014/53/EU) if wirelessly controlled
  • German or English manual, spare-parts list, warranty and remote-service terms with a named EU service partner
  • Reference list of completed wall installations with project names and operating hours
