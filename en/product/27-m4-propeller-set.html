Description

Propellers are the most common wear and replacement part of a Matrice 4E or 4T: after ground contact, debris strikes or material fatigue they should be replaced immediately.

More

According to the DJI specification the propeller measures 10.8 inch; the low-noise variant reduces noise and costs a little performance (maximum flight time 46 instead of 49 minutes, hover time 39 instead of 42 minutes). Professional operators expect balanced, crack-free blades, a clean fit on the motor shaft and clear marking of clockwise and counter-clockwise rotation. Selling makes sense only as a spare part with a clear notice on compatibility and origin, since third-party parts can affect the drone's class conformity and warranty services.

Request from the supplier

  • Clarify original or replica: original DJI goods from China are a trademark parallel import (exhaustion only in the EEA); replicas may be sold only as ‘compatible’ without DJI logo.
  • Define the unit per listing in writing (piece, pair or set of 4) and have clockwise/counter-clockwise rotation marked.
  • Test samples on a real Matrice 4E: fit on the motor shaft, balance, vibration, thrust and noise level against the original.
  • Measure material, crack-freeness and weight per blade against the reference (10.8 g per Foto Erhardt).
  • Check the effect on C2 conformity, DJI Care and warranty and state it transparently in the offer.
  • Clarify supplier status: the only China supplier found is a trading company (Shenzhen Pioneer Times Trading); appoint an EU responsible person under GPSR and clarify packaging for LUCID.
