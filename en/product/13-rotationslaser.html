Description

The self-levelling rotary laser projects a horizontal or inclined reference plane across the whole building area and replaces the classic levelling run when setting screed, drywall and excavation levels.

More

Dual-axis inclined planes (±10 %) and variable scan angles allow falls for drainage and ramps, and the remote control puts operation at the workplace. Professional users in Germany expect laser class 2, a receiver for outdoor use, a shock-protected housing and a factory adjustment record. CE marking (EMC, RED for the remote control, RoHS) and a battery with UN 38.3 proof are prerequisites for placing it on the EU market.
