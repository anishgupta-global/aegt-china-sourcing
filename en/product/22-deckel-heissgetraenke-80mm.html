Description

The flat lid closes hot-drink cups with an 80 mm opening, typically 8 oz or 200 ml, and turns the cup into a complete coffee-to-go product.

More

The paper version is made of coated board with a water-based barrier and replaces the plastic lid; the same size is available in CPLA as a more rigid alternative. Demanding buyers look for a precise fit on the cup rim, a drip-tight closure, taste- and odour-neutral material, a defined drinking opening and a PFAS-free test report. As part of the packaging the lid falls under the German Packaging Act and PPWR (EU) 2025/40; if it contains plastic (CPLA, PE layer), the rules for single-use plastic products apply in addition.

Every cup sold needs a lid, and Franz Mensch lists lids separately from the cup as a buy-together accessory.

More

Price anchor (retrieved 2 Nov 2026): paper flat lid with drinking opening 8 cm, art. no. 40386, EUR 52.45 net per 1,000 pieces (EUR 62.42 gross); further paper lids on the same page from EUR 22.05 (PaperOn) and from EUR 36.40 (Only Paper) per 1,000 pieces were marked not available at retrieval. The goods are light, stackable and a repeat-purchase item with a peak in autumn and winter (assessment, not measured). Return risk lies in the wrong diameter (80 instead of 90 mm) and in drip complaints, so show the size and the matching cup prominently in the shop.

Request from the supplier

  • Check the fit: test samples on the range's 8 oz cups (rim profile, tightness with water at 90 °C) and define the size of the drinking opening.
  • Have the coating confirmed in writing: water-based, no PE/PLA, no PFAS; request a PFAS test report (total fluorine) from an accredited laboratory.
  • Declaration of conformity under Reg. (EC) 1935/2004 and a migration test report (hot water / ethanol simulant) plus GMP evidence under Reg. (EC) 2023/2006.
  • Clarify quantities and timing: MOQ 100,000 pcs, lead time per manufacturer 20 to 30 days after deposit, sample run about 7 days; ask for a mixed pallet with cups.
  • For the CPLA variant clarify the plastic content and single-use plastic duties (marking, fund levy); if compostability is advertised, an EN 13432 certificate for exactly this model.
  • Photo-documented spot check on odour, heat deformation and stackability before releasing the series.
