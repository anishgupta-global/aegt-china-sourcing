Description

The telescopic ladder extends rung by rung to up to 3.8 m (13 rungs, 29 cm rung spacing) and measures only about 89 cm when collapsed, so it fits in a car boot, a van or a storeroom.

More

The stiles are made of aluminium with a wall thickness of 1.3 to 1.5 mm and, according to the manufacturer, carry up to 150 kg. Trade and discerning private buyers expect a telescopic ladder to be tested to EN 131-6, to have a stabiliser bar for a secure stand, finger protection between the rungs and a controlled, slow retraction. For model TLS38040A the manufacturer states exactly these features; the EN 131-6 test report of a recognised test institute must be requested before listing and checked against model and length.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EN 131-6 test report (model TLS38040A, 3.8 m) from an accredited test institute (e.g. TÜV, SGS, Intertek, DEKRA); test date, model and length must match the lot. The model designation in the listing is inconsistent (TLS38040A / TLS38040X6): clarify in writing beforehand.
  • Check samples: locking of every rung, soft-close, finger protection and tip stability with the stabiliser bar fitted.
  • Approve marking to EN 131-3 (pictograms, 150 kg, manufacturer, model, month of manufacture) and the German instruction manual before series production.
  • Prove wall thickness (1.3 to 1.5 mm) and aluminium alloy by material certificate or spot check; weigh the sample (listing: net 10.5 kg, gross 11.5 kg).
  • Packaging: single carton 88 × 49 × 9 cm with protective bag, drop test of the shipping carton; label with GPSR information (EU responsible person).
  • Clarify accessories (hooks, carry bag, rubber feet) and price tiers (listing: USD 50 to 70 from 100 pcs, sample USD 70).
