Description

The promotional ballpoint pen accompanies site managers, foremen and sales staff to site meetings, handovers and client appointments, making it the most visible promotional item of the group.

More

Professional users in Germany expect a smoothly running click or twist mechanism, a replaceable refill per ISO 12757-1 with an even line and a logo that does not flake off after months in a trouser or tool pocket. As importer, AE Global Traders is responsible for product safety under Regulation (EU) 2023/988, for REACH limits, for example for heavy metals in paints and inks, and for labelling with importer address and batch number. Chinese list prices only apply from lot sizes of 3.000 units; smaller runs are usually more economical via a German promotional-products dealer.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Pre-production sample with your logo including an abrasion test (dry and wet) before series release
  • Test report from an accredited body (e.g. SGS, TÜV, Intertek) on REACH restrictions (lead, cadmium) for barrel, coating and refill
  • Refill data sheet with type, ink colour and proof of writing performance per ISO 12757-1
  • GPSR details: manufacturer name and address, type or batch number and confirmation of conformity with Regulation (EU) 2023/988
  • Packaging details with material and weight per packaging component for LUCID and the Packaging Act
  • Written confirmation of logo technique, Pantone colour, set-up costs and minimum order quantity
