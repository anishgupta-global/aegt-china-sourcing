Description

Baked rice crackers in a bag as a long-life snack for site breaks and as a range extension for a private label.

More

Crisp, evenly baked crackers in gas-flushed film bags with clean seasoning and clear allergen labelling are expected. Rice products need particular attention: the EU limits inorganic arsenic in rice crackers, and the maker must demonstrate acrylamide mitigation under Reg. (EU) 2017/2158. German labelling under the FIC Regulation is mandatory before placing on the market.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Valid HACCP / ISO 22000 / BRCGS / IFS certificate of the production site.
  • Lab report per batch on inorganic arsenic, mycotoxins and acrylamide (benchmark levels under Reg. (EU) 2017/2158).
  • Recipe with allergen matrix and proof that additives are authorised under Reg. (EC) 1333/2008.
  • Declaration of compliance of the film for food contact under Reg. (EC) 1935/2004.
  • German label draft under the FIC Regulation with ingredient list, allergen highlighting, nutrition and importer.
  • Sample, tasting and batch traceability before series release.
