Description

The series consists of soft mini plush figures of about 10-15 cm with a metal carabiner or loop, worn as bag charms and sold in sealed blind boxes.

More

Six to twelve own characters, a rare secret design and quality box printing make collecting and swapping attractive. Professional buyers expect cleanly sewn plush with securely fixed eyes, tested carabiners (nickel release), EN 71 reports, clear random-content labelling and German warnings. Own characters instead of look-alikes of known figures keep the range legally safe.

Request from the supplier

  • EN 71-1, -2, -3 test reports from an accredited lab for the finished sample, incl. pull tests on seams, eyes and carabiner and cord length.
  • Own characters: written design-rights assignment, tooling/sample fees and mould ownership clarified; no Labubu, Pop Mart or Sanrio look-alikes.
  • Blind-box mechanics: set series size (6-12), share of the secret design and distribution key by contract; label random content clearly on the box; packaging for VerpackG/LUCID.
  • Metal parts: nickel release (EN 1811), lead, cadmium; PAHs and phthalates in vinyl/PVC parts.
  • Factory audit (e.g. ISO 9001, BSCI or Sedex), EU reference customers, lead time and price tiers with blind-box packaging as a separate item.
  • Age grade (3+ or 14+), German warnings and GPSR information (EU responsible person, batch) on product or pack.
