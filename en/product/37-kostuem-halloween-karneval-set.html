Description

The skeleton jumpsuit is a one-piece adult costume in 100% polyester in sizes XS to 3XL and works for Halloween, carnival and theme parties without further accessories.

More

A one-piece garment replaces the classic multi-part set, which lowers the risk of missing parts and the stock effort per size. According to the listing the maker offers custom digital prints and logos, so an own line (skeleton first, later witch or vampire) with an identical cut is possible. EU sales are subject to GPSR, textile labelling and REACH; children's sizes would fall under the Toy Safety Directive and are not part of this adult item.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Halloween remains a stable market: the HDE expects about €537m for 2026 (+3.3%), although costumes are not reported separately.

More

Deiters runs costumes as a trend section (vampire dress €49.99, coffin costume €49.99, witch dress €99.99); PartyDeko.de sells the adult 'X-ray skeleton' jumpsuit (M/L) for €24.29 and the 'Elegant vampire' set for €35.69. The skeleton jumpsuit therefore sits in the low to mid price band. Risks: strongly seasonal goods (Halloween in autumn, carnival from 11 Nov), size returns and leftover stock after the season, so keep first orders small and agree the size split with the supplier.

Request from the supplier

  • Order adult sizes only: the listing also names children; children's costumes are toys and need EN 71 testing
  • Request a test report from an accredited lab on REACH (azo dyes, CMR) and flammability per fabric and print ink
  • Clarify labels: fibre composition in German, care symbols, size, and name and address of manufacturer and EU responsible person (GPSR)
  • Check the size chart in cm and pre-production samples in two sizes (fit, zipper, seams, print fastness)
  • Clarify company status: the storefront shows no business type; ask for business licence and factory audit, MOQ 500 per style
