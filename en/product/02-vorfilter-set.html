Description

The pre-filter is the cheapest wear part on a respirator and noticeably extends the service life of the P3 filter because it catches coarse dust, paint mist and sanding particles beforehand.

More

The set consists of a fleece pad and a filter cover that fixes the pad on the bayonet filter and is easily replaced at each change. Professional users in Germany look for a precise fit to the filter system in use without leakage and for a traceable assignment in the filter manufacturer's EN 143 certificate. The pre-filter is part of the filter system and not an independently certified PPE product; it is used only in the tested combination of mask and filter.

More

Price anchor per NonPaints (retrieved 2026-10-31, confirmed again on 2026-11-02): 3M 501 filter covers EUR 5.76 per 2 pcs gross (EUR 4.84 net); further anchors (3M D701 filter holder, pre-filters of other brands) could not be evidenced and were removed. Hoffmann (set HF-3021) and Sundstrom (BASIC PACK) include pre-filters in their kits per earlier research; not re-checked. Main risk is system fit: holders and pads are brand-specific, wrong orders lead to returns; compatibility must be stated exactly on the product page.

Request from the supplier

  • Clarify in writing what a “pair” contains (number of fleece pads and covers per pair) and request drawing and dimensions.
  • Check fit to the bayonet of the filters in use (e.g. Safetree PFFM750P) with a sample; the listing names no compatible systems.
  • Require evidence that the pre-filter is covered by the EN 143 certificate of the filter system (or identical filter manufacturer).
  • Request a test report on fleece material, filter efficiency and breathing resistance (listing: only “Cotton + Plastic”).
  • Obtain a REACH declaration for the plastic parts and packaging data for VerpackG/LUCID.
  • Clarify plant versus trading company (Safetree is a trading company per the Made-in-China audit).
