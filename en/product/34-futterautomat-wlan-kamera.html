Description

The smart feeder portions dry food on a schedule and is controlled via app over Wi-Fi; the camera version adds a wide-angle camera overlooking the bowl, optionally with microphone and speaker for two-way audio.

More

High-quality units have a stainless-steel bowl, a removable washable hopper, jam detection and a battery backup for power cuts. Premium buyers expect a mature app with German-language interface, GDPR-compliant cloud connection and updates. Legally it is radio equipment (RED) with a power supply and, with a camera, data-protection relevant.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Fressnapf lists 20 feeders under “smart solutions”, almost all EUR 69–195; Petlibro holds 10 of the 20 listings.

More

Camera models cost EUR 99 (Zedar A900, 6 l) to EUR 195 (Petlibro Granary Dual with camera); PETKIT with camera EUR 139. Most items have at most 6 reviews – the market is still young. Risks: radio testing and cybersecurity (RED), app/cloud data protection, higher return rate for electronics, brand price pressure from Petlibro/PETKIT.

Request from the supplier

  • Request RED test report (radio, EMC, safety, cybersecurity per EN 18031) and EU declaration of conformity for the specific model
  • Clarify app provider, cloud location and data-protection concept (GDPR, camera and audio data, update and support period)
  • Power supply: CE/LVD report, EU plug (type C/F) and cable length; backup battery type and battery marking
  • Food-contact materials (stainless bowl, plastics) with LFGB / food-contact evidence; test jam and spill safety
  • Check German app language, German manual, time-zone/daylight-saving function and offline operation if Wi-Fi fails
  • Endurance-test samples (e.g. 500 portions, different kibble sizes) and define incoming inspection per batch
