Access Equipment & Work at Height

Mobile elevating work platforms, rolling towers, platform and multi-purpose ladders and system scaffolding are the daily access tools on strip-out, refurbishment and asbestos sites …

Direct import of lifts from China rarely pays off; buying through EU dealers or EU production (mind the origin) is more realistic. 2) For lifts demand the CE declaration of conformity, a German operating manual and EN 280 evidence from the manufacturer. 3) Ladders: request EN 131 test reports from an accredited body; the listings show no certificates. 4) Scaffolding: EN 12810/12811 are only claimed; in Germany also clarify the general building-authority approval (abZ) and the assembly and use instructions; the Tianjin Easy and Wellmade profiles are flagged as unverified, and the Wellmade price of 0.10 USD per piece (FOB, MOQ 1,000) is not reliable and was not adopted as an SKU. 5) Prices: the DE scissor lift price of 12,900 EUR net is a single dealer ad without a seller name; no CN price found for the lift (the Dingli catalogue shows no prices).
