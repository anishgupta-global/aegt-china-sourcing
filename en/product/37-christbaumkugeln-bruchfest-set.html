Request from the supplier

  • Require heavy-metal evidence (EN 71-3 or REACH test report on lead, cadmium, chromium VI) for paint and glitter.
  • Glitter finish: obtain written confirmation that the glitter is bound and does not fall under the microplastics restriction (EU) 2023/2055.
  • Document shatter resistance with a drop test (agree the test method with the supplier) and check fragment behaviour.
  • Cap and hanging eyelet: check tensile strength, no sharp edges, cap metal free of nickel/lead residues.
  • Approve colour and size on samples (Pantone match); label the PVC box with EAN, GPSR label and piece count.
  • Check the audit: request BSCI/SGS reports and verify TÜV Rheinland audit ID MIC-ASR2531122 on the official site.
