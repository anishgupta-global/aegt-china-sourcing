Samsung Flip WM75FX for EUR 2,876 and Yealink MB75C (with camera and microphones) for EUR 4,264; the 65-inch variant iiyama TE6513A-B3AG costs EUR 1,329. The list price of USD 699-850 (Ikinor IK75) is far below, but requires safeguards on software (Android 11), service and warranty (1 year per listing); the unit weighs 68 kg gross and ships as freight-forwarder goods. Buyers in education and administration typically demand branded service, EDLA certification and data-protection evidence (assessment, not evidenced). Risks: transport damage, touch calibration, returns, missing updates.

Request from the supplier

  • Clarify the software in writing: Android version (11 is dated), update and patch commitment, EDLA/Google services or AOSP without Play Store, no forced cloud and no unexplained telemetry (GDPR).
  • Test touch on a sample: infrared or P-cap (the maker offers both), touch points, pen and palm detection, calibration, behaviour in sunlight.
  • Check optics: brightness (listing max. 400 cd/m2 typ.), panel maker and type, pixel-fault class, optical bonding per company profile, 4 mm glass.
  • Price the OPS module (i3/i5/i7) separately, clarify OPS standard and Windows licence; fix the scope of supply (pens, wall bracket with load rating for 68 kg gross, remote control, camera).
  • Request test reports: EN 62368-1, EN 55032/EN 55035, RoHS, for Wi-Fi/Bluetooth RED with EN 18031; verify BV audit report MIC-ASR252695 on the BV website; declaration of conformity with EU responsible person.
  • Logistics and warranty: pallet 1,820 x 1,240 x 200 mm at 68 kg, freight with lifting aid and transport insurance; negotiate the 1-year warranty up to 2-3 years with an exchange process.
