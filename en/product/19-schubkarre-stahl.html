Description

The construction wheelbarrow is the standard tool for moving rubble, screed residue, mortar and building materials during demolition and strip-out.

More

Professional versions have a deep-drawn, galvanised steel tray with reinforcing ribs, welded tubular handles and a pneumatic wheel with steel rim and ball bearings. Users in Germany expect a design tested for its rated load (typically 100 l / 250 kg), handles and tyres low in PAHs and product safety under the ProdSG and GPSR; a GS mark is regarded as a strong quality signal.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Static load test on the sample (e.g. 1.5 times rated load without permanent deformation) with test report
  • GS/TÜV test report or at least a test report to AfPS GS 2019:01 PAK for handles and tyres
  • REACH declaration (SVHC) and declaration of conformity under the GPSR naming a responsible economic operator in the EU
  • Salt-spray test report (ISO 9227) and information on zinc coating thickness or the steel-sheet material certificate
  • Have tray volume (l), rated load (kg) and tyre specification confirmed in writing (CN listing gives no capacity data)
  • Packaging: tray, wheel and frame shipped knocked-down (KD) with palletising plan, labelling and spare parts (inner tube, axle)
