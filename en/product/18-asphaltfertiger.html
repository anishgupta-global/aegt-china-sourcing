Description

The crawler paver lays asphalt layers with a basic width of 2.5–4.75 m and an electrically heated screed that extends hydraulically to up to 6 m; hopper capacity of 14 t …

More

laydown rate of up to 400 t/h and layer thicknesses of up to 350 mm cover municipal and rural roads. According to the dealer listing, the RP603 runs a Cummins QSB6.7 engine (129 kW), CAN communication, ultrasonic material-level control and constant-speed travel control. What needs clarifying above all is the Stage V engine (the listing only states EC Stage 3), the manufacturer attribution and the availability of screed heating and wear parts in Germany.

E-commerce potential

Not a classic online volume product: asphalt pavers are bought via specialist dealers, tenders, machinery exchanges (maschinensucher.de, mascus.de, truckscout24.de) or direct import …

More

new-machine prices in Germany are mostly not public ('on request'). No bestseller or trend data was collected for this class. Return risk is high (transport, registration, Stage V) – only offer with documents and a service partner clarified in advance.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EU type-approval or Stage V certificate (Reg. (EU) 2016/1628) for the exact engine fitted; for the Cummins QSB6.7 the listing only states 'EC Stage 3' – get a Stage V variant (e.g. with exhaust aftertreatment) confirmed in writing. A Stage V compliant engine is a prerequisite for placing the machine on the EU market.
  • Request the CE declaration of conformity, technical file and a German operating manual; check the nameplate with serial number and LWA sound power level.
  • Get spare-parts and service coverage in Germany/EU confirmed in writing (dealer, parts lists, response time, warranty terms).
  • The listing gives no brand/manufacturer (trademark 'Top brand'): RP603 is the XCMG model designation and look-alikes are on the market – have XCMG confirm original manufacture, serial number and screed type in writing.
  • Transport dimensions 6.68 × 2.50 × 4.00 m, 18.5 t: plan a low-loader/flat-rack; specify screed width, conveyor and heating (generator) in the quote.
