Description

The set turns the compressor into a workshop and garage tool: the blow gun cleans workpiece and workplace, the tyre inflator fills tyres …

More

and the PU spiral hose with quick coupling connects both to the compressed-air outlet. Discerning buyers in Germany expect an ageing- and kink-resistant PU hose, a blow gun with a metal nozzle, a tyre inflator with brass valve clamp and pressure gauge, and a coupling profile that fits the compressor outlet (usual in Germany: Euro profile NW 7.2). The chosen set is designed for 0–8 bar, is available with 5, 8 or 10 m hose and, according to the listing, contains a tyre inflator without a stated pressure gauge. Hoses and couplings are wear parts and therefore repeat-purchase items; for EU online sales, GPSR labelling with an EU responsible person, REACH compliance (plasticisers, lead in brass) and a German manual stating the pressure are required.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the coupling profile (Euro NW 7.2) in writing and test samples at the outlet of the target compressor: tightness, locking force, coupling under pressure
  • Hose: outside/inside diameter, length, working and burst pressure test report, recoil and kink resistance; PU compound and plasticisers under REACH
  • Tyre inflator: low-lead brass, tightness of the valve clamp on car valves, add a pressure gauge with accuracy class (EN 837-1) if wanted
  • Request CE, RoHS and REACH statements from the listing and company profile as test reports (so far self-declarations only)
  • Continuous-pressure and coupling-cycle test on samples at 8–10 bar; sample inspection for 100 % tightness before shipment
  • German labelling and manual (max. pressure, eye protection, do not point at people), blister card with EAN, EU responsible person under GPSR
