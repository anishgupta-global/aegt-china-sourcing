Description

The layout robot transfers CAD and BIM plans true to scale onto the floor slab: wall axes, door openings …

More

installation and fit-out positions are positioned by total station or RTK-GNSS and marked directly with ink or paint. It replaces chalk lines and manual setting-out and saves surveyor and foreman time on large floors. Professional users in Germany expect a verifiable marking accuracy in the millimetre range, compatibility with common total stations (e.g. Leica, Trimble, Topcon), DXF / DWG / IFC import and an accuracy report to ISO 17123. Electrics, radio (RTK / remote control) and laser must be documented as CE-compliant.

Internal · notes

Request from the supplier

  • Accuracy report (set vs. actual marking over at least 20 m) and calibration certificate of the positioning to ISO 17123
  • EU declaration of conformity under the Machinery Directive incl. test reports for radio (RED, RTK / remote control) and EMC
  • Proof of total-station compatibility (Leica / Trimble / Topcon interface) and of the DXF / DWG / IFC import formats with a test run
  • Laser classification to EN 60825-1 and data sheet of the marking paint (REACH / safety data sheet, can be painted over without residue)
  • German software interface and manual, software updates, EU spare-parts commitment and 24-month warranty
