Description

The assortment delivers 100 to 200 mini toys per batch: soft mini squishies and fidget minis as the core, complemented by erasers, stamps and yo-yos.

More

It is designed for pinata filling, party-favour bags, children's birthdays, carnival and school cones and thus covers demand all year round. Commercial buyers look for a controlled number of articles, clean individual packs or bags with EU labelling, and the exclusion of magnets, button cells and water beads. Every single piece is a toy in its own right and needs its own EN 71 conformity.

Request from the supplier

  • EN 71-1/-2/-3 test reports per single article (every mini toy has its own report); ask for a matrix article to report and limit the number of articles
  • Chemistry: have phthalates (REACH entries 51/52), PAH (entry 50) and heavy metals on TPR and coloured parts verified by an accredited lab; include an odour test
  • Exclusion list in the supply contract: no magnets, button cells, water beads, liquid-filled parts; subject food imitations (dumpling, croissant) to a risk assessment
  • Individual pack/bag with manufacturer or importer, batch number, German warnings and EU responsible person (GPSR)
  • Spot-check the assortment composition before loading (AQL); agree price per piece or weight per article, keep the MOQ per article low
