Description

The headset with a D-shaped ear hook and lapel PTT microphone is the discreet standard solution for security staff, reception, hospitality and site management.

More

The swivelling ear hook fits either ear, and the Kevlar-reinforced PU cable (coiled or straight) and metal clip for lapel or collar withstand daily use. The electret condenser microphone transmits speech clearly, and the PTT button starts transmission without reaching for the radio. Professional buyers look for the right plug type (Kenwood K type, 2-pin), a documented PTT life, REACH-compliant cable materials and GPSR labelling with an EU responsible person.

Request from the supplier

  • Fix the plug type in writing (Kenwood K type 2-pin vs Motorola M type) and test on the target radio: speech, microphone, PTT.
  • Request CE/RoHS test reports and a REACH declaration for the PU cable; GPSR: EU responsible person plus batch and type marking on the packaging.
  • Have PTT life (a competitor's reference: 100,000 cycles) and a cable pull test documented; compare series samples against the approved sample.
  • OEM packaging with type, pin-out and barcode; order spare ear pieces and clips too.
  • Quality assurance: pre-shipment inspection (AQL) and payment via Made-in-China Secured Trading or letter of credit.
