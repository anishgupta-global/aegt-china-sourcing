06 · Construction supplies

Hand Tools

Hand tools for demolition, strip-out and renovation – from shovels, sledge hammers and wrecking bars to bolt cutters, spirit levels and brick trowels …

More

are made in China in specialised forging, stamping and extrusion clusters with very low unit prices but widely varying quality. For professional use in Germany, what counts is a material certificate, a secure head-to-handle fit, GS testing and an EU responsible person; the price advantage over retail is large for standard goods but shrinks after freight, duty, testing costs and minimum order quantities. Extended by broom, spade, carpenter's hammer, concreter's nippers, hand stapler, builder's bucket and mortar tub so that every tool variant appears exactly once with a single supplier.

Hand tools cluster in China by product: steel shovels and spades in Tangshan/Luannan (Hebei), forged hammers, axes and wrecking bars in Shandong (Laoling, Linyi) and Hebei (Xingtai, Cangzhou), pliers and bolt cutters in Zhangjiagang (Jiangsu) and Hebei, spirit levels and small ironware in the Yongkang/Jinhua cluster (Zhejiang), while Hangzhou hosts the GreatStar group headquarters with its own EU distribution. Tangshan Miracle (Hebei, next to Renhe), Linyi Weiye (Shandong, next to Pangu) and Lanxi Kangfeng (Zhejiang, in the Jinhua cluster next to Guide) join as neighbours of the known suppliers.

The Pangu price of US$2.50 is a single figure across all head weights of 2–16 lb; for the 5 kg hammer, the Fixtec/EBIC listing (US$11.50–12.20 per piece complete with handle, MOQ 2 pcs, basis not stated) is used instead – a small-quantity price, request a quote for container quantities; the Zhengyang price applies to 'Multiple Sizes' 14–48 in and is not specific to 750 mm. German comparison prices are retail gross prices (Hornbach €18.00; Toolineo €156.32 gross/€131.36 net as the upper professional segment; Amazon €41.90); cheap offers from about €30 (5 kg hammer) and about €18 (bolt cutter 750 mm) showed up in the search but the pages were not opened. Standards/certificates: German professional buyers expect DIN 1042 and the GS mark for sledge hammers and the German patterns (Frankfurt/Holstein, 130 cm handle) for shovels – Chinese standard goods have 1,200 mm handles and different blade shapes and must be specified as OEM; the listings mostly name only ISO 9001 (Pangu: GS per profile), so request GS/test certificates before ordering; the EU General Product Safety Regulation (GPSR) requires an EU responsible person and labelling. Duty: bolt cutters fall under 8203 40 00 (pipe cutters, bolt cutters), not 8203 30; third-country duty for origin CN per Access2Markets is 1.7 % (8201 10, 8203 40) and 3.7 % (8205 20 per zolltarifnummern.de); anti-dumping measures for these codes were not shown on the pages opened, check TARIC before clearance; from 1 July 2026 a €3 duty per item applies to small consignments up to €150 (TM1066). Excluded: Zhangjiagang Superfix Tools (trading company, only 6–10 in pliers), C&A Hardware Tools (US$0.95 for 'European Type Bolt Cutter' implausible for 750 mm), Wedo/Cangzhou Dean/Hebei Ouyang (non-sparking special hammers, premium prices), Luannan Fada, Tangshan Shushi and the Linyi hammer suppliers (directory/press profiles only, no own page opened), Hebei Sinotools (only an 18 in listing). Contacts: No own website was found for Pangu, Renhe and Guide, only Made-in-China storefronts without phone/e-mail (Pangu names 'Ms. May Liu, Manager' there, Guide 'Mr. Tom, Sale Manager'); GreatStar contact via en.greatstartools.com or BeA Ahrensburg; BeA's affiliation with GreatStar is documented on the GreatStar website (Ahrensburg site), while bea-group.com does not name the parent company. Not included as a product: buckets/mortar tubs (plastic, different cluster e.g. Taizhou/Huangyan), brooms, side cutters, staplers – GreatStar (Arrow staplers, Workpro pliers) covers these in its range. Method: Made-in-China and Alibaba search pages block fetches. Addendum v2 (30 Oct 2026), new products wrecking bar, aluminium spirit level, brick trowel: All CN prices come from search snippets of Made-in-China listing pages (price, MOQ and supplier not verified on the individual page); the wrecking-bar price applies to 24 in (610 mm), not 750 mm; for the spirit level the listing does not state the length and the price suggests entry-level quality; the trowel prices apply to stamped blades, forged trowels cost roughly three to five times as much. German references: OBI wrecking bar 800 mm Comfort €22.99, Amazon.de Stanley Klassik 120 cm €28.55 (Contorion magnetic €43.99 or €33.99 depending on variant), Amazon.de Haromac brick trowel €10.51 (OBI brick trowel €5.19 with an outdated page status, Hornbach Triuso forged €24.13); Amazon prices are consumer prices, check VAT shown in the basket. The HS codes of the new products (8205 59 80, 9015 30 90, 8205 59 10) come from tariff knowledge and were not seen on any page, duty rates stay empty – check TARIC/customs information before clearance; likewise check standard numbers (e.g. DIN 1042, EN 10132-4) against the standard text before ordering. The new makers (Tangshan Miracle, Lanxi Kangfeng, Linyi Weiye) are evidenced only by Made-in-China listing pages (no contact data, factory status open). Images: The schema has no image field; for the three original products the earlier listing images were kept, for the new products the German retailer page is stored as the reference-image page (direct image files not extracted; manufacturer reference images of German brands such as Stabila, Haromac or Hilti were not collected in this run). New in v3: no manufacturer was evidenced for broom and mortar tub; carpenter's hammer, concreter's nippers and hand stapler have no manufacturer price (hand stapler only a reference price from Hebei Sinotools); the builder's bucket price comes from a directory hit without a single-listing URL; HS codes of the seven new products are provisional and duty rates not yet verified; some DE prices are Austrian prices (spade, mortar tub) and are flagged.
