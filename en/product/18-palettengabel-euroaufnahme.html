Description

The pallet fork with Euro hitch turns a compact loader or farm loader into a pallet handler for building sites, farms and warehouses.

More

The fork carrier is hooked into the loader's Euro quick-hitch frame and locked; the tines can be adjusted sideways and carry Euro pallets. Quality markers are structural steel with clean welds, a fork carrier with load backrest or load guard, hardened tines with defined length and capacity, and a reliably locking Euro hook. Before purchase, hitch standard (Euro, Weidemann, skid-steer plate), carrier width, tine length and capacity must be matched with the carrier machine and load centre.

Request from the supplier

  • Match hitch standard and dimensions bindingly: Euro frame (hook and pin spacing, locking) against drawing and sample of the target machine; match tine length and carrier width to the Euro pallet.
  • Fix rated load and load centre in writing: the listing model DT10 carries 1,000 kg, German competitors offer Euro forks with 2 t; require a rating plate with the rated load and enquire about a reinforced custom version if needed.
  • Request a 3.1 material certificate for the steel, weld inspection and a load test (e.g. 1.25 × rated load); check hardened tines and load backrest.
  • Require CE marking and EC declaration of conformity for the attachment (Machinery Directive, from 20 Jan 2027 Machinery Regulation) and operating instructions in German.
  • Clarify price tiers per model (listing: US$ 470 for 1–9 pcs, US$ 370 from 10 pcs, without model allocation); fix warranty (listing: 1 year), paint finish and packaging (pallet/iron crate).
  • Check the supplier: BV audit (ID MIC-ASR245124), certificates (ISO, CE) and references in the Euro area; clarify business type (manufacturer and trader).
