Description

The FFP1 mask is the entry level of filtering half masks to EN 149 and protects when working with non-toxic, non-fibrogenic dusts, for example when sanding, sweeping or gardening.

More

It retains at least 80 % of the test particles and, under DGUV Regel 112-190, is rated up to four times the occupational exposure limit. Buyers in Germany expect CE marking with the notified body number, batch and standard marking on the mask and German instructions; FFP1 is not sufficient for quartz, hardwood or asbestos dust.

Internal · notes

E-commerce potential

Consumable with repeat purchase: Hornbach sells the 3M 8710PRO20 (FFP1, pack of 20) for EUR 24.50, i.e. EUR 1.23 per mask; competition runs on brand trust and pack size.

More

Regulatory risk (PPE category III, counterfeits) is high, so sell only with an evidenced EU certificate.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the EU type-examination certificate to EN 149 with NB number for exactly this model and cross-check it in NANDO; the listing only says “EN149”.
  • Check that the mask itself carries the standard number, class (FFP1 NR D), CE and NB number, batch and expiry date.
  • Have samples tested by an independent lab (filter penetration, breathing resistance, total inward leakage).
  • Have the plant, production control (module C2/D) and manufacturer status evidenced; confirm prices from 10,000 pcs in writing.
