Description

The electric scarifier removes coatings, adhesives, bitumen residues, markings and loose screed layers with a rotating milling drum and roughens concrete for subsequent coatings.

More

With a 250 mm working width and 7.5 kW drive it is the economical solution in demolition and renovation when hand tools would be too slow. Professional users expect a robust drum bearing, a quick cutter-ring change, a continuously adjustable milling depth and a large dust port for class H industrial vacuums. Prerequisites are CE marking under the Machinery Directive, electrics for 400 V three-phase and a German manual; where asbestos is suspected, use is possible only in a sealed-off area under TRGS 519.

Request from the supplier

  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC and 2014/30/EU with CE marking (Xingyi gives no CE information for the CS250E)
  • Risk assessment to EN ISO 12100 and test report on the electrics to EN 60204-1 for 3~ 400 V / 50 Hz

  • German manual and spare-parts list; cutter rings, bearings and belts as a spare-parts package with a price list
  • Proof of dust extraction (port matching class H vacuums) and sound power and vibration values
  • At least 24 months warranty; check a sample before the series order
