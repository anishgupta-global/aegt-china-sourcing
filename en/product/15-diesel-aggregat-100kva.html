A professional user in Germany expects a robust, soundproofed frame with a spill tray, a tank for at least one working day, Stage V for the engine and a controller with emergency stop, mains monitoring and optional changeover (ATS/AMF). The unit can only be approved with CE conformity under the Machinery Directive, LwA marking under the Outdoor Noise Directive 2000/14/EC and a German operating manual. When buying, clarify whether the stated 100 kVA means standby (ESP) or prime power (PRP), because the manufacturer listing does not say.

Request from the supplier

  • Stage V evidence (EU type approval of the engine with approval number and engine plate); not documented for the listed WP4D108E200 engine
  • EU declaration of conformity under 2006/42/EC (or Regulation 2023/1230 from 20 Jan 2027), 2000/14/EC (LwA value, notified body) and 2014/30/EU
  • Factory test report with load test (25/50/75/100 %) and written statement whether 100 kVA is ESP or PRP
  • German operating manual, wiring diagram and spare parts list; spare parts availability of at least 10 years
  • Warranty of at least 12 months or 2,000 operating hours and a named service partner in the EU
  • Confirm in writing: tank size, spill tray, emergency stop, ATS/AMF, controller brand and alternator brand (e.g. Stamford type)
