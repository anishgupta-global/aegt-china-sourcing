Description

The bricklayer's hammer is the standard tool for fitting, splitting and tapping masonry units and for driving nails and chisels.

More

The 600 g head is made of forged carbon steel and carries a ground face and a chisel edge; the weight is designed for all-day work on masonry. Professionals expect a uniformly hardened head free of forging defects, broken edges against chipping, a firmly joined handle with a non-slip grip zone and a clean finish. The German trade pattern (Berlin or Rhenish pattern) follows a DIN standard; as an imported good it needs GPSR labelling with an EU responsible person, low-PAH grip materials and registered sales packaging.

Request from the supplier

  • Approval sample by drawing: 600 g, Berlin or Rhenish pattern, length approx. 300 mm; the Singyee listing states neither pattern nor length
  • EN 10204 type 3.1 material certificate for the 45# steel, HRC record on face and chisel edge, impact and chipping test
  • Handle joint: pull-out and bending test; for wooden handles check species, origin (FSC/PEFC) and the EU Deforestation Regulation
  • Grip material: REACH/PAH report per AfPS GS 2019:01 (TPR/rubber), clarify the test house if a GS mark is wanted
  • Include an edge guard (plastic cap) in the delivery – the Hornbach comparison model ships without one; rust protection for sea freight
  • GPSR details (EU responsible person, batch, German instructions), LUCID registration, MOQ 1,200 pcs, lead time and factory audit (listing states QC 080000)
