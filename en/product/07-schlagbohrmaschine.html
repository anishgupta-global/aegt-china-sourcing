More

Electrical device with elevated return risk; clarify WEEE (ElektroG) and GPSR before listing; cross-sell with drill bit sets and a case.

Request from the supplier

  • Request the CE declaration of conformity and a test report to EN 62841-1/-2-1 from an accredited lab; verify the CE and GS marks named in the listing by certificate number
  • Cross-check the manufacturer's BV audit (report ID MIC-ASR2531351 per storefront) on the BV website
  • Sample test: endurance run, temperature, hammer performance in concrete/brick, earth/high-voltage test, power rating (810 W vs 850 W in the title)
  • EU plug (type F/Schuko), cable length, German manual and safety notes; GPSR labelling and EU responsible person
  • Clarify WEEE registration number (stiftung ear), packaging data (LUCID) and warranty terms (12 months, 2% spare parts per listing)
