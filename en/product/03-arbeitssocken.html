Description

Work socks are the wear part under every safety shoe: they wick moisture, cushion heel and ball and prevent blisters during eight to ten hours in a shoe with a steel or composite cap.

More

Quality models use a cotton blend fabric with polyamide and elastane (benchmark engelbert strauss Allround Socken Classic: 85 % cotton, 13 % polyamide, 2 % elastane) with volume padding at toe and heel, a mid-calf cut and an elastic cuff that does not slip. Professional buyers expect a stable toe seam, padding that keeps its shape after many washes, machine washing at 40 °C and a complete label with fibre composition. The 3-pack is a classic add-on to safety shoes and workwear, where repeat purchase and volume discounts for businesses are the focus.

E-commerce potential

Work socks are a cheap pick-up item with a high repeat rate: Strauss lists twelve lines in its socks section (Allround, work socks, merino, winter, boot socks), the e.s.

More

Allround Socken Classic light/high 3-pack costs EUR 10.59 (EUR 9.40 from 3 packs), the e.s. work socks warm/high EUR 17.73; Hornbach sells a work socks 3-pack (Terrax Workwear, 100 % cotton, reinforced heel) at EUR 4.95. Per the market scan, Proforto advertises free socks with jackets and shoes and Krahe has its own underwear category including socks. The price band of roughly EUR 5 to 18 per 3-pack leaves room for a mid-range own brand; cross-sell to safety shoes (01-safety-shoe-s3, 01-safety-boot-s3) and thermal base layers. Return risk is low (hygiene item, few size steps), winter peak from October to February (assessment, no sales figures found).

Request from the supplier

  • Have the fibre composition per colour proven by lab test (85/10/5 per listing) and approve the label per (EU) 1007/2011 in German.
  • Compare padding at toe and heel, weight per pair (listing: 62 g) and the EU 39 to 46 size run on the sample against Strauss Allround and the Hornbach 3-pack; check toe seam strength.
  • Request an OEKO-TEX Standard 100 or REACH test report (azo dyes, CMR substances); have wash durability tested after 20 washes at 40 °C (colour, dimensional change, pilling).
  • Clarify the minimum order: the listing shows price tiers from 2,000 pairs but states 3,000 pairs per design, colour and size in the text; confirm in writing the lead time of 30 to 35 days from sample approval.
  • No PPE or protection claims on packaging and in the shop (socks are not PPE under (EU) 2016/425); have cotton origin and supply chain documented (EU Forced Labour Regulation (EU) 2024/3015 applies from 14 Dec 2027).
  • Agree packaging (3-pack band or bag) with EU responsible person, batch marking and LUCID number.
